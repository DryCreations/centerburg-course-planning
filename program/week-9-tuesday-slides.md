## 3. Aviation UAS: Mission Briefings

**Today:** keep researching the four missions and start the client emails.
**Check the standards** against your worksheet and swap any that do not match.

### Slide 1: Standards and agenda

> Create ONE slide for a high school drone class. Title: "This Week: Mission Briefings." Top half,
> headed "What we are covering," lists these with their FULL text: 7.11.4 Describe weather products and
> resources required for preflight planning, current and forecast weather for departure, enroute, and
> arrival phases of flight. 7.1.9 Describe classes of airspace and associated requirements and
> limitations. 7.11.8 Define and describe piloting requirements for special use airspace (SUA), special
> flight rules areas (SFRA), temporary flight restrictions (TFR), and other airspace areas. 1.2.5
> Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended
> audience and purpose. Bottom half, headed "This week": "MON to WED: research four missions: airspace,
> TFRs, weather (7.1.9, 7.11.8, 7.11.4)." "TUE and WED: write each client an email (1.2.5)." "THU: fly
> if the weather is good. If it rains, keep working." "FRI: finish, submit anything missing." One slide.

### Slide 2: Today

> Create one slide for a high school drone class titled "For Each Mission." A short checklist in large
> text: "Airspace: what class? Need authorization?" "TFRs: anything at that place and time?" "Weather:
> METAR and TAF from the nearest airport, at the mission time. Times are in Zulu: subtract 4 hours."
> "Part 107: 400 ft, 3 miles visibility, 500 ft below clouds." Then a box: "The email: your decision in
> the first line, the reason in plain words, what would need to change." One slide.

### Board version

```
THIS WEEK: MISSION BRIEFINGS

          for preflight planning, current and forecast
          weather for departure, enroute, and arrival
  7.1.9   describe classes of airspace and associated
          requirements and limitations

          audience and purpose

  zulu - 4 = ohio time
```
