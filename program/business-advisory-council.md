# Business Advisory Council (BAC)

## Ohio Legal Requirements

### Ohio Revised Code §3313.82 — "Business advisory councils" (Effective March 2, 2021)

Each school district board of education **shall appoint** a business advisory council. Exceptions exist if:
- The district has an agreement with an educational service center (ESC) under §3313.843 or §3313.845 and both parties agree the ESC's council will represent the district.
- The district has an agreement with the business advisory committee of a joint vocational school district (§3313.82(D)).
- Joint vocational school districts and cooperative education school districts are exempt (§3313.82(E)).

The council shall advise and provide recommendations to the board on matters including, but not limited to:
1. The delineation of employment skills and the development of curriculum to instill these skills
2. Changes in the economy and in the job market, and the types of employment in which future jobs are most likely to be available
3. Suggestions for developing a working relationship among businesses, labor organizations, and educational personnel

**Each board determines the membership and organization of its council.** (The state law does not specify minimum member counts or meeting frequency.)

### Ohio Administrative Code §3301-61-03 — "Criteria for career-technical programs" (Effective May 9, 2024)

Section (E) requires:

> Career-technical advisory committees reflecting career fields and authorized by local boards of education will engage business/industry and postsecondary representatives and utilize input from professional associations, labor, government, and the community. Advisory committees will identify new and emerging careers; advise current programs on curriculum, assessment, work-based learning, facilities and equipment; and engage educators to improve and expand programs; and ensure the quality of the program using the department's quality program standards.

| Requirement | Source |
|---|---|
| District must appoint a BAC (or use ESC/JVS agreement) | ORC §3313.82(A) |
| Advise on employment skills and curriculum | ORC §3313.82(B)(1) |
| Advise on economy/job market changes | ORC §3313.82(B)(2) |
| Develop business–education working relationships | ORC §3313.82(B)(3) |
| Engage business/industry and postsecondary representatives | OAC §3301-61-03(E) |
| Utilize input from professional associations, labor, government, community | OAC §3301-61-03(E) |
| Identify new and emerging careers | OAC §3301-61-03(E) |
| Advise on curriculum, assessment, work-based learning, facilities, equipment | OAC §3301-61-03(E) |
| Ensure quality using Ohio's Quality Program Standards | OAC §3301-61-03(E) |
