# BPA Competition — Master Event Reference

**Advisor:** Sole advisor responsible for all four CTE courses
**Courses covered:** Aviation UAS (177024), Design Techniques (145095), Video and Sound (145110), 3D Techniques (145120)

> Students may compete in **any BPA event**, regardless of which course they are enrolled in. Events listed under a specific course are strong natural fits, but students are free to pursue any event that matches their interests, skills, or career goals. Cross-course event choices are encouraged.

---

## Competition Pathway

```
School Competition (January)
        ↓  top performers may choose to advance
Ohio State Leadership Conference (February–March — confirm dates with Ohio State Advisor)
        ↓  state qualifiers may choose to advance
National Leadership Conference (NLC) — Nashville, Tennessee | May 6–10
```

- Advancement at every stage is **the student's choice** — there is no obligation to advance
- **Virtual events** skip the entire qualifying path — any student may register and submit directly to nationals
- All students preparing a BPA submission will participate in the school-level competition (January)
- State and national travel requires additional planning and registration (see dates below)

## Choosing an Event

**Students should ask:**
1. What is my strongest skill so far? (design, video, 3D, speaking, coding, audio)
2. Do I want to work alone or with a team?
3. How much time can I invest outside of class to polish this?
4. Do I want to skip regionals/state and compete nationally only? (→ virtual events)

**General guidance by interest:**
- *I like making visuals / logos / graphics* → 410 Graphic Design Promotion, 460 Visual Design Team, V10 Virtual Branding Team
- *I like making videos* → 420 Digital Media Production, 430 Video Production Team, 445 Broadcast News
- *I like audio / podcasts* → 450 Podcast Production Team
- *I like 3D modeling / animation* → 425 Computer Modeling, 440 Computer Animation Team, V11 2D Animation
- *I like photography* → V06 Promotional Photography
- *I like designing apps / websites / UX* → 455 User Experience Design Team, 435 Website Design Team
- *I like presenting / public speaking* → 545 Prepared Speech, 555 Presentation Individual, 525 Extemporaneous Speech
- *I like running a business / entrepreneurship* → 505 Entrepreneurship, 510 Small Business Management Team
- *I want the lowest barrier entry* → 490 DC&D Concepts Open, 391 IT Concepts Open, 525 Extemporaneous Speech
- *I want a strong résumé regardless of outcome* → 515/520 Interview Skills, V15 Virtual Interview & Digital Portfolio

## BPA Option vs. Class Topic

For every major project in the course, students have two options:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Class Topic** | Use the teacher-assigned concept or client for the project. Graded by the teacher on the class rubric. |
| **BPA Topic** | Use the official WSAP assigned topic/prompt for the project. Graded by the teacher on the same class rubric — but the student is also building a competition entry. May require additional polish and time outside of class. |

Both options fulfill the class assignment. Students who want to compete should be transparent with the teacher early so their work can be structured appropriately.

1. Create a BPA account at bpa.org (chapter advisor handles chapter setup)
