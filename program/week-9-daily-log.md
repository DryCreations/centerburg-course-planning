## Decisions made

**No large new units in the last week of the quarter**, by request. Each class either continues
something already in motion or takes a small next step that fills Monday to Thursday.

**DT: Three Moods**, already built in Week 8 as the Week 9 plan, and updated rather than rebuilt.
**Students now use a photo they edited last week** (the cityscape, the portrait, or a color photo), so
there is no new photo set to source. The mood table now uses the tools they gained after it was first
written, HSL and color grading, and adds a fifth option, **one color only.** Neutral version Monday, one
mood Tuesday, one Wednesday, defend Thursday. **Due Thursday because Friday is the quiz.**

**Typography (Unit 1.3) was offered and set aside.** It was on the map for Weeks 6 and 7 and is still
owed; it goes on the list for next quarter.

**V&S: titles and motion in CapCut, as version 2 of the edit.** Audio was offered as the natural next
unit and declined; three-point lighting and a shoot-and-cut sequence were also offered. The chosen
option is the smallest: no camera checkout, and it builds on the scrolling credits they just made. Four
rules for text (read it twice, never touching the edge, stands out from the picture, two fonts at most),
then a lower third Monday, an In and Out animated title Tuesday, custom keyframed text Wednesday (**move
in, hold, move out**, with the hold as the part people skip), export Thursday.

**Unit 1.3 Audio remains owed**, about 16% of the WebXam. Flagged for next quarter.

**Aviation: meteorology practice and reports**, the lighter of the options. One practice METAR a day
from the same airport, written to tell one story: **a cold front arriving, passing and leaving**, so
decoding and last week's fronts and air masses finally get used together. Warm humid southerly air
Monday with a TAF that forecasts the wind shift; a thunderstorm, a 1-degree spread and falling pressure
Tuesday; a northwest wind, a dew point crash and rising pressure Wednesday; calm high pressure Thursday.
**Every day ends in a go or no-go with the deciding number.** The rest of each period is ODOT Module 3.

**Question 11 is the one worth discussing out loud:** a perfect clear sky behind the front and still a
likely no-go on a 26-knot gust.

**The practice reports are labeled as practice.** Every day they also pull the real KCMH METAR, and
**Thursday's real METAR decides whether they fly**, using last week's drills.

**Unit 2.2 Preflight was offered as the next unit and set aside.** Its lab is a strong start for next
quarter: sectional chart reading on a printed excerpt, NOTAMs and TFRs, an aircraft inspection and
density altitude.

**MS CS: build week**, with grade check-ins. The project is due Friday.

**Yearbook keeps working.** The Week 9 check-in is due Friday.
