**Status:** deferred out of Week 7, to run across all courses at once.

## Why it moved

Aviation was going to select on Fri Oct 2. **Too early**, and it would have happened in isolation.

Several of these courses point at BPA events, and a student in two of them should not get the
conversation twice, in two different framings, a week apart. **Run it once, deliberately, across the
courses in the same stretch.**

## What a selection day looks like

Fifteen minutes on the options, then students read, decide, and submit three things:

1. **Which event**
2. **Their topic or business idea**, one or two sentences, first draft, can change
3. **Why this one**, one sentence

**The only criterion worth giving them:** pick the one you will still be interested in during February.
