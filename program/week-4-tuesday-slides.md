### Slides

- **Slide 2, Standards + Today:** "Today's standards: 7.6 weather, 7.11 preflight, 7.13 flight environment. Agenda: watch last week's footage, decode a real METAR, work go/no-go, groups pick new roles for Thursday."
- **Slide 3, Footage review:** "As we watch: what worked? What would you change about the shot, the framing, or the timing? Be specific."
- **Slide 4, Reading a real METAR:** "Pulled live from aviationweather.gov. Wind: three digit direction plus speed, like 27008G15KT (that G is a gust). Visibility: statute miles, Part 107 needs at least 3. Clouds: a layer and a height, like BKN008 means broken at 800 feet."
- **Slide 5, Go or no-go:** "Given today's real METAR: what is the wind, gust, visibility, and ceiling? Based on Part 107 limits, is this a go or a no-go?"
- **Slide 6, Do Now:** "With your group: complete the weather worksheet, then write your footage review notes and pick NEW roles (pilot, visual observer, logger) for Thursday's flight."
