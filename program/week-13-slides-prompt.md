# Week 13 Slides: One Deck, One Slide per Class

**Teacher-only.** Mon Nov 9 to Fri Nov 13. Paste the box into Google Slides' "Help me create a
presentation." Each class gets one slide that stays up all week: what to do when you come in, today,
and this week's goals. The career-tech slides open with their standards.

**Each day, change only the "Today" lines** from the table at the bottom.

```
Create a 5-slide presentation for a high school career-tech program, one slide per class. Dark navy background, bold yellow and light blue headings, large readable text, no images except a small simple icon in the corner of each slide. Every slide uses the same layout: a title, a small "Standards" section at the top where listed, then three sections side by side or stacked: "As you come in," "Today," and "This week's goals." Keep text short.

Slide 1, title "Design Techniques": "Standards," in smaller text: "2.9.1 Identify the scope and purpose of branding." "7.5.5 Prepare a type style guide." "2.9.6 Develop and present a comprehensive proposal to stakeholders." "1.3.8 Verify compliance with computer and intellectual property laws and regulations." "As you come in": "Open your BPA project." "Today": "Quiz: copyright and first quarter review (1.3.8)." "Then: brand kit work (2.9.1, 7.5.5)." "This week's goals": "1. Brand kit FIRST: logo, fonts, colors, and a one-pager explaining why, and what each is for." "2. Build everything else from your kit." "3. Start your presentation slides (2.9.6)."

Slide 2, title "Video & Sound": "Standards," in smaller text: "7.7.3 Set up audio, lighting, and scenery for the shoot." "7.7.4 Select a video recording format and shoot the video." "2.9.6 Develop and present a comprehensive proposal to stakeholders." "As you come in": "Check in with your group. What are you recording today?" "Today": "Record as much as you can (7.7.3, 7.7.4)." "Need quiet? Say so before you roll." "This week's goals": "1. Get your footage and audio recorded." "2. Downtime? Build your presentation: about 10 minutes of slides (2.9.6)." "3. We wrap up before Thanksgiving."

Slide 3, title "Unmanned Aircraft Systems": "Standards," in smaller text: "7.11.14 Perform and analyze a preflight assessment." "7.12.10 Demonstrate postflight procedures." "1.2.2 Deliver formal and informal presentations." "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." "As you come in": "Open your BPA project." "Today": "Quiz: preflight, flight safety, and postflight (7.11.14, 7.12.10)." "Then: BPA work (1.2.5)." "This week's goals": "1. A workable version of your actual submission (1.2.5)." "2. Start your presentation slides (1.2.2)." "3. Use every minute of work time."

Slide 4, title "Middle School CS": "Focus: programming with Codeblocks." "As you come in": "Log in to Tinkercad and Classroom." "Today": "How to submit: screenshot, link, turn in." "What is on the quiz, and practice problems." "This week's goals": "1. Build a city with a loop inside a loop." "2. Turn it in Friday: screenshot and link." "3. Quiz Friday, Nov 20."

Slide 5, title "Yearbook": "As you come in": "Check Classroom for your assignments." "Today": "Work on your spreads and photos." "This week's goals": "1. Keep shooting and designing." "2. Upload new photos to eDesign before you leave." "3. Progress Check 2 due Friday, Nov 13."
```
