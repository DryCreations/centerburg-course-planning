# Q2 Gradebook: Assignments and Points

**Teacher-only.** Every graded assignment for Q2, by class, with points and category.

---

## How PowerSchool weighting actually works

**The category weight is a multiplier on points, not a share of the grade.** A 10 point assignment in
a 40% category counts as 4 points.

So a category only gets its intended share **if every category has about the same raw point total.**
If Projects held 300 raw points and Labs held 100, Projects would count far more than its 40% suggests.

**The rule used below: every category totals 100 raw points for the quarter.** Then the multipliers do
exactly what the category names say, and the whole quarter comes to 100 counted points.

| Raw points | Category weight | Counts as |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | 40% | 40 |
| 100 | 30% | 30 |
| 100 | 20% | 20 |
| 100 | 10% | 10 |
| | **Total** | **100** |

**Keep it that way when things change.** If you cut an assignment, move its points to another
assignment in the same category. If you add one, take the points from the same category.

**Extra credit** is entered in a category like anything else, so it is multiplied too. Its counted
value is listed with each one.

**Quiz count:** every other Friday. Q2 has about four quizzes per class, five for Aviation, depending
on where the quarter ends and the holidays fall. If the count changes, split the category's 100 points
evenly across however many there are.

## Yearbook

| Category (weight) | Assignment | Raw | Counts as |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Production and Deadlines (40%)** | Fall spread, finished in the theme by its deadline | 50 | 20 |
| | Winter spread, laid out with photos placed | 50 | 20 |
| **Coverage (25%)** | Assigned shoots, 4 at 25: 15 usable photos, uploaded within 3 school days | 100 | 25 |
| **Craft (20%)** | Photo quality: your best 10 photos | 30 | 6 |
| | Theme applied on your fall spread | 30 | 6 |
| | Spread reviews, 2 at 10 | 20 | 4 |
| | Final edit of a spread | 20 | 4 |
| **Staff Contribution (15%)** | Q2 survey | 10 | 1.5 |
| | Check-ins every other week, 5 at 18 | 90 | 13.5 |

**Extra credit:** any shoot outside your assignment, 15 usable photos, **4 raw in Coverage each, up to
5 shoots** (counts as 1 each, up to 5).

**Why that cap works:** one missed assigned shoot costs 25 raw, which counts as 6.25. The most extra
credit can earn is 5. **Extra shooting never fully covers a skipped assignment.**

**When the backup covers a missed shoot,** the backup gets the 25 for it, and it replaces one of their
own four or counts as extra credit, whichever helps them more.

**Alternative path:** a student who cannot shoot or design gets the same assignments with different
work, for the same points. For example, editing or tagging a batch of 30 incoming photos replaces one
shoot.
