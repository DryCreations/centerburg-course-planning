## Ohio WebXam (End-of-Course Test)

All five courses use the **Ohio CTE WebXam** as their EOC assessment. Key facts:
- Students take a **pretest** in Week 1 as a baseline
- **Initial WebXam:** Target date April 23 (~Week 33 of the school year)
- **Retake window:** Opens 30 days after initial; closes by May 23
- WebXam is administered externally — needs a proctor, room, and scheduling coordination
- Scores directly reflect on program quality reviews

## Business Advisory Council (BAC)

Ohio law (ORC §3313.82) requires each district to appoint a BAC. The BAC advises on:
- Curriculum and employment skills
- Economy and job market changes
- Business–education relationships

Per Ohio Administrative Code §3301-61-03, advisory committees must engage business/industry and postsecondary representatives and advise on curriculum, equipment, facilities, and work-based learning.

**Confirmed:** Knox County Career Center serves as the BAC connection for this program.
