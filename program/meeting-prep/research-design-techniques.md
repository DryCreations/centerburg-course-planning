## What This Course Is

A year-long digital design and marketing course built around Adobe Express. Students learn graphic design principles, typography, photography, UX/UI concepts, and digital marketing. The primary credential target is the **Adobe Certified Professional (AC PRO) — Content Creation and Marketing Using Adobe Express**.

---

## CTAG Dual Credit

Eligible for **CTIM008 Digital Page Layout and Design (3 semester hours)**. Requires alignment documentation and a postsecondary partner agreement. Not automatically active — must be confirmed with the principal.

---

## Standards

Covers two parallel sets:
- **Ohio CTE Strands 1, 2, 7** (Employability, IT Fundamentals, Digital Media — specifically Interactive Media, Graphics, Typography, Photography, UX/UI)
- **AC PRO Exam Domains 1–5** (Digital Marketing, Design Principles, Content Creation, Content Management, Sharing & Publishing)

The Ohio **WebXam** EOC assesses this course. Top EOC weights: 7.4 Graphics (14%), 7.3 Production (12%), 7.5 Typography (12%), 7.9 Photography (12%).
