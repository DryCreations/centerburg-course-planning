## What This Course Is

A year-long course on drone operations, FAA regulations, aerodynamics, meteorology, airspace, GIS, and mission planning. Semester 1 covers foundations and theory; Semester 2 is hands-on flight operations using a school-owned DJI Mavic 3 fleet plus FAA Part 107 exam preparation. The course is the most specialized in the program — it requires outdoor flight approval and carries real regulatory considerations.

## Regulatory Considerations

- The school **cannot administer the official FAA Part 107 exam** — students take it independently at a PSI testing center ($175 fee). No school subsidy documented.
- Outdoor flight requires **LAANC authorization** or a Certificate of Waiver/Authorization (COA) through FAA DroneZone. The school's location relative to controlled airspace determines the process.
- A **site safety plan and principal/district approval** is typically needed before any outdoor flight operations.
