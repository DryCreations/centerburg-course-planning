## What This Course Is

A year-long production course where students plan, shoot, edit, and distribute video and audio content. Students write scripts, create storyboards, operate cameras and microphones in the field, and edit using DaVinci Resolve. The course culminates in a BPA competition entry (video or podcast) that doubles as the summative production project.

## CTAG Dual Credit

Eligible for **CTIM006 Digital Video Production (3 semester hours)**. Requires alignment documentation and postsecondary partner agreement. Must be confirmed — not automatically active.
