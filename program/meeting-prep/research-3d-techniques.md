---

## What This Course Is

A year-long foundational course in 3D design, technical drawing, and fabrication. Students learn CAD modeling in OnShape, polygonal modeling and animation in Blender, basic Python scripting, and 3D printing workflows. It is the **entry point** for students who may continue into Hard Surface Modeling (145140) the following year.
