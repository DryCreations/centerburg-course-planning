## Fixed in the Gimkit reviews

Factually wrong answers corrected, in the Q1 reviews and in the older banks they came from:

- **Towers over 400 feet (2):** Part 107 allows flight above 400 feet AGL within 400 feet of a
  structure, so "you must go around it" was wrong. Now: the tower rises above you, keep clear of it
- **Registration (2):** the 0.55 pound threshold applies to recreational flyers. Under Part 107, every
  drone is registered regardless of weight. Both now say "a recreational flyer"
- **Night flight:** now says anti-collision lights visible for 3 miles
- **LiPo fire:** "water will not put it out" was wrong; water is used to cool lithium battery fires.
  Replaced with: a fire that looks out can reignite
- **Beyond visual line of sight:** removed a distractor that could be argued correct
- **"Ceiling":** one question used it to mean a flight limit while another used the weather meaning.
  Now it asks about a LAANC grid ceiling
- **Heading:** in aviation, heading is where the nose points and **track** is the path over the
  ground. The Week 3 controller handout defined heading as the direction of travel. The Gimkit now
  uses the aviation definitions; the handout was left as is, since it was already shared
- **V&S, offloading:** "footage left on the card gets overwritten" is now "a card can fail or be wiped,
  and the footage is gone"
