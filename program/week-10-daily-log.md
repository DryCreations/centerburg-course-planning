## Decisions made

**Q2 documents paint in broad strokes, by request.** Each class has a `q2-plan.md` with the quarter in
three or four rough phases, so plans can change without breaking a promise.

**Yearbook: no event list.** Coverage is by area (sports, clubs, arts, student life, academics), not
by event. **Fall is over:** finish placing photos, source missing ones from coaches and advisors, and
apply the theme, **Seasons of Our Lives**, so fall pages look consistent. **Winter** gets a main
photographer and a backup per area. Goals for the quarter: fall pages fully edited, winter laid out with
all photos placed.

**Yearbook workload, per student:** 4 shoots, 15 usable photos per shoot, 2 spreads designed, 2
reviewed, 1 edited. **Students who cannot shoot or design** earn the same credit with small tasks:
editing incoming photos, tagging in eDesign, name checks, captions, collecting coach photos.
**Students who made real progress on a Q1 spread usually keep it.** No work, no credit, but the backup
system makes sure nothing goes uncovered.

**Today is the survey.** 25 questions in a Google Form: roles, fall and winter spreads from the ladder,
winter areas, availability, the theme, and an alternative path. The teacher notes explain how to turn
the answers into assignments.

**DT: Q2 is BPA.** Everyone enters **Promotional Photography** plus one chosen event, individual or
team. **V&S: color grading this week, then BPA**, with Promotional Photography optional. **Aviation:
Presentation (alone or team) is the main recommendation**, since the topic can cover any of the
aviation standards, which make up about 83% of the end-of-course test. Entrepreneurship is the second
option and becomes the capstone. ODOT and review keep running.

**Event names and numbers are not in student documents.** The repo's BPA references are from an older
guide, and this year's list could not be confirmed online. The WSAP you post is what counts.

**MS CS: one broad unit, Exploring Tinkercad.** 3D design (Weeks 10 to 12, a few prints to test the
printers), Codeblocks to build 3D models with block code (13 to 14), circuit labs on how computer
hardware works: input, processing, output (15 to 17), then a final project combining two parts.
Onshape is a possible Q3 step.

**Quizzes stay every other week.** **Aviation quizzes this Friday** on weather and the mission
briefings: METAR and TAF decoding, Zulu time, the Part 107 weather limits, airspace, TFRs, and writing
to a client. 24 questions. **MS CS skips this week's quiz** to start Tinkercad; the first is Fri Oct 30
on 3D design. **DT and V&S** quizzed Oct 16, so their next is Fri Oct 30.

## Grading: points, not percentages

**PowerSchool's category weight multiplies points** rather than setting a share of the grade, so a
10 point assignment in a 40% category counts as 4. **Every class's Q2 assignments are now planned so each
category totals 100 raw points**, which makes the multipliers come out to the intended weights and the
whole quarter to 100 counted points. The full list, with extra credit for each class, is in
`program/q2-gradebook.md`.

**Yearbook in points:** spreads 50 and 50 (Production and Deadlines), 4 shoots at 25 (Coverage), photo
quality, theme, reviews, and edit (Craft), survey and weekly check-ins (Staff Contribution). Extra
credit is 4 raw per unassigned shoot in Coverage, counting 1 each, up to 5, less than the 6.25 a missed
assigned shoot costs. The survey and `q2-plan.md` now show points.

**MS CS final project:** to be decided near the end of the quarter, since the circuits are simulated and
combining all three parts may not fit.
