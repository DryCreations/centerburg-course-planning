## Design Techniques

```
This Week: Brand Kit First

Your brand kit comes first, before anything else in your project:
- Your logo
- Your fonts
- Your colors
- A one-pager that explains your reasoning: what each one is and what it is for

Then build every other piece from your kit, using the same logo, fonts, and colors. Goal this week: a finished brand kit and one-pager, and a start on your presentation.

Standards: 2.9.1 Identify the scope and purpose of branding. 7.5.5 Prepare a type style guide.
```

## Video & Sound

```
This Week: Record

This week we record as much as we can.
- Need quiet? Tell everyone before you roll, and keep it quiet when others ask
- Slate every take and check your audio levels
- Downtime, when you cannot record or edit? Work on your presentation: about 10 minutes of slides. You can work on different parts at different times

We are wrapping up before Thanksgiving.

Standards: 7.7.3 Set up audio, lighting, and scenery for the shoot. 7.7.4 Select a video recording format and shoot the video.
```
