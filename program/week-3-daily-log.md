## Tuesday Sep 1: today's plan

### Design Techniques: hierarchy tools, then sketch
- Run the **Hierarchy Toolbox** mini-lesson: `courses/design-techniques/planning/week-3/handouts/hierarchy-toolbox.md`
  (the six tools, the three-levels rule, the squint test).
- Then students **sketch on paper**: three levels, one starred focal point, which tools make it win.
- No Express until the sketch has a clear #1. Keep pulling photos for the chosen topic.

### Video & Sound: daylight vs. indoor white balance
- Take the cameras to the **cafeteria / end of the hallway** for a **daylight** shot, plus one **indoors** under
  classroom light, and one deliberately wrong white balance, so they **see the color shift** for themselves.
- Shoot **RAW**, offload the files. Editing (in **Adobe Camera Raw**) is tomorrow.

### Aviation UAS: get both groups to a workable plan
- Hand out the **Mission Plan Worksheet**: `courses/aviation-uas/planning/week-3/handouts/mission-plan-worksheet.md`.
- **Over-scoped group:** use the "Scope too big? Trim it down" box, cut to **one** primary clip, push the rest
  to a stretch list.
- **No-subject group:** use the "Stuck on a subject? Idea prompts" box to pick one and make it theirs.
- Both groups leave today with: subject, one primary shot, airspace check started, boundaries, roles.

### Middle School CS: bell ringer + example, then lab
- Run **`courses/middle-school-cs/planning/week-3/handouts/bell-ringer-tue.md`**: a decision-trace bell ringer
  plus the worked **`if`-on-overlap** example (good → score up, bad → lose a life) and the win/lose checks.
- Then break out to the **Decision Game** lab; today's target is the overlap decision.

### Yearbook: eDesign walk-through + Page Checklist module
- Hand out the **Page Checklist module** (`courses/yearbook/planning/week-3/handouts/page-checklist-module.md`);
  students paste it into a side text box on **every** spread.
- Go through eDesign **live**: show the **preset dropdown in the top-left** and the difference between a
  caption, byline, and photo credit (tell them **ask if unsure**). Frame as **choices, not a formula:** page vs
  spread + cohesion (split spreads still read as one), the focal point isn't always a big hero (team photo
  edge-to-edge is fine), **stories are optional and new** (body + byline only where there's a story; encourage a
  few real stories since I want some in the book), and everyone signs a **"design by"** credit. Drive home
  **placeholders/templates**; put filler text in every box.
- Students continue the spread/page they chose Monday (old or new book); Friday is the graded PDF check-in.
