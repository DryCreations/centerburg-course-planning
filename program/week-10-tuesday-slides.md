## 2. Video & Sound: Color Grading

### Slide 1: Standards and agenda

> Create ONE slide for a high school video production class. Title: "Q2, Week 1." Top half, headed
> "What we are covering," lists these with their FULL text: 7.2.7 Select colors based on color theory
> and psychology. 7.3.7 Edit video footage. 2.9.2 Determine the scope and purpose of the project. 2.9.5
> Develop a timeline, a communication plan, a task breakdown, costs (e.g., equipment, labor),
> deliverables, and responsibilities for completion. Bottom half, headed "This week": "TUE: color
> grading together, then color your Bridge Scene (7.2.7)." "WED: finish color, music, title, credits
> (7.3.7). Tour the BPA events (2.9.2)." "THU: choose your event and team (2.9.2)." "FRI: Bridge Scene
> due. Start your BPA plan (2.9.5)." One slide.

### Slide 2: Today

> Create one slide for a high school video class titled "Color Grading." Two numbered steps. "1.
> CORRECT each clip: Contrast and Blacks until something is truly dark. Temperature until white looks
> white. Copy to matching clips." "2. GRADE the whole scene: add an adjustment layer above every clip,
> then use color wheels, HSL, and curves on it." Then a box: "Pick one mood. Keep it subtle. Free tools
> only: if it has a crown, skip it." One slide.

### Board version

```
Q2, WEEK 1
  7.2.7  select colors based on color theory and psychology
  7.3.7  edit video footage
  2.9.2  determine the scope and purpose of the project
  2.9.5  develop a timeline, task breakdown, deliverables,
         and responsibilities for completion

  TUE color   WED finish + BPA tour   THU choose
  FRI bridge scene due, BPA plan

COLOR GRADING
  1. CORRECT each clip. copy to matching clips.
  2. GRADE on an adjustment layer above everything.
  one mood. subtle. crown = skip it.
```
