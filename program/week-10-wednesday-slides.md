## 1. Design Techniques: Through the Frame

**Handout:** `through-the-frame.md`. Show five or six "frame within a frame" examples on the projector,
then work time.

### Slide 1: Standards and agenda

> Create ONE slide for a high school design class. Title: "This Week." Top half, headed "What we are
> covering," lists these with their FULL text: 7.9.4 Determine composition, formal qualities, scale, and
> use of space. 7.9.5 Use International Standards Organization (ISO), shutter speed, aperture, and white
> balance settings to shoot manual photographs. 2.9.5 Develop a timeline, a communication plan, a task
> breakdown, costs (e.g., equipment, labor), deliverables, and responsibilities for completion. Bottom
> half, headed "This week": "WED: Through the Frame, examples and a shoot plan (7.9.4)." "THU and FRI:
> shoot, then work on your second event (7.9.5, 2.9.5)." Then a small line: "First drafts: Promotional
> Photography Fri Nov 6, second event Fri Nov 13." One slide.

### Slide 2: Today

> Create one slide for a high school photography class titled "Through the Frame." Body: "Something in
> the scene frames your subject." Then a short list of frame ideas: "Doorways and windows." "Arches and
> hallways." "Branches." "Fences and gaps." "Reflections." "Light and shadow." Then a box: "The frame
> points at the subject. Keep it darker or out of focus." Then a small line: "Then: plan five places to
> shoot, and keep working on your second event." One slide.

### Board version

```
THIS WEEK
  7.9.4  determine composition, formal qualities, scale,
         and use of space
  7.9.5  use ISO, shutter speed, aperture, and white
         balance to shoot manual photographs
  2.9.5  develop a timeline, task breakdown, deliverables,
         and responsibilities for completion

  WED frame examples + plan   THU-FRI shoot, second event
  first drafts: photo nov 6, second event nov 13

THROUGH THE FRAME

  reflections, light and shadow.
  the frame points AT the subject.
```

## 3. Aviation UAS: Know Your Event

**Handout:** `know-your-event.md`. Walk the room. Every student should be able to tell you what they are
making by the end of the period.

### Slide 1: Standards and agenda

> Create ONE slide for a high school drone class. Title: "This Week." Top half, headed "What we are
> covering," lists these with their FULL text: 1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas,
> vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose. 7.9.4 Describe operating
> limitations for small unmanned aircraft systems. 7.11.4 Describe weather products and resources
> required for preflight planning, current and forecast weather for departure, enroute, and arrival
> phases of flight. Bottom half, headed "This week": "WED and THU: know your event, from the WSAP
> (1.2.5)." "THU: fly if the weather is good (7.9.4)." "FRI: quiz on weather and mission briefings
> (7.11.4)." Then in bold: "ODOT Module 3 due Friday." One slide.

> Module 3 due Friday. First draft due Fri Nov 13." One slide.
