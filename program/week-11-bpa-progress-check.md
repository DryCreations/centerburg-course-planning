# BPA Progress Check: Classroom Posts

**Post Friday, Oct 30, in DT, V&S, and Aviation.** Labs, **20 points**. Graded on real progress since
choosing an event, not on how finished it is.

---

## Design Techniques

```
BPA Progress Check

Show me where your BPA work stands. Attach what you have so far for each event, like photos, drafts, sketches, or files, and write two or three sentences: what you finished in the last two weeks, and what you will do next. You are graded on real progress, not on how finished it is.

Standard: 2.9.5 Develop a timeline, a communication plan, a task breakdown, costs (e.g., equipment, labor), deliverables, and responsibilities for completion.
```

## Video & Sound

```
BPA Progress Check

Show me where your BPA project stands. Attach what you have so far, like a script, storyboard, footage, a rough cut, or animation frames, and write two or three sentences: what you finished in the last two weeks, and what you will do next. On a team, each person submits and says what they did. You are graded on real progress, not on how finished it is.

Standard: 2.9.5 Develop a timeline, a communication plan, a task breakdown, costs (e.g., equipment, labor), deliverables, and responsibilities for completion.
```

## Aviation UAS

```
BPA Progress Check

Show me where your BPA project stands. Attach what you have so far, like research notes, an outline, slides, or a business plan draft, and write two or three sentences: what you finished in the last two weeks, and what you will do next. You are graded on real progress, not on how finished it is.

Standard: 1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose.
```

## Grading, 20 points

| Points | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **20** | Clear, real progress since choosing the event, and a specific next step |
| **12** | Some progress, but thin, or no clear next step |
| **5** | Submitted, but little or nothing new |
| **0** | Not submitted |
