# Project guidance for Claude

Centerburg HS CTE course-planning repo (Design Techniques, Video & Sound, Aviation UAS, Middle School CS,
Yearbook). Planning docs live under `courses/<course>/planning/` and cross-course docs under `program/`.

## Writing style

**Do not use em dashes (`—`) in any document.** Replace them with a colon, a comma, or restructure the
sentence, whichever reads best:

- Label or definition before an explanation: use a **colon** (`Hierarchy: the order people read a design`).
- A parenthetical or appositive aside: use a **comma** (`the sketch, done on paper first, is the guardrail`), or
  wrap it in parentheses.
- Two independent thoughts jammed together: **split into two sentences.**

Other symbol notes:
- **En dashes (`–`) are fine for numeric ranges** (`Mon–Fri`, `20–40 sec`, `grades 7–8`). Only the em dash is
  banned.
- Avoid other decorative or unusual symbols in student-facing text; keep punctuation plain and standard.

Apply this to everything you write or edit here, including new files, and fix em dashes you come across while
editing a file.

## Student-facing vs. teacher-facing

- `outline.md`, `project.md`, `vocab.md`, and `handouts/` are **student-facing** (safe to paste into Google
  Classroom / Docs). Keep teacher pacing, assessment, and answer keys out of them.
- `teacher-notes.md` and `quiz.md` (which holds answer keys) are **teacher-only**.
- Do not modify documents that have already been shared with students unless explicitly asked.
