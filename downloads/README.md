# Downloads

Prebuilt bundles for grabbing on a machine where signing in is a hassle.

| File | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| `week-6-materials.zip` | **Week 6 (Mon Sep 21 to Fri Sep 25).** Three quiz banks, Monday slide prompts, the Yearbook two week photo assignment, the Aviation box pattern flight task, and the Middle School CS digital citizenship unit. Open `POST-THIS.md` first. |
| `week-5-materials.zip` | **Week 5 (Mon Sep 14 to Fri Sep 18), the whole week.** Full five day week. Slide prompts for all five days in `SLIDE-PROMPTS/`, plus every class's materials and both Friday quiz banks. Note: the Design Techniques project runs two weeks, through Fri Sep 25. Open `INDEX.md` inside for the map and the prep list. |
| `week-4-materials.zip` | **Week 4 (Wed Sep 9 to Fri Sep 11), the whole week.** Three day week: Labor Day Monday, PD Tuesday. Slide prompts for all three days in `SLIDE-PROMPTS/`, plus every class's materials and both Friday quiz banks. Open `INDEX.md` inside for the map. |
| `week-3-materials.zip` | Week 3 (Aug 31 to Sep 4): all five classes, the Friday quiz CSVs, the Gimkit banks, and the daily slide prompts. |

Each zip unpacks to a single folder (`week-4-materials/`), so it will not scatter files wherever you open it.

To rebuild a week's bundle after changes, re-zip that week's paths:
`courses/<course>/planning/week-N/` for all five courses, the matching `program/week-N-*` docs, and
`grading-and-categories.md`. Course folders are numbered in the bundle (`1-design-techniques` and so on) so
they sort in the order the day runs.
