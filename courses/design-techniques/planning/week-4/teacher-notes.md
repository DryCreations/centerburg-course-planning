# Week 4 Teacher Notes: Design Techniques

Teacher-facing pacing for Week 4. Student doc is `outline.md`; assignment is `project.md`; Wednesday's handout is
`handouts/wed-portfolio-update.md`; vocab is `vocab.md`. No new quiz this week: Friday is work time plus an
**independent, optional retake** of last week's Week 3 hierarchy and typography quiz for anyone who wants a
better score. **Decided: the retake score replaces the original, one time only, since this is the first quiz of
the year and many students had not yet learned how to study for one.**

## Assessment Notes

- Part A (portfolio update) is graded on completion: poster posted, description written, nav labeled.
- Part B is the **Figma Intro Lab**, graded in the **Labs** category on **completion**: two frames built to
  the spec, connected both ways, working in Present. If it clicks, it is done.
- **Do not grade this on design quality.** They are copying a spec. Judging aesthetics on a copy exercise
  punishes them for following instructions correctly.
- The design judgment gets assessed next week, on the redesign project.
- Retake policy is settled: replaces the original score, one time only.

## Differentiation

- **Finished early:** restyle in their own colors (keeping the play button dominant), add a third frame, try
  a non-Instant transition, or rebuild from memory without the spec. That last one is the best of them.
- **Needs support:** pair them up, and point them at the "Stuck?" table at the bottom of the handout. Most
  problems are the same three: still holding a draw tool instead of `V`, the layer landed outside the frame,
  or they lost the canvas and need `Shift + 1`.
- **Absent students:** `handouts/figma-intro-build.md` is written to be self-serve start to finish. Post it
  and they can catch up without you reteaching it.
- **Moves fast:** they will finish Thursday's frame early. Send them to Part 4 (prototyping) rather than
  inventing filler. Getting a day ahead here is fine.

## Figma logistics

- **Free Starter accounts are enough.** Unlimited personal drafts, real frames, real prototyping. Do not make
  today depend on Figma for Education: that verification can take days. Apply in the background and let it
  upgrade them later.
- **Build it on the projector alongside them, one step at a time.** Do not demo the whole thing first and
  then set them loose. Step, wait, look around, next step.
- The spec uses exact `X`/`Y`/`W`/`H` numbers so students can type values into the right panel instead of
  dragging by eye. **Teach that panel early**, it removes most of the frustration.
- Sign-in is **figma.com, Log in, Continue with Google, school account.**
- **Tell them to skip the onboarding tour.** It eats fifteen minutes and teaches nothing they need.
- Thursday's Figma goal is exactly one thing: a named phone frame in a saved file. That is it.
- **If sign-in fails for a student,** they keep working on paper and pair up Friday. The paper work is the
  real work, so nobody is stuck.
- Friday needs only four tools: R rectangle, T text, V move, F frame.

means they show up Monday already having a target in mind. Note the actual date it wraps once known.
