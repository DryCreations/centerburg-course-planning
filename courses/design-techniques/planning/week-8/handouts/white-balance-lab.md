## Part 5: Make one color the whole photo

**Scroll down past Basic.** Find the panel called **HSL** or **HSL / Color**, or in some versions
**Color Mixer**. Three tabs: **Hue, Saturation, Luminance**, each with a row of color sliders.

**This is where you control one color without touching the others.**

### The task

Take a photo with at least two or three different colors in it. **Make exactly one color survive.**

1. Go to the **Saturation** tab
2. Pull **every** color slider down to −100 **except one**
3. That one color stays. Everything else goes grayscale

**13.** Which color did you keep, and why that one?

**14.** Which sliders did you have to pull down? Was it obvious which ones controlled what was in your
photo?

**15.** Some colors in your photo probably came from more than one slider. **Which ones fought you?**

**Export it.** Name it `LastName_ColorPop_01`

### Then push further

Pick **at least one** and try it.

| Try | Where |
|---|---|

| Make one color brighter or darker without changing how strong it is | **Luminance** tab |
| Drop everything to grayscale, then bring one color partly back | Saturation tab, then back off from −100 |
| Find the **split toning** or **color grading** panel and put one color in the shadows and another in the highlights | Further down the panel list |

**16.** What did you try, what did it do, and would you keep it?

## Words

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **White balance** | Setting the image so things that are white look white |
| **Temperature** | The blue to yellow axis, measured in Kelvin |
| **Tint** | The green to magenta axis |
| **Color cast** | An unwanted overall tint across the whole image |
| **Neutral** | A gray, white or black area with no color in it |
| **As Shot** | The white balance the camera chose when you pressed the shutter |
| **Non-destructive** | Your changes are instructions. The original file is never altered |
| **Export** | Making a new file, in a new format, from your edited version |
| **HSL** | Hue, Saturation, Luminance. Controls colors one at a time |
| **Hue** | Which color it is |
| **Saturation** | How strong that color is |
| **Luminance** | How bright that color is |
