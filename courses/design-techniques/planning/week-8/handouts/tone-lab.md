# One Photo, One Graph

**Design Techniques | Tuesday**

**We are doing this together, step by step.** Everyone edits the same photo. One file out by the end.

**7.9.6** Edit photographs (e.g., color corrections, cropping, enhancements).
**7.4.7** Optimize and export graphics files for intended use.

---

## Step 1: Get the photo

Click the link in Google Classroom and **save the file into your own folder.** Not the desktop.

**1.** What is the file named, and what folder did you put it in?

## Step 2: Open it and turn the warnings on

Open it in **Camera Raw.** Then find the **two small triangles in the top corners of the histogram**,
top right, and **click both.**

**Blue on the photo means shadow detail is gone. Red means highlight detail is gone.** Leave them on.

**2.** Is anything red or blue right now? Where?

## Step 3: Read the graph before you touch anything

**Left is dark. Right is bright. Height is how much of the photo is that bright.**

**3.** Where is this photo's data piled up: left, right, or the middle? **So is this photo dark, bright,
or flat?**

---

## Step 4: Round 1. Two sliders only.

**Exposure and Contrast. Nothing else.** Make the photo as good as you can get it.

**4.** Write down your Exposure and your Contrast.

**5.** **What is still wrong that those two could not fix?** Say what, and say where in the photo. If you
think nothing is wrong, look at the histogram again.

## Step 5: Round 2. Unlock four more.

**Blacks, Shadows, Highlights, Whites are now allowed. Exposure and Contrast stay where you left them.**

| Slider | What it owns |
|---|---|
| **Blacks** | The darkest point. Where black begins |
| **Shadows** | The dark areas, without touching black |
| **Highlights** | The bright areas, without touching white |
| **Whites** | The brightest point. Where white begins |

**6.** Fix the thing you named in question 5. **Which sliders, and which direction?**

**7.** Look at the histogram now compared to question 3. **What changed about its shape?**

## Step 6: Export it

**Save Image**, bottom left. Format **JPEG**. Name it `LastName_Tone_01`. Save into your folder, then
upload it.

**8.** Round 1 or Round 2, which photo is better? **And what did Round 1 prove about why those four extra
sliders exist?**
