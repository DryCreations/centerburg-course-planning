## What you are aiming for

**This is the target. Check your photo against it before you export, both times.**

1. **The graph reaches toward both ends.** A photo that only uses the middle looks washed out and dull
2. **No tall spike jammed against either wall.** That means detail is gone, not dark or bright
3. **No red or blue overlay**, except on things that were never going to hold detail: a light bulb, a
   window, sun off chrome
4. **The subject sits in the middle**, not at an edge. If a face reads at the far left, the photo is dark
   no matter how good the sky looks
5. **Turn the edits off and on.** You should see an obvious difference

## The only sliders you may use today

**Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.** That is it.

**No Temperature, no Tint, no Saturation, no Vibrance, no Clarity.** Those are the rest of the week.
Camera Raw has no separate midtone slider: **Exposure is the midtones.**

**2.** With every slider still at 0, **is there any red or blue on your photo already?** If yes, where?

## Push it too far on purpose

**3.** Drag **Exposure** up to about **+2.00.** Look at your photo. **What appears, and where?**

**4.** Bring Exposure back to **0.** **Did it come back?** So what is the difference between red that was
there at 0 and red that only showed up when you pushed?

**5.** Now pull **Blacks** down to **−100.** **What color appears, and where?** What is happening to
those areas?

**6.** Put Blacks back to 0.
