## What you are aiming for

**This is the target. Check your photo against it before you export, both times.**

1. **White things look white.** No color cast
2. **The graph reaches toward both ends.** A photo that only uses the middle looks washed out and dull
3. **No tall spike jammed against either wall.** That means detail is gone, not dark or bright
4. **No red or blue overlay**, except on things that were never going to hold detail: a light bulb, a
   window, sun off chrome
5. **The subject sits in the middle**, not at an edge. If a face reads at the far left, the photo is dark
   no matter how good the sky looks
6. **Turn the edits off and on.** You should see an obvious difference

## The only sliders you may use today

**Temperature and Tint**, then **Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.** In that
order.

**No Saturation, no Vibrance, no Clarity, no HSL.** Those are the rest of the week. Camera Raw has no
separate midtone slider: **Exposure is the midtones.**
