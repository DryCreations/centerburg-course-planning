# Portfolio Update: Photography

**Design Techniques | Friday**

**Add two photos from this week to your portfolio, each as a before and after.**

## Standards

**7.9.6** Edit photographs (e.g., color corrections, cropping, enhancements).
**7.9.5** Use International Standards Organization (ISO), shutter speed, aperture, and white balance
settings to shoot manual photographs.
**7.2.7** Select colors based on color theory and psychology.
**7.4.7** Optimize and export graphics files for intended use.

---

## What to do

1. **Pick two different photos** from this week:
   - **Your best photo.** The one you would show someone
   - **Your best editing.** The one where you used the sliders the most, and it worked
2. **Export a before and an after of each**, as JPEG. Four images
3. On your portfolio site, make a page called **Unit 2.1 Photography**, and put each before and after
   **side by side**
4. Under each pair, **describe what you changed and why** (see below)
5. **Publish**, and post the **published** link on Classroom

**Getting the before:** export the after first. Then reset the photo to its defaults (the small menu at
the top right of the panels), Save Image, and press **Command + Z** to get your edits back.

---

## What to write under each pair

**Three or four sentences.** Say what you changed, and why. Name the sliders.

**You can talk about:**

- **White balance:** the color cast it had, and what you used as your neutral
- **Exposure and tone:** what was too dark, too bright, or flat, and how you fixed it. Where you set
  your black point
- **Clarity, Dehaze, Vibrance, Saturation:** which you used, and which you decided to leave alone
- **Color:** which color you made stand out, and how
- **Export:** why JPEG is the right format for a website

**Write about what you did, not the standard numbers.** "I warmed it up because the shade made the skin
look blue" is better than "I met 7.9.6."

---

## Graded on

**Four images on the published page, and a description under each pair that says what you changed and
why.**
