## Step 1: Pick two. They have to be different photos.

Look through everything from Monday to Thursday.

| | Pick the one that |

| **Entry 1: My best photo** | **You like the most.** The one you would show someone |
| **Entry 2: My best editing** | **Shows the most skill with the sliders.** Not always the prettiest one. The one where you did the most, and it worked |

**Not finished editing one of them? Finish it first.** That is the first part of today.

---

## Step 2: Export a before and an after for each

**Export the AFTER first**, so your edit is safe no matter what happens next.

1. Open the photo in Camera Raw with your edits
2. **Save Image**, JPEG. Name it `LastName_Best_After` or `LastName_Editing_After`

**Then the BEFORE**, which is the photo with no edits at all:

3. Open the **small menu icon** at the top right of the panel area, and choose **Camera Raw Defaults**.
   Some versions call it **Reset to Default.** The photo goes back to how it opened
4. **Save Image**, JPEG. Name it `LastName_Best_Before` or `LastName_Editing_Before`
5. **Press Command + Z** to get your edits back

**If it was one of the photos I gave you**, there is an easier way to get the before: download the
original again, into a **new folder**, open it, and Save Image without touching anything.

**You should have four files:** a before and an after, for each of your two photos.

---

## Step 3: Put them on your portfolio

1. Open your portfolio site and make a page called **Unit 2.1 Photography**
2. **Insert, Images**, and add each before and after **side by side**
3. Under each pair, write the short reflection below
4. **Publish**, then open the published site in a new tab and check it

### Under Entry 1: My best photo

**Two or three sentences.** What were you going for, and what makes this the one you would show someone?

### Under Entry 2: My best editing

**Name the three decisions that mattered most**, the same way you have written them before:

> **What I did. The problem it solved. How I can tell it worked.**

"I pulled Blacks down until the warning just appeared, because the photo looked foggy, and now the
shadows feel solid" is a decision. "I made it look better" is not.

### Name the standards in words

**Somewhere in the two reflections, write about what you did in plain language that matches these.** Use
the words, not the numbers.

| Standard | What to write about |
|---|---|
| **Edit photographs** | What you corrected and what you enhanced. White balance, exposure, tone, color |
| **White balance settings** | How you dealt with the color cast, and what you used as your neutral |
| **Colors based on color theory and psychology** | Which color you made win, or the mood you went for, and why |
| **Optimize and export graphics files** | What you exported, in what format, and why JPEG for the web |
