# Color Grading

**Design Techniques | Optional. Not worth points. Due Friday.**

**This is where the "look" of a photo comes from.** We will go deeper into it later in the year. This is
a taste of it, for anyone who finishes early.

**Use any photo you have already edited.**

---

## Finding the panel

**Scroll past HSL / Color.** You are looking for one of two things, depending on which version of Camera
Raw you have:

| If you see | It looks like |
|---|---|
| **Color Grading** | Three color wheels, labeled **Shadows, Midtones, Highlights** |
| **Split Toning** | A **Hue** and **Saturation** pair for Highlights, the same pair for Shadows, and a **Balance** slider between them |

**They do the same job:** putting one color into the bright parts of the photo and a **different** color
into the dark parts.

---

## The one rule

> **Keep Saturation low. Around 5 to 15.**

**At 60 it looks like a broken television.** The good version is almost subtle enough to miss. If you can
see it before you see the photo, you went too far.

---

## Four looks to try

| Look | Highlights | Shadows | Where you have seen it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teal and orange** | Warm, orange or gold | Cool, teal or blue | Every action movie poster of the last twenty years |
| **Golden hour, faked** | Warm gold | Warm, slightly brown | Travel photos, nostalgia, adverts |
| **Cold and cinematic** | Leave neutral | Blue, pushed a little further than feels safe | Thrillers, night scenes, anything lonely |
| **Faded film** | Pale yellow | Soft green | Old photographs, anything meant to feel like a memory |

---

## Things you will notice

- **Shadows take color much more easily than highlights.** You will need less than you think
- **If you have a Balance slider**, it decides where "shadow" stops and "highlight" starts. Moving it
  changes the whole look without touching a single hue
- **Watch any skin.** Teal shadows on a face read as cold and ill, fast
- **Toggle it off and on.** Cannot see it? Go further. Notice it before you notice the photo? Go back
