## About clipping: a correction from yesterday

**"Avoid red and blue" was too strict.** A clipped area is a solid block of one color, with no detail or
texture. The warning tells you **where** that is. It does not tell you that you were wrong.

**Most good photos have at least one thing that is solid black.** Push until the warning *just barely*
appears, then stop. **Never a large area, and never on the subject.** A blown-out light bulb is fine. A
blown-out face is not.
