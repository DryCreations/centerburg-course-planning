# Three Moods

**Design Techniques | Tuesday to Thursday**

Edit one photo three ways: one neutral, two with different moods. Then put all three on your portfolio.

## Standards

**7.9.6** Edit photographs (e.g., color corrections, cropping, enhancements).
**7.2.7** Select colors based on color theory and psychology.
**7.4.7** Optimize and export graphics files for intended use.

## Requirements

- Use your own photo if you can. It can be the one from Monday
- Use color grading in at least one mood
- Use at least four of last week's tools across the project: white balance, tone sliders, Clarity or
  Dehaze, Vibrance or Saturation, HSL

---

## Tuesday: neutral and mood 1 (7.9.6)

1. Reset the photo. Make it look natural: correct white balance, a true black, nothing important too
   bright. Export as `LastName_Neutral_01`
2. Pick a mood from the list below. Start from the neutral version. Export as `LastName_Mood1_01`

**1.** Which photo did you pick, and what did you fix to make it look natural?

## Wednesday: mood 2 (7.2.7)

Pick a different mood. Start from neutral again. Export as `LastName_Mood2_01`.

**Mood ideas:**

- **Bright and open:** Exposure up, Shadows up, Contrast down, a little warm
- **Dark and dramatic:** Blacks down, Contrast up, a little cool, teal shadows
- **Faded:** Contrast down, Blacks up, warm, Saturation down
- **Cold:** cool, Contrast up, Saturation down, Clarity up
- **One color:** lower the Saturation of every color but one in HSL

**2.** For each mood, which three sliders did the most work?

**3.** Which mood was hardest to get right on this photo, and why?

## Thursday: portfolio (7.4.7)

**4.** Which version would you publish, and who is it for?

**5.** Is the neutral version the best photo? Answer yes or no, then explain in three sentences.

Add all three photos to your Unit 2.1 Photography portfolio page, side by side. Write three or four
sentences about what you changed and why. Publish, then check it in a new tab.

**Last week of the quarter:** submit anything missing today.
