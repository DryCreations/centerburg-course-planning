# Three Moods

**Design Techniques | Unit 2.1 Photography | Week 9, Tuesday to Thursday**

**One photo. Three versions. Three different feelings.** Then all three go on your portfolio.

## Standards

**7.9.6** Edit photographs (e.g., color corrections, cropping, enhancements).
**7.2.7** Select colors based on color theory and psychology.
**7.4.7** Optimize and export graphics files for intended use.

---

## The idea

**Correct is a measurement. Mood is a decision.** You take one photo, make it honest, then push it to
two different feelings **on purpose**, and defend the choice.

**Your photo:** one **you took** is best, from your Week 7 shoot. If none of yours work, use one of last
week's class photos. It can be the same photo you graded Monday.

## The requirements

- **Color grading in at least one mood**, from Monday
- **At least four of last week's tools** across the project: white balance, the tone sliders, Clarity or
  Dehaze, Vibrance or Saturation, HSL

---

## Tuesday: neutral, then mood 1

1. **Reset the photo to its defaults**, then make it **honest:** white balance right, one real black,
   nothing you care about clipped. **No mood.** Export as `LastName_Neutral_01`
2. Pick your first mood from the list below. Start from the neutral version. Export as `LastName_Mood1_01`

**1.** Which photo did you pick, and what did you have to fix to make it honest?

## Wednesday: mood 2

**Pick a second mood that feels different from the first.** Start from neutral again. Export as
`LastName_Mood2_01`.

| Mood | A place to start, not a recipe |
|------|--------------------------------|
| **Bright and open** | Exposure up, Shadows and Blacks up, Contrast down, slightly warm. Vibrance up a little |
| **Heavy and cinematic** | Blacks down, Contrast up, Highlights down, slightly cool. **Teal shadows** in color grading |
| **Faded and nostalgic** | Contrast down, Blacks **up**, Whites down, warm. Saturation down. **Yellow highlights, green shadows** |
| **Cold and clinical** | Cool, Contrast up, Saturation down, Clarity up. Pull every warm color down in HSL |
| **One color only** | Everything neutral, then HSL: drop the Saturation of every color but one |

**2.** For each mood, **which three sliders did the most work?**

**3.** One of your moods fought the photo. **Which one, and what in the photo resisted?**

## Thursday: defend it, and put it on your portfolio

**Put all three side by side.**

**4.** **Which one would you publish**, and who is the audience that makes it the right call?

**5.** Your neutral version is the most honest. **Is it the best photo?** Yes or no, then defend it in
three sentences.

**Then add all three to your Unit 2.1 Photography portfolio page**, side by side, with **three or four
sentences** describing what you changed between them and why. Publish, and check it in a new tab.

**Last week of the quarter:** anything missing, submit it today.
