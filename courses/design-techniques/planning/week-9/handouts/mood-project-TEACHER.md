# Three Moods: Teacher Notes

**Teacher-only.** Student sheets: `color-grading.md` (Monday) and `mood-project.md` (Tuesday to Thursday).

**Monday** the class shot photos outside, offloaded, and tried color grading. The Color Grading sheet
is still graded, now due Thursday. Slower students use Tuesday to learn the tools before starting. **Tuesday to Thursday** is Three Moods: color grading in
at least one mood, and at least four of last week's tools. **Thursday** all three go on the portfolio
with a short description. Everything is due Thursday, since Friday is the quiz.

**Photos:** Monday's shoot first, then their Week 7 photos, then last week's class photos. Nothing new
to download.

## What to look for in the answers

| Q | Full credit |
|---|---|
| **1** | Names the photo and at least one real fix (white balance, a true black, a highlight pulled back) |
| **2** | Three named sliders for each mood, with a direction. "Contrast up, Blacks down, Temperature cooler" |
| **3** | A reason tied to this photo. Skin fights cold moods, green leaves fight heavy contrast, gray skies fight bright moods |
| **4** | Names an audience. Any mood is right for somebody. No audience means no decision |
| **5** | Either answer earns full credit. You are grading the explanation |

**Questions 4, 5 and the portfolio description count the most.** The exports show the work. The answers
show the decisions.

## Say this at least once

A mood that does not work on a photo is not a failure. It tells you something about the photo. That is
question 3.
