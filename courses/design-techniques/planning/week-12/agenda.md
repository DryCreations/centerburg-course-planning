## Vocab

- **Typeface:** one design of letters, like Arial or Georgia
- **Font family:** all the styles of one typeface: regular, bold, italic, and so on
- **Serif:** type with small feet on the letters. Traditional and trustworthy, good for printed text
- **Sans serif:** type with no feet. Clean and modern, easy to read on screens
- **Script:** type that looks handwritten. Elegant. Use it sparingly
- **Display:** decorative type made for big headlines, never for paragraphs
- **Point (pt):** the unit for type size. 72 points is about one inch
- **Pica:** a unit for line length and spacing. 12 points make one pica
- **Em:** a space as wide as the type size. In 12 pt type, one em is 12 points
- **Kerning:** the space between two specific letters
- **Tracking:** the space across a whole word or line
- **Leading:** the space between lines of text
- **Type style guide:** a short list of the fonts, sizes, and colors a project uses, so every piece
  matches
- **Copyright:** the creator's legal right to their work. It starts the moment the work is made
- **Fair use:** limited use of someone else's work without permission, like commentary or teaching. It
  is decided case by case, so do not count on it for a competition
- **Creative Commons:** licenses that let creators share their work if you follow the rules, like
  giving credit
- **Public domain:** work no one owns anymore, free for anyone to use
- **Model release:** written permission from a person to use their photo
