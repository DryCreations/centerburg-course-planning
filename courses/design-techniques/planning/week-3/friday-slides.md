# Friday Sep 4: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Design Techniques · Week 3 · Day 5.** Quiz day. Two slides is all you need. One prompt per slide for Google
Slides AI. Quiz CSV: `program/quizzes/week-3-friday/design-techniques-quiz.csv`.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school class on a quiz day. Big title: "As You Come In." Body:
> "Open Google Classroom and wait. I will open the quiz once everyone is ready. From that point the room is
> SILENT until I say everyone has finished. Stay on the quiz tab the whole time. This is NOT open note and NOT
> open resource." Large, clean, minimal, easy to read from the back of the room. One slide.

### Slide 2: Do Now (the quiz)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school class taking a quiz. Big title: "Do Now: Take the Quiz." Body:
> "Take the quiz now. Stay silent until I say everyone is done. Stay on the quiz tab. If you finish early: first
> make sure everything for THIS class is submitted (your poster PDF and your portfolio image), then work quietly
> on something for another class." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.
