# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Design Techniques · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = a fresh-eyes peer review + squint test, a short lesson on
exporting the poster (PDF to submit, image to embed on the portfolio), then **Gimkit** where students write their
own review questions before playing. One prompt per slide for Google Slides AI. Order: As You Come In,
Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now (Gimkit questions), Play, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school graphic design class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body:
> "Open your poster in Adobe Express. Then ask someone near you who has NOT really seen it yet to review it:
> have them do the squint test and tell you (1) what they think the poster is about, and (2) the order they read
> it in (what they saw first, second, third). If that is not the order you wanted, note what to fix." Large,
> clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

> Create one standards and agenda slide titled "Today: Review, Export, and Gimkit." List these Ohio Interactive
> Media standards with full text: 7.2.10 Select and create visual design elements appropriate for the intended
> audience and use; 7.5.2 Mix families of type within a project; 7.5.4 Identify appropriate typefaces (e.g.,
> serif, sans serif, Web Safe, screen, print); 7.4.7 Optimize and export graphics files for intended use. Then a
> short agenda: 1) Fresh-eyes peer review + squint test, 2) Work time + how to export and submit, 3) Gimkit
> review (you write the questions). Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Export and post your poster

> Create one slide titled "Export and Post Your Poster." Two short columns. Left, "To submit": export your poster
> as a PDF and turn it in on Google Classroom. Right, "For your portfolio": also export it as a PNG or JPG image,
> then embed that image on your Google Sites portfolio page. Note: PDF is for handing in, the image is for
> showing it on your site. One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Do Now (Gimkit, write your questions)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school graphic design class titled "Do Now: Add Gimkit Questions." Left
> side, the task: "Go to the Gimkit link on Google Classroom and use the tool to ADD YOUR OWN review questions.
> I will review them and add the good ones to the game. You may also add a few school-appropriate pop culture
> questions if you want." Right side, a boxed "What to write questions about" with this week's terms as examples:
> hierarchy, focal point, visual weight, contrast, alignment, proximity, whitespace, reading order, typeface,
> type pairing. Add: "Not sure? Check this week's vocabulary posted in Google Classroom." One slide, clear
> two-column layout.

### Slide 5: Play the game

> Create one slide titled "Now Play." Body: "Questions in? Now go to THIS link to join and play the Gimkit game:
> [paste your Gimkit play/join link or code here]." Big join-code space in the middle. One slide.

### Slide 6: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your poster is exported (PDF submitted, image on your
> portfolio). Tomorrow: the cumulative quiz." One slide, large text.
