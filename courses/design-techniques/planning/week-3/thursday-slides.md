# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Design Techniques · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = refine the poster against the hierarchy checklist, peer
review, then a **Gimkit review game** before Friday's quiz. Each block below is one prompt to paste into Google
Slides AI. Deck order: As You Come In, Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now, Gimkit, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school graphic design class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body:
> "Open your poster from yesterday in Adobe Express and get it on screen. We are going to make it stronger
> today." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

> Create one standards and agenda slide titled "Today: Refine Your Hierarchy." List these Ohio Interactive Media
> standards with full text: 7.2.10 Select and create visual design elements appropriate for the intended
> audience and use; 7.5.2 Mix families of type within a project; 7.5.4 Identify appropriate typefaces (e.g.,
> serif, sans serif, Web Safe, screen, print); 7.4.2 Select color, shape, size, and texture of objects; 7.4.7
> Optimize and export graphics files for intended use. Then a short agenda: 1) Refine your poster's hierarchy, 2)
> Peer review, 3) Gimkit review game to prep for tomorrow's quiz. Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Refine checklist

> Create one slide titled "Refine Your Poster." Checklist: one dominant focal point (not two things fighting); a
> real size jump between headline, details, and fine print; aligned edges; generous whitespace; strong contrast;
> a clean heading + body font pair. Add: "Run the squint test: the first thing you can still read is your focal
> point." One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Peer review

> Create one slide titled "Peer Review." Steps: 1) Trade posters with a partner. 2) Your partner says the reading
> order out loud (what they see first, second, third). 3) If they cannot tell the order, your hierarchy is not
> done yet. 4) Fix it and check again. One slide.

### Slide 5: Do Now (breakout to work)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school graphic design class. Big title: "Do Now: Refine and Review." Body:
> "Work on this now: run the squint test on your poster, then fix the one element competing with your focal
> point. Get a partner to read your reading order out loud. Goal for today: a stranger can read your poster in
> the right order (1, 2, 3), and it is posted to your portfolio." Large, clean. One slide.

### Slide 6: Gimkit review

> Create one slide titled "Gimkit Review." Body: "Join the Gimkit review game: [paste your Gimkit join link or
> code here]. We are reviewing hierarchy and typography terms to get ready for tomorrow's quiz." Big join-code
> space in the middle. One slide.

### Slide 7: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your refined poster is posted to your portfolio.
> Tomorrow: the cumulative quiz and your final export." One slide, large text.
