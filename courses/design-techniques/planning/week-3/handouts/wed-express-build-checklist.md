# Build Day Checklist: Poster in Adobe Express

**Design Techniques · Week 3 · Wednesday Sep 2**

Your paper sketch is the plan. Today you **build it in Express** and make the hierarchy real. Keep the
[Hierarchy Toolbox](hierarchy-toolbox.md) open. Build from the sketch, do not start over on the screen.

## Set up

- [ ] Open a **poster canvas** (portrait, 18 × 24 in; Express "Poster" preset is fine).
- [ ] Rough in your **three levels** from the sketch: headline, key details, fine print.

## Make the hierarchy real

- [ ] **One dominant element** the eye hits first (your headline or your image, not both fighting).
- [ ] A real **size jump** between the three levels (no "everything the same size").
- [ ] **Type pairing:** one heading font + one body font that clearly contrast.
- [ ] **Alignment:** line elements to shared edges or a center line (use the smart guides).
- [ ] **Contrast:** the headline reads instantly against its background.
- [ ] **Whitespace:** the poster can breathe; nothing is crammed.
- [ ] At least **one image**, placed and cropped on purpose.

## The check before you leave

- [ ] **Squint test:** the first thing you can still read is your focal point.
- [ ] Ask a neighbor to say the reading order (1, 2, 3) out loud. If they can't, fix the hierarchy.
