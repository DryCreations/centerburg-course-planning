## Grading the project

Weights are in `week-5/project.md`. What to actually look at:

- **Does it work?** Every screen reachable, a way back from each, interactions firing in Present
- **Is the scope one task?** A student who tried to build all of Instagram will have five half-screens
- **Did anything change after the partner tests?** Two tests with no changes means the tests were performed,
  not run
- **Does the write-up name reasons?** Four decisions, each with the problem it solved

**Do not grade visual polish.** Six weeks in, a plain prototype that works beats a pretty one that dead-ends.
