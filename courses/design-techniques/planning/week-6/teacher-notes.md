## Friday's quiz

42 questions, cut to 20. **Half carried forward from the Week 3 material, half new from this unit.**

The suggested cut is 9 carried forward, 11 new, which leans slightly toward what was taught these two weeks
while keeping the first quiz's content live.

**The best question on the bank:** *during a user test, every time the designer wants to explain something,
it means they have found a design problem.* A student who gets that has understood what testing is for.

**Give the quiz first**, then work time. Anyone finishing the project needs the back half of the period.

## Grading the project

Weights are in `week-5/project.md`. What to actually look at:

- **Does it work?** Every screen reachable, a way back from each, interactions firing in Present
- **Is the scope one task?** A student who tried to build all of Instagram will have five half-screens
- **Did anything change after the partner tests?** Two tests with no changes means the tests were performed,
  not run
- **Does the write-up name reasons?** Four decisions, each with the problem it solved

**Do not grade visual polish.** Six weeks in, a plain prototype that works beats a pretty one that dead-ends.
