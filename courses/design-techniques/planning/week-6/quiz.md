## The questions worth keeping

- **"How many primary actions should a screen have?"** and **"Which is NOT a reliable way to make a primary
  action stand out?"** These are the through-line from hierarchy in Week 3 to UI in Week 6, and they show
  whether the connection landed.
- **"Breaking a well-known convention costs you because..."** Tests whether they understood *why*
  conventions matter rather than memorizing a list.
- **"During a user test, every time the designer wants to explain something, it means..."** The single best
  question on the bank. A student who gets this has understood what testing is for.
