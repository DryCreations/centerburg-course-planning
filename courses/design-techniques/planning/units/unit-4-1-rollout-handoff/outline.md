## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

You have a portfolio full of work. This unit is about making that work look polished and professional — organized, well-named, and ready for someone else to actually use. You will practice how designers wrap up a project: cleaning up files, writing a clear guide to a brand, and making sure things are published and accessible.

This is also preparation for the capstone. You want your earlier work cleaned up before you start the big final project.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Organize and name your files in a logical, consistent way
- Write a short brand guide that someone else could follow
- Export files in the right format for different uses (PNG for web, PDF for sharing)
- Add finishing touches to your portfolio so it looks complete and intentional

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Brand guidelines are just the style guide copy-pasted:** Guidelines should be written for a client, not a designer. No jargon; explain what each rule means in plain language.
- **Training notes are too technical:** Test the notes by having a student who hasn't seen the brand try to follow them.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a deployment checklist template with 10 example items to edit.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — create a 1-page "what not to do" bad example guide using deliberately broken brand applications.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab-2 uses a binary checklist. Key evidence: 7 deliverables present; brand guidelines PDF exported (not just Express link); QR code or URL in the package points to live website.
