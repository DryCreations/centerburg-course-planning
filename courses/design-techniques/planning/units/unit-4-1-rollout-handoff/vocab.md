# Vocabulary: Rollout, Handoff & Professional Publishing

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Rollout** | The scheduled, phased release of a new brand, product, or campaign to its intended audience — may happen in stages (soft launch → full launch) |
| **Handoff package** | All files, documentation, and guidelines delivered to a client at project completion — includes source files, exports, asset library, brand guidelines, and training notes |
| **Asset library** | An organized, named collection of all design assets for a project — logos (all formats), fonts (licensed copies), color codes, photography — delivered to the client |
| **Brand guidelines document** | A reference guide that specifies how brand elements must be used — colors (hex, RGB, CMYK), fonts, logo clearance zones, and usage do's/don'ts |
| **Platform publishing** | The process of uploading, configuring, and publishing content to its intended digital platform — including websites, social media, and digital downloads |
| **Deployment checklist** | A step-by-step list verifying that all technical and creative requirements are met before a design is published or delivered |
| **Training documentation** | Written instructions created for a client explaining how to update or maintain a delivered design using the provided tools |
| **Rollout timeline** | A calendar or Gantt-style schedule mapping each phase of a launch: pre-launch → launch day → post-launch follow-up |
| **Soft launch** | A limited release of new content or brand identity to a subset of the audience before a full public launch — allows final testing and adjustment |
| **Print-ready file** | A file prepared specifically for commercial printing — correct color mode (CMYK), resolution (minimum 300 DPI), PDF format with bleed and trim marks |
| **Digital asset management (DAM)** | The organized storage, retrieval, and sharing of digital files — proper naming, folder structure, and metadata tagging |
| **Usage rights** | The specific terms under which a font, image, or asset may be used — commercial use, web use, print use — must be verified and documented before client delivery |
| **Client acceptance** | The formal moment when a client reviews and approves final deliverables — often marked by a signature, confirmation email, or acceptance form |
