# Lab 1: File Organization & Naming Practice

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 4.1 — Rollout, Handoff & Professional Publishing
**Duration:** Week 28, Days 1–2

---

## Objective

Organize your project files so they are easy to find, clearly named, and make sense to anyone who looks at them (including future you).

---

## Part A — Organize Your Files

Pick one project you made this year that has multiple files (your brand style guide, your photography edits, your promotional pieces — whatever you have the most to organize).

### Step 1: Name Things Consistently

Rename your files using a consistent pattern. Example:

`[YourBrand]_[WhatItIs]_[Version].[ext]`

> Examples: `JordanDesigns_Logo_v2.png` | `JordanDesigns_BrandGuide_Final.pdf`

### Step 2: Group Into Folders

Create a simple folder structure for your chosen project:

```
[YourBrand]_ProjectFiles/
├── Logos/        (all logo files, different sizes/formats)
├── Colors/       (a note file with your hex codes)
├── Photos/       (any photos used in the project)
├── Exports/      (final PNG/PDF versions ready to share)
└── Notes.txt     (brief description of what's in each folder)
```

You don't need all of these folders if your project doesn't have all of those things. Just organize what you have.

### Step 3: Check Your Work

Answer these questions after organizing:
- If a classmate needed to find your logo file, could they in under 30 seconds?
- Are there any files with names like "untitled" or "copy of copy"?
- Could you find the final version of each piece without guessing?

## Part B — Brand Quick-Reference

Create a 1–2 page document in Adobe Express summarizing your brand:

| What to Include | Notes |
|----------------|-------|
| Logo (screenshot or embedded) | Show how it looks on light and dark backgrounds |
| Brand colors | Hex codes for each color |
| Fonts | Name, weight, what each is used for |
| One "do" and one "don't" for using the brand | Example: "Do use these exact hex codes. Don't stretch the logo." |

Export as PDF.
