## Instructions

### Step 1 — Select and Upload Your Photos

Upload your photos to Adobe Express. Pick your 3–5 best ones to work with.

---

### Step 2 — Edit Each Photo

For each photo in Adobe Express:
- **Crop** to improve the composition. Try to apply one of: rule of thirds or leading lines.
- **Adjust tone:** Fix white balance (warmth), brightness, or contrast as needed.
- **Optional:** Apply a filter, remove background, or make other enhancements.

---

### Step 4 — Reflection

Write a short reflection (3–5 sentences total) covering:
- Which photo are you most proud of and why?
- What was hardest to fix?
- What would you do differently next time you take a photo?

Submit your Express link. If you are happy with your results, add the best photo(s) to your portfolio. It is optional for this lab — only add what you are proud of.

## Overview

Shoot a themed photo series using manual camera controls. Edit your best images in Adobe Express using color correction, cropping, and effects. Build a multi-page Express document presenting the series with technical annotations explaining setting and editing decisions. This becomes a portfolio piece and, for BPA students, a potential competition entry.

---

## Class Prompt: Photo Essay & Edit Suite

### What You're Producing

A multi-page Adobe Express document presenting 6–8 edited photos from your themed series, with technical documentation that connects every shooting decision (aperture, shutter, ISO, lighting) to a visible outcome — and every editing decision to a design rationale.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Photos | 6 edited images from a themed series (minimum 8 shot) |
| Camera settings | All 3 manual controls used and documented (aperture, shutter, ISO) |
| Composition variety | Rule of thirds, leading lines, detail, and environmental shots |
| Lighting | 3-point or intentional natural lighting setup documented |
| Editing | Color correction + at least 1 enhancement per image in Express |
| Technical annotation | 1–2 sentences per image explaining settings + editing decisions |
| Export | All final images exported in appropriate format |

### Document Structure (Adobe Express)

| Page | Content |
|------|---------|
| Cover | Series title + theme + photographer name |
| Technical Specs | Table: each photo's f-stop, shutter speed, ISO, lighting setup |
| Images + Annotations | 1 image per page with caption: settings used + editing decisions |
| Before/After | 2 images shown pre- and post-editing with 1-sentence edit rationale |
| Reflection | 1 paragraph: "What I learned, what I would do differently, what surprised me" |
