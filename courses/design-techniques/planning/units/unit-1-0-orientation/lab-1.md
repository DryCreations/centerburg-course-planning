# Lab 1: Getting to Know You

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 1.0 — Course Orientation
**Duration:** Week 1 (Days 1–3)

---

## Objective

Get settled in the class, help the teacher set up a comfortable environment, and start thinking about what you want to get out of this year.

---

## Part A — Get-to-Know-You Form

Complete the form your teacher shares. There are no wrong answers.

The form will ask:
- What do you go by? Any name preferences?
- What are you into outside of school — hobbies, content you make or watch, games?
- Do you have any experience with design tools, photo editing, video, art, etc.?
- What do you hope to get out of this class?
- What is one thing that makes it hard for you to focus or do your best?
- Is there anything else you want your teacher to know?

---

## Part B — Seating Chart Preferences

Think about where you focus best and write your preferences below (or on the form provided). The teacher will use these to build the initial seating arrangement.

- Where do you focus best (front, back, window, aisle)?
- Anyone you work great with? Anyone you know will be a distraction?
- Anything else?

> *Write your preferences here.*
