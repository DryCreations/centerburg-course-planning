## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

These first two weeks are about getting settled, getting to know each other, and setting up the tools you will use all year. You will share your interests, help shape the classroom setup, start building your Google Sites portfolio site, and create the personal brand kit that will follow you through the entire course.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Navigate Adobe Express and understand how the course tools work
- Configure a brand kit (colors, fonts, personal monogram) in Adobe Express
- Build a live portfolio website in Google Sites that you will add to all year

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Express account not verified:** Set aside Day 3 of Week 1 to troubleshoot logins; have backup plan.
- **Students unsure what to put in brand kit:** Remind them this will evolve — pick what feels right now and refine it in Unit 1.2 and 1.3.
- **Monogram too complex:** Encourage simple, legible initials first; they can redesign it after learning more in Unit 1.3.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who finish early:** Start exploring Adobe Express templates and document what they like or want to try.
- **Students who need more support:** Pair with a peer for the portfolio site setup; teacher should demo each step before students build.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab-1 is participation/completion — not graded for quality. The goal is building culture.
- Lab-2 uses binary demonstrated/not-demonstrated. Key evidence: portfolio site is live and shareable, brand kit has 2+ colors with hex codes, 2 fonts, and a monogram.
- This is a low-stakes setup unit. Focus feedback on portfolio structure and goal quality, not design polish.
