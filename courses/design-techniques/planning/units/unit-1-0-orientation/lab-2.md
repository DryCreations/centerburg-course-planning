# Lab 2: Portfolio Website & Brand Kit Setup

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 1.0 — Orientation
**Duration:** Week 2

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab you will have a live Google Sites portfolio website you will add to all year, a personal brand kit configured in Adobe Express, and a monogram you created yourself.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Create Your Portfolio Website in Google Sites

1. Go to [sites.google.com](https://sites.google.com) and sign in with your school Google account.
2. Click **+ Blank site** (or open the teacher-provided starter site link as a reference). Name your site: `[YourName] Design Portfolio`.
3. Build the following pages (mostly empty for now — you will fill them in all year):
   - **Home / About** — your name, a sentence or two about you, a placeholder for your best project image
   - One page per major project unit (use the **Pages panel** on the right to add new pages; placeholders are fine: "Unit 1.1," "Unit 1.2," etc.)
4. Click **Publish** in the top-right corner, set a web address, and copy the shareable link.

---

### Step 2 — Configure Your Brand Kit

In Adobe Express, go to **Brand** → **Brand Kit** and set up your personal brand:

- **Colors:** Choose 2–3 colors for your personal brand. Find their hex codes (you can use [color.adobe.com](https://color.adobe.com/create/color-wheel) to explore). At minimum: one main color and one neutral.
- **Fonts:** Choose a heading font and a body font from the Adobe Express font library.
- **Logo / Mark:** You will add this in Step 3.

Save the brand kit. It will be used in almost every project this year.

---

### Step 3 — Create Your Monogram

A monogram is a simple personal mark made from your initials. It will be temporary — you may redesign it after the typography unit.

1. Create a new canvas in Adobe Express (any size — square works well).
2. Type your initials. Experiment with:
   - Different fonts
   - Sizes and spacing
   - Overlapping or stacking letters
   - Adding a simple shape behind them
3. Export the monogram as a PNG.
4. Upload it to your Brand Kit as your logo mark.
