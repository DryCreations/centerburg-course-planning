# Project: Brand Website

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 1.4 — Multimedia Tools, UX/UI & Content Strategy
**Duration:** Weeks 8–9
**⚑ End of Q1**

---

## Overview

Build and publish a website in Google Sites that follows the brand style guide you made in Unit 1.3. The site must have real written content — not just placeholder text. You will also draw a wireframe before you build.

---

## Choose Your Direction

Your website subject should match your style guide subject from Unit 1.3:

- **A) Your personal portfolio site** — If you used your own brand, this becomes your personal About/Bio page. Write about yourself, your interests, and what you are learning.
- **B) A bio or wiki page for your character or subject** — If you designed for a character or subject, write a page about them. Treat it like a fan wiki or biography page. Cite your sources.
- **C) A company or persona page** — If you invented a fictional brand or used a persona, build their website. Write the "About," services, or mission content for them.
- **D) BPA-aligned** — If you are pursuing BPA, check with your teacher for the appropriate direction.

> **Citing sources:** If you looked anything up (character facts, brand history, real-world content), cite it at the bottom of the page or in a note to the teacher. A simple link is fine.
