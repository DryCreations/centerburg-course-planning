## Part A — Wireframe

### Step 1: Storyboard Your Website (Week 8, Days 1–2)
1. On paper or in a simple digital tool, draw a low-fidelity wireframe of your one-page brand website. Your site should include:
   - **Header:** Logo, navigation (at least 3 links), and one CTA button
   - **Hero section:** Large visual + headline + subheadline + CTA
   - **Content section:** 3+ content blocks (features, services, or about info)
   - **Image section:** At least 1 photo or visual
   - **Footer:** Contact info, social media links, secondary navigation

2. Label each section with its purpose (not the content — the purpose).

### Step 2: SEO & Accessibility Planning
3. For your website, plan the following before building:
   - **Page title** (what would appear in the browser tab — include your brand name and a keyword)
   - **Meta description** (1–2 sentence summary a search engine would show)
   - **3 primary keywords** you want this page to rank for
   - **Alt text** for every image (write the text before you even have the images)

---

## Part B — Build in Adobe Express (Week 9, Days 1–3)

### Step 3: Build the Website
4. Open Adobe Express → Create → Website.
5. Apply your brand kit (colors, fonts, logo) from Unit 1.3 immediately.
6. Build each section per your wireframe. Keep deviations intentional.

### Step 4: Add SEO & Accessibility Elements
7. For each image you add:
   - Write and add alt text in Express's image properties (or document it alongside the submission)
8. Use your planned keywords naturally in headings and copy (not keyword-stuffed).
9. Add a clear CTA button in the hero section that uses action language: "Schedule a Consultation," "Download the Guide," "Get Started" — not "Click Here."

### Step 5: Responsive Preview
10. In Express, use the preview function to see how your site looks on mobile.
11. Note: Does the navigation work? Do images scale correctly? Is the CTA still visible?
12. Document any responsive issues you notice (even if you can't fix them in Express).

### Step 6: Stakeholder Presentation Prep
13. Prepare a 2-minute verbal description of your site to present to a classmate acting as "the client."
14. Cover: target audience, design decisions, CTA strategy, SEO keywords used.
