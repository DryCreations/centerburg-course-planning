## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

UX (user experience) design is about making things easy and enjoyable to use. This unit covers wireframing, SEO basics, accessibility, and content strategy — then puts it all together in a real published website. By the end of Week 9, you will have a live URL in your portfolio.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Create a wireframe showing page layout and navigation before building
- Plan SEO elements: page title, keywords, and alt text for images
- Build and publish a one-page brand website in Adobe Express
- Present a design rationale to a live audience

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students skip wireframe and go straight to Express:** Require wireframe teacher sign-off before granting access to project template.
- **Website not actually published:** Walk through Express "Share → Publish" vs. "Share → Link" — only published sites have persistent URLs.
- **Presentations are too short:** Provide the 2-minute structure: (1) who is this for, (2) key design decision, (3) one thing you'd change.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a wireframe grid template (6-block layout) to reduce cognitive load.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — write a 3-month content strategy plan for the website.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Presentations happen Day 4 of Week 9 — assess during presentations; students should be able to explain at least 2 design decisions unprompted.
- ⚑ BPA event selection should be recorded before end of Week 9. Keep a class roster log.
