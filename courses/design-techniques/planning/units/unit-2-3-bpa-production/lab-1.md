# Lab: Campaign Production Lab

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 2.3 — BPA Project Production & Social Media Campaign
**Duration:** Weeks 16–18

---

## Objective

Produce a full promotional campaign in Adobe Express: one primary promotional piece + a 3-post social media campaign across at least 3 platforms. Apply brand kit, grid, campaign consistency, and accessibility principles. Conduct a peer design critique before finalizing.

---

## Background

This lab is the primary production phase for BPA #410 Graphic Design Promotion and the class project. All decisions trace back to the project brief and marketing brief from Unit 2.2. By the end of Week 18, your BPA-track deliverable must be ready for the December 1 virtual events registration.

---

## Part A — Primary Promotional Piece

### Week 16 — Draft

1. Using your Unit 2.2 project brief, begin designing your primary promotional piece in Adobe Express:
   - Minimum: 1 print piece OR 1 large digital promotional graphic (poster, banner, or flyer at 8.5×11 or equiv.)
   - Apply your Unit 1.3 brand kit (brand colors, fonts, logo)
   - Activate the Express grid for alignment
   - Add bleed guides if going to print

## Part B — Social Media Campaign (3-Post Set)

### Week 17 — Social Media Production

3. Produce one social media post for each of these 3 platforms (designed to exact platform specs):
   - Platform 1: Instagram Feed Post (1080×1080 px, 1:1)
   - Platform 2: Instagram/Facebook Story (1080×1920 px, 9:16)
   - Platform 3: Student's choice (Twitter/X header, LinkedIn post, YouTube thumbnail — must document dimensions used)

## Extension Task
