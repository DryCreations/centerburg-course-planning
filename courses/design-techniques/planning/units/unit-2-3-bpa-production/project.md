# Project: Promotional Campaign + Social Media Set

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 2.3 — BPA Project Production & Social Media Campaign
**Duration:** Weeks 16–18

---

## Overview

Produce a complete promotional campaign in Adobe Express: one primary promotional piece (poster, flyer, or large digital graphic) plus a 3-post social media campaign across 3 platforms. Apply your brand kit from Unit 1.3, your marketing brief from Unit 2.2, and campaign consistency principles throughout.

---

## Class Prompt: Promotional Campaign

### What You're Producing

A campaign package: one primary piece + 3 social media posts + a production log. Every piece should be instantly recognizable as part of the same campaign through consistent brand elements.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| 1 primary promotional piece (poster, flyer, or digital banner) | 7.3.1 / 7.3.2 |
| 3 social media posts (3 different platforms, correct specs) | 7.3.3 |
| Brand kit applied across all 4 pieces | 7.3.9 |
| Alt text documented for all images | 2.12.2 |
| Color contrast checked (4.5:1 minimum) | 2.12.2 |
| CTA present in each piece | 1.8 |
| Peer critique documented with 2 recorded changes | 2.12.5 |
| Production log (file names, formats, dimensions) | 2.9.5 |

## Standards & Rubric

Walk the room as students work. Ask students to point to evidence for each standard before scoring.

| Standard | Description | Look-For (Proficient = 3) | Beginning (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|

## Extension Task

Create a "campaign brief one-pager" that summarizes the entire campaign — as if you were presenting to a client before production. Include: campaign goal, target audience, 3-sentence UVP, platform choices + rationale, CTA, and a small mockup of each piece. Format it as an Adobe Express document, one page, professionally designed.
