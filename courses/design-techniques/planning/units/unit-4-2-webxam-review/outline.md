## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This unit is dedicated to earning your two credentials: the WebXam (Ohio ITA) and the Adobe Certified Professional (AC PRO) exam. You have done a full year of work that covers everything on both tests — now the goal is to make sure that knowledge is easy to recall when you're sitting in front of the screen.

You will compare your current knowledge to your pretest from Week 1, focus your review on the areas that will help you most, take a practice test, and then sit for both exams.

> **On grades:** Taking both exams and completing the review work earns full credit. The WebXam score itself does not affect your class grade — but the credential you earn is real and can be listed on college applications and resumes.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Identify which WebXam strands are highest-weight and target your review there
- Look back at your pretest data and see how much you've grown
- Make a short study plan based on your actual weakest areas
- Take (and hopefully pass) the WebXam and AC PRO exams

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students don't study because they "already did the work":** Emphasize that course completion ≠ test readiness. The WebXam tests recall under time pressure.
- **AC PRO practical task anxiety:** Practice the interface repeatedly in short sessions; speed comes from muscle memory.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a strand-by-strand vocabulary card set for tactile review.
- **Retake students:** After Initial Test results, identify 2–3 strands with most room for improvement; use lab-2.md retake planning section.

### Assessment Notes

- WebXam score does not factor into class grade — taking the test and demonstrating review effort does.
- Retake window opens 30 days after the Initial Test. Schedule retakes during Unit 4.3 work time.

### BPA Connections

Credential exam preparation is separate from BPA. Students advancing to state or NLC work on BPA polish alongside exam prep.
