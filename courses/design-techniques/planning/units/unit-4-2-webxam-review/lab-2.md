# Lab 2: Credential Exams + Post-Exam Reflection

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 4.2 — WebXam & AC PRO Review and Testing
**Duration:** 2 weeks | **Weeks:** 32–33
**Tools Needed:** Testing computer, Adobe Certification Portal access, review notes from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Attempt the WebXam (Ohio ITA) and AC PRO credential exams after completing the review in lab
- Complete a post-exam reflection documenting confidence areas, surprises, and a retake plan if needed

---

## Background

You have completed the strand-by-strand review and practice test in the lab. This lab-2 is the exam itself plus the reflection that follows. Taking the exam with a plan is the point — the review work is how you prepared.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — WebXam (⚑ April 23)

Sit for the WebXam in class. Use the time — don't rush. After the test, note your strongest and weakest strand areas from your memory of the questions (your teacher may not be able to show you the breakdown until results are returned).

### Step 2 — AC PRO Exam (Weeks 32–33)

Log in to the Adobe Certification Portal. Sit for the AC PRO — Content Creation and Marketing Using Adobe Express exam. Follow all proctoring rules.

### Step 3 — Post-Exam Reflection (Both Exams)

For each exam, write a 1-paragraph reflection answering: (1) What topics felt most confident? (2) What surprised you? (3) What would you study differently if preparing again? (4) What was your target score going in?

### Step 4 — If Retake Needed

If you did not pass either exam, complete a retake plan: identify the 3 lowest-scoring strands, write 5 new vocabulary terms for each, and schedule a retake with the teacher (retake window opens 30 days after the Initial Test).

## Post-Exam Reflection (Both Exams)

After completing each exam, write a 1-paragraph reflection (3–5 sentences):

1. What topics did you feel most confident about on the exam?
2. What surprised you — a question you didn't expect or a topic that felt unfamiliar?
3. What would you study differently if you could prepare again?
4. What was your target score going in?
