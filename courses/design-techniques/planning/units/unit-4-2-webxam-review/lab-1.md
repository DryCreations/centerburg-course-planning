## Part B — Targeted Strand Review Mini-Labs

### Week 30–31

Choose 2 strand-specific mini-reviews from the list below, matching your 2 lowest strands:

**7.4 Graphics Mini-Review:**
- Without looking at notes: list the differences between RGB and CMYK, vector and raster, 72 PPI vs. 300 DPI.
- Export the same design from Adobe Express in 3 formats (PNG, JPEG, PDF). Write 1 use case for each.

**7.5 Typography Mini-Review:**
- Using your Unit 1.3 brand kit, set up a 3-level type hierarchy sample in Express: H1, H2, body.
- Measure 3 pairs of letters in your headline using Express's kerning info. Document: is default spacing adequate or did you adjust?

**7.9 Photography Mini-Review:**
- Without a camera: sketch diagrams for "shallow depth of field" and "wide depth of field" showing aperture, subject, background.
- Open one of your Unit 2.1 photos in Express. Document the editing steps you would take if it were too cool in color and slightly under-exposed.

**7.3 Production Mini-Review:**
- Using the Unit 3.1 revised campaign materials, verify all 4 pieces meet print and digital platform specs. Fill in a specs table.
- Identify any piece where "bleed" would be required for commercial printing. Explain why.

**2.12 Quality / Accessible Mini-Review:**
- Open any Express design. Run a mental WCAG audit: does every text element pass 4.5:1 contrast? Document findings.
- Write the alt text for 5 images in your portfolio.
