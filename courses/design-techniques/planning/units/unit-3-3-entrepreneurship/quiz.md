## Questions

| # | Question | A | B | C | D | Answer |
|---|----------|---|---|---|---|--------|
| 1 | A "freelance designer" is best described as: | A designer who works for one company exclusively | A self-employed designer who works on a project-by-project basis for multiple clients | A designer who only creates logos | A designer employed by an advertising agency | B |
| 2 | A business plan includes: | A list of the designer's personal portfolio pieces | The business concept, market analysis, operating plan, financial projections, and marketing strategy | Only the designer's hourly rate | A list of every client's contact information | B |
| 3 | Before starting a paid design project for a new client, a professional designer creates: | A mood board | A client contract — defining deliverables, timeline, payment, revisions, and IP ownership | A social media post | A wireframe | B |
| 4 | "Scope of work" in a design contract: | Lists the designer's educational credentials | Specifies the exact deliverables, tasks, and boundaries of the project — prevents scope creep | Describes the client's budget history | Defines the designer's personal working hours | B |
| 5 | SEO (Search Engine Optimization) refers to: | Paid social media advertising | Techniques that improve a webpage's ranking in organic search results — including keywords, metadata, and content quality | Scanning QR codes | Publishing content on all social platforms | B |
| 6 | A "meta description" in web design is: | A page heading visible to users | The alt text on images | A short description of the webpage used by search engines in search results — not visible on the page itself | The website's navigation menu | C |
| 7 | QR codes on printed design pieces: | Replace the need for a phone number | Link print materials to digital content (websites, portfolios, social pages) — scannable with a phone camera | Only work on white backgrounds | Are required by law on all business cards | B |
| 8 | A design studio wants to adapt marketing materials for audiences in multiple countries. The appropriate design workflow would involve: | Reprinting everything with new typefaces | Localization — adapting text and design elements for each target language and cultural context while maintaining brand standards | Only creating new photography | Ignoring language differences entirely | B |
| 9 | A "leave-behind" in a client meeting context is: | An invoice sent after work is complete | A printed or digital 1-page summary of services, pricing, or portfolio given to a prospect after a meeting | A thank-you note sent by mail | The final digital files delivered at project close | B |
| 10 | **[HOT]** A designer launching a freelance service charges $10 for every project, no matter how long it takes. What pricing strategy problem does this represent, and what should she do instead? | Nothing — flat fees are always correct | She is undervaluing her work — a professional pricing strategy considers time, complexity, market rate, and value delivered; she should develop a rate sheet (hourly or per-project type) | She is overcharging | She should work for free until she has more experience | B |
| 11 | "Personal brand" for a designer means: | The fonts and colors used in their logo | The consistent image, voice, and values they present publicly — including portfolio, social presence, and professional communication | Their preferred software | Their hourly rate | B |
| 12 | **[HOT]** A designer uploads her portfolio as a PDF and shares it via a QR code on her business card. She also adds keyword-optimized "alt descriptions" to all portfolio images on her website. She's addressing: | Just one strategy — only the QR code matters | Two distribution strategies: print (QR + PDF leave-behind) and digital SEO (keyword alt text) — combining both maximizes how clients can find and access her work | A legal requirement | Only a portfolio requirement | B |
| 13 | **[HOT]** A client asks a designer to create a logo, a website, and "a few social posts — whatever you think." Two weeks in, the client keeps adding requests: email headers, a business card, a brochure. The designer agreed to everything in text messages. What is the professional error? | The designer should have worked faster | There was no client contract — the scope of work was never formally defined, so the designer had no documented basis for limiting revisions or extra deliverables | The designer should have charged more upfront | The client is always right; this is normal | B |
| 14 | Exporting a design as a PDF for professional client distribution is preferred over PNG because: | PDF files are always smaller | PDFs preserve layout, fonts, and colors across devices and can be opened by any client without design software | PNGs cannot be emailed | PDFs are required by law for print work | B |
