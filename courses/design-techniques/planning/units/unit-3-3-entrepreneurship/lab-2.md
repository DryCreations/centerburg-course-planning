# Lab 2: Design Studio Business Package

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 3.3 — Business, Entrepreneurship & Content Optimization
**Duration:** 4 days | **Weeks:** 26–27
**Tools Needed:** Adobe Express, business plan outline and rate sheet from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Compile a 4-document freelance business starter package: business plan, rate sheet, client contract template, and marketing leave-behind
- Export all documents as PDFs with correct settings
- Generate and test a QR code linking to a live portfolio

---

## Background

You drafted the business plan outline and rate sheet in the lab. This lab-2 builds out the full professional package using your brand kit from Unit 1.3.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Finalize the Business Plan (1 page)

Using your lab outline, write the final 1-page business plan in Adobe Express. Required sections: services offered, target clients, pricing model, competitive advantage, startup costs, revenue goal, 3-month plan, and a QR code linking to your portfolio. Apply your brand kit.

### Step 2 — Finalize the Rate Sheet

List 5 services with 2 pricing tiers (e.g., Standard and Rush). Include a brief note on how you calculated each rate. Export as PDF.

### Step 3 — Create the Client Contract Template

Build an 8-section contract template: scope of work, deliverables, revision limit, timeline, payment terms, IP ownership, late fee, and kill fee. Personalize sections to your services — not a copied template.

### Step 4 — Design the Marketing Leave-Behind

Create a 1-page Express design piece: your name/studio name, 3 key services, contact info, and a QR code that links to your live portfolio. Export as PDF and test the QR code on a phone.

> **Checkpoint:** Test your QR code on a device before submitting. A QR code that doesn't work = that checklist item not demonstrated.

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit

Assemble all 4 documents (or links) in a single submission. Add to portfolio as Artifact #10.
