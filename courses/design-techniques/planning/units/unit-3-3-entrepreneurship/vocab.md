# Vocabulary: Business, Entrepreneurship & Content Optimization

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Entrepreneurship** | The process of starting and running a business — taking financial risk in order to earn profit; in design, this often means freelance or studio ownership |
| **Freelance designer** | A self-employed designer who works on a project-by-project basis for multiple clients, rather than being employed by a single company |
| **Business plan** | A written document that describes a business's concept, market analysis, operating plan, financial projections, and marketing strategy |
| **Revenue model** | The strategy by which a business earns income — examples: hourly rate, flat project fee, retainer, subscription, licensing |
| **Client contract** | A legally binding document outlining the scope of work, timeline, deliverables, payment terms, revision limits, and IP ownership for a design project |
| **Scope of work** | The specific tasks, deliverables, and boundaries of a project as formally agreed upon between a designer and client — prevents scope creep |
| **Personal brand** | The consistent image, voice, and values a professional presents publicly — to potential clients, employers, and collaborators; your portfolio is part of your personal brand |
| **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** | Techniques used to improve a webpage's ranking in search engine results — including keyword usage, metadata, page speed, and quality backlinks |
| **Keyword optimization** | Placing relevant search terms naturally in web content so search engines recognize what the page is about and rank it for related searches |
| **Metadata** | Data that describes other data — in web design: page title, meta description, and alt text, which are used by search engines to index the page |
| **PDF for distribution** | A Portable Document Format file formatted for sharing — used for downloadable leave-behinds, presentations, and client-facing documents |
| **QR code** | A two-dimensional barcode that encodes a URL or information — scanned by a phone camera to link print materials to digital content |
| **Translation / localization** | Adapting content for different languages and cultural contexts; design localization ensures text, imagery, and formatting are appropriate for each target audience |
| **Leave-behind** | A printed or digital piece given to a client or prospect after a meeting — typically a 1-page summary of offerings, pricing, or portfolio |
| **Pricing strategy** | The method used to determine what to charge for design work — considerations include market rate, time, complexity, value, and competition |
| **Networking** | Intentional relationship-building with professionals, classmates, and community members who may become clients, employers, or references |

## Review Questions

1. What is the difference between an "employee designer" and a "freelance designer"? Name one advantage and one disadvantage of each.
2. You're starting a freelance design business. A client says "just send me a quick logo." What document would you create before starting any work, and what would it include?
3. Explain the difference between SEO and paid advertising. Can a business do both?
4. Why would a professional designer add a QR code to a printed business card? What problem does it solve?
5. What is a "leave-behind" and what makes a good one? (What should be on it? What format should it be?)
