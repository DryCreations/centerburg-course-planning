## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

What would it look like if you ran your own design business? This unit explores the business side of creative work — not in a dry, textbook way, but as a real thought experiment: what services would you offer, who would you work with, how would you charge for your time, and how would you find clients?

You will also design a marketing leave-behind (a mini business card / one-pager) with a QR code that links to your portfolio. That's a real, practical thing you could use.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Describe a basic freelance business model (services, pricing, how you find work)
- Draft a simple client agreement covering what you'll do, what you'll deliver, and what happens if things change
- Design a leave-behind in Adobe Express with a QR code linking to your portfolio
- Export your work as a PDF

### Choose Your Studio

Pick a context that interests you for the business-planning exercises:

- **A) Your real personal brand** — Plan an actual freelance design business based on your skills right now
- **B) A future version of yourself** — Plan the business you'd want to run in 5 years once you have more skills
- **C) A fictional design studio** — Invent a studio with a name, style, and specialty that sounds cool to you
- **D) BPA #505 Entrepreneurship** — Follow the BPA event format; check with your teacher

> The leave-behind (marketing piece) will be designed for whichever context you choose.

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Contract template is copied verbatim from online:** Require students to personalize the scope and revision sections to their specific services.
- **QR code links to wrong page:** Test every QR code before submission is accepted.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a 3-column rate sheet template with sample entries.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — research a real freelancer's rate structure and compare to their own.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab-2 uses a binary checklist. Key evidence: all 4 documents present; QR code tested and working; all docs use brand kit from Unit 1.3; PDF exports submitted (not just Express links).

### BPA Connections

**#505 Entrepreneurship Presented Business Plan:** This lab-2 is the foundation for #505. BPA students extend the 1-page plan into a full 10–15 page document with financial projections for competition.
