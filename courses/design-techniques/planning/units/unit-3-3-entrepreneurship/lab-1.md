## Part C — Simple Client Agreement

Write a short (1-page) agreement that covers what would happen if someone hired you. Include:

- What you'll make for them
- How many changes they can ask for before you charge extra
- When you get paid (all upfront? half now, half when done?)
- Who owns the finished design

It doesn't have to be in legal language. Write it the way you would actually want it to work.
