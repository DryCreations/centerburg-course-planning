# Lab 2: BPA Project Proposal & Marketing Brief

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 2.2 — Production Workflow, Project Proposals & Marketing Plans
**Duration:** 4 days | **Weeks:** 14–15
**Tools Needed:** Adobe Express or Google Docs, project brief outline from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Write a complete project brief covering all 8 required sections
- Create a detailed buyer persona with demographics, behaviors, and a persona quote
- Develop a marketing brief and 4-week, 3-platform content calendar

---

## Background

These planning documents guide everything you produce in Units 2.3–3.1. Strong planning now = stronger production later. You'll reference this package repeatedly.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Project Brief (Day 1)

Using your lab outline as a starting point, write the final project brief. Required sections: (1) Problem statement, (2) Target audience, (3) Deliverables list, (4) Milestones, (5) Constraints, (6) Success criteria, (7) Scope note, (8) Version history.

### Step 2 — Buyer Persona (Day 1–2)

Create one detailed buyer persona. Required fields: name, age/demographic, goals, frustrations, media habits, purchasing behavior, and a direct quote in their voice. Specificity is required — "busy professional" is not a persona.

### Step 3 — Marketing Brief (Day 2–3)

Write the marketing brief. Required sections: campaign goal, UVP, 3 channels (each with a distinct key message and CTA), asset list.

### Step 4 — Content Calendar (Day 3–4)

Create a 4-week content calendar with 3+ platforms and at least 12 entries. Each entry must include: date, platform, content type (reel/story/email/post), caption/CTA, and a note about which campaign message it serves.

> **Checkpoint:** Teacher reviews your project brief before you build the marketing brief. Get sign-off on the problem statement and deliverables list first.

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit

Assemble all 4 documents in a single file (Adobe Express multi-page or Google Doc). Copy the shareable link and add to portfolio as Artifact #6.
