## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Before jumping into a big design project, it helps to actually plan it out. In this unit you will practice the planning tools that designers use: writing a brief (what you are making and for whom), defining your audience, and sketching out a basic plan for how you would promote something. Think of it as learning how to think like a designer before you start making things.

You will apply these skills to a project of your choice — your own portfolio, a BPA prompt, or a made-up scenario you find interesting.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Write a simple project brief covering what you are making, who it is for, and what success looks like
- Describe an audience using specific details
- Sketch out a basic content or promotion plan

### Choose Your Direction

For the lab and project in this unit, you can apply the planning tools to:
- **Option A:** Your own brand/portfolio (continue what you started in Q1)
- **Option B:** A BPA event prompt (use the planning tools to prep for competition)
- **Option C:** An invented scenario you find interesting (a band, a community event, a small business)

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Personas are too generic:** Require a name, quote, and specific frustration — "busy parents" is not a persona; "Maria, 34, frustrated by checkout processes that require account creation" is.
- **Content calendars have vague entries:** Each entry must have a specific content type (reel, story, blog, email) and a specific CTA (buy, follow, share, read more).

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a completed persona template with labels for each required field.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — extend the content calendar to 8 weeks.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab-2 uses a binary checklist. Key evidence: all 4 documents present; content calendar has 12+ entries with platforms and CTAs.
- These planning documents are graded NOW and referenced again when Unit 2.3 production begins. Incomplete planning = weak production.
