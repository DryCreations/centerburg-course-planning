# Vocabulary: Refinement, Testing & Quality Review

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Refinement** | The iterative process of improving a design based on feedback, testing, and evaluation — making successive small improvements to reach the best possible solution |
| **Usability testing** | Evaluating a design by testing it with real or representative users — observing how they navigate, completing tasks, and identifying points of confusion |
| **A/B testing** | Showing two versions (A and B) of a design to different audience groups to determine which performs better based on measurable outcomes |
| **Design audit** | A systematic review of all design pieces for quality, consistency, accessibility, and alignment with the project brief |
| **Rubric** | A scoring guide specifying criteria and performance levels — used in design critiques and evaluations to assess work against defined standards |
| **Revision history** | A documented record of changes made to a design at each iteration — shows what changed, why, and when |
| **WCAG** | Web Content Accessibility Guidelines — the international standard for accessible digital design, including color contrast, font size, and keyboard navigability |
| **Readability** | How easily a reader can process and understand written text — determined by font size, line length, leading, contrast, and reading level |
| **Scanability** | How quickly a viewer's eye can move through a layout and absorb key information — aided by headings, bullets, white space, and visual hierarchy |
| **Pre-submission review** | A final check of all deliverables against the project brief and evaluation criteria before the submission deadline — verifies required formats, content accuracy, and technical specifications |
| **Feedback documentation** | Written record of critique comments received and the specific changes made in response — demonstrates professional development and revision practice |
| **Portfolio curation** | The process of selecting, organizing, and presenting your best work — choosing what to include, what to exclude, and how to frame each piece for a specific audience |
| **Annotation** | An explanatory note added to a design showing the rationale for a visual decision — used in portfolio presentations and professional deliverables |

## Review Questions

1. What is the difference between a "design critique" and "usability testing"?
2. You receive peer feedback saying your poster is "hard to read." What 4 specific design elements might you adjust to improve readability?
3. A company is trying to decide between two versions of a landing page button — one blue, one green. They run both versions with different users for two weeks. What is this process called and what does it measure?
4. Why is a pre-submission review important before delivering a design project?
5. What is the purpose of annotating a design in a portfolio — why not just show the finished piece?
