## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

A first draft is never the final version. In this unit you will look back at your best work from Q1 and Q2 with fresh eyes: find what isn't working, fix it, and document your thinking. This is one of the most important skills in any creative field — knowing how to revise intentionally, not just randomly change things.

BPA students also use this time to finalize competition entries and prepare for the local showcase.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Choose which pieces from your portfolio are worth refining and explain why
- Run a design audit against a quality checklist
- Get feedback from a partner and translate it into specific changes
- Annotate a finished piece to explain the decisions you made

### Choose Your Focus

Pick **2–3 pieces** from your portfolio to refine this unit. Choose work you are most proud of — or work you know could be significantly better with another pass. You can revisit your brand style guide, your website, your photography edits, or your promotional campaign.

> **BPA students:** This unit is also when you finalize virtual event entries. Check with your teacher for your event's submission deadline and requirements.

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Revisions are cosmetic, not substantive:** Require each revision to trace back to a specific audit finding or usability test result.
- **Annotation is just description:** An annotation explains WHY a decision was made, not just WHAT is there. Prompt: "Why did you choose [X] over the alternative?"

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a sentence frame for annotations: "I chose [element] because [principle], which achieves [effect]."
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — write a post-competition debrief after the local showcase.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Walk room during annotation writing — students should be able to verbally explain each annotation before you score it.
