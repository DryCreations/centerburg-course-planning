## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

The capstone is the biggest project of the year. You will design a complete brand or creative identity — logo, style guide, at least one promotional piece, an animated element, and a website — and present your finished work to a live audience.

This is your chance to show everything you have learned. You get to choose what you build it around.

### Choose Your Capstone Brand

Pick a subject that gives you something real to design:

- **A) Your personal brand / portfolio** — Design the final, polished version of the brand you've been building all year
- **B) A fictional brand you care about** — A studio, a business concept, a creative project — something you'd actually want to exist
- **C) A BPA-aligned project** — Use this as your BPA submission work for a brand-identity event; check with your teacher
- **D) A real-world client (optional)** — If a community client opportunity is available, the teacher will share details

> You can continue and improve something you started earlier, or start fresh with a new direction. Either is fine.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Design a logo (with at least 2 variations: color and a simplified/icon version)
- Build a complete brand style guide covering logo, colors, type, and usage
- Produce at least one branded collateral piece (social post, poster, or flyer)
- Make a short animated element in Adobe Express (slideshow, animated post, or short video)
- Publish or update your portfolio website to feature the finished brand
- Present your work and explain your design decisions to a live audience

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Logo suite is just one logo resized:** A horizontal lockup, icon, and reversed version all require distinct design decisions — not just repositioning.
- **Process book is a photo dump:** Require narrative text for each phase: what was the problem, what did you try, what did you decide and why?
- **Presentations run over/under 15 minutes:** Practice timing in Week 36 or early Week 37.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Allow the collateral set to build on existing work (e.g., the social media posts from Unit 2.3 as one collateral piece).
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — write a Year in Review reflection comparing Artifact #0 goals to Artifact #12 outcomes.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Schedule individual walkthrough conferences in Week 37 alongside presentations. Students should be able to point to evidence for every standard.
- Grading breakdown: 7 deliverables (60%), process book (15%), portfolio finalization (10%), presentation (15%).
