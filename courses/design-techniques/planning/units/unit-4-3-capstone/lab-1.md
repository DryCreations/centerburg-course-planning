# Lab 1: Capstone Design Brief & Mood Board

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 4.3 — Capstone: Brand Identity Project
**Duration:** Week 34, Days 1–2

---

## Objective

Define what you are making for the capstone. Lock in your brand direction before you start designing. Your teacher will sign off on your brief before you move to logo work.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Your Brand Subject

Before filling out the brief, confirm your direction (from the outline):

- **A) Your personal brand** — The final, polished version of what you've been building all year
- **B) A fictional brand you care about** — A studio, a business, a creative concept
- **C) BPA-aligned** — Check with your teacher
- **D) Real-world client** — If available, teacher will provide details

Write your choice and a 2-sentence explanation of why you chose it:

## Step 3 — Mood Board

Create a 1-page mood board in Adobe Express. Include 6–8 reference images that show the visual direction you want — colors, typography style, photography, layout energy. Add short labels to explain why each image belongs.

> **Sources:** Images can come from anywhere (the internet, Adobe Stock, screenshots) — this is a reference document, not for publication. Label the source if you can.

## Week 37 — Process Book + Portfolio + Presentation

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Design challenge | The problem the brand was designed to solve |
| Research | Audience insights, competitor overview, mood board |
