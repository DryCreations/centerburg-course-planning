| 6 | **[HOT]** A designer presents a completed brand project to a professional panel. She shows only the final poster and says "Here's my project." A panel member asks "Why did you choose this color palette?" She says "I liked it." What is the professional weakness here? | She should have used more colors | She lacks a design rationale — professional designers connect every decision to audience research, brand strategy, and visual communication principles | Her presentation was too short | She should have used a different format | B |
| 7 | Portfolio finalization involves: | Uploading every file you created | Curating your best work, annotating each piece with a design rationale, and organizing the portfolio for a specific audience | Only the most recent project | Exporting everything as JPEG | B |
| 8 | The animated social ad component of a brand campaign should be: | At least 60 seconds with voiceover | A slide deck | 5–15 seconds, brand-aligned, containing brand identity elements and a clear message | A still image in story format only | C |
| 9 | **[HOT]** A designer has 10 minutes for a professional panel presentation. She spends 8 minutes explaining technical process and 2 on the actual work and results. | 10 | **[HOT]** A designer has completed 11 artifacts. She's selecting 5 for her portfolio.
