# Vocabulary: Capstone — Brand Identity Project

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Culminating project** | A comprehensive project demonstrating mastery across multiple skill areas, requiring planning, production, presentation, and reflection |
| **Brand identity package** | The complete set of visual and communicative assets that define and represent a brand — typically includes logo suite, color palette, typography system, style guide, collateral, and campaign materials |
| **Logo suite** | The complete family of logo variations for a brand: primary logo, horizontal variant, icon/mark only, full-color, one-color, and reversed (white on dark) versions |
| **Collateral** | Physical or digital marketing materials produced using the brand identity — business cards, letterhead, social templates, merchandise mockups |
| **Process documentation** | A record showing the complete design process: research, ideation, sketches, iterations, final decisions, and reflection — demonstrates thinking, not just outcome |
| **Design rationale** | The explanation for why specific design decisions were made — connecting visual choices to communication goals, audience needs, and brand strategy |
| **Animated social ad** | A short video or motion graphic (typically 5–15 seconds) created for social platforms — conveys the brand message with animation, timing, and sound |
| **One-page website** | A single-page published website serving as the live digital face of a brand |
| **Marketing strategy brief** | A concise summary of the brand's target audience, UVP, channels, and campaign objectives — translates brand identity into actionable marketing |
| **Portfolio finalization** | The process of curating, annotating, and organizing all course artifacts into a professional portfolio for presentation — selecting pieces, writing rationales, ensuring consistency |
| **Professional presentation** | Presenting completed design work to a client, panel, or stakeholder audience — demonstrating communication skills alongside technical skill |
| **Design thinking** | A human-centered problem-solving methodology: empathize → define → ideate → prototype → test — used to structure the capstone design process |
| **Professional poise** | The composure, preparation, and communication skill demonstrated when presenting work to a professional audience |

## Review Questions

1. What 7 elements are typically included in a complete brand identity package? List them.
2. Why does process documentation matter in a portfolio? What does it show that the finished piece alone can't?
3. What is a "logo suite" and why does a brand need more than just one logo version?
4. What is the difference between "brand collateral" and "marketing campaign materials"?
5. You have 5 minutes to present your brand project to a professional panel or stakeholder audience. What are the most important 3 things you would communicate?
