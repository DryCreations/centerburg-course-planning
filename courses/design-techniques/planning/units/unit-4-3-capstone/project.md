# Project: Brand Identity Capstone

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 4.3 — Capstone: Brand Identity Project
**Duration:** Weeks 34–37

---

## Overview

Design and deliver a complete Brand Identity Package for a fictional (or real school/community) brand. Apply every major skill from the course: brand strategy, typography, color theory, photography, production workflow, accessibility, AI-assisted design, business/client practice, and professional handoff. Present to class and/or Business Advisory Council.

---

## Class Prompt: 7-Deliverable Brand Identity Package

### What You're Producing

A comprehensive brand system from brief to delivery — ready to hand off to a real client.

### The 7 Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Description | Artifact |
|---|-------------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Logo suite | 6 variants: primary, horizontal, icon, full-color, one-color, reversed | Wk 34 |
| 2 | Brand style guide | 6-section guide: cover, logo, colors, typography, imagery, tone | Wk 35 |
| 3 | Collateral set | 2 branded pieces from the collateral options | Wk 35 |
| 4 | Animated social ad | 5–15 sec, brand-aligned, message + CTA | Wk 36 |
| 5 | One-page website | Updated final version, published, WCAG-compliant | Wk 36 |
| 6 | Marketing strategy brief | Audience, UVP, channels, 3-month content plan, KPI | Wk 36 |
| 7 | Process book | Brief → research → ideation → iterations → final → reflection | Wk 37 |
