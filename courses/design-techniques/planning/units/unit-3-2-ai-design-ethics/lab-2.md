# Lab 2: AI-Assisted Design Piece + Ethics Reflection

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 3.2 — AI in Design & Generative AI Ethics
**Duration:** 4 days | **Weeks:** 24–25
**Tools Needed:** Adobe Express (AI features — text to image, generative fill, background removal), prompt table from Lab 1

---

## Objective

Make a design that mixes AI-generated content with your own creative work. Then reflect honestly on the ethical side of using AI in design.

---

## Choose Your Piece

Pick something that interests you to design:

- **A) Something for your brand** — Add a Firefly-generated image element to your brand style guide, website, or a new piece in your brand's voice
- **B) A poster or graphic** — For a band, cause, game, show, sport, or anything you care about
- **C) Conceptual / experimental** — Use Firefly to explore a visual mood or idea — no brand needed
- **D) BPA-aligned** — Use this as production time toward a BPA entry that includes graphics

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Plan Your Piece

Write down:
- What format are you making? (social post, poster, banner, infographic)
- What subject are you designing for?
- What are you going to ask Firefly to generate?
- What are YOU going to make yourself?

### Step 2 — Build the Design in Adobe Express

Apply your brand kit (or create a simple one for the new subject). Use at least one Firefly feature (text-to-image, generative fill, or background removal). Make sure you are also contributing original work — layout, type, color choices, etc. are yours.

Document what you used Firefly for:

| AI Feature Used | What you prompted it to do | What you got | Did you use it? |
|---------------------|-----------------------------|-------------|----------------|
| | | | Yes / No / Modified |

### Step 3 — Add a Disclosure Note

Somewhere on the finished piece, add a small note explaining that AI was used and what it was used for. It can be subtle but it should be there.

Example: *"Background image: Adobe Express AI (text-to-image). All other elements by [your name]."*

### Step 4 — Ethics Reflection

Write a short reflection (about a paragraph total, 4–6 sentences). Address at least 2 of these questions:

- Who "made" the AI-generated part of your design? Does it feel like yours?
- Did the AI outputs seem to favor certain looks, people, or styles? What did you notice?
- Would it be okay to use this design commercially? Why or why not?
- What did the AI decide, and what did you decide? Does the balance feel right?

There are no wrong answers here — the goal is honest thinking.

## Extension Task

> **Finished early?** Imagine you run a small design studio. Write a short "AI Use Policy" for your studio: when is AI okay to use, what do you tell clients, and what are the limits? Keep it to 1 page or less. Make it feel real — like something you'd actually post.


### Step 1 — Plan Your Design Piece

Choose a format: social post, promotional banner, or infographic (your choice). The piece must include: at least 1 Adobe Firefly-generated element AND at least 1 original element (photo or graphic you created).

### Step 2 — Produce the Design in Adobe Express

Apply your brand kit. Use the **Generate AI → Generate image** feature or **Insert object** (generative fill) for the AI elements. Document which features you used and what decision you made at each step.

### Step 3 — Add the Disclosure Note

Add visible text to the finished piece noting: which AI feature was used and what it was used for. Example: "Image background generated using Adobe Express AI. Layout, typography, and brand elements by [your name]."

### Step 4 — Write the Ethics Reflection

In a separate page or document, write 2–3 sentences on each of these 4 topics:
1. **Attribution** — Who "made" the AI-generated element? What attribution is appropriate?
2. **Bias** — What might the AI model reflect in its outputs? Did you notice anything?
3. **Licensing** — Is this design safe for commercial use? Why or why not?
4. **Creative control** — What did you decide, and what did the AI decide? Does that matter?

### Step 5 — Export and Submit

Export design as PNG. Compile design + ethics reflection in one Express document or Google Doc. Submit shareable link.
