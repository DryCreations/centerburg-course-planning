## Objective

Explore the generative AI features built into Adobe Express. Practice writing better prompts and start thinking critically about what AI can and can't do.

## Part C — AI Ethics Reflection

### Week 24

10. Answer the following questions in writing (2–3 sentences each):

**Attribution:**
- If you submit a design to BPA that includes an AI-generated element, what should you disclose in your process documentation?

**Bias:**
- Look at 10 Firefly outputs from a general search (or your own). Do you notice any patterns in how people, places, or cultural contexts are depicted? What might cause this?

**Commercial Licensing:**
- Why is it safer to use Adobe Express's AI image tools (powered by Firefly models trained on licensed content) than tools like Midjourney or DALL·E for a design you'll sell or submit to competition?

**Human Creative Control:**
- List 3 specific decisions YOU made in this lab that were creative decisions, not AI decisions.

---

## Part D — AI-Integrated Design Piece

### Week 25

11. Create one design piece in Adobe Express that:
    - Uses at least 1 AI-generated element from Express (generated image, generative fill, or expanded image)
    - Integrates your own photography or graphics
    - Applies your Unit 1.3 brand kit
    - Includes a visible AI Disclosure note in small text: "AI assistance: Adobe Express AI tools used for [specific element]"

12. Write a brief (3–5 sentences) process note:
    - What did the AI provide?
    - What did YOU decide/create/refine?
    - How do you distinguish the human creative contribution from the AI contribution?
