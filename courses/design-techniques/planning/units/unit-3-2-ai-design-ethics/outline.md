## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

AI tools — including generative image features built into Adobe Express (powered by Adobe's Firefly AI models) — are already part of how designers work. This unit teaches you how to use generative AI in a way that is intentional, responsible, and honest. You will experiment with what it can do, think critically about where it falls short, and produce a piece that combines AI-generated content with your own creative work.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Use Adobe Express's AI features (text to image, generative fill, background removal)
- Write effective prompts and explain why different prompts give different results
- Describe the ethical considerations around AI-generated content: who owns it, who it might leave out, and how to be transparent about using it
- Make a design that combines AI assistance with your own creative choices

### Choose Your Direction

For the design piece in this unit, pick a subject that interests you:

- **A) Extend your brand** — Use Firefly to add AI-generated imagery to your brand style guide or website
- **B) Poster or graphic for something you care about** — A band, a cause, a game, a sport, a show
- **C) Experimental / conceptual piece** — Use Firefly to explore a mood, aesthetic, or idea with no real-world brand attached
- **D) BPA-aligned work** — Use this as production time toward a BPA entry that involves graphics or multimedia

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students treat the AI piece as "done" after generation:** Require that human-created content is also present and that the disclosure note names the AI tool and describes what it was used for.
- **Ethics reflection answers are shallow:** Require citing a specific example, case, or scenario for at least 2 of the 4 topics.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a sentence starter for each ethics topic.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — test the same prompt in two different AI tools and compare outputs; analyze what the differences reveal about training data.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab-2 uses a binary checklist. Key evidence: prompt table with 3 distinct versions; design piece has both AI and original elements; ethics reflection covers all 4 topics with 2–3 sentences each.

### BPA Connections
