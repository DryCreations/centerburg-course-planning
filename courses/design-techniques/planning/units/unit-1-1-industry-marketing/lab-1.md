# Lab 1: Interactive Media Career Jigsaw

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 1.1 — The Interactive Media Industry & Digital Marketing Foundations
**Duration:** Week 2, Days 1–3

---

## Objective

Learn about the wide variety of careers in the interactive media field by researching and teaching each other.

---

## How It Works — Jigsaw Activity

The class is split into small groups. Each group gets a different interactive media career area to research. After researching, everyone comes back together and each group teaches the rest of the class what they found.

---

## Step 1 — Get Your Career Area

Your teacher will assign your group one of the following:

- **Graphic / Visual Designer** — logos, branding, print, digital graphics
- **UX / Web Designer** — wireframes, user flows, websites, apps
- **Video Editor / Motion Graphics** — editing, animation, titles, effects
- **Social Media / Content Creator** — platforms, content strategy, analytics
- **Game / Interactive Media Designer** — game assets, level design, interactivity
- **Photographer / Visual Media Specialist** — photo editing, promotional photography
- **AI + Emerging Media** — AI tools in design, generative content, new platforms

---

## Step 2 — Research (20–25 min)

As a group, look up your career area. Find answers to these questions:

| Question | Your Group's Answers |
|----------|---------------------|
| What does someone in this role do day-to-day? | |
| What tools or software do they use? | |
| What do they typically make? Give 2–3 examples. | |
| What industries hire these people? | |
| What is a realistic starting salary? | |
| What is one thing that surprised your group? | |

---

## Step 3 — Prepare to Share (5–10 min)

Decide how your group will share. You might:
- Present verbally (talk through your answers)
- Write key facts on the board or a shared doc
- Show an example of work from that career

Each person in the group should be ready to say something.
