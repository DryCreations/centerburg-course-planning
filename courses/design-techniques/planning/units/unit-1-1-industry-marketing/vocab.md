# Vocabulary: Interactive Media Industry & Digital Marketing Foundations

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Interactive media** | Digital media that responds to user input — websites, apps, games, social media, and multimedia presentations |
| **Digital marketing** | Promoting products or brands through digital channels — websites, social media, email, search engines, and paid ads |
| **4 P's of marketing** | Product, Price, Place, Promotion — the four pillars of any marketing strategy |
| **Promotional mix** | The combination of advertising, PR, personal selling, sales promotion, and direct marketing used to reach an audience |
| **Target audience** | The specific group of people a product or message is intended to reach, defined by demographics, behaviors, and interests |
| **Buyer persona** | A detailed fictional profile representing the ideal customer — includes age, goals, pain points, media habits, and decision drivers |
| **B2B (Business-to-Business)** | Marketing strategy where the customer is another business rather than an individual consumer |
| **B2C (Business-to-Consumer)** | Marketing strategy where the customer is an individual consumer |
| **Owned media** | Content channels fully controlled by a brand — website, email list, social profiles |
| **Earned media** | Free publicity gained through shares, press, reviews, or word-of-mouth — not paid for |
| **Paid media** | Advertising a brand pays to place — display ads, sponsored posts, PPC |
| **Content marketing** | A strategy of creating valuable, useful content to attract and engage a target audience rather than directly advertising |
| **Interactive media genre** | Categories of interactive media: games, websites, apps, VR experiences, kiosks, digital signage, e-learning platforms |
| **Intellectual property (IP)** | Legal protection over creative works — copyright, trademark, and patents protect different types of IP |
| **Creative Commons** | A flexible licensing system that lets creators share work with conditions: attribution, no commercial use, share-alike |
| **Public domain** | Work that is no longer (or never was) copyright-protected — free for anyone to use |
| **Fair use** | A legal doctrine allowing limited use of copyrighted material without permission — for Commentary, Criticism, Education, or Parody |

---

## Key Distinctions

| Term 1 | Term 2 | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Owned media | Earned media | Owned = you control it; Earned = others create or share it voluntarily |
| B2B | B2C | B2B = selling to businesses; B2C = selling to individual consumers — different messaging, channels, and buyer journeys |
| Copyright | Creative Commons | Copyright = all rights reserved by default; Creative Commons = creator grants specific permissions to the public |
| Fair use | Public domain | Fair use = limited use exception for copyrighted work; Public domain = no longer protected at all |
