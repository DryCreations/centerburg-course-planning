# Quiz: Interactive Media Industry & Digital Marketing Foundations

---

## Questions

| # | Question | A | B | C | D | Answer |
|---|----------|---|---|---|---|--------|
| 1 | Which of the following is the best example of interactive media? | A print magazine | A radio advertisement | A website that changes content based on user clicks | A static billboard | C |
| 2 | Which of the following is a characteristic of interactive media that print media lacks? | It uses color | It responds to user input — the experience changes based on what the user does | It can include photos | It can be produced cheaply | B |
| 3 | The 4 P's of marketing refer to: | Page, Photo, Post, Publish | People, Process, Product, Promotion | Product, Price, Place, Promotion | Platform, Page, Placement, Position | C |
| 4 | A brand posts a series of free tutorials about using their product. This is an example of: | Paid media | Earned media | Content marketing (owned media) | Display advertising | C |
| 5 | Which media type is a brand's email newsletter? | Earned media | Paid media | Owned media | Borrowed media | C |
| 6 | A buyer persona differs from a target audience because: | A buyer persona is hypothetical | A buyer persona is a detailed fictional profile of one ideal customer — it includes name, goals, pain points, behaviors, and media habits; a target audience is a broader demographic description | A target audience has a name and backstory | A buyer persona is used only for B2B marketing | B |
| 7 | A software company sells its product directly to other businesses, not to individual consumers. This company uses a: | B2C strategy | B2B strategy | Content marketing strategy | Earned media strategy | B |
| 8 | A student wants to use a photo from Flickr in her design project. The photo is labeled "CC BY-NC." She plans to include the work in a paid client project. Can she use it? | Yes — CC means free to use by anyone | No — NC means Non-Commercial, and paid work is commercial use; she needs to find a CC BY license or public domain image | Yes — she just needs to give credit | Yes — educational use is always exempt from licensing | B |
| 9 | Which of the following best describes "earned media"? | A sponsored Facebook post | A Google display ad | Coverage from a journalist who reviewed your product without being paid | Your brand's YouTube channel | C |
| 10 | Which IP protection is automatically applied to a creative work the moment it is created? | Patent | Trademark | Trade secret | Copyright | D |
| 11 | In the context of interactive media, "genre" refers to: | The file format | The category of interactive media experience — such as games, apps, educational tools, or websites | The color scheme used | The programming language | B |
| 12 | **[HOT]** A designer creates an interactive infographic for a competition entry. A judge notes that it "doesn't seem to be designed for any particular audience." Which early design step was skipped? | Choosing a color palette | Defining a target audience / buyer persona — all designs must start with WHO receives the message | Selecting fonts | Exporting to PDF | B |
| 13 | The promotional mix in marketing includes: | Color, font, layout, images | Advertising, PR, personal selling, sales promotion, and direct marketing | Brand kit, template, library, and portfolio | SEO, alt tags, keywords, and backlinks | B |
| 14 | **[HOT]** A student says: "Copyright doesn't matter for my school video project — it's for school, not profit." Why is this reasoning flawed? | School projects are not covered by copyright law | Copyright still protects the work regardless of profit motive; educational use may qualify as fair use only in limited, non-commercial, and transformative cases — public screening or display may not qualify | Copyright only applies to music, not images | Once you download a video, you own it | B |
| 15 | **[HOT]** A local restaurant wants to reach new customers who live within 5 miles, aged 25–40, who frequently dine out. They ask your advice on the first step.
