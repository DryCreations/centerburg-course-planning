# Unit 1.1 — The Interactive Media Industry & Digital Marketing: Outline

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Weeks:** 2–3 | **Duration:** 2 weeks

---

## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This unit maps the interactive media industry: what kinds of media exist, who makes them, how they're marketed, and what intellectual property rules govern creative work. You'll research the landscape and produce your first real portfolio piece — an industry infographic.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Classify types of interactive media by genre, format, and audience
- Explain at least one digital marketing concept (4 P's, content types, or buyer journey)
- Identify copyright and IP considerations relevant to design work
- Create and publish a single-page informational infographic in Adobe Express

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students list only social media:** Push them to include interactive media beyond social (games, AR/VR, interactive video, web experiences).
- **IP section treated as optional:** Make the IP note a required checklist item before submission is accepted.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a completed example row in the media survey table.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — add a "trend" section to the infographic covering one emerging technology in interactive media.

### Assessment Notes

- Lab uses a binary checklist — 10 categorized examples and 1 media critique are required evidence.
- Lab-2 uses a binary checklist — all five content elements must be visually present and labeled for credit.

### BPA Connections

No direct BPA event, but this unit builds Express layout fluency that applies to **#410 Graphic Design Promotion** and **V12 Social Media Campaign**. Students interested in #410 should use a real-world brand or the WSAP-assigned client for their infographic research.
