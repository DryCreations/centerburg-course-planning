# Lab 2: Interactive Media Infographic

**Course:** Design Techniques (145095)
**Unit:** 1.1 — The Interactive Media Industry & Digital Marketing Foundations
**Duration:** 2–3 days | **Week:** 3
**Tools Needed:** Adobe Express (use a provided template), browser for research

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab you will have produced a one-page infographic in Adobe Express that informs an audience about a topic in the interactive media industry.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Your Topic

Pick **one** of the following topics to research and design your infographic around:

- **A) The Interactive Media Career Landscape** — What jobs exist in this field? What do they do? What tools do they use?
- **B) How Digital Marketing Works** — Explain the 4 P's, media types (owned/earned/paid), and content strategy to someone who has never heard of it.
- **C) A Specific Interactive Media Genre** — Deep-dive one genre (gaming, social media, UX/web design, etc.): history, examples, audience, career paths.
- **D) IP and Copyright in Digital Media** — What are the rules? What happens when they're broken? What licenses exist?

---

## Step 2 — Research (20–30 min)

Use your topic to find 4–6 key facts or concepts to include. Write them below before you open Express.

> *Notes:*

---

## Step 3 — Open the Template

Your teacher will share an Adobe Express template link. Open it and make a copy.

The template has sections built in — edit text, swap images, adjust colors. Apply your personal brand kit colors and fonts where possible.

---

## Step 4 — Build Your Infographic

Your infographic must include:

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Clear title and visual hierarchy | 1 title, 1–2 heading levels used |
| Key facts or concepts | 4–6 with labels or visuals |
| At least 1 image or icon | From Express built-in library |
| Brand kit applied | Your colors and fonts used |
| IP note | Brief note on your image source (e.g., "Adobe Express library — royalty-free") |

---

## Step 5 — Review and Submit

Check: Is the text readable? Is there a clear hierarchy? Is it something you'd want to show someone?

Export as PNG and copy the shareable link.
