## What This Week Covers

**State.** The reason your prototype needed four nearly identical frames is that something in it changes,
and duplicating screens is how you fake a change you cannot yet build.

A **variable** is how you stop faking it. One value, stored, that screens read from and buttons change.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Point at a screen and say which parts of it are **state** and which are **layout**
- Create a variable, give it a sensible name and type, and change it from a button
- Bind a text layer or a component property to a variable so it updates on its own
- Replace a stack of near-identical frames with **one** frame that changes
- Explain why designers and programmers mean the same thing by "variable"
