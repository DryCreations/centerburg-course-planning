**Secondary Course Code:** 145095
**CTAG Course:** CTIM008 — Digital Page Layout and Design
**Credit Value:** 3 Semester Hours
**CTAG Eligibility Status:** Eligible ✅

---

## Eligibility Overview

CTIM008 credit is awarded to students who:

1. Successfully complete DEW secondary course **Design Techniques (145095)** and earn a qualifying score on the corresponding End-of-Course (WebXam) exam.
2. Matriculate to an Ohio institution of higher education with an approved or comparable program **within 3 years** of completing the approved secondary course.
3. Grant permission for credit posting — either through the end-of-course WebXam or directly with the attending institution.

### Secondary Institutional Approval

- Secondary programs must have **pathway approval from the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW)**
- Certificate of Affirmation assurances are incorporated into the **CTE-26 application process**
- This course is approved under the Interactive Media (N1) pathway

### Coverage Notes

- **7.4.1 / 7.4.10 (Color profiles):** Adobe Express primarily works in RGB. Explicitly teach when CMYK is required (print production) and when Pantone applies (brand consistency). Students should understand the conversion and use case even if the tool handles it automatically.
- **7.4.5 (Vector vs. raster):** Ensure Unit 1.2 includes a practical exercise distinguishing vector scalability from raster resolution — a common exam question.
- **7.4.7 / 7.4.9 (Export & compress):** These are addressed in the rollout/handoff unit Q4. Consider adding a brief format-and-export exercise to Unit 2.3 so students encounter it mid-year, not just at the end.
- **7.5.5 (Type style guide):** Currently a Q1 deliverable. This is high-value CTAG evidence — ensure it is saved in the student portfolio and referenced in the design brief in Q2.

## Articulation — Eligible Credit Path

Students who complete **Design Techniques (145095)** with a qualifying WebXam score may receive **3 semester hours** of postsecondary credit (CTIM008) at:

- Any Ohio public college or university with an approved Interactive Media, Graphic Design, or comparable program
- Credit applies toward technical requirements in the specified area; institutions without a direct equivalent are encouraged to apply equivalent credit toward relevant program requirements
- Application window: **within 3 years** of secondary course completion
