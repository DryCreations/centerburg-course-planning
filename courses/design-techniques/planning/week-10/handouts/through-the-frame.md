# Through the Frame

**Design Techniques | Promotional Photography | Wednesday**

This year's prompt is **through the frame:** a photo where something in the scene frames your subject.
Check the exact wording in the WSAP.

## Standards

**7.9.4** Determine composition, formal qualities, scale, and use of space.
**7.9.5** Use International Standards Organization (ISO), shutter speed, aperture, and white balance
settings to shoot manual photographs.

---

## Kinds of frames (7.9.4)

- **Doorways and windows:** shoot through one, with your subject on the other side
- **Arches, tunnels, hallways:** the walls lead the eye to the end
- **Branches and leaves:** soft edges around the subject
- **Fences, railings, gaps:** shoot through an opening
- **Reflections:** a mirror, a puddle, a window
- **Shadows and light:** a bright subject surrounded by dark
- **Hands or objects:** a camera, a ring, a cup held up in front

**The frame should point at the subject, not compete with it.** Keep the frame darker or out of focus
when you can.
