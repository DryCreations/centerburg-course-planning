# 3D Techniques — CTAG Alignment

**Secondary Course Code:** 145120 (current) → 145140 (target)
**CTAG Course:** CTIM007 — 3-D Modeling and Animation
**Credit Value:** 3 Semester Hours
**CTAG Eligibility Status:** Pending — requires 145140 (Hard Surface Modeling)

---

## Eligibility Overview

CTIM007 credit is awarded to students who:

1. Successfully complete DEW secondary course **Hard Surface Modeling (145140)** and earn a qualifying score on the corresponding End-of-Course (WebXam) exam.
2. Matriculate to an Ohio institution of higher education with an approved or comparable program **within 3 years** of completing the approved secondary course.
3. Grant permission for credit posting — either through the end-of-course WebXam or directly with the attending institution.

> **Important:** The qualifying secondary course for CTIM007 is **145140 Hard Surface Modeling**, not 145120. The current course (145120 — Technical Drawing and 3D Printing) is running with HSM-aligned content as the **foundation year** of a planned transition to 145140. Students completing 145120 this year are building the prerequisite skills for 145140, but CTAG credit will only accrue once the program officially runs under 145140 and students pass that course's WebXam.

### Secondary Institutional Approval

- Secondary programs must have **pathway approval from the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW)**
- Certificate of Affirmation assurances are incorporated into the **CTE-26 application process**
- When 145140 is offered, it must be submitted under the Interactive Media (N1) pathway

### Coverage Notes

- **7.6.3 (Keyframes/Tweens):** Covered in animation unit Q2. For CTAG readiness, ensure students produce at least one keyframe-animated sequence (object transform or armature-based) rendered to a video file.
- **7.6.5 (3D Assets):** OnShape→STL export and Blender file import cover this well. Add an explicit exercise where students import an external mesh (e.g., downloaded asset from Sketchfab/Poly Haven) and modify it.
- **7.6.8 (Materials):** UV mapping and PBR shader work are in Q2. Verify students create at least one material with a texture map (albedo + normal) in Blender and render to final image.
- **7.6.9 (Box Modeling):** This is the primary skill of Unit 2.1. Document student model archives as evidence for CTAG alignment audit.

## Articulation — Eligible Credit Path

Students who complete **145140 Hard Surface Modeling** with a qualifying WebXam score may receive **3 semester hours** of postsecondary credit (CTIM007) at:

- Any Ohio public college or university with an approved Interactive Media or comparable program
- Credit applies to courses in the technical area; in absence of direct equivalent, institutions are encouraged to apply equivalent credit toward program technical requirements
- Application window: **within 3 years** of secondary course completion

> **Advising note:** Students currently in 145120 who plan to continue in this program should be told explicitly that (a) their skills are being built toward HSM, and (b) CTAG credit becomes available when they complete 145140. If a student does not return for 145140, they will not earn CTAG credit from 145120 alone.
