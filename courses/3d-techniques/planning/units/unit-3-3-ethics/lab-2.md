## Instructions

### Step 1 — 4 Ethics Case Studies (Days 1–2)

Research and document 4 real-world cases. At least 1 must be from each category: (1) AI-generated content ownership or bias, (2) 3D asset theft or plagiarism, (3) open-source licensing in a commercial product. The 4th is your choice. For each case, write: what happened, which IP/ethics law applied, and 1 professional recommendation.

### Step 2 — Open-Source License Comparison (Day 2)

Choose 2 open-source licenses (GPL, MIT, Creative Commons BY-SA, or CC0). For each: summarize what you can and cannot do commercially, whether derivative works must share the same license, and one real tool or asset that uses this license.

### Step 3 — Sustainability Analysis (Day 3)

Write 1 paragraph on the environmental impact of 3D printing (material waste, failed prints, energy use) and 1 paragraph on the environmental impact of digital rendering (compute energy, cloud servers). Include 1 specific statistic per paragraph from a citable source.

### Step 4 — Personal Ethics Reflection (Day 4)

Write 2–3 sentences on each of the 4 topics below:
1. **Attribution** — When you use Blender assets, open-source models, or AI-generated geometry, what attribution do you owe?
2. **Ownership** — If you create a model at school on school equipment, who owns it?
3. **Fair use** — You want to 3D print a branded prop for a personal project — what are the IP risks?
4. **Decision-making** — Describe one real decision you'll face in your career and how you'd handle it ethically.

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit

Assemble in a Google Doc: case studies, license comparison, sustainability analysis, and reflection. Add sources at the end (minimum 3 citable sources total). Label the document `[YourName]_Unit3.3_EthicsReflection`. Add to portfolio as Artifact #11.

## Class Prompt: IP & Licensing Case Study

### What You're Producing

A short professional report (1–2 pages) that reads like something you would write as a 3D professional advising a client, studio, or employer about an IP risk situation — not a summary of "what the law says" in the abstract, but a concrete analysis of a specific situation.

### Topic Options (Choose One)

| Topic | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **AI-generated 3D content ownership** | A studio uses an AI tool to create 3D assets — who owns the output? Analyze a specific AI art copyright dispute or policy document |
| **3D scan copyright dispute** | Examine a case where a 3D scan of a real-world copyrighted object led to legal action |
| **Asset theft / plagiarism** | A real case where a 3D model was stolen or re-sold in violation of license terms (ArtStation takedowns, Unity Asset Store disputes) |
| **Open-source licensing in commercial products** | How GPL requirements affected a game studio or software company that used Blender or another GPL tool in their pipeline |
| **Freelance contract dispute** | A documented case (blog post, legal article) where the absence of a contract led to IP ownership conflict |

**Your topic must be based on a real, citable case or policy document — not a hypothetical.**

### Report Requirements

**Section 1: Background (1–2 paragraphs)**
- What happened? Who was involved? What industry context is important?

**Section 2: Legal Framework (1 paragraph)**
- Which IP law(s) apply (copyright, trademark, work-for-hire, GPL, Creative Commons)?
- What does the law say about this situation?

**Section 3: Professional Findings (1 paragraph)**
- Given the law and the facts: what was the correct or incorrect action taken?
- What professional documentation was missing or present?

**Section 4: Recommendations (1 paragraph)**
- If you were advising a 3D professional in a similar situation, what 3 specific actions would you recommend to prevent this problem?

**Sources**
- Minimum 2 citable sources (news article, legal document, official license text, court record)
