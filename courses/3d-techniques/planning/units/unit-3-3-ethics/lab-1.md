# Lab: IP & Licensing Scenario Analysis

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 3.3 — Business, Ethics & Intellectual Property
**Duration:** Week 26, Days 1–3

---

## Objective

Apply intellectual property law and professional ethics principles to realistic 3D industry scenarios — determining what is legal, what is ethical, and what documentation would prevent each situation from becoming a problem.

---

## Background

IP disputes in the 3D industry are real and career-ending. A stolen asset, a wrong license on a free texture, an undocumented verbal agreement — these create legal exposure for studios, clients, and freelancers alike. This lab gives you the decision-making framework to navigate them.
