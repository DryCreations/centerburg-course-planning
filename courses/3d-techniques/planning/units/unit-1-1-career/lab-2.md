# Lab 2: 3D Industry Landscape Document

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 1.1 — Course Introduction & Career Landscape
**Duration:** 3 days | **Weeks:** 2–3
**Tools Needed:** Browser for research, design tool (Canva, Adobe Express, Figma, etc.)

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Create a one-page visual infographic mapping the 3D/digital media industry
- Identify career pathways (entry/mid/senior) for at least one sector
- Connect at least one emerging technology to real industry impact

---

## Background

You completed the industry research table in the lab. This lab-2 is about turning that research into a professional-quality visual document — the kind of piece a design professional would actually produce to explain their field.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Plan Your Layout

Sketch a rough wireframe on paper: title placement, sectors section, career pathway, tools per sector, emerging tech section. Aim for visual hierarchy — not a wall of text.

### Step 2 — Build the Infographic

Create a digital infographic (minimum 1920×1080 px or 11×17 in print). Include:
- 4+ sectors of the 3D/digital media industry with examples
- A career pathway for at least 1 sector (entry → mid → senior roles)
- At least 2 specific tools named per highlighted sector
- At least 1 emerging technology with a visual element or caption
- Your name, date, and course in a footer

### Step 3 — Export and Submit

Export as PNG or PDF. Save source file to your portfolio folder (`[YourName]_Unit1.1_IndustryLandscape`). Submit the export and add to portfolio as Artifact #1.

## Class Prompt: Career Landscape Infographic

### What You're Making

A single-page visual document (infographic, poster, or designed information layout) that clearly answers:
- What industries use 3D modeling / digital media skills?
- What are the entry-level, mid-level, and senior roles in at least one sector?
- What tools are used in that sector?
- What emerging technologies are reshaping this industry right now?
- How does this course (and BPA) fit into a career pathway?

### Format Requirements
- One page (digital: minimum 1920×1080 px; printed: minimum 11×17 in)
- Must include at least 1 visual element (icon, simple diagram, or chart) — not just text
- Designed using any tool you have access to (Canva, Adobe Express, Illustrator, Figma, etc.)
- Professionally readable — appropriate font sizes, clear hierarchy, organized layout
- Include your name, date, and course name in a small footer

### Milestones

| Milestone | Due | What to Show |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| Industry survey table + research brief (from lab) | End of Day 1–2 | Completed in the lab activity |
| Rough draft layout | Day 3 | Thumbnail sketch or low-fidelity digital draft showing structure |
| Final infographic | End of Week 3 | Completed, polished, saved to portfolio folder |
