**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Weeks:** 3–4 | **Duration:** 2 weeks

---

## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Who uses 3D modeling skills professionally? This unit maps the entire landscape: manufacturing, product design, architecture, gaming, film/VFX, medical, and military. You'll research specific job titles, understand how 3D workflows differ by industry, and create a comparison document showing where you see yourself.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Name 10+ job titles that use 3D modeling skills across different industries
- Explain how the 3D workflow differs in manufacturing vs. entertainment vs. medical applications
- Describe the tools (CAD vs. DCC) associated with each industry sector
- Create a one-page industry landscape document comparing at least 3 sectors

### Common Issues & Responses

- **All sectors are entertainment:** Require 1 manufacturing, engineering, or medical sector in the comparison.
- **Tool column is blank:** Every sector comparison must include the primary software tool used.
