# Lab: 3D Industry Research & Emerging Technology Exploration

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 1.1 — Career Landscape
**Duration:** 1–2 days (Week 2)

---

## Objective

Survey the 3D and interactive media industry to understand where 3D modeling and animation skills are in demand. Research one emerging technology in depth to connect course skills to real-world innovation.

---

## Background

3D modeling is used across film, games, architecture, engineering, medicine, education, and manufacturing. Understanding the full landscape — not just game art — is essential for making smart career decisions. The emerging technology you pick today may define where your 3D skills take you in 5–10 years.

## Part B — Emerging Technology Deep Dive

### Step 3: Choose Your Technology
Pick **one** of the following for your deep dive:
- Generative AI for 3D content creation (Meshy, CSM, Luma AI, etc.)
- Photogrammetry (RealityCapture, Polycam, Metashape)
- Extended Reality (VR/AR/MR) production workflow
- Real-time rendering in game engines (Unreal Engine 5 Lumen/Nanite)
- 3D printing advances (multi-material, bioprinting, large-format)

### Step 4: Research Brief
Write a 1-page (or equivalent) brief answering:
1. **What is it?** Define the technology in your own words.
2. **How does it work?** Describe the fundamental process (one paragraph).
3. **Who uses it?** Name at least 2 real companies or industries.
4. **What skills transfer?** How do the skills you're building in this course apply to this technology?
5. **What's the opportunity?** What jobs or roles exist in this technology space?

## Part C — Class Share

### Step 5: Prepare a 60-Second Verbal Summary
Be ready to give a 60-second summary of your chosen technology to the class. Hit: what it is, who uses it, and one reason it matters.
