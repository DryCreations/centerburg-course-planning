## Answer Key

| # | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | B | A 3D generalist creates assets (models, environments, rigs) used in-engine |
| 2 | B | AR = Augmented Reality — digital content overlaid on the real world |
| 3 | C | Photogrammetry reconstructs 3D form from photo analysis |
| 4 | B | A WebXam credential (CTAG pathway) plus portfolio is the recommended high school priority for creative/tech careers |
| 5 | B | ACM SIGGRAPH is the professional organization for computer graphics; BPA is a CTSO for all CTE students |
| 6 | B | Architecture viz = pre-construction visualization for client communication |
| 7 | C | Portfolios are the art/design industry's primary hiring filter; skills without evidence are invisible |
| 8 | C | VR replaces your environment — ideal for walkthrough simulation of spaces before construction |
| 9 | C | AI as a tool accelerates artists who understand 3D; foundational skills are needed to evaluate, guide, and fix AI output |
| 10 | B | Connecting modeling to an industry problem (career plan) = Standard 1.1.3 |
