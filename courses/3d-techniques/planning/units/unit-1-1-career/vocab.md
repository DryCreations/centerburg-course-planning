## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Career pathway** | A sequence of related courses, credentials, and jobs leading toward a career field | 1.1.1 | 1 |
| **Portfolio** | A curated set of work samples that demonstrates skills to employers, colleges, or clients | 1.1.5 | 1 |
| **Entry-level skills** | The knowledge, abilities, and behaviors expected of a new hire in a career field | 1.1.1 | 1 |
| **Networking** | Building professional relationships that can lead to referrals, mentorship, and job opportunities | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **Professional organization** | An industry group (like BPA, SIGGRAPH, ACM SIGGRAPH) that supports career development and community | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **Emerging technology** | A technology that is currently developing and is expected to significantly impact an industry within 5–10 years | 2.4.1 | 1 |
| **VR (Virtual Reality)** | A fully computer-generated environment experienced through a headset that replaces the user's real surroundings | 2.4.4 | 1 |
| **AR (Augmented Reality)** | Technology that overlays digital content on the real world, viewed through a phone screen or smart glasses | 2.4.4 | 1 |
| **XR (Extended Reality)** | An umbrella term covering VR, AR, and MR — all technologies that blur the line between physical and digital | 2.4.4 | 2 |
| **Photogrammetry** | A technique for creating 3D models by analyzing dozens or hundreds of photos taken from different angles | 2.4.2 | 2 |
| **Generative AI (3D)** | AI systems that can generate 3D models, textures, or scenes from text prompts or reference images | 2.4.3 | 2 |
| **Architecture visualization** | Using 3D software to create realistic renderings of buildings and spaces before they are built | 2.4.4 | 2 |
| **VFX (Visual Effects)** | Digital effects added in post-production to film and video — explosions, creatures, environment extensions | 7.6.4 | 1 |
| **Game engine** | Software (Unreal Engine, Unity) that runs real-time 3D environments and is increasingly used for animation and visualization | 2.4.2 | 2 |
| **BPA (Business Professionals of America)** | A national CTSO that hosts competitive events in business, technology, and digital media for CTE students | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **Self-promotion** | The ability to effectively communicate your skills, work history, and value to potential employers or clients | 1.1.5 | 2 |
| **Professional development** | Ongoing learning, certification, and skill-building activities after entering the workforce | 1.1.2 | 1 |

## Key Distinctions

| Term 1 | Term 2 | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| VR | AR | VR replaces your surroundings; AR overlays digital content on your real environment |
| Photogrammetry | Box modeling | Photogrammetry captures existing real-world objects from photos; box modeling builds objects from scratch using polygons |
| Portfolio | Resume | A portfolio shows *what the work looks like*; a resume lists *what you did* |
| BPA event | CTAG credential | BPA events are competitive performances; CTAG is an academic college-credit pathway based on WebXam score |
