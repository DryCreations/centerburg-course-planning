# Vocabulary: Pretest & Course Orientation

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 1.0 — WebXam Pretest & Course Orientation

> Use these terms in all written responses throughout the year. They establish the shared language of the course on Day 1.

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **3D modeling** | The process of creating a three-dimensional representation of an object using software | 7.6 | 1 |
| **CAD** (Computer-Aided Design) | Software used to create precise 2D drawings or 3D models, especially for engineering and manufacturing | 7.6.9 | 1 |
| **OnShape** | A cloud-based CAD platform used in this course for technical drawing and constraint-based modeling | 7.6.9 | 1 |
| **Blender** | A free, open-source 3D creation suite used in this course for polygon modeling, materials, animation, and rendering | 7.6.5 | 1 |
| **Additive manufacturing** | A process that builds objects layer by layer from digital files — 3D printing is the most common form | 2.4.4 | 1 |
| **Slicer software** | Software (e.g., Cura, PrusaSlicer) that converts a 3D model file into machine instructions (G-code) for a 3D printer | 2.4.4 | 1 |
| **STL file** | Standard Tessellation Language — a common file format for 3D printing that describes surface geometry using triangles | 7.6.5 | 1 |
| **WebXam** | The Ohio Department of Education and Workforce End-of-Course (EOC) assessment for CTE courses | — | 1 |
| **EOC** (End-of-Course exam) | A standardized test aligned to Ohio CTE standards, administered at the end of the course | — | 1 |
| **CTAG** | Career-Technical Assurance Guide — a framework for earning college credit based on WebXam performance | — | 1 |
| **Strand** | A major category in the Ohio CTE standards (e.g., Strand 7 = Digital Media, Strand 5 = Programming) | — | 1 |
| **Hard surface modeling** | 3D modeling focused on mechanical, man-made, or rigid objects — pipes, vehicles, electronics | 7.6.9 | 1 |
| **Polygon mesh** | A 3D model made of connected polygons (usually triangles or quads) — the fundamental structure in most real-time and film 3D work | 7.6.9 | 2 |
| **Portfolio** | A curated collection of work samples used to demonstrate skills to employers, colleges, or clients | 1.1.5 | 1 |
| **BPA** (Business Professionals of America) | A national career and technical student organization; BPA events are integrated into this course and are optional competition entries | 1.1.4 | 1 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. What is the difference between CAD and Blender? When would you use each?
2. Why would a designer export a model as an STL file?
3. What is additive manufacturing, and give one real-world industry that uses it.
4. What is the WebXam, and what do you need to do to pass it?
5. What does "hard surface modeling" mean? Give an example of a hard surface object.
