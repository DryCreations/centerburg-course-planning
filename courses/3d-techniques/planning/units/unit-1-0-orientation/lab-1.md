# Lab: WebXam Pretest & Score Analysis

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 1.0 — Pretest & Course Orientation
**Duration:** 2 days (Day 3 = pretest; Days 4–5 = score analysis + goal setting)

---

## Objective

Establish a personal baseline score on the WebXam End-of-Course practice exam and use the strand-by-strand results to set focused learning goals for the year.

---

## Background

The WebXam is the Ohio CTE End-of-Course exam for this course. Your score determines your CTE credential level and may qualify you for college credit (CTAG). The pretest gives you — and your teacher — a picture of where you are *right now*, before instruction. There is no grade pressure on the pretest; honest effort under real conditions is all that is asked.

## Day 3 — Pretest Administration

### Setup
1. Clear your desk of all materials except a pencil and scratch paper.
2. Access the pretest link provided by your teacher (logged into your student account).
3. Review the rules: no phones, no asking neighbors, no looking up answers. Treat it exactly like the real WebXam in April.

### Taking the Pretest
4. Complete all questions within the time limit.
5. If you finish early, review flagged questions before submitting.
6. Submit when done. Do not share answers afterward.

## Days 4–5 — Score Analysis & Learning Goals

### Step 1: Identify Your Two Weakest Strands
8. Look at your pretest strand breakdown. Circle your two lowest-scoring strands on the table above.

### Step 2: Review the EOC Blueprint
9. Your teacher will project the EOC blueprint. Identify which strand has the **highest point value** (Strand 7, ~51%). Note whether your weak strands are high-weight or low-weight.

### Step 3: Complete the Learning Goals Form

Answer the following in writing (paragraph or list format):

- **Where I am:** What strand do I feel strongest in? Where did I score lowest?
- **What matters most:** Which strand has the highest EOC weight? How does my score compare there?
- **My goals:** Write 2–3 specific, measurable goals for the year. Example: *"I want to score 80%+ on Strand 7 items. I will do this by completing all Blender labs and seeking help on rendering during Unit 1.3."*
- **What I'm excited about:** What part of 3D/animation work interests you most?
