## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Before anything gets modeled or printed, you need to know where you're going. In this unit you'll set up your course portfolio, complete a diagnostic pretest, explore careers in 3D modeling and design, and learn the studio safety rules for 3D printing equipment.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Set up a digital portfolio (Google Sites or equivalent) to house all course work
- Complete a pretest that identifies your strongest and weakest areas in 3D design
- Write 3 SMART goals based on pretest results
- State the equipment safety rules for all 3D printers and workstations
