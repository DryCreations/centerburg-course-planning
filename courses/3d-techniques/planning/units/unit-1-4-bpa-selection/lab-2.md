# Lab 2: BPA Research-to-Production Plan

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 1.4 — Multimedia Tools, BPA Showcase & Event Selection
**Duration:** 5 days | **Weeks:** 8–9
**Tools Needed:** BPA WSAP documents, design brief outline from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Finalize their BPA event selection with a completed design brief (5 sections)
- Produce a storyboard (8+ panels) or concept sketches (3 views) depending on event type
- Create a production schedule from Week 10 through the local showcase

---

## Background

This package is the foundation of every production decision you make in Q2. Judges evaluate not just the finished entry but the process documentation — start that documentation now.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — BPA Event Selection (Day 1)

Confirm your BPA event with the teacher. Download the WSAP. Note the required deliverables, length/size requirements, and the top 3 scoring criteria. Record your team (if applicable) and role assignments.

### Step 2 — Design Brief (Days 1–2)

Write the final 5-section design brief: (1) project description, (2) audience profile and judge profile, (3) WSAP alignment note, (4) constraints (tools, deadlines, team), (5) deliverables list.

### Step 3 — Planning Document (Days 2–3)

**For animation events (#440, V11):** Produce a storyboard of at least 8 panels. Each panel must have: action description, timing note, and any audio/sound effect note.

**For modeling events (#425):** Produce 3-view concept sketches with labeled features and dimension notes.

### Step 4 — Production Schedule (Days 3–4)

Create a milestone timeline from Week 10 to the local showcase. Include all hard deadlines (Dec 1 virtual registration, Jan 15 V11 pre-submission), major production milestones (base mesh, materials, render, final submission).

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit (Day 5)

Save all files to your portfolio folder: `[YourName]_Unit1.4_[DocType]`. Submit links. Add to portfolio as Artifact #4.
