## Objective

Review real BPA competition entry examples, download and read the WSAP for your selected event, and produce a pre-production planning package — the first formal documentation of your BPA project.

---

## Background

Professional productions begin with thorough planning documents before a single byte is produced. Great BPA competition entries look effortless precisely because the planning was rigorous. This lab produces the planning documents that will guide your production work in Q2.

## Part C — Pre-Production Planning Package (Week 9, Days 1–4)

By the end of Week 9, every student must produce a pre-production package for their BPA project. Students NOT doing BPA should still complete this for the Class Project version of their Q2 Hard Surface Scene.

### Step 4: Design Brief
Write a short design brief (1 page or equivalent) that answers:
1. **Project:** What are you creating?
2. **Audience:** Who is the judge/viewer? What do they care about?
3. **WSAP alignment:** How does your concept respond to this year's WSAP topic?
4. **Constraints:** Software tools, deadline, team size (if applicable)
5. **Deliverables:** What files/materials must be submitted to BPA?

### Step 5: Storyboard (animation events) or Concept Sketches (modeling)
- **#440 / V11 students:** Create a storyboard with at least 8 panels showing key scenes, camera angles, and brief descriptions of action and timing.
- **#425 students:** Create 3 concept sketches of your planned object from different angles, noting dimensions and key modeling features to include.

### Step 6: Production Schedule
Create a week-by-week production schedule from Week 10 to the local showcase (January). Include:
- Major milestones (base mesh complete, materials applied, animation rough cut, final render, etc.)
- Hard deadlines (December 1 for V11 registration, January 15 for V11 pre-submission, local showcase)
