## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

BPA competitions don't appear in February — they require planning that starts in October. This unit introduces the BPA events relevant to 3D modeling, reviews competition requirements and scoring rubrics, and has you commit to an event and develop a production timeline. You'll also research copyright and IP ownership for 3D work — essential knowledge for competition and professional practice.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Describe the requirements and scoring criteria for at least 2 BPA 3D events
- Write a project timeline for one BPA event with milestones and deadline check-ins
- Explain the copyright status of 3D-printed objects under U.S. law
- Create a research-to-production plan document with event selection justification

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Event selection is not realistic:** Students who have never used Blender before should not target the most advanced event. Coach on feasibility.
- **Timeline has no buffer:** Require a "contingency week" in every timeline. Projects always take longer than expected.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a BPA event quick-reference card with deliverables and deadlines for each 3D event.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — research 2 real Thingiverse entries and analyze their license and attribution notes.

### BPA Connections

**All 3D BPA events:** This unit is the planning foundation for any BPA competition entry this year. Students who skip this planning typically fall behind in Q3.
