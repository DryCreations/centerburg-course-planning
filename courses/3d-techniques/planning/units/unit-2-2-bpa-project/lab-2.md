**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 2.2 — BPA Project Production
**Duration:** 5-week production cycle | **Weeks:** 14–18
**Tools Needed:** Blender and/or OnShape, slicer software, production log from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Submit 3 iteration screenshots (with a change log) showing how the entry evolved over the production cycle
- Document a slicer screenshot or render settings screenshot showing production-ready output
- Write a design rationale explaining the 3 most significant creative decisions

---

## Background

The finished entry is in project.md. This lab-2 captures the PROCESS — the iterations, the decisions, the problems solved. Judges value process documentation; this is your evidence.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Collect 3 Iteration Screenshots

From your production log and saved checkpoints, select 3 screenshots that best show how the model, animation, or design evolved. Each screenshot must be from a different milestone week (not 3 shots from the same day). Save with descriptive file names.

### Step 2 — Write the Change Log

For each of the 3 screenshots, write 2–3 sentences: What changed from the previous version? Why did you make that change (feedback, problem encountered, aesthetic decision)?

### Step 3 — Production-Ready Documentation Screenshot

**For modeling/print (#425):** Take a screenshot of the slicer (Cura/PrusaSlicer) showing your model sliced with settings (layer height, supports, infill). **For animation (#440/V11):** Screenshot render settings panel with final output specs.

### Step 4 — Design Rationale (3 Decisions)

Choose the 3 most significant creative or technical decisions you made. For each: what decision did you make, what alternatives did you consider, and why was this the right choice for the WSAP or the design goal?

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit

Assemble in a Google Doc or PDF: 3 iteration screenshots + change log, production-ready screenshot, and design rationale. Submit link. Add to portfolio as Artifact #7.
