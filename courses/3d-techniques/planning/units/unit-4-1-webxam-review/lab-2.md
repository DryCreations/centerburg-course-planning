# Lab 2: WebXam Attempt + Post-Exam Reflection

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 4.1 — WebXam Preparation & Initial Test
**Duration:** 2 weeks | **Weeks:** 28–29
**⚑ WebXam Initial Test — administer by April 23**
**Tools Needed:** Testing computer, WebXam access (3D Techniques 145120), review notes from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Sit for the WebXam Initial Test after completing the strand review and gap analysis in the lab
- Complete a post-exam reflection documenting confidence areas, surprises, and a retake plan if needed

---

## Background

You completed the strand gap analysis, vocabulary review, and practice quiz in the lab. This lab-2 is the exam itself plus the reflection. The review work is preparation — this is the payoff.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Readiness Check (before sitting)

Confirm with the teacher that your readiness checklist is complete: lab strand review, practice quiz scored, personal review list, and pretest comparison table. Do not sit for the exam until these are complete.

### Step 2 — WebXam Initial Test (⚑ by April 23)

Sit for the WebXam in class. Use your full time. Note strands that felt most and least confident — you won't see the breakdown until scores return.

### Step 3 — Post-Exam Reflection (same day or next class)

Write a 1-paragraph reflection answering: (1) What strands felt most confident? (2) What surprised you? (3) What would you study differently? (4) What was your target score?

### Step 4 — Retake Plan (if below target)

Identify the 2–3 strands most likely to need improvement. List 5 vocabulary terms per strand for review. Set a retake date with the teacher (window opens 30 days after the initial test). Use Unit 4.2 capstone work time for embedded retake prep.

### Step 5 — Portfolio Entry

Add a "Credentials" page to your portfolio: note the exam attempt, approximate date, and whether you are pursuing a retake. Update this page when official results come back.
