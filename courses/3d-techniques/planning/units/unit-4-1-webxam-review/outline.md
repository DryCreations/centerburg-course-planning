## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This unit is dedicated to preparing for and passing the Ohio ITA WebXam. You'll compare your current knowledge to your Week 1 pretest, identify your weakest strands, review systematically, and take a full practice test before the real exam.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Identify which WebXam strands are highest-weight and create a targeted study plan
- Demonstrate readiness by scoring 70%+ on a practice test
- Attempt (and pass) the WebXam credential exam

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students think portfolio work = test readiness:** Emphasize that the exam tests vocabulary and recall under time pressure.
- **Practice test anxiety:** Frame it as diagnostic — the score tells you where to study, not whether you'll pass.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a strand vocabulary card set and a 5-day structured study schedule.
- **Retake students:** Focus retake review on the 2–3 strands with the largest remaining gaps after the Initial Test.

### Assessment Notes

- Exam score does not factor into class grade — demonstrated preparation effort and exam attempt does.
- Retake window opens 30 days after Initial Test. Schedule retakes during Unit 4.2 capstone production time.

### BPA Connections

Credential preparation is separate from BPA. Students continuing BPA work toward state or NLC work on competition materials alongside exam prep.
