| 2 | What is the difference between revising and fixing? | They are the same thing | Fixing corrects an error; revising improves the work based on feedback or new understanding | Revising means starting over; fixing means adjusting one element | Fixing is required; revising is optional | B | 2 | 1.1.7 |
| 3 | A WSAP rubric is most useful during the refinement phase because: | It tells you which software version to use | It defines exactly what judges value — you can score yourself and target specific gaps | It contains a list of all registered competitors | It tells you the event registration deadline | B | 2 | 1.1.4 |
| 4 | Who is considered a stakeholder in a BPA competition entry? | Only the student who created the work | Anyone with a legitimate interest in the outcome — including the student, teammates, judges, and teacher | Only the BPA state office | The software company whose tool was used | B | 2 | 7.2.9 |
| 5 | Process documentation (design brief, storyboard, production log) matters to BPA because: | It increases render quality | It is worth points in many events and proves that planning and production discipline were applied | It is required for all BPA events to submit to the state database | It is a school grade requirement only | B | 2 | 7.3.10 |
| 6 | When receiving a peer critique that you disagree with, the most professional response is: | Ignore the feedback | Argue that the feedback is wrong | Thank the reviewer, note the feedback, and evaluate it against the rubric before deciding whether to act | Post negatively about the reviewer | C | 2 | 1.1.9 |
| 7 | **[HOT]** A student's BPA entry has been critiqued 3 times. Each critique has identified the same issue: the lighting on the hero render makes the model hard to read. The student has not changed the lighting. What is the most likely cause? | The critique feedback was wrong | The student doesn't know how to adjust lighting — they need targeted instruction on Blender lighting | The render engine automatically controls lighting | The hero render cannot be changed after submission | B | 3 | 1.1.7 |
| 8 | **[HOT]** A team of 3 students disagrees about which music track to use in their BPA animation. Two want a dramatic orchestral track; one wants a minimalist electronic track. They have 15 minutes to decide. What is the most constructive process? | The team leader decides unilaterally | A coin flip — random is faster | Review the WSAP rubric together — identify if sound design is scored and what criteria it's evaluated against; choose the track that best satisfies the rubric | Submit both versions and let the judge pick | C | 3 | 1.2.4 |

| 10 | **[HOT]** A BAC (Business Advisory Council) judge at the local showcase tells a student: "I see a lot of potential, but I would want to understand more about the *application* of this design — who uses objects like this and in what context?"
