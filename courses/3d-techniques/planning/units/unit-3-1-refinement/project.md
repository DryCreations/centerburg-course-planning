**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)

**Duration:** Weeks 19–22

---

## Overview

Apply the revisions identified through rubric self-assessment and peer critique to finalize your BPA entry. Present the polished result at the local showcase in January to BAC industry judges. Students whose work advances to the Ohio State Leadership Conference will have the entry ready to submit.

---

## Class Prompt: BPA Entry — Final Polish

### What You're Doing

There is no new production in this unit — you are taking what you built in Unit 2.2 and making it the best possible version before external judging. The work is revision, not creation.

### Process

1. **Week 19:** WSAP rubric self-assessment + peer critique lab (see lab.md for guided process)
2. **Week 20:** Execute priority revisions from lab analysis
3. **Week 21:** Compile final submission package per event requirements; confirm all materials are present
4. **Week 22:** Local showcase — present to BAC judges; debrief afterward

---

## Local Showcase Format

*Teacher sets the specific date and logistics. These are the general expectations:*

- **#425 students:** Present 3D model and renders. Be ready to explain: design intent, WSAP alignment, production process, and key technical decisions. BAC judges have backgrounds in product design, engineering, architecture, or game development.
- **#440/#V11 teams:** Screen the animation. Be ready to answer: what was the WSAP topic, how did you interpret it, what were each team member's roles, and what would you change?
- **All students:** Know your WSAP rubric. If a judge asks "how did you approach [category X]," be able to answer.

## Standards & Rubric

Walk the room as students work. Ask students to point to evidence for each standard before scoring.

| Standard | Description | Look-For (Proficient = 3) | Beginning (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **1.1.7** | Problem-solving + critical thinking | Revision prioritized using WSAP rubric analysis; decisions documented | No revision plan | Some changes made | Revisions prioritized using rubric; decisions documented | Rubric-based priority list + rationale per revision decision |
| **1.1.9** | Give + receive feedback | Peer critique completed + self-assessment documented | No feedback | Critique received; not documented | Peer critique + self-assessment both submitted | Critique + self-assessment + specific design changes linked to feedback |
| **1.2.10** | Teamwork + interpersonal skills | Team communication documented (meeting notes or log) | No documentation | 1 team note | Meeting notes or production log across the revision cycle | Log + documentation of how team resolved at least 1 conflict or disagreement |
| **1.2.4** | Negotiation + conflict resolution | Team decision-making process documented in log | Not documented | 1 decision noted | Final revision decisions documented with team input | Decision log + note on any compromises made for the submission |
| **7.3.10** | Produce, test, debug, archive | Complete submission package assembled, tested, and archived | Incomplete package | Most deliverables present | Full package: all deliverables named and organized | Full package + final test (play-through or render check) documented |
