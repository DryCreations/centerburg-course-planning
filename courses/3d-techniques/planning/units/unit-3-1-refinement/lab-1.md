# Lab: WSAP Rubric Self-Assessment & Targeted Revision

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 3.1 — Testing, Refinement & Local Competition
**Duration:** Weeks 19–20 (self-assessment → targeted revision)

---

## Objective

Score your BPA entry against the official WSAP rubric, identify your highest-ROI revision targets, and execute specific improvements before the local showcase in January.

---

## Background

Professional artists and designers don't revise randomly — they triage. You have limited time; every hour should go toward the improvement that most increases your score or impact. This lab teaches the discipline of evidence-based revision.

---

## Part A — WSAP Rubric Self-Assessment

### Step 1: Gather Materials
1. Open your BPA entry file (model or animation project).
2. Open the WSAP rubric for your event (download from BPA website if you don't have it).
3. Get a printed or digital copy of this lab.

### Step 2: Score Each Rubric Category
4. For each rubric category, review your entry critically — as if you were a judge who has never seen it.
