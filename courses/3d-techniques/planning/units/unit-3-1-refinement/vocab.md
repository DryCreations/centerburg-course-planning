# Vocabulary: Testing, Refinement & Competition Prep

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 3.1 — Testing, Refinement & Local Competition
**Weeks:** 19–22

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Critique** | A structured, specific evaluation of creative work that identifies both strengths and actionable areas for improvement | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Constructive feedback** | Feedback that is specific, evidence-based, and directed at improvement — not vague ("looks good") or personal | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Revision** | A purposeful change to a project based on feedback, self-assessment, or new understanding — not a fix to a mistake | 1.1.7 | 2 |
| **Iterative process** | A workflow where a project is repeatedly improved through cycles of create → review → revise | 1.1.7 | 3 |
| **Rubric** | A scoring guide listing specific criteria and performance levels used to evaluate a project consistently | 1.1.9 | 1 |
| **Stakeholder** | Anyone with a legitimate interest in a project's outcome — in BPA, judges are the primary stakeholders | 7.2.9 | 2 |
| **Peer critique** | A structured review where classmates provide feedback on each other's work using defined criteria | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Portfolio selection** | The process of choosing which artifacts best represent your skills for a specific audience | 1.1.5 | 3 |
| **Interpersonal skills** | Non-technical skills: communication, active listening, empathy, respect — crucial during critique and competition | 1.2.10 | 1 |
| **Collaboration** | Working jointly with others toward a shared goal, including dividing tasks, resolving disagreements, and integrating contributions | 1.2.10 | 2 |
| **Negotiation** | A process for resolving disagreement or competing priorities to reach a mutually acceptable decision | 1.2.4 | 2 |
| **Digital communication** | Using electronic tools (email, shared docs, chat) professionally — with clear intent, appropriate tone, and accountability | 1.2.9 | 2 |
| **Process documentation** | The record of how a project was made — sketches, screenshots, log entries, version history | 7.3.10 | 2 |
| **Submission checklist** | A list of all required materials verified present before an entry is submitted — prevents missed items | 7.3.10 | 1 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. What makes feedback "constructive"? Give an example of vague feedback and rewrite it as constructive.
2. Why is process documentation important to a judge — not just the final deliverable?
3. What does "iterative process" mean? How many revision cycles should a competition entry undergo?
4. When a teammate disagrees with your creative decision, what is the most professionally appropriate response?
5. What is a "stakeholder" in the context of your BPA entry? Name at least 2 for your event.
