## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Your BPA entry is due January 15. This unit is about finishing it: a structured quality audit, critiques, final mesh cleanup, lighting refinement, and a polished render ready for submission. You'll also print a physical version and present your complete project to a panel.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Conduct a quality audit of your BPA model against published scoring criteria
- Implement specific, documented revisions based on audit findings
- Prepare a final render at competition specifications
- Print a physical version of the model and document the physical output
- Present your complete project to a panel, explaining design and technical decisions

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Render still has fireflies/noise:** Require 256+ samples for the final render; show denoising node in compositor.
- **Print failed overnight:** Have a recovery plan — start prints early in the week; a Tuesday print leaves time for a reprint by Thursday if needed.
- **Presentation is too short:** Require students to verbally cite a specific design decision for every BPA scoring category.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a 1-page "what judges look for" guide summarizing the BPA V25 scoring rubric in student-friendly language.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — paint or post-process the physical print and document the outcome.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Verbal presentation is required. Student must point to evidence in the model and render for each standard assessed.

### BPA Connections

**BPA V25 3D Modeling:** ⚑ January 15 submission deadline.
**Local BPA competition (January):** Present physical print + render to panel. Top performers advance to state conference.
