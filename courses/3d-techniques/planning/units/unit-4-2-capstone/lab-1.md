# Lab: Capstone Development Checkpoints

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 4.2 — Capstone Project
**Duration:** Weeks 30–37

> This is a process lab, not a single-session activity. Checkpoint steps are distributed across 8 weeks. Complete each checkpoint on schedule — missed checkpoints cannot be made up as "last-minute submission."

---

## Objective

Execute a full-production 3D project from design brief through final delivery. Demonstrate professional workflow: concept → documentation → production → critique → revision → submission.

---

## Background

The capstone is the culminating artifact of the course. It is designed to mirror a real professional project: it requires planning documents, iterative production, peer critique, and a polished final delivery package. It is also the primary vehicle for WebXam retake remediation — students in the retake track complete focused review during production time.

---

## Week 30 — Project Launch

### Step 1: Finalize Capstone Design Brief
Retrieve the draft design brief from Unit 3.3 project. Review and confirm:
- Track selection (Hard Surface / Animation / 3D-Printed Prototype)
- Scope is achievable in 8 weeks
- All 6 design brief fields are complete

Submit: finalized design brief (signed off by teacher before Week 31 production begins).

### Step 2: Reference Collection
For your chosen track, gather reference materials:
- **Hard Surface / Product Viz:** 10+ reference photos of your target object from multiple angles
- **Animation:** Storyboard (minimum 8 panels) + shot list
- **3D-Printed Prototype:** Concept sketch with labeled dimensions + at least 1 comparable product reference

## Week 32–33 — Production Sprint

### Step 4: Mid-Project Process Documentation Entry
At the end of Week 32, write a 1-paragraph process note:
- What have you completed so far?
- What decisions have you made (materials, camera angle, topology approach) and why?
- What problems have you encountered? How did you solve them?

## Week 35 — Retake Window + Revision

### Step 6: Targeted Revisions
Using peer review notes and rubric self-assessment, execute at least 2 targeted revisions. Document what changed and why.

### Retake Track (Students with WebXam below target)

During production time in Week 35 teacher pulls a small group for targeted retake review:
- 30 minutes: work through the 5 Retake Remediation questions from quiz.md with teacher
- 15 minutes: targeted practice problems by strand (teacher-led)
- Retake administered before end of Week 35
