# Quiz: Capstone Standards & Process

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 4.2 — Capstone Project
**Weeks:** 30–37

> **Note:** This quiz serves two functions.
> 1. **Bell ringers** during Weeks 30–37 to maintain engagement with standards during long project work.
> 2. **Retake remediation** warm-up — Q1–Q10 mirror the highest-missed WebXam question types from strands where Retake Track students need reinforcement.

---

## Questions

| # | Question | A | B | C | D | Answer | DOK | Standard |
|---|----------|---|---|---|---|--------|-----|---------|
| 1 | What is the minimum information a professional design brief must include? | Project title only | Title, concept, constraints, deliverables, and tools | Client name and budget | A storyboard or sketch | B | 2 | 1.2.12 |
| 2 | **[HOT]** A student's capstone render looks technically correct — clean topology, good materials, accurate lighting — but a portfolio reviewer says it "lacks artistic intent." What does this mean? | The render is too dark | The technical execution didn't communicate a deliberate creative idea — the work looks made mechanically, not thoughtfully | The render is missing a background | The file was saved in the wrong format | B | 4 | 7.3.10 |
| 3 | An animator compiles her render to MP4 directly (not via image sequence). Blender crashes mid-render at frame 340 of 400. What is the result? | The video is saved up to frame 339 automatically | The entire render is likely corrupted or lost — she must restart from frame 1 | Only frames 1–100 are lost | The render continues automatically after restart | B | 3 | 7.8.1 |
| 4 | A student wants his product visualization to look like a professional studio photo. What lighting setup should he use? | One bright point light above the object | Three-point lighting: key + fill + rim | Eevee ambient occlusion only | HDRI without any manual lights | B | 2 | 7.8.1 |
| 5 | Which Blender modifier rounds sharp edges on a high-poly mesh by adding a subtle bevel, most often used in product visualization? | SubDiv Surface | Mirror | Bevel Modifier | Displace | C | 2 | 7.6.9 |
| 6 | What is the purpose of process documentation in a portfolio? | It adds page count | It demonstrates how decisions were made and how work evolved — showing professional judgment, not just final output | It proves the work was done quickly | It is required for all file submissions | B | 3 | 1.2.12 |
| 7 | A student finishes the capstone and reviews the rubric. She gave herself full marks on "modeling complexity" but not on "documentation." What should she prioritize to improve her grade? | Re-render the hero image | Add more polygons to the model | Complete the design brief, process notes, and reflection | Export to a different file format | C | 2 | 1.1.7 |
| 8 | **[HOT]** During a peer critique, a classmate tells a student: "This animation is really cool." This feedback is: | Helpful — positive reinforcement is important | Not useful as revision guidance — it doesn't identify what specifically is working or how to improve | A complete rubric evaluation | Sufficient for self-assessment | B | 3 | 1.2.4 |
| 9 | Which FDM 3D printing file format does OnShape export (and slicers require)? | OBJ | SVG | STL | MP4 | C | 1 | 7.4.2 |
| 10 | **[HOT]** A student says their project "demonstrates 7.6.9" but cannot point to any specific element of their work that shows it. Their teacher says: "Unlabeled evidence may not receive credit even if the work is good." What should the student do? | Submit the work without labels and hope the teacher notices | Add callouts to the documentation package labeling which specific model features demonstrate 7.6.9 (e.g., "control loops used here to hold bevel under SubDiv — demonstrates 7.6.9") | Redo the model | Ask for extra credit | B | 4 | 7.6.9 |
