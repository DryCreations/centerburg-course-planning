# Vocabulary: Capstone Preparation

**Course:** 3D Techniques (145120)
**Unit:** 4.2 — Capstone Project
**Weeks:** 30–37

> **Note:** This vocabulary list is not new content — it is a curated synthesis of the highest-value professional and technical terms from across the course. Use it as a comprehensive review reference and as the framework for your capstone documentation.

---

## Professional Practice Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | Priority |
|------|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| **Design brief** | Written project specification: scope, concept, constraints, deliverables, tools | 1.2.12 | HIGH |
| **Process documentation** | A record of decisions made during a project: sketches, reference images, version history, reflective notes | 1.2.12 | HIGH |
| **Technical report** | A structured professional document that presents findings, methods, or specifications with clarity and precision | 1.2.12 | HIGH |
| **Portfolio artifact** | A completed work sample that demonstrates specific skills or standards in a professional collection | 1.1.5 | HIGH |
| **WSAP** | Work Sample and Assessment Plan — BPA's rubric and project specification document | 7.6.1 | HIGH |
| **Rubric** | A scoring guide that defines expectations across multiple levels of performance | 1.1.7 | MED |
| **Iterative process** | A workflow of repeated cycles: make → evaluate → revise; the professional alternative to "do it once and submit" | 1.1.7 | MED |
| **Deliverable** | A specific, tangible output committed to for a project | 1.2.12 | MED |
| **Milestone** | An intermediate checkpoint in a project schedule | 1.2.12 | MED |

## Technical Terms by Capstone Track

### All Tracks

| Term | Definition | Standard |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Hero render** | The primary, highest-quality render in a project — the image that represents the work at its best | 7.8.1 |
| **Artistic intent** | The deliberate creative decisions made in a work — what the piece is about and how technique serves that purpose | 7.3.10 |
| **Peer critique** | Structured feedback from a classmate using specific criteria (not general opinions) | 1.2.4 |
| **Self-assessment** | Evaluating your own work against a rubric or standard before submission | 1.1.7 |

### Track 1 — Hard Surface / Product Visualization

| Term | Definition | Standard |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Three-point lighting** | Key + Fill + Rim lights used to shape form and separate subject from background | 7.8.1 |
| **Product viz** | Photorealistic render of a product for marketing or client presentation | 7.6.9 |
| **Orthographic render** | Render with no perspective distortion — used in technical documentation | 7.4.2 |

### Track 2 — Animated Short

| Term | Definition | Standard |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Video Sequencer** | Blender's built-in video editor for assembling image sequences into a final video with sound | 7.7.9 |
| **Storyboard** | Pre-production panel-by-panel visual plan for an animation | 7.2.1 |
| **Shot list** | A table listing every camera shot: content, angle, duration, and purpose | 7.2.10 |
| **Image sequence** | A series of individually rendered PNG frames, assembled in post into a video | 7.8.1 |
| **MP4** | Compressed video format — suitable for delivery and presentation; not for re-editing | 7.7.9 |

### Track 3 — 3D-Printed Prototype (HSM 145140 Preview)

| Term | Definition | Standard |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **CAD** | Computer-Aided Design — parametric, constraint-based 3D modeling in OnShape or similar software | 7.4.2 |
| **FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling)** | 3D printing method that extrudes melted filament layer by layer | 7.4.2 |
| **Prototype** | A functional or representative early model of a product created for testing | 7.4.2 |
| **Design process** | The structured sequence from concept → sketch → CAD → prototype → evaluate; mirrors the engineering design cycle | 1.1.7 |
| **Concept sketch** | A rough hand-drawn or digital sketch created before CAD modeling to define form, dimensions, and intent | 7.4.2 |
