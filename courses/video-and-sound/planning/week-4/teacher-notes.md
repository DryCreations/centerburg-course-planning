# Week 4 Teacher Notes: Video and Sound

Teacher-facing pacing for Week 4. Student doc is `outline.md`; assignment is `project.md`; Tuesday's handout is
`handouts/tue-bpa-prompt-read.md`; vocab is `vocab.md`. No new quiz this week: Friday is work time plus an
**independent, optional retake** of last week's Week 3 RAW and editing quiz for anyone who wants a better
score. **Decided: the retake score replaces the original, one time only, since this is the first quiz of the
year and many students had not yet learned how to study for one.**

**Bring an actual past BPA Video Production Team (#430) prompt** from your WSAP guidelines or event archive to
hand out or display Tuesday. `handouts/tue-bpa-prompt-read.md` has the discussion structure only, not a prompt
text, since students should react to the real thing.

## Pacing Guide

| Day | Activity | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Tue | First half: hand out or display a real past BPA Video Production prompt. Students read it, mark requirements/limits/questions, discuss in small groups, then write a short reaction. Second half: post `vocab.md`, explain pre-production (idea, script or shot list, storyboard, schedule). Students start brainstorming their own video ideas. | Gauge real interest in the BPA event honestly; this is a recruiting moment, not a sales pitch. |
| Wed | Students narrow to one idea and write a shot list or short script. | Push for specific shots, not vague ideas; "show the hands doing the task" beats "show the process." |
| Thu | Storyboard each shot, panel by panel. Sketches only, framing and action, not finished art. | This is where students discover a shot is missing or a transition does not make sense. |
| Fri | Finish storyboards. Work time. **Mention this is also open for an independent retake of last week's quiz.** | State clearly: retaking is optional, done independently, replaces the original score. |

## Assessment Notes

- Graded per the rubric in `project.md`: BPA reaction notes, a complete shot list or script, a storyboard for
  every shot, noted production needs, and a rough schedule.
- Retake policy is settled: replaces the original score, one time only.
