## What This Week Covers

Reading and reacting to a **real BPA Video Production Team prompt**, then **pre-production**: turning an idea
into a script or shot list and a **storyboard** (a panel-by-panel sketch of what each shot looks like).

## Weeks 1 to 3 recap

The exposure triangle, camera modes, RAW capture, white balance, and a first edit in Adobe Camera Raw.
