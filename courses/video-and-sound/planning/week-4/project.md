# Week 4 Project: Five Shot Sequence

**Course:** Video and Sound | **Assigned:** Tue Sep 8 | **Due:** Fri Sep 11
**Category:** Labs/Projects | **Tools:** DSLR + the five shot sequence | **Late:** accepted, ask to reopen

Shoot **five shots of one activity** so the sequence tells a clear story, then arrange and caption them.

---

## Part A: Shoot (Wed)

Pick a real activity around school (someone building something, a sport or club practicing, a class project in
progress). Shoot:

| # | Shot |
|---|------|
| 1 | Close up of the hands doing the activity |
| 2 | Close up of a face (concentration, effort, reaction) |
| 3 | A wide shot showing the whole scene |
| 4 | An over the shoulder shot |
| 5 | One unusual angle, your choice |

Control exposure and white balance on purpose for each shot; do not leave the camera on Auto.

## Part B: Order and caption (Thu to Fri)

- Arrange the five shots in the order that tells the clearest story.
- Write one caption per shot: what is happening and why it is placed there.
- Export the ordered, captioned sequence (as a set of labeled images or a simple slide arrangement).

---

## Turn in

- [ ] Five shots, each showing deliberate exposure and white balance control
- [ ] The shots in a clear order with one caption each
- [ ] One sentence: why did you put shot X first and shot Y last?

## Grading

| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|-------:|
| All five shot types present and correctly framed | 10 |
| Exposure and white balance controlled on purpose | 6 |
| Order tells a clear story | 6 |
| Captions are accurate and useful | 5 |
| Reflection answered | 3 |

## Extension (finished early)

Shoot a second five shot sequence of a different activity and compare, in two sentences, which sequence reads
more clearly and why.
