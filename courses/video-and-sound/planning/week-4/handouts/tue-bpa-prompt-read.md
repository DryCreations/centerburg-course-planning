# Reading a Real BPA Video Production Prompt

**Video & Sound · Week 4 · Tuesday Sep 8**

**Teacher note:** paste or hand out an actual past BPA Video Production Team (#430) prompt from your WSAP
guidelines or event archive here before class. This handout only has the discussion structure; it does not
include a specific prompt text, so students read the real thing rather than a made-up example.

---

## Before you read

BPA competitors get a prompt like this and have to plan and produce a video that answers it. Today you read one
for real and react to it the way a competitor would on day one.

## While you read

Read the prompt once all the way through. Then read it again and mark:

- What is the video **required to do or show**?
- Any **limits**: time length, format, what is or is not allowed.
- Anything **unclear** you would want to ask a judge or coach about.

## Discussion (small group, then share out)

1. What is this prompt actually asking a team to make?
2. What would be the hardest part of this prompt to pull off well?
3. What is one idea you would try if this were your team's prompt?
4. Does this kind of competition sound like something you would want to try? Why or why not?

## Do Now (write it down)

In two or three sentences: what did you notice about this prompt, and does the Video Production event interest
you? There is no wrong answer, this is about finding out what students want to try.
