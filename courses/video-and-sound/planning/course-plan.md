**VM (Grades 7-10):** Yes

## Course Overview

Students will create professional video and audio productions for distribution in traditional and new media channels. Students will plan, produce, edit, and launch media products. Students will develop scripts and storyboards, compose shots and operate cameras, capture sounds using microphone hardware, apply special effect techniques, and edit to achieve the final product. Students will be able to use animation and graphic design for video.

## BPA Competition Integration

See the [BPA competition planning document](../../../program/bpa/competition-planning.md) for the full season calendar, national deadlines, and how to run a local competition.

### Course Alignment

Video and Sound has the most direct 1:1 alignment with BPA events of any course in this program. Strand 7.7 (Video Production) maps directly to Video Production Team (#430) and Broadcast News Production Team (#445). Strand 7.8 (Audio Production) maps directly to Podcast Production Team (#450). BPA competition entries **are** the course's production projects — students do not need to create separate work.

### Recommended Events

Students select **one primary event** and one backup. Most video/audio events are team-based — teams of 2–3 form within the class. Encourage students to form teams early in Q1.

| Event # | Event Name | Format | Standards Aligned | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **430** | Video Production Team | Team (up to 3) | Strand 7.7 (Video Production) | 3–5 min video on assigned topic. Most direct alignment with this course. |
| **445** | Broadcast News Production Team | Team (2–3) | Strand 7.7 (Video Production) | 3–5 min news broadcast, 2 segments. Strong real-world format. |
| **450** | Podcast Production Team | Team (2–3) | Strand 7.8 (Audio Production) | 3–5 min podcast on assigned topic. Direct alignment with audio strand. |
| **420** | Digital Media Production | Ind. or Team | Strand 7.1 (Multimedia), 7.7 | 1–2 min production. More accessible scope; good for first-year students or smaller classes. |
| **V11** | 2D Animation Team | Team (Virtual) | Strand 7.1 (Multimedia Tools) | 1:30–2:30 min animation. **National-only virtual event** — no state qualification needed. Submit online by Jan 15. |
| **490** | DC&D Concepts — Open | Objective Test | Strand 2, 7 (general) | Knowledge test taken at competition. Lowest barrier — no project required. Good backup option. |

### School Year Integration Timeline

| Quarter | BPA Activity | Connection to Course Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** (Aug–Oct) | Introduce BPA; form teams; review WSAP guidelines; select event | Foundation: camera operation, storyboarding, audio capture, pre-production — students identify which production skills map to their event |
| **Q2** (Oct–Jan) | Production begins; topic/prompt received from WSAP guidelines; first cut complete | Applied production: scripting, recording, editing, sound design — BPA entry **is** the course production project |
| **Q3** (Jan–Feb) | Finalize production; hold **local school competition / screening** (January) | Export, peer screening and critique using WSAP rubric; presentation of finished work |
| **Q3** (Feb–Mar) | **Ohio State Leadership Conference** *(confirm exact date with Ohio State Advisor)* | State competitors attend; remaining students compile showreel or begin next project |
| **Q4** (Apr–May) | **NLC May 6–10** (Nashville) for state qualifiers; debrief for all | Showreel compilation; career connections in media production |

> **Virtual event students (V11):** Register by **December 1** and submit animation by **January 15** via upload.bpa.org — no state qualification needed.

### Local Competition (January)

Organize a school screening event before the state conference:
- Set up as a film festival or broadcast screening; invite BAC members, administration, and other students
- Teams screen their video production, broadcast, or podcast
- Judges use the WSAP scoring rubric; top teams advance to state
- Can run as a class period event or an after-school screening
