# Lab 2: Emerging Tech Analysis + AI-Assisted Media Piece

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 3.2 — Emerging Technologies in Media
**Duration:** 5 days | **Weeks:** 23–24
**Tools Needed:** Browser, chosen AI tool (approved list), DaVinci Resolve for final clip

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Build a tool comparison matrix for 3 emerging media technologies
- Produce a 30–60 second AI-assisted clip with a visible disclosure note
- Write a structured ethics reflection covering attribution, bias, licensing, and creative control

---

## Background

You researched one technology in depth for the lab presentation. This lab-2 broadens that to a 3-tool comparison and asks you to actually produce with AI tools — then document the ethical decisions that come with that.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Tool Comparison Matrix (Day 1)

Choose 3 different emerging technologies (AI audio, spatial audio, AI video generation, volumetric capture, XR, or similar — teacher can suggest options). For each, fill in: tool name, what it does, adoption stage (experimental / emerging / mainstream), an example of professional use, and an ethical or IP consideration.

### Step 2 — Produce an AI-Assisted Clip (Days 2–3)

Using one of the approved AI tools, produce a 30–60 second clip or audio piece where AI meaningfully contributed to the output (e.g., AI-generated background music, AI-enhanced foley, AI-generated b-roll element). The clip must also include original footage or audio that YOU recorded.

### Step 3 — Add the Disclosure Note

At the end of the clip (or in a title card), include visible text stating: which AI tool was used and what it contributed. Example: "Background music generated using [Tool]. Narration, footage, and edit by [Your Name]."

### Step 4 — Write the Ethics Reflection (Days 4–5)

Write 2–3 sentences on each of the 4 topics:
1. **Attribution** — Who "made" the AI-generated element?
2. **Bias** — What might the AI reflect in its outputs?
3. **Licensing** — Is this piece safe for commercial use?
4. **Creative control** — What did you decide vs. the AI?

### Step 5 — Submit

Upload the clip to Google Classroom (or share a Drive link). Submit the comparison matrix and reflection as a Google Doc link. Add to portfolio as Artifact #8.
