## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

AI is changing every part of video and audio production — from automatic transcription and color matching to AI voice generation and deepfakes. This unit surveys current and emerging technologies, explores their ethical implications, and has you produce a short AI-assisted media piece while documenting your process and ethical decisions.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Identify at least 5 AI-assisted tools currently used in professional video/audio production
- Use an AI-assisted production tool (transcription, noise removal, color match) on a real project
- Explain 3 ethical challenges raised by AI in media: disclosure, authenticity, job displacement
- Produce a structured emerging tech analysis document comparing AI tools across categories

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students produce clip entirely with AI:** Require that the student made editing decisions (selection, arrangement, pace) — not just pressed "generate."
- **Ethics reflection lacks examples:** Require each of the 3 topics to cite a real case, tool, or news event.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a tool comparison matrix template with headers and 2 example rows.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — test whether AI-generated content passes a "deepfake detection" tool; document what the tool looks for.

### BPA Connections

No dedicated BPA event. AI disclosure and ethics documentation required as part of any BPA entry that uses AI-assisted tools.
