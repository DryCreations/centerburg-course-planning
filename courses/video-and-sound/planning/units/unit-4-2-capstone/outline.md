## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

The capstone is the most complete media project you've ever made. You choose the format (documentary, narrative short, or branded video series), go through a full production pipeline from concept to delivery, and present the finished work to a live audience. This becomes the centerpiece of your production portfolio.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Develop an original concept with a clear audience, tone, and purpose
- Complete a full production pipeline: pitch → pre-production → production → post-production → delivery
- Produce a finished 5–8 minute video at broadcast-quality standards
- Present your production process and final video to a panel, explaining creative and technical decisions

All VS production standards — cumulative assessment across all Units 1.2–3.1.

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Concept is too ambitious:** Require pitch approval before any shooting begins. Ask: "Can you realistically complete this in 5 weeks?"
- **Process book is a photo dump:** Require narrative text connecting each phase — not just screenshots.
- **Presentation is descriptive only:** Require students to cite specific production decisions (lighting setup, audio technique, editing choice) per standard.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Allow the capstone to build on the BPA project footage (extended cut or director's cut version).
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — produce a 60-second highlight reel and write a social media distribution plan.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric across all production standards. BAC presentation is a required component. Grading: deliverables (50%), process book (15%), portfolio (10%), presentation (25%).
