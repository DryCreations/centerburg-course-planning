## Week 35 — Final Export + Showreel Start

1. Export capstone final cut using appropriate delivery settings.
2. Begin showreel assembly:
   - Review all year's work (Artifacts #0–#9)
   - Select best clips — aim for variety: audio, video, composition, lighting, editing
   - Sequence clips for the most compelling opening 15 seconds

---

## Week 36 — Showreel Polish

### Showreel Requirements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|------------|
| Length | 60–90 seconds (strictly enforced) |
| Title card | Creator's name, course, year |
| Music bed | Licensed/royalty-free; mixed under footage |
| Credits | End card with music attribution |
| Clip variety | At least 3 different types of work shown |
| No talking heads only | Must show range of skills |

**⚑ WebXam Retake — deadline May 23** (if retake was needed)

For retake students: Continue attending Mon/Thu review sessions. Confirm retake is scheduled.

## Week 37 — End-of-Year Screening

Each student presents:
1. **Capstone production** (full length)
2. **Showreel** (60–90 sec)
3. **1-minute verbal introduction** — describe the capstone concept and one thing you learned from making it
4. **Q&A** — 2–3 questions from the audience (BAC members, classmates, teacher)
