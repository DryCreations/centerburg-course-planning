# Vocabulary: Capstone Production & Showreel

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 4.2 — Capstone Production & Showreel
**Weeks:** 30–37

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Capstone project** | A culminating production at the end of a course — demonstrates mastery across all skills and standards from the year; student-chosen format | 1.1.5 | 2 |
| **Documentary short** | A non-fiction video typically 3–10 minutes — combines interviews, B-roll, narration, and archival material to explore a real-world subject in depth | 7.7 | 1 |
| **Music video** | A short film created to accompany a piece of music — uses visual storytelling, editing rhythm matched to music, and creative cinematography | 7.7 | 1 |
| **Podcast series episode** | A single audio episode within a longer-form series — structured with intro, segments, interviews, and outro; branded and consistent | 7.8 | 1 |
| **Animated short** | A brief film produced entirely through animation — requires storyboarding, visual design, motion, and sound design; connects to V11 BPA pathway | 7.3 | 1 |
| **News feature** | A journalistic video segment exploring a specific topic or issue in depth — structured as a broadcast segment with anchor intro, reporter package, and outro | 7.7 | 1 |
| **Showreel** | A 60–90 second highlight reel of a creator's best work from the year — includes title card, music bed, and credits; the primary video production portfolio piece | 1.1.5 | 2 |
| **Title card** | A full-screen graphic at the start of a production or showreel — displays the creator's name, the title, and any other essential identifying information | 7.3.9 | 1 |
| **Music bed** | Background music played underneath narration, dialogue, or visuals — sets tone without overpowering (typically 15–25 dB below peak narration) | 7.3.10 | 2 |
| **Proposal (capstone)** | A written document submitted before production — describes the format, concept, target audience, production approach, and timeline for the capstone production | 2.9.1 | 2 |
| **Pre-production package** | The full set of planning documents for a production: script or detailed outline, storyboard, shot list, and production schedule | 7.2 | 2 |
| **Remediation** | Targeted review of content in which a student demonstrates weakness — for WebXam retake students, structured review sessions Mon/Thu in Unit 4.2 | Course | 1 |
| **Retake deadline** | The last date a student may take the WebXam retake — **May 23**; requires that the Initial Test was taken at least 30 days earlier (April 23) | Course | 1 |
| **End-of-year screening** | A public presentation event where students present their capstone productions and showreels to classmates and the Business Advisory Council | 1.2.9 | 2 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. What format will your capstone production take, and why did you choose it? What skill do you most want to demonstrate?
2. How is the capstone production different from your BPA entry? (Hint: it should show a different format and range.)
3. What is the purpose of a showreel? If someone watched only your showreel, what would they conclude about you as a media producer?
4. The WebXam retake deadline is May 23 and requires a 30-day gap from the initial test (April 23). Why does timing strategy matter?
5. Who is in the audience at your end-of-year screening? How does knowing your audience shape how you present your work?
