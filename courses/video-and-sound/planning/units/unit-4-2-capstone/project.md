# Project: Capstone Production & Professional Showreel

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 4.2 — Capstone Production & Showreel
**Weeks:** 30–37

---

## Overview

The capstone is your final production of the year — your own creative project, in a format of your choosing, demonstrating mastery across the full range of skills from this course. Paired with your professional showreel, it becomes the centerpiece of your portfolio.

---

## Class Prompt: Capstone Production

### What You're Producing

**Two linked deliverables:**

1. **Capstone Production** — a complete, finished video, audio, or multimedia piece in a format different from your BPA entry. Demonstrates range and mastery.
2. **Professional Showreel** — a 60–90 second highlight reel of your best work from the full year.

### Format Options

| Format | Key Technical Demonstration |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Documentary short | Interviews, B-roll, narration, editing structure |
| Music video | Editing rhythm to music, cinematography, visual storytelling |
| Podcast series episode | Mic technique, multi-track mixing, narrative audio structure |
| Animated short | Connects to V11 BPA pathway; motion, design, sound design |
| News feature | Broadcast structure, on-camera presence, editing, lower thirds |

*Choose a format DIFFERENT from your BPA entry.*

### Requirements

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Capstone production | Complete, finished — appropriate length for format |
| Pre-production package | Script/outline, storyboard, shot list, schedule |
| Production log | Weekly entries, Weeks 32+ |
| Showreel | 60–90 sec; title card; music bed; credits; 3+ clip types |
| End-of-year screening | Capstone + showreel presented; 1-min intro + Q&A |
| Reflection | Post-screening written response |

## Portfolio Note

Artifact #11 (Capstone) + Artifact #12 (Showreel). These two artifacts together close the loop on the year. Compare Artifact #0 (pretest goals) to Artifact #12 (showreel) — that distance is your story. The showreel is what you take with you: to a portfolio review, a college interview, a job application, or any next step in media production.

## Extension Task

Prepare a one-page production diary for the capstone — a narrative description of the most significant creative or technical decision you made during production. Describe the problem you faced, the options you considered, what you chose, and what you learned. This reflection becomes a supplemental portfolio note that gives a future reviewer insight into your creative process, not just the finished product.
