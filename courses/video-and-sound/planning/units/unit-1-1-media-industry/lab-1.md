# Lab: Critical Media Review

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.1 — The Media Industry & Interactive Media
**Duration:** Week 2

---

## Objective

Analyze professional media productions using the vocabulary of the media industry. Apply critical review skills to evaluate purpose, audience, technique, social/cultural implications, and IP considerations.

---

## Part 1 — Critical Review: Watch a Professional Production

The teacher will screen one or two professional examples (video or podcast). View attentively. Then complete the following:

**Production title / description:** ___________________________________

### A. Audience & Purpose (7.1.6, 7.1.10)

1. Who is the target market for this piece? Describe them specifically (age, interest, platform).
2. What is its primary purpose? Circle: *Entertain / Inform / Persuade / Educate / Promote*
3. Is this linear or non-linear media? How can you tell?

### B. Production Technique (7.1.6)

4. List 3 specific production techniques you observed (camera angle, lighting, music choice, pacing, visual style, sound design, etc.). For each, explain what effect it creates for the audience.

| Technique | Effect on Audience |

### C. Social & Cultural Implications (7.1.8)

5. Identify one social or cultural implication this piece carries. Does it challenge or reinforce a stereotype? Make a claim about a community or identity? Represent or misrepresent a group?
6. Do you think the creators were aware of this implication? Should they have addressed it differently?

### D. IP Considerations (1.3.8)

7. What copyrightable elements are present in this production (music, footage, images, script, logos)?
8. How might a student creator working on a similar project ethically obtain or substitute those elements?
