# Lab 2: Media Industry Landscape Document

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.1 — The Media Industry & Interactive Media
**Duration:** 2–3 days | **Weeks:** 2–3
**Tools Needed:** Browser for research, Google Docs or equivalent

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Analyze 3 media samples across different genres and platforms
- Identify IP/copyright considerations and career roles in each sample
- Write a structured reflection on patterns in successful media

---

## Background

Producers and directors need to understand the landscape they work in before they pick up a camera. This survey documents your starting-point awareness of the media industry.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Select 3 Media Samples

Choose 3 pieces of media from 3 different genres/platforms. At least 1 must be video and at least 1 must be audio (podcast, song, radio segment, or audio documentary). Note the title, platform, and genre for each.

### Step 2 — Analyze Each Sample

For each sample, record in a table: target market (specific, not generic), 1 IP note (what copyrightable elements appear + how a creator could ethically source them), and 1 career role actively involved in producing the piece.

### Step 3 — Write the Reflection

Write 3–5 sentences answering: What patterns do you notice about how successful media is designed? What decisions did the creators make that seem intentional?

### Step 4 — Format and Submit

Organize your table and reflection in a clean Google Doc. Add a title, your name, and the date. Submit the shareable link. Add to portfolio as Artifact #1.
