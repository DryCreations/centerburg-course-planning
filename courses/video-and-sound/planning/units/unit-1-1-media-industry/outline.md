**Course:** Video & Sound Production (145110)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Weeks:** 3–4 | **Duration:** 2 weeks

---

## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Who makes films, podcasts, and commercials — and what does each person actually do? This unit maps the entire media production industry: job titles, workflow stages, industry structure, and how money flows in the business. Understanding the industry is the foundation for every production decision you'll make this year.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Name and describe 10+ production roles across pre-production, production, and post-production
- Explain how a media project moves through the three-stage production pipeline
- Identify multiple industry sectors (broadcast, streaming, advertising, podcast) and how they differ
- Create a one-page industry landscape document comparing at least 3 sectors

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Role research is a Wikipedia summary:** Require at least one industry source (IMDb Pro, BLS, CreativeCow, etc.) per role researched.
- **Industry landscape misses a sector:** Must include at least one non-entertainment sector (corporate video, medical, education, news) in the comparison.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a 10-row role research table with pre-populated column headers.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — interview a working professional (3 questions + response summary).
