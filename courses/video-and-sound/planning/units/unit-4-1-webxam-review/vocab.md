# Vocabulary: WebXam Review & Initial Test

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 4.1 — WebXam Review & Initial Test
**Weeks:** 28–29

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Test blueprint** | The official document specifying how each content strand is weighted on a standardized exam — guides both test design and study priorities | Course | 2 |
| **Strand weight** | The percentage of total test questions attributed to a specific content strand in the WebXam blueprint — 7.3, 7.7, and 7.8 each carry approximately 16% in Video and Sound | Course | 2 |
| **Baseline vs. growth** | Comparing a student's initial pretest score (Unit 1.0) to current practice performance — measures what was learned during the course year | Course | 2 |
| **Targeted review** | Studying specifically in the areas of greatest gap relative to the test weight — not reviewing material you already know well, but focusing where improvement has highest return | Course | 2 |
| **Practice question analysis** | The process of reviewing each missed practice question — identifying whether the error was a vocabulary gap, concept misunderstanding, or reading error — to guide further study | Course | 3 |
| **Credential exam** | A standardized assessment that, upon passing, earns a recognized industry or academic credential — the WebXam is ITA-administered and earns the Video and Sound CTE credential | Course | 1 |
| **ITA (Information Technology Academy)** | The organization that develops and administers the WebXam — sets the test blueprint and credential standards | Course | 1 |
| **Makeup test** | A second opportunity to take the Initial Test for students who were absent — administered the same week as the original test | Course | 1 |
| **Test-taking strategy** | Techniques for improving performance on a standardized exam — process of elimination, managing time, answering easy questions first, previewing and reviewing | Course | 2 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. Compare your pretest strand scores (Unit 1.0) to your current practice scores. In which strand did you improve the most? In which do you still have the biggest gap?
2. The three highest-weight strands (7.3, 7.7, 7.8) together account for nearly 48% of the WebXam. Name 3 specific vocabulary terms from each strand you are least confident about.
3. On a practice question you got wrong, describe the type of error: was it a vocabulary confusion, a concept you didn't understand, or a reading mistake? What would you do differently?
4. What is a test-taking strategy you actually use? Does it work for you on standardized tests?
5. What is the ITA WebXam credential, and how does passing it benefit you after this course?
