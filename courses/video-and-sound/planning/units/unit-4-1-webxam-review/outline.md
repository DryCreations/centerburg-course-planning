## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This unit is dedicated to earning your credential: the Ohio ITA WebXam (CTIM006 pathway). You'll compare your current knowledge to your pretest from Week 1, review the highest-weight test strands, take practice tests, and then sit for the exam.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Identify which WebXam strands are highest-weight and prioritize accordingly
- Use your pretest data to target review to your personal weakest strands
- Demonstrate readiness by scoring 70%+ on a practice test before the real exam

### Standards Addressed

All CTIM006 strands reviewed. Emphasis on production, editing, and audio — typically highest-weight areas.

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students skip review because they "already edited a video":** Emphasize that the exam tests vocabulary and technical knowledge, not just practical experience.
- **Practice test anxiety:** Frame the practice test as diagnostic — the score tells you where to study, not whether you'll pass.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a strand-by-strand vocabulary card set and a 5-day review schedule.
- **Retake students:** After Initial Test results, analyze strand scores and focus remaining review time on the highest-gap strands.

### Assessment Notes

- Exam score does not factor into class grade — demonstrated review effort and exam attempt does.
- Retake window opens 30 days after Initial Test. Schedule retakes during Unit 4.2 production time.

### BPA Connections

Credential exam preparation is separate from BPA. Students advancing to BPA state or NLC continue working on BPA materials during this unit.
