# Lab 2: WebXam Attempt + Post-Exam Reflection

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 4.1 — WebXam Review & Initial Test
**Duration:** 2 weeks | **Weeks:** 28–29
**Tools Needed:** Testing computer, WebXam access (CTIM006), review notes from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Sit for the WebXam (CTIM006) Initial Test after completing the strand review in lab
- Complete a post-exam reflection documenting confidence areas, surprises, and a retake plan if needed

---

## Background

You completed the strand comparison and practice quiz in the lab. This lab-2 is the exam itself plus the reflection that follows. The review work is how you prepared — this is the payoff.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — WebXam Initial Test (⚑ by April 23)

Sit for the WebXam in class. Use your full time. After finishing, note the strands you felt most and least confident about — you won't see the breakdown immediately.

### Step 2 — Post-Exam Reflection (same day or next class)

Write a 1-paragraph reflection answering: (1) What topics felt most confident? (2) What surprised you? (3) What would you study differently? (4) What was your target score going in?

### Step 3 — Retake Plan (if score is below target)

If you did not hit your target: identify the 3 strands most likely to need improvement, list 5 vocabulary terms per strand you want to review, and set a retake date with your teacher (retake window opens 30 days after the initial test).

### Step 4 — Portfolio Entry

Add a "Credentials" page to your portfolio. Note the exam attempt, the approximate date, and whether you are pursuing a retake. Update this page when results come back.
