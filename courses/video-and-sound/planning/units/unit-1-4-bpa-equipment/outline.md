## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Before you pick up a camera for real production, you need to know the rules. This unit covers equipment operation at a professional standard, copyright and licensing for all media you use or produce, and the FCC/broadcast standards that govern professional production. It also introduces BPA competition requirements so you're prepared to plan your entry early.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Operate the primary studio camera using safe handling, mounting, and storage procedures
- Explain the difference between copyright, fair use, Creative Commons, and public domain
- Apply proper attribution for any licensed media in a project
- Describe the applicable FCC broadcast standards and how they affect production decisions

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students assume all "free" media is copyright-free:** The compliance report requires citation of license type for every media element — even "free" ones.
- **Students pick a BPA event they can't complete alone:** Coach on realistic scope; #406 and V21 are doable; in-person events require advance planning.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a copyright type reference card (4 types with examples).
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — research one real FCC enforcement case and summarize what went wrong.

### BPA Connections

**Orientation for all VS BPA events:** Students who committed to #406, #407, or V21 use the BPA memo from this unit to begin advance planning. Registration opens by November.
