# Lab 2: BPA Event Selection Package + Equipment Readiness

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.4 — BPA Showcase, Event Selection & Equipment Orientation
**Duration:** 5 days | **Weeks:** 8–9
**Tools Needed:** BPA WSAP documents, camera + audio kit from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Select a BPA event and document the team, roles, and WSAP scoring criteria
- Complete hands-on equipment checkout and audio configuration
- Set up a production folder structure with a 3-2-1 backup plan

---

## Background

This package is the contract with yourself for the year. It records your BPA commitment, team, and equipment readiness baseline. You'll reference this in every production unit.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — BPA Event Selection (Days 1–2)

Review all available BPA events. Select your event and team. Complete the Event Selection & Team Agreement form with: chosen event, reason for selecting it, team roster, and role assignments. Get teacher sign-off by Day 2.

### Step 2 — WSAP Summary (Day 2)

Read your WSAP. Document: the required video/audio length, the top 3 scoring criteria (with point values), and the submission format. Add a note about virtual event registration if applicable (Dec 1 deadline).

### Step 3 — Equipment Checkout (Day 3)

Complete the camera system checkout checklist: follow the checkout procedure, apply the naming convention, record your test clip. Show the teacher the 30-second test clip before leaving class.

### Step 4 — Audio Orientation (Day 3–4)

Configure the shotgun mic and lav mic in turn. Record a brief test with each. Write 1–2 sentences comparing the results: when would you use each?

### Step 5 — Production Folder Structure (Day 4–5)

Create your production folder on the class server or drive. Document your 3-2-1 backup plan (3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite). Add a folder structure screenshot to your portfolio.
