---

### Part D: Audio Gear Orientation (Day 3)

Practice setting up and using two microphone configurations:

**Configuration 1 — Shotgun on boom pole:**
- Mount shotgun mic on boom pole
- Position above subject (angled slightly down, 6–8 inches from mouth)
- Connect to camera or audio interface
- Record 30 seconds; note quality

**Configuration 2 — Lavalier (wireless or wired):**
- Clip lav to subject (center of chest, below collar)
- Route cable neatly (conceal if possible)
- Record 30 seconds; compare to boom recording

Which configuration produced cleaner audio? When would you prefer each?
