## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Before anything gets recorded, you need to know where you're going. In this unit you'll set up your course portfolio, build your personal learning goals based on a pretest, explore what careers exist in media production, and review the classroom agreements that keep shared equipment safe.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Set up a digital portfolio site (Google Sites or equivalent) to house all course work
- Complete a pretest that identifies your strongest and weakest production knowledge areas
- Write 3 SMART goals for the course based on pretest data
- State the equipment checkout and studio safety rules

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Portfolio link doesn't work for others:** Require students to share the link in a shared doc and test it from a different device.
- **SMART goals are vague:** Model the difference: "I want to get better at audio" vs. "By the end of Quarter 1, I will set and monitor audio levels on 3 live recordings."

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a SMART goals sentence frame.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — write a 2-paragraph bio for the portfolio "About" page.
