# Lab 2: Portfolio Setup & Learning Goals

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.0 — WebXam Pretest & Course Orientation
**Duration:** Week 1 | **Weeks:** 1–2
**Tools Needed:** Google Sites (or teacher-approved platform), strand score table from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Create a live, publicly accessible portfolio site with navigation structure for all course units
- Write 3 SMART learning goals connected to pretest strand gaps
- Complete the equipment safety orientation acknowledgment

---

## Background

This portfolio is the container for every artifact you submit this year. Set it up carefully now — fixing structure later wastes production time.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Create Your Portfolio Site

Create a new Google Site (or teacher-approved platform). Title it: `[Your Name] — Video & Sound Portfolio`. Add navigation pages for: Home / About, each Unit (1.0 through 4.2), and Credentials.

### Step 2 — Equipment Safety Acknowledgment

Complete the signed equipment orientation form. Keep a copy and note the date on your portfolio Home page.

### Step 3 — Pretest Strand Score Table

Using your pretest results from the lab, build a table showing: Strand name, score %, WebXam weight %, gap, and priority (High/Medium/Low). Circle your 2 lowest-scoring strands.

### Step 4 — Write Your SMART Goals

Choose 3 strands with the lowest scores. For each, write a SMART goal using the format: *By [date], I will [specific skill], which will demonstrate [standard].*

### Step 5 — Assemble and Submit

Add to your portfolio Home/About page: the strand table, your 3 SMART goals, and a 1-sentence production goal (the type of production you want to create this year). Make the site publicly viewable and copy the shareable URL.
