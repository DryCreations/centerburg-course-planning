# Vocabulary: Course Orientation & WebXam Baseline

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.0 — WebXam Pretest & Course Orientation
**Week:** 1

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **WebXam** | The statewide end-of-course credential exam for CTE courses — administered by ITA; measures content knowledge across all course strands | Course | 1 |
| **EOC (End-of-Course) test** | An assessment administered to measure student mastery of course standards; the WebXam is the EOC for Video and Sound | Course | 1 |
| **Strand** | A grouping of related competencies in the CTE standards — Video and Sound is assessed across strands 7.1–7.8, 2.9–2.10, and Strand 1 | Course | 1 |
| **Baseline score** | The initial score on a pretest — used to identify knowledge gaps and measure growth over the course year | Course | 1 |
| **Production team** | The group of 2–3 students who collaborate on major projects and BPA events — roles include director, camera operator, audio engineer, and editor | 2.9.2 | 1 |
| **BPA (Business Professionals of America)** | A career and technical student organization (CTSO) with competitive events in business technology, media production, and design — has events aligned directly with this course | Course | 1 |
| **WSAP (Written Student Advancement Plan)** | The official BPA competition guidelines document for each event — specifies deliverables, scoring rubric, time limits, and submission requirements | Course | 2 |
| **Video production** | The process of planning, recording, and editing video content — encompasses pre-production, production, and post-production phases | 7.3 | 1 |
| **Pre-production** | All work accomplished before a camera rolls: scripting, storyboarding, planning, location scouting, equipment prep | 7.2 | 1 |
| **Production** | The phase in which actual video and audio are captured — principal photography, on-location recording | 7.7 | 1 |
| **Post-production** | Editing, color grading, audio mixing, adding effects and titles — the phase after all footage is captured | 7.7.5 | 1 |
| **Portfolio** | A curated collection of a student's best work — for video students, this typically includes a showreel and documentation of the production process | 1.1.5 | 1 |
| **Showreel** | A 60–90 second highlight reel of the best footage and productions from a creator's body of work — the primary video production portfolio artifact | 4.2 | 2 |
| **Learning goal** | A specific, measurable target a student sets for themselves — in this course, connected to WebXam strand performance and production skill development | Course | 2 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. What do you already know about video or audio production? Where did you learn it?
2. What is one specific type of video you'd like to produce by the end of this course?
3. If you were on a 3-person production team, what role would you most want? Why?
4. What does "pre-production" mean, and why would you do it before picking up a camera?
5. What is a portfolio, and why does it matter for a career in media production?
