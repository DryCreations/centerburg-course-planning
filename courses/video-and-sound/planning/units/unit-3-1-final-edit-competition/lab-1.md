## Week 21–22 — Film Festival / Local Competition

### Part C: Screening Preparation

**For all teams:**
1. Create a title card slide (or on-screen title) identifying: team name, event, production title
2. Prepare for a brief (1–2 minute) introduction before your screening: title, what the piece is about, your team roles

**For events with Q&A (e.g., Q&A component in #445 Broadcast News, or event-specific oral component):**

Practice answering these questions:
1. Describe one major creative decision you made and why.
2. What was the biggest technical challenge you faced, and how did you solve it?
3. If you made this production again, what would you do differently?
4. How does your production match the WSAP topic/theme?
