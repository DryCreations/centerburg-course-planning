# Vocabulary: Final Edit & Local Competition

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 3.1 — Final Edit & Local Competition
**Weeks:** 19–22

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Fine cut** | The near-final version of an edited production — clips trimmed, pacing refined, transitions polished, audio mixed, color graded; pending final approval before export | 1.2.1 | 2 |
| **Export settings** | The parameters used when rendering a video for delivery — codec, resolution, frame rate, bit rate, audio sample rate; BPA has specific submission requirements | 1.2.4 | 2 |
| **H.264** | A widely used video codec that balances quality and file size — the most common delivery format for web video and many BPA event submissions | 1.2.4 | 1 |
| **Delivery format** | The specific file type, codec, resolution, and frame rate required for a particular platform or competition — BPA specifies delivery formats in the WSAP | 1.2.4 | 2 |
| **WSAP rubric** | The official scoring criteria document for a BPA event — used by judges during competition and by students as a self-assessment checklist | 1.2.5 | 2 |
| **Self-evaluation** | A student's structured assessment of their own work against defined criteria — comparing the finished production to WSAP rubric standards | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Constructive feedback** | Specific, actionable commentary on a work product that identifies what is done well and what should be improved — based on criteria, not personal preference | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Peer review** | A structured process of evaluating a classmate's work using a shared rubric or criteria — provides perspective the creator cannot get independently | 1.2.5 | 2 |
| **Q&A presentation** | For BPA events with a presentation component (e.g., #445 Broadcast News), a live question-and-answer session before judges — tests the team's knowledge and reasoning | 1.2.7 | 3 |
| **Film festival format** | A screening event where completed productions are shown to an audience — often followed by brief Q&A — used as the format for the school BPA local competition | 1.2.9 | 2 |
| **Portfolio presentation** | A structured presentation of artifacts, documentation, and reflections demonstrating growth and competency over a period of time | 1.2.2 | 2 |
| **Normalization** | Setting audio levels consistently by adjusting the peak or average loudness — ensures dialogue, music, and SFX are balanced appropriately for delivery | 1.2.4 | 2 |
| **Submission deadline** | The date by which a BPA entry must be fully submitted — missing this date disqualifies the entry regardless of quality | Course | 1 |
| **State Leadership Conference** | The Ohio BPA competition at which qualifying teams may advance after school/regional competition — occurs February/March | Course | 1 |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. What is the difference between a rough cut and a fine cut? Give one example of a change you would make when moving from rough to fine.
2. You're about to export your BPA video. What format requirements should you check in the WSAP before exporting?
3. Why is self-evaluating your own work against the rubric — before submitting — a professional practice?
4. A classmate gives you feedback: "The audio is bad." Is this constructive feedback? Why or why not? What would make it constructive?
5. What is the purpose of a Q&A session at a BPA competition, and how should you prepare for it?
