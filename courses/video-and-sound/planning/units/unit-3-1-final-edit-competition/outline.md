## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Your BPA video is due January 15. This unit is about getting it there: a structured self-critique, peer screening, color and audio refinement, closed captioning, final export, and a polished presentation to your class or BAC panel. The local competition is this quarter.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Conduct a structured self-critique comparing your video to BPA scoring criteria
- Implement specific, documented revisions based on critique findings
- Add closed captions and verify they are accurate and synchronized
- Present your project verbally to a panel, explaining specific creative and technical decisions

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Captions are auto-generated but never corrected:** Require students to manually check every caption line before submission.
- **Presentation is just "I made a video":** Require students to cite specific decisions (lighting setup used, mix technique, editing technique) — not general summaries.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a critique checklist template with blank spaces for evidence and revision plan.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — write a post-competition debrief after receiving feedback.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Verbal presentation is a required component — students must point to evidence per standard.
