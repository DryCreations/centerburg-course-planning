## Standards & Rubric

Walk the room as students work. Ask students to point to evidence for each standard before scoring.

| Standard | Description | Look-For (Proficient = 3) | Beginning (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **1.1.9** | Self-evaluation | WSAP rubric self-check submitted with scores per criterion | Not submitted | Submitted; incomplete | Self-check with scores on all criteria | Self-check + specific action note per low-scoring criterion |
| **1.2.1** | Fine cut | Completed fine cut at competition quality | Rough cut only | Fine cut started | Fine cut complete at competition quality | Fine cut + color grade + sound mix finalized |
| **1.2.2** | Portfolio documentation | Production process documented (proposal through fine cut) | Not documented | Partial documentation | Full process documented | Full documentation + reflection on key creative decisions |
| **1.2.4** | Export / delivery | Final cut exported per WSAP specs (format, resolution, naming) | Wrong format | Correct format; wrong specs | WSAP-compliant format, resolution, and naming | WSAP-compliant + delivery checklist |
| **1.2.5** | Rubric application | WSAP rubric used as editing guide; criteria reference documented | Not referenced | Referenced once | WSAP criteria referenced in editing notes | WSAP self-score per criterion with editing decision linked |
| **1.2.7** | Q&A / film festival presentation | Introductory statement and Q&A delivered at screening | Not presented | Introduced only | Introduction + Q&A completed | Introduction + Q&A + written post-screening reflection |
| **1.2.9** | Public screening / competition | Local competition participation confirmed | Not participated | Participated; no intro | Participation + competition recorded | Participation + competition result + debrief notes |
