## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Running a media production business is different from just making great videos. This unit covers the operational side: budgeting a production, writing client contracts, negotiating rights and licensing, and building a basic business plan. You'll produce a freelance business package using your own brand identity.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Build a production budget with equipment, crew, location, and licensing line items
- Write a client contract covering scope, revisions, IP ownership, and payment terms
- Explain the difference between work-for-hire, royalty, and licensing agreements
- Create a marketing leave-behind for a freelance media production business

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Budget doesn't account for time:** Require a "production days × day rate" calculation for all crew including themselves.
- **Contract is copied from online:** Contract must be personalized to their specific services; require a revision limit and scope section that reflects their own work.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a 5-line-item budget template with labels and example rates.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — research an actual production company's rate card online and compare to their own.
