# Lab 2: Freelance Production Business Package

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 3.3 — Operations, Entrepreneurship & Media Business
**Duration:** 5 days | **Weeks:** 25–27
**Tools Needed:** Google Docs, rate sheet draft from lab

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Write a 1-page production business plan with pricing model and revenue goal
- Create a rate sheet with 5 services and 2 pricing tiers
- Draft a client contract template and a professional marketing leave-behind

---

## Background

You built the operations outline and equipment inventory in the lab. This lab-2 is the full professional package — the documents a freelance videographer would actually hand to a client.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Business Plan (Days 1–2)

Write a 1-page business plan. Required sections: company name and tagline, services offered (3+), target clients, pricing model (project-based, hourly, or retainer), monthly overhead estimate, break-even note, and a 3-month launch plan.

### Step 2 — Rate Sheet (Day 2)

List 5 specific services with 2 pricing tiers (e.g., Standard and Rush). Include a brief note on how you calculated each rate. Export as PDF.

### Step 3 — Client Contract Template (Days 2–3)

Write a contract template with these 7 sections: scope of work, deliverables, timeline, payment terms, number of revisions included, IP ownership clause, and a kill fee. Make it specific to your services — not a copied generic template.

### Step 4 — Marketing Leave-Behind (Day 4)

Create a 1-page document (Google Doc or design tool): your name/company name, 3 key services, contact info, a brief client testimonial (real or hypothetical placeholder), and a QR code or link to your portfolio. This is the document you'd hand to a prospective client.

> **Checkpoint:** Show the teacher your rate sheet before building the contract. Pricing and scope must be consistent between the two documents.

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit (Day 5)

Assemble all 4 documents and submit links. Add to portfolio as Artifact #9.
