## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

Audio is the most overlooked and most important part of video production. This unit focuses entirely on sound: recording techniques, microphone selection, basic acoustics, editing in GarageBand or Audacity, and producing a finished audio demo reel. Everything you create here will inform how you record audio during the production phase.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Set and monitor appropriate audio levels using a DAW meter
- Identify and use at least 3 microphone types (dynamic, condenser, lavalier) for different scenarios
- Record, arrange, and export a multi-track audio sequence
- Apply noise reduction, compression, and equalization to improve recordings

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Clipping on recordings:** Make gain staging the first-day lab focus. Require a screenshot of the level meter in the submission.
- **Music drowns out voice-over:** Teach sidechain compression (or manual ducking) before the music bed session.
- **Credits are missing:** Any licensed audio (even "free") requires attribution. Missing credits = checklist item not completed.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a step-by-step GarageBand/Audacity setup guide with screenshots.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — create a second version of the demo reel targeting a different use case (e.g., one for a documentary, one for advertising).

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Walk the room during recording sessions — check levels in real time. The demo reel presentation at the end of Week 10 is the verbal walkthrough checkpoint.
