# Lab: Pre-Production Package Lab

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 1.2 — Pre-Production: Scripts, Storyboards & Planning
**Duration:** Weeks 3–4

---

## Objective

Create a complete pre-production package for a 60-second concept video. Develop every planning document before any camera work begins. Present to the class for stakeholder feedback.

---

## Part 1 — Concept & Logline (Day 1, Week 3)

You will create a short 60-second concept video on a topic of your choice. It must have a clear purpose and defined audience. Start here:

1. **Topic/Subject:** ___________________________________
2. **Purpose:** Circle: *Inform / Persuade / Entertain / Promote*
3. **Target Audience:** ___________________________________
4. **Logline** (1–2 sentences — what is this video about, and why should someone watch it?):

---

## Part 2 — Script (Days 2–3, Week 3)

Write a two-column script for the full 60 seconds of your concept video.

**Format requirements:**
- Left column: Visual directions (exterior/interior, subjects on screen, transitions, graphics)
- Right column: Audio (narration or dialogue — write out full text; note music/SFX with [MUSIC] or [SFX] cues)
- Include a title card at the beginning and a credits/call-to-action at the end
- Time markers every 15 seconds

Review your script at least once before proceeding to the storyboard.

---

## Part 3 — Storyboard (Day 4–5, Week 3)

Translate your script into a storyboard (minimum 8 panels for 60 seconds).

**Each panel must include:**
- Sketched frame (stick figures okay)
- Shot type labeled (Wide Shot, Medium, Close-Up, Over-the-Shoulder, etc.)
- Brief caption connecting to the matching script line
- Camera movement note if applicable (pan, tilt, static)
