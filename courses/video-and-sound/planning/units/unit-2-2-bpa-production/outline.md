## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This is where you make the video. Using the script, storyboard, and footage from earlier units, you will edit a 2–3 minute polished short video in DaVinci Resolve. This is the project that BPA students submit for #406 Video Production.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Import, log, and organize media in a DaVinci Resolve project
- Perform a rough cut followed by a picture-lock assembly edit
- Apply color correction (primary corrections with wheels and curves)
- Mix audio tracks (dialogue, music, SFX) with appropriate levels and fades
- Export a finished video to H.264/MP4 at broadcast-ready settings

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Skipping rough cut phase:** Rough cut screening is required before color work begins. Students who skip it produce disjointed final videos.
- **Color corrections not applied to every clip:** Walk the timeline clip-by-clip during the color session.
- **Export settings wrong:** Require a screenshot of the export settings in the submission.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a step-by-step DaVinci Resolve color page tutorial card.
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in project.md — apply a custom LUT and document the creative rationale.

### Assessment Notes

- Project uses the 1–4 look-for rubric. Walk the room during the color and audio phases — these are the hardest checkpoints for students to self-assess.
