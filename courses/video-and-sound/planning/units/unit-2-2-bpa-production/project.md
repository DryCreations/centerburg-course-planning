## Overview

This is the centerpiece project of the course. Produce your official BPA competition entry from first proposal through first cut. The BPA production IS the course project — there is no separate or alternative assignment.

## Class Prompt: BPA Competition Production

### What You're Producing

Your BPA competition submission — a polished video, broadcast piece, podcast, or digital media production matching the requirements of your chosen event. The deliverable here is the rough cut reviewed in Week 18; the fine cut and final export happen in Unit 3.1.

## Standards & Rubric

Walk the room as students work. Ask students to point to evidence for each standard before scoring.

| Standard | Description | Look-For (Proficient = 3) | Beginning (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **2.9.1–2.9.4** | Project management | Proposal, team roles, schedule, and production log all submitted | 1 document only | 2–3 documents | All 4 documents present and complete | All 4 documents + WSAP self-score table |
| **7.7.5** | Principal photography | Usable footage captured and organized by scene | No footage or disorganized | Footage present; unorganized | Footage organized by scene with file naming | Organized footage + shot log matching the shot list |
| **7.7.6** | Cutaway / B-roll | B-roll shots inserted at key cuts in rough cut | No B-roll | 1–2 B-roll cuts | B-roll inserted at key transitions | B-roll + coverage list tracking A-roll moments covered |
| **7.7.7** | Jump cut elimination | Jump cuts covered by cutaways throughout | Jump cuts remain | Most covered | All jump cuts covered | No jump cuts + explanation of coverage decision |
| **7.7.8** | Continuity | Continuity maintained through production log | No continuity work | 1 issue flagged | Log tracks continuity across all scenes | Log + notes on any deliberate continuity choices |
| **7.3.7** | Transitions | Appropriate transitions used (motivated, not random) | Random wipes/effects | Cuts only | Motivated transitions match pacing and tone | Transitions + written rationale per transition type used |
| **7.3.8** | Color grading | Color correction + creative grade applied in rough cut | No color work | Correction only (white balance) | Correction + creative grade applied | Grade + reference image or look applied deliberately |
| **7.3.9** | Titles / lower thirds | Titles present and correctly formatted | No titles | Title only | All required titles present and formatted | Titles + lower thirds with name/role correctly formatted |
| **7.3.10** | Sound mix | Dialogue, music, and SFX balanced | No mixing | 1–2 tracks | All 3 layers balanced; dialogue intelligible | Full mix + production notes on levels |
| **7.3.11** | Rough cut screening | Rough cut screened and reviewed; notes documented | Not screened | Screened; no notes | Screened + reviewer notes documented | Screened + 3 specific revision actions listed |

Artifact #6. The BPA submission is the most visible production artifact in your portfolio for this year — it's the work that judges, the BAC, and future employers will see. Document the full workflow (proposal through rough cut). The process tells the professional story as much as the product does.

## Extension Task

Self-evaluate your rough cut using the complete WSAP rubric for your event. Score yourself on every criterion. Identify the 3 lowest scores and write a specific action plan for addressing each before the final cut (Unit 3.1). The most competitive teams do this before every peer review, not just once.
