# Lab 2: Field Production Report

**Course:** Video and Sound (145110)
**Unit:** 2.1 — Camera & Lighting
**Duration:** 3 days | **Weeks:** 11–12
**Tools Needed:** Camera, lighting kit, annotation table from lab, Google Docs

---

## Objective

By the end of this lab, students will be able to:

- Document camera settings and creative rationale for 10+ composition shots
- Record and compare 3 lighting setups with a structured comparison table
- Write a reflection identifying which setup decisions they would repeat on a real production

---

## Background

You already shot your composition portfolio in the lab. This lab-2 is about analyzing what you shot, documenting it professionally, and connecting the technical decisions to real production thinking.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Annotation Table (Day 1)

For each of your 10+ shots, fill in the annotation table: shot type, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, white balance setting, and 1-sentence creative rationale. Every row must be complete before moving on.

### Step 2 — Lighting Comparison Table (Day 1–2)

For your 3 lighting setups (3-point, natural, and one other), fill in a comparison table: light sources used, color temperature, mood/effect, and 1 sentence on when you'd use each in a real production.

### Step 3 — B-Roll Coverage List (Day 2)

Identify 5 of your shots that could serve as B-roll for a real video. For each, note: what scene it could cover, how long it holds before cutting, and any movement or adjustment needed to make it usable.

### Step 4 — Reflection (Day 3)

Write 3–5 sentences: Which of the 3 lighting setups best supported your story goal, and why? What's 1 setting you'd change if you reshot the exercise?

### Step 5 — Compile and Submit

Assemble annotation table, lighting comparison, B-roll list, and reflection in a Google Doc. Submit shareable link. Add to portfolio as Artifact #5.
