## Lab B — 3-Point Lighting Setup (Week 11)

Set up a 3-point lighting configuration for a seated interview subject.

### Setup Procedure

1. Place the **key light** at 45° to one side of the subject — confirm the shadow falls naturally
2. Place the **fill light** on the opposite side at a reduced intensity — soften the key shadow
3. Place the **back/rim light** behind and above the subject — check for rim separation from background
4. Set white balance to match the light source (tungsten or custom)
5. Record 30 seconds of the subject speaking
