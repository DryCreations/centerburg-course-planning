**Course:** Video & Sound Production (145110)
**Quarter:** Q2 | **Weeks:** 13–16 | **Duration:** 4 weeks

---

## Student Overview

### What Is This Unit About?

This is where pre-production becomes actual production. You'll learn to operate a camera with manual settings, set up professional lighting configurations, and record footage in the field using the storyboard and shot list you created in Unit 1.2. The footage captured here feeds directly into the BPA project in Unit 2.2.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- Set exposure (aperture, shutter speed, ISO) manually for controlled and field environments
- Set up a 3-point lighting configuration using key, fill, and rim lights
- Execute a multi-shot sequence matching a pre-approved storyboard
- Monitor audio levels while recording video using a field recorder or on-camera mic

### Common Issues & Responses

- **Students shoot everything handheld on auto:** Camera auto mode is not accepted after Week 13. Spot-check settings during field production.
- **Coverage gaps discovered in post:** Daily footage reviews prevent this — require students to check their shot list at the END of every production session.
- **Audio not monitored in the field:** Require headphones during every recording session; check at start of each production day.

### Differentiation Notes

- **Students who need more support:** Provide a manual exposure cheat card (common lighting situations + suggested starting settings).
- **Students who finish early:** Extension in lab-2.md — create a lighting diagram for a scene type not covered in class (e.g., night exterior).
