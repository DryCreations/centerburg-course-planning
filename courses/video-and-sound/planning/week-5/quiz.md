## The two questions that matter most

The ones testing **filmable action**:

> Which of these belongs in a screenplay's action line? **She checks the clock three times**

> Why does "He is furious but hiding it" not belong in a script? **A camera cannot record what someone
> feels, only what they do**

That is the week's whole lesson. If they get those two, the format details will follow.
