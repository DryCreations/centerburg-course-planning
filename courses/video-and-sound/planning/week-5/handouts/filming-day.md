# Filming Day: In the Room

**Video & Sound, Week 5, Wednesday and Thursday.** Groups shoot in eight minute slots on the schedule you
signed up for.

---

## The room rule

> **When a camera is rolling, the room is silent.** Sound carries onto their footage and cannot be removed
> later. One conversation ruins a take that somebody has to shoot again.

If you are not in the slot that is filming, you are on the **review packet**, silently.

---

## Your eight minutes

Eight minutes is short. It is enough **only** if you are set up before your slot starts.

### Before your slot (do this while the group ahead of you is shooting)

- [ ] Camera out, battery in, card in
- [ ] **Run the setup checklist:** movie mode, manual exposure, resolution and frame rate, shutter at double
      the frame rate, ISO, white balance preset, autofocus **off**
- [ ] Focus set with the magnifier on where your subject will be
- [ ] Call sheet out, shots in order
- [ ] Everyone knows their job: who holds the camera, who is on screen, who watches audio
- [ ] Props or anything you need, in hand

**Setting up during your slot is how a slot becomes four minutes.**

### During your slot

1. **Shoot ten seconds and play it back.** Even now. Especially now
2. Work your call sheet in order
3. **More takes than you think you need.** They cost nothing now and everything later
4. Do not stop to review every take. Keep moving, review at the end
5. **Record room tone.** 30 seconds, nobody talking, nobody moving

### Hand off on time

The next group is waiting. **Card out, camera back, on time.** If you did not get everything, log what you
missed and take a later slot or tomorrow's.

---

## After your slot

Immediately, at a computer:

- [ ] **Offload** everything to your project folder
- [ ] **Name every file:** `LastName_Project_Shot03_Take2.MOV`
- [ ] **Watch it.** Actually watch it, not just confirm it copied
- [ ] Update your **shot log:** done, needs a retake, still missing
- [ ] Note anything that went wrong so tomorrow's group avoids it

**The goal by end of tomorrow: your footage is on a computer, named, and ready to cut.**

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---------|---------|
| Footage is too dark | Open the aperture, then raise ISO. **Do not** slow the shutter |
| Footage is too bright | Close the aperture (higher f number), then lower ISO |
| Image keeps going soft then sharp | Autofocus is on. Switch the lens to MF |
| Brightness pulses during the take | Exposure is on auto. Switch to manual |
| Audio sounds distorted | Level is too high. Lower it and reshoot. **Clipped audio cannot be fixed** |
| Battery dies | Swap the spare. Log where you stopped |
| Card is full | Offload first. **Do not delete on the card** to make room |
| You are out of time | Log exactly what you still need and take a later slot |
