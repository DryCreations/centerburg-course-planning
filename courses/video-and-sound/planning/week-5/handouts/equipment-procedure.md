# Equipment Procedure

**Video & Sound.** The rules for touching, carrying, using, and returning a camera. These are not
suggestions, and they are not about being fussy. Every one of them exists because somebody broke something or
lost something by skipping it.

---

## Why this is the gate

**Shooting outside this room is a privilege that depends on this.**

Next week, groups are supposed to spread out into the building: hallways, the gym, the commons, outside. That
only happens for groups that can prove they handle equipment correctly **in a room where I can see them.**

If a group cannot follow a schedule they wrote themselves, and cannot put a lens cap back on, they are not
going to be trusted with a camera somewhere I am not standing. **That is not a punishment. That is the same
call any production company makes.**

---

## Checking out

**Nobody touches a camera before this is done.**

1. **Sign it out.** Your name, the body you are taking, and the time. Every time, even for eight minutes
2. **Check it in front of me:**
   - [ ] Lens attached and **not loose**
   - [ ] **Lens cap present**, either on the camera or in your hand, not on a table
   - [ ] Battery in, charged
   - [ ] Card in, with space
   - [ ] Screen and lens glass **clean and unscratched**
   - [ ] Tripod plate, if you are taking a tripod
3. **If anything is wrong, say so now.** You are not in trouble for reporting a problem you found. You are in
   trouble for handing back a problem you did not report

> **The camera is your responsibility from the moment you sign for it until the moment I sign it back in.**

---

## Carrying it

- **Strap around your neck or over your shoulder. Always.** Not in one hand, not by the lens
- **Never set a camera on the edge of anything.** Center of the table, or in the case
- **Lens cap on any time you are not actively shooting.** Walking across the room counts
- **Never leave it unattended.** Not for a second, not to grab something, not to ask a question
- **Never hand it off without both people having hands on it** and one of you saying "got it"

---

## While shooting

- **Lens cap goes in a pocket**, not on a table, not on a chair, not on the floor
- **One person is responsible for the camera** at any given moment, and everyone in the group knows who
- **No adjusting someone else's camera** without asking them first
- **Tripod: legs spread wide**, and never walk away from a camera on a tripod

---

## Returning it

**This is the part that has been going wrong.** All of it, every time, before the bell.

1. [ ] **Lens cap back on**
2. [ ] Power **off**
3. [ ] Card **offloaded**, then back in the camera
4. [ ] Battery **out** and on the charger
5. [ ] Camera **in its case**, lens down, case closed
6. [ ] Tripod **collapsed** and returned to where it lives
7. [ ] **Sign it back in**, and tell me anything that went wrong
8. [ ] **Table cleared** of caps, cards, cables, and lens cloths

> **Not done until I have seen it.** Do not leave a camera on a table and walk away. Hand it back.

---

## Packing up starts before you think it does

Returning gear correctly takes **about five minutes.** If you start when the bell is about to ring, you will
skip steps, and skipping steps is exactly what we are fixing.

**Start packing with five minutes left.** Losing a minute of shooting is cheaper than a lost lens cap or a
camera left on a desk.
