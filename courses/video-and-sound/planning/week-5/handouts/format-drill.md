# Format Drill: Fix This Scene

**Video & Sound, Week 5, Monday.** Before you write your own, prove you can spot what is wrong. Below is the
same scene written badly, then a blank page to rewrite it correctly.

Reference: `screenplay-format.md`

---

## Part 1: What is wrong with this?

Read it. **Find at least eight problems.** Number them in the margin.

```
scene 1 - the gym, nighttime, inside

the gym is empty. Dev walks in. He is nervous because he knows
he is going to fail the tryout and he has been dreading this all
week. He remembers when his brother made the team.

ZOOM IN ON DEV'S FACE. PAN LEFT TO THE HOOP.

DEV: (nervously, with a lot of worry in his voice) Okay. Okay okay okay.

CUT TO:

the gym, still nighttime

MAYA: (walking in) You know tryouts were yesterday, right?

Dev feels humiliated.

CUT TO:
```

<details>
<summary>The problems (do not open until you have listed yours)</summary>

1. **Scene heading is not formatted.** Should be `INT. GYM - NIGHT`, all caps, INT first.
2. **"He is nervous because he knows he is going to fail"** is not filmable. A camera cannot record why.
3. **"He has been dreading this all week"** is backstory, not action.
4. **"He remembers when his brother made the team"** is a memory. Not filmable.
5. **Camera directions in the script.** `ZOOM IN`, `PAN LEFT` belong on the shot list, not here.
6. **Character name is inline with dialogue.** `DEV:` should be on its own line above the dialogue.
7. **The parenthetical is too long** and does the actor's job for them. "(nervously, with a lot of worry in his voice)" should be cut entirely.
8. **Second scene heading is wrong and unnecessary.** Same place, same time, so no new scene, and no `CUT TO:` needed.
9. **"Dev feels humiliated"** is not filmable. Show it.
10. **Ends on a `CUT TO:` with nothing after it.**
11. **DEV is not capitalized on his first appearance** in the action.
12. **No character ages or descriptions** the first time they appear.

</details>

---

## Part 2: Rewrite it correctly

Same story, correct format, only what a camera can see. Aim for the same length.

Two things to decide before you start:

- What **action** shows that Dev is nervous, without saying it?
- What **action** shows that he is humiliated at the end?

```
Write your version here.




```

<details>
<summary>One correct version (there are many)</summary>

```
INT. GYM - NIGHT

The gym is empty. The overhead lights hum. DEV (16, gym clothes,
a duffel over one shoulder) stops just inside the door.

He looks at the empty court. He sets the bag down, picks it up
again, sets it down.

                    DEV
          Okay.

He takes a ball off the rack and spins it in his hands without
shooting it.

MAYA (16, jacket, keys in hand) leans in through the doorway.

                    MAYA
          You know tryouts were yesterday, right?

Dev does not turn around. He sets the ball back on the rack,
picks up his bag, and walks past her without a word.
```

**What changed:** every feeling became an action. Setting the bag down twice is nerves. Not turning around
and leaving without a word is humiliation. The camera can record both.
</details>
