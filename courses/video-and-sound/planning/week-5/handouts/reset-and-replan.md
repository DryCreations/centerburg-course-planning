# Reset: New Groups, New Plan

**Video & Sound.** Production restarts today with new groups and a new plan.

---

## Where this stands

Filming outside this room was going to start next week. **For most groups, it is not.**

The standard was simple and it was agreed in advance: follow the schedule you wrote, stay where you are
supposed to be, handle the equipment correctly, and let other people work. That did not happen, in a room
with a teacher standing in it.

**A group that cannot follow directions with supervision does not get sent somewhere without it.** That is
not a punishment and it is not personal. It is the same call any production company makes about a crew.

**This is reversible.** Groups earn it back by running a period correctly. Some groups will be shooting out
of the room today, because they already did.

---

## Your new group

Groups are assigned. They are **smaller on purpose**: pairs and trios.

In a group of two or three, there is nowhere to be a passenger. Everyone has a real job and everyone can see
whether everyone else is doing it.

## Part 2: How this class runs from here

These are not new. They are being written down so there is no confusion about what is expected.

### When you are told to work

- **The assigned tab is the only tab.** Not email, not another class's work, not a game, not a video
- **An assignment does not need to be assigned twice.** If it has been given, it is being done now
- **Quiet means quiet**, not "quiet until I have something to say"

### When someone is recording

- **Silent.** Your voice goes onto their footage and it cannot be removed
- **Stay out of frame**, which means out of the whole end of the room they are shooting in
- **Do not walk through a shot.** Wait

### With equipment

- **Lens cap back on**, every time
- **Sign it out, sign it back in**, every time
- **Start packing with five minutes left**
- **Hand it back to me.** Do not leave it on a table

### Where you are

- **You are in this room** unless I have said otherwise, to you, today
- **Not in the hallway.** Not "just getting something." Not at another teacher's door

## Part 3: Earning it back

**A group gets cleared to shoot outside this room when it has run one full period correctly.**

That means, in one period:

- [ ] Worked when told to work, without being told twice
- [ ] Stayed in the room
- [ ] Stayed quiet while others recorded
- [ ] Followed your own written schedule
- [ ] Signed equipment out and back in
- [ ] Returned everything correctly, starting five minutes early
- [ ] Finished what was assigned that day

**One period. That is the whole bar.** It is the same thing that was expected on day one.
