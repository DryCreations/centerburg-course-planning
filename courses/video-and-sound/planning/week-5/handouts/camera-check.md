# Camera Check

**Video & Sound, Week 5.** What you should have worked out while exploring the camera. Answer from memory,
then go back to a camera and confirm anything you were not sure about.

---

## Part 1: Answer these

**1.** Which mode do you put the camera in to record video?

**2.** How do you get the camera to let YOU set the shutter speed, aperture, and ISO instead of deciding for
itself?

**3.** Where do you change the resolution and frame rate?

**4.** Where is the switch that turns off autofocus, and what does it say?

**5.** How do you check focus on something small before you record?

**6.** How do you know your audio is too loud?

**7.** You just recorded something. How do you play it back on the camera?

**8.** How do you get the footage from the card onto the computer?

**9.** Which three bodies let you set exposure manually in movie mode?

**10.** Name one thing you tried that did not work, and what you figured out.

---

## Part 2: Go confirm

Pick up a camera and check anything above you guessed at. **Do not leave a question unanswered.**

Then run the whole sequence once, start to finish:

- [ ] Movie mode
- [ ] Manual exposure
- [ ] Resolution and frame rate set
- [ ] Shutter at double the frame rate
- [ ] ISO set
- [ ] White balance on a preset
- [ ] Autofocus off, focus set with the magnifier
- [ ] Record ten seconds
- [ ] Play it back on the camera
- [ ] Offload to the computer
- [ ] Watch it on the computer

**If any step stops you, that is the thing to fix today.** Not Thursday, when you are trying to shoot.

---

## Part 3: The three things that will cost you a shot

Be able to say, without looking:

1. What happens if you leave **autofocus on**?
2. What happens if you leave **exposure on auto**?
3. What shutter speed goes with **24fps**? With **30fps**?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. The camera hunts and racks focus in and out mid-shot. **Unfixable afterward.**
2. Brightness drifts mid-shot every time the light changes or someone walks past. **Unfixable afterward.**
3. **1/50** at 24fps, **1/60** at 30fps. Double the frame rate.

</details>
