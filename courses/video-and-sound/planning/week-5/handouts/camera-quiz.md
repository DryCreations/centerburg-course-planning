# Camera Settings Check

**Video & Sound, Week 5.** Ten questions. Closed note, five minutes.

---

**1.** Before recording video, exposure should be set to:

a) Manual, so brightness does not drift mid-shot
b) Auto, so the camera adjusts as you move
c) Program
d) Whatever the last person used

**2.** The 180 degree rule says to set your shutter speed to:

a) About double your frame rate
b) About half your frame rate
c) The same as your frame rate
d) As fast as the light allows

**3.** You are shooting at 24fps. Your shutter speed should be about:

a) 1/50
b) 1/24
c) 1/125
d) 1/500

**4.** You are shooting at 30fps. Your shutter speed should be about:

a) 1/60
b) 1/30
c) 1/120
d) 1/250

**5.** Autofocus is turned off for video because:

a) The camera hunts, racking focus in and out mid-shot, and that cannot be fixed later
b) It drains the battery
c) Manual focus is sharper at every distance
d) Autofocus does not work in movie mode

**6.** White balance is set to a preset rather than auto because:

a) Auto shifts between takes, so footage will not match when it is cut together
b) Presets are more accurate in every situation
c) Auto is unavailable in movie mode
d) Presets use less battery

**7.** Which of these CANNOT be fixed after the shoot?

a) Clipped audio
b) A shot that is slightly too wide
c) A file with a bad name
d) Footage in the wrong folder

**8.** Room tone is:

a) 30 seconds of a location's ambient sound with nobody talking
b) A test tone played through speakers
c) The echo added during editing
d) The hum of the camera

**9.** When a shot is too dark, the correct order is:

a) Open the aperture, then raise ISO, then add light
b) Slow the shutter, then raise ISO
c) Raise ISO as high as it goes first
d) Slow the shutter below the frame rate rule

**10.** The best check before shooting anything real is:

a) Shoot ten seconds and play it back
b) Check the battery level
c) Read the manual
d) Ask a classmate if the settings look right

---

**Answer key (teacher):** 1a, 2a, 3a, 4a, 5a, 6a, 7a, 8a, 9a, 10a

> **Shuffle the options before you post it**, or read the questions aloud and have students write a, b, c, or
> d on paper. Every correct answer is currently option a.
