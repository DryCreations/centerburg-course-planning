# Mic Station: Teacher Plan and Sign-Off

**Teacher-only.** One DJI Mic Mini kit, one T4i. Station card for students:
`handouts/mic-setup.md`.

## Before Monday

- Charge the transmitter and receiver. Fresh T4i battery, empty card
- Test the kit once yourself: the cable from the receiver into the T4i MIC jack, Sound recording on
  Manual, and the level. **The T4i has no headphone jack**, so judge by the meter and camera playback.
  Bring headphones and a laptop if you want students to listen closely on the computer
- Pick a quiet corner for the station with a chair for the talent

## Rotation

- **Groups of 2 or 3, about 8 to 10 minutes each.** Everyone else works from your feedback
- **Monday:** video production teams first, then any group that will record soon
- **Tuesday:** the groups not reached Monday, individual competitors next. Then rotate to every group
  for competition progress
- **End of class, both days:** groups that are ready to record can take the kit for real footage
- **Each student does steps 3 to 6 themselves.** Roles rotate inside the group
- If a group finishes early, have them swap who wears the mic and do it again

## Three clips for Tuesday's review

During Monday's first group, record three short clips on purpose:

1. **Good:** mic placed right, talking around -12 to -6
2. **Too hot:** record level all the way up, so it clips
3. **Too far:** the transmitter on the table across the room instead of on the talent

Tuesday's review plays them and asks the class to name the problem. Pull them onto a laptop Monday
after school.

## Sign-off

Copy the roster into a sheet with these columns. Check each box when you see the student do it.

| Student | Group | Placed the mic (7.8.4) | Plugged in, Manual, level (7.8.6) | Slated the take (7.8.7) | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Points:** sign-off only. If you enter it in the gradebook, a small completion grade (for example, 5
points) in the same category as Labs.

## Thursday recording

Only one kit, so **groups sign up for T4i time slots** on Thursday. Groups without a slot keep editing,
or shoot B-roll that does not need dialogue.
