# Bridge Scene Rubric

**Video & Sound | Due Friday, Oct 23**

Five things are graded, 4 points each. I check the first two with you during playback.

## Standards

**7.2.5** Make preliminary sketches showing placement of images and text on screen.
**7.3.7** Edit video footage.
**7.3.9** Integrate sound with visuals.
**7.7.6** Add transitions (e.g., dissolves, wipes, cuts), titles, special effects, and digital effects.
**7.2.7** Select colors based on color theory and psychology.

---

## 1. Storyboard (7.2.5), checked with you

- **4:** Every shot in your video is in the storyboard, in order, with its framing
- **2:** The storyboard is there but does not match the video
- **0:** No storyboard, or no sign of planning

## 2. Music and sound (7.3.9), checked with you

Play it with the level meter showing.

- **4:** Music fits the mood. Levels stay between −12 and −6 dB, never red. Fades in and out
- **2:** Music is there but does not fit, or the level jumps or hits red once or twice
- **0:** No music, or it clips often

## 3. The whole scene (7.3.7)

- **4:** The scene runs start to finish in an order that makes sense. Best takes, no gaps, at least one
  cut on action
- **2:** It mostly works, but has a gap, a weak take, or a confusing part
- **0:** The scene does not reach the end, or the order does not make sense

## 4. Title and credits (7.7.6)

- **4:** A title card, and credits that scroll with keyframes. Credits include the film's list, you as
  editor, and your song
- **2:** Both are there, but the credits do not scroll or are missing names
- **0:** No title card or no credits

## 5. Color (7.2.7)

- **4:** Shots from the same angle match. One mood across the whole scene. Skin still looks natural
- **2:** Some color work, but shots do not match or the mood changes partway
- **0:** No color work

---

## Total: 20 points

**Before you call me over:** storyboard out, video ready to play from the start, level meter showing.
