## CapCut check, five minutes, one lab Mac

I could not confirm which color tools are free in the current CapCut. Check these:

| Check | If it fails |
|---|---|
| **Adjust panel:** Basic, HSL, Curves, Color wheel have no crown | Drop the locked ones from the handout. Basic and HSL are enough |
| **Adjustment** in the top toolbar adds an adjustment layer | Students grade one clip and copy it to the rest |
| **Right-click a clip** to copy and paste adjustments | Students match clips by eye |
| **A free 1080p export** works with everything above | This is the one that matters. Test it before Monday Oct 19 |
| **Music library** has free tracks with no crown | Point them to a royalty-free site you already use |

**Say the crown rule often.** A Pro filter used on Tuesday shows up as a blocked export on Friday.
