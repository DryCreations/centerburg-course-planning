## The quiz stays Monday

It was promised Friday. **Moving it twice, immediately after a reset about following through, would undercut
the reset more than the scheduling convenience is worth.** The bank is ready: 62 questions, cut to 20, with
`quiz.md` carrying a suggested split.

The transitions and framing section is new, from the reading worksheet. **Students who did the worksheet have
seen every question on it**, which is the point and worth saying before they start.

## Production under the new structure

Teacher-assigned pairs and trios, each with a plan, jobs by name, and a realistic schedule.
`week-5/handouts/reset-and-replan.md` has the template and the standard.

**The standard for earning location privileges is one full period run correctly**, and the checklist is on
the handout. Hold to it exactly: worked when told without being told twice, stayed in the room, stayed quiet
while others recorded, followed their own schedule, equipment signed out and back, packed up five minutes
early, finished what was assigned.

**Clear groups out loud, by name, when they earn it.** That is what makes the standard read as a standard
rather than a mood, and it gives everyone else a visible example that it is achievable.

## What to watch this week

- **Whether pairs and trios actually fix the passenger problem.** If a two-person group still has one person
  doing nothing, the problem was not group size.
- **Whether the schedules are realistic.** A group claiming they will shoot eight shots in one period has not
  internalized the estimating lesson. Push back Monday, not Thursday.
- **Whether the worksheet got finished.** It was due Friday. Anyone who still has not done it has a bigger
  problem than the assignment.
