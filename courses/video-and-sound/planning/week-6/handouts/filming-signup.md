# Filming Sign-Ups

**Video & Sound Production (145110) | Week 6, starting Tue Sep 22**

Filming restarts today. **In this room.** You sign up for a slot, you show up ready, you shoot, you hand
the camera to the next group.

---

## Why sign-ups

There are **three cameras that shoot manual video** (T4i, T5, T6) and more groups than cameras. A slot is
how you know when the camera is yours, and it is how everyone else knows to be quiet while you have it.

The T3 does not do manual video. It is available for **stills and second angles only**.

---

## The sign-up board

Every period is split into slots. Write your group name in one box.

| Slot | Minutes | Camera A (T4i) | Camera B (T5) | Camera C (T6) |
|------|---------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | first 15 | | | |
| 2 | next 15 | | | |
| 3 | next 15 | | | |

**Rules for the board:**

- **One slot per group per day** until everyone has had one. Then seconds are open.
- Sign up for the slot you can actually be ready for. A group that signs up and is not ready loses the
  slot to whoever is.
- You may trade slots with another group. Tell me before you do, not after.
- If your shot needs a quiet room, say so when you sign up so it can go in a slot where nobody else is
  recording sound.

---

## Ready means ready

When your slot starts, the clock starts. Setup time is **your** time. Before your slot, have:

- [ ] Your **shot list** printed or on a screen, in the order you will shoot it
- [ ] Anyone who is on camera actually in the room
- [ ] Props and anything you are holding, already here
- [ ] The corner of the room you are using, cleared
- [ ] Camera settings written down so you are not looking them up: **1/60 shutter, 30fps, manual, white
      balance set**
- [ ] Someone assigned to hold the shot list and call the next shot

> **In a group of two, the person not holding the camera holds the shot list.** There is no job called
> watching.

---

## While another group is recording

This is the part that decides whether anybody films outside this room.

- **Silent.** Not quieter. Silent. A microphone picks up your conversation from across the room.
- Stay out of the frame. If you are not sure where the frame is, ask once, then stay out of it.
- No walking behind the camera during a take.
- Work on something. Shot lists, scripts, storyboards, logging footage you already have.

---

## Equipment, every time

**Signing out:**

1. Check the card is in and formatted
2. Check the battery
3. Confirm the settings with me before your first take of the day
4. Sign the log: name, camera, time out

**Signing back in:**

1. Offload your footage before you hand it back
2. Card back in the camera, lens cap on, battery on the charger if it is low
3. Sign the log: time in
4. **Five minutes before the period ends, not when it ends**

A camera that is handed back late costs the next group their slot.

---

## Footage, same day

Before you leave the period you filmed in:

- Offload to your group's folder
- Name the files so someone else could find a shot: `scene2_shot3_take1`
- Log what you got and what you still need on your shot list

Footage that is not offloaded is footage that gets overwritten.

---

## Getting cleared to shoot outside this room

Unchanged. **One full period run correctly**: worked when told without being told twice, stayed in the
room, stayed quiet while others recorded, followed your own schedule, equipment signed out and back in,
packed up starting five minutes early, finished what was assigned.

Running your filming slot well is the most direct way to show it.
