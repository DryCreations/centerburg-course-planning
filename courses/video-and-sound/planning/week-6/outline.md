### Monday's Quiz

Covers camera setup, preproduction documents, shot types, scheduling, and the transitions and framing reading.
**The reading worksheet is the study guide.**

### Earning location privileges

**One full period run correctly.** Worked when told, without being told twice. Stayed in the room. Stayed
quiet while others recorded. Followed your own schedule. Equipment signed out and signed back in. Packed up
starting five minutes early. Finished what was assigned.
