# Footage Options for the Edit Project

**Video & Sound Production | Teacher reference** | **Not for students**

All from **Cinestudy** (cinestudy.org), which publishes free practice projects: raw footage, scripts,
and in some cases music, for anyone to download and cut.

> **I could not open these pages to check their content.** Outbound fetch is blocked in the environment
> these notes were written in, so everything below comes from titles and published descriptions.
> **Preview anything before you put it in front of students**, especially the ones flagged.

---

## Recommended: best for split edits

Conversations and interviews are where J-cuts and L-cuts actually live.

| Project | URL | Why |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Hospital scene** | `cinestudy.org/2025/03/12/interactive-project-l-cuts-split-edits/` | **Built specifically to practice split edits.** Lower-resolution version available, royalty-free music included. The default pick |
| **Documentary format** | `cinestudy.org/2020/05/02/edit-challenge-documentary-format/` | Interview footage from the Mandalorian press kit (Filoni, Favreau, Pascal, Herzog). **Interviews are nothing but J-cuts and L-cuts**, and the subject matter will land with students |
| **Mockumentary** | `cinestudy.org/2020/06/05/edit-challenge-mockumentary/` | Comedic interview format. **Preview the tone before offering it** |

**The documentary one is the strongest second option.** Real interviews, recognizable people, and split
edits are the entire craft of cutting an interview.

## Practical notes

- **Download and test before class.** Put the clips in a shared folder rather than having thirty
  machines hit the site at once
- **Grab the lower-resolution versions.** UltraHD will choke the Macs and it buys nothing here
- Several projects host footage on Vimeo or YouTube, so the download path varies by project. **Check it
  for whichever ones you actually offer**
- **Offering two or three options is plenty.** Choice is motivating; a menu of ten is paralysis, and
  every extra option is another download for you to stage

---

## Why choice is worth the setup

Students who pick their own footage **argue about their edit**, and the comparison at the end is richer
when two people cut different scenes and have to explain their reasoning to each other rather than just
pointing at where the cuts landed.

**But the default matters.** Say out loud that the hospital scene is the easiest path to a good grade,
because it was built for exactly this. That way choosing something else is a deliberate decision rather
than an accident.
