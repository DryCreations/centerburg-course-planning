# Video and Sound — CTAG Alignment

**Secondary Course Code:** 145110
**CTAG Course:** CTIM006 — Digital Video Production
**Credit Value:** 3 Semester Hours
**CTAG Eligibility Status:** Eligible ✅

---

## Eligibility Overview

CTIM006 credit is awarded to students who:

1. Successfully complete DEW secondary course **Video and Sound (145110)** and earn a qualifying score on the corresponding End-of-Course (WebXam) exam.
2. Matriculate to an Ohio institution of higher education with an approved or comparable program **within 3 years** of completing the approved secondary course.
3. Grant permission for credit posting — either through the end-of-course WebXam or directly with the attending institution.

### Secondary Institutional Approval

- Secondary programs must have **pathway approval from the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW)**
- Certificate of Affirmation assurances are incorporated into the **CTE-26 application process**
- This course is approved under the Interactive Media (N1) pathway and also serves Networking (N2), Programming (N3), and Information Support & Services (N0)

### Coverage Notes

- **7.7.1 (Equipment identification):** The standard lists drones, stop action, DSLR, mirrorless, compact, and 360 cameras. Equipment orientation in Q1 should name and distinguish all listed types even if only DSLR/mirrorless are available in the lab. Students should be able to identify formats and their professional use cases for the WebXam.
- **7.7.4 (Format selection):** Both the camera format (codec, resolution, frame rate) and the *decision-making process* for format selection need to be taught. Add a brief lesson: "When do you shoot 4K vs. 1080p? 24fps vs. 60fps? Why?" — these are WebXam-tested concepts.
- **7.7.5 (Editing system selection):** Linear vs. nonlinear editing is a core concept. Students should understand what NLE software is and be able to name the software they are using (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, etc.) and why it was selected.
- **7.7.7 / 7.7.8 (Audio + Export):** Export and soundtrack work are split across units. Ensure both appear in Q2 production projects — not only at the end of Q3/Q4 — so students have documented evidence of these skills.
- **7.3.6 (Incorporate footage):** Import workflows (log and capture, cloud transfer, file organization) should be explicitly taught rather than assumed. A short exercise on media management (naming conventions, folder structure, proxy workflow) aligns here.

## Articulation — Eligible Credit Path

Students who complete **Video and Sound (145110)** with a qualifying WebXam score may receive **3 semester hours** of postsecondary credit (CTIM006) at:

- Any Ohio public college or university with an approved Interactive Media, Digital Media Production, or comparable program
- Credit applies toward technical requirements in the specified area; institutions without a direct equivalent are encouraged to apply equivalent credit toward relevant program requirements
- Application window: **within 3 years** of secondary course completion
