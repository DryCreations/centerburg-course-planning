# Choosing Your BPA Event

**Video & Sound | Due Thursday**

## Standards

**2.9.2** Determine the scope and purpose of the project.
**2.9.5** Develop a timeline, a communication plan, a task breakdown, costs (e.g., equipment, labor),
deliverables, and responsibilities for completion.

---

## The options (2.9.2)

**Teams of 2 or 3:**

- **Video Production Team:** a 3 to 5 minute video on this year's topic. The main fit for this class
- **Broadcast News Team:** a short newscast with separate stories
- **Podcast Team:** a podcast on this year's topic. Audio only

**Alone, or a team:**

- **Digital Media Production:** a 1 to 2 minute video or animation on a set topic
- **Multimedia and Promotion:** a short digital promotion

**Optional second event:** **Promotional Photography**, photos shot and edited to this year's prompt.

Read each event's guidelines in the WSAP before you choose. **Names, lengths, and topics change every
year, and the WSAP is what counts.**

**1.** Which event is your first choice? Which is your backup?

**2.** Alone or on a team? If a team, who?

**3.** Are you also entering Promotional Photography? Yes or no.

**4.** What is this year's topic or prompt for your event, in your own words?

## Your plan (2.9.5)

**5.** What are the deadlines for your event? Find them in the WSAP.

**6.** What will your team have finished by the end of Week 4?

---

## Graded on

All six answered. Question 6 counts the most: a plan you can actually check.
