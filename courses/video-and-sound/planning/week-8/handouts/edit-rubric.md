# Edit Project Rubric

**Video & Sound | Due Friday**

**Five things are graded.** Three I check with you in person during playback. Two I check after you
upload.

---

## 1. Storyboard *(checked in person)*

**Have it out on your desk.** I compare it to your video while it plays.

| Level | Points | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4 | Storyboard is there, and **every scene and cut** in your video is in it, with the **framing** your storyboard shows |
| **Partial** | 2 | Storyboard is there, but it **does not match the video exactly** |
| **Missing** | 0 | No storyboard, it **does not match the video at all**, or it shows **no sign of planning ahead** |

---

## 2. Split edits *(checked in person)*

**Point them out to me on your timeline** during playback.

| Level | Points | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4 | **Two** split edits (J-cut or L-cut) that you can point to, and both play smoothly |
| **Partial** | 2 | **One** split edit, or two where one does not actually work when played |
| **Missing** | 0 | No split edits, or you cannot find them on your timeline |

---

## 3. Sound balance *(checked in person)*

**Play it with the audio on and the level meter showing.** I watch the meter while you play.

| Level | Points | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4 | Speech stays at a **steady level**, peaking around **−12 to −6 dB**. **Never hits 0 dB** (red). No volume jumps between clips. Music or background sits under the voices, not over them |
| **Partial** | 2 | **Mostly even**, with one or two jumps between clips or a brief spike into the red |
| **Missing** | 0 | Frequent jumps, clipping into the red, or speech you cannot hear over other sound |

---

## 4. The whole story *(checked after you upload)*

**Depends on which project you picked.**

| Level | Points | Documentary | Scripted scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4 | At least **one interview that cuts away to B-roll partway through, then comes back** to the person speaking | **Every line in the script** is in your edit, in order |
| **Partial** | 2 | B-roll is used, but never in the **middle** of an interview | **One or two lines** are missing |
| **Missing** | 0 | Interviews only, no B-roll | **Several lines** missing, or the scene does not reach the end |

---

## 5. Title card and credits *(checked after you upload)*

| Level | Points | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 4 | A **title card** at the start, and **credits that scroll up the screen** at the end, animated with keyframes. Credits are complete (see below) |
| **Partial** | 2 | Both are there, but the credits **do not scroll**, or names are missing |
| **Missing** | 0 | No title card, or no credits |

### What your credits need

**Documentary:** short is fine. **Who was interviewed**, **you as the editor**, and **any other names you
can find** in the footage.

**Scripted scene:** the credits are **already written for you.** Go back to the page where you downloaded
the footage, copy the **block of credits text**, paste it in, and **put your name in the blank for
editor.**

---

## Total: 20 points

**Before you call me over:** storyboard out, video ready to play from the start, audio on, and the level
meter where I can see it.
