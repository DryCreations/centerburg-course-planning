## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Detach a clip's audio from its picture and move each independently
- Build a J-cut and an L-cut, and say what each one is for
- Explain why **top covers bottom** on a video timeline
- Hide a bad cut with a cutaway on an upper track
- Say why a cutaway's own audio has to be muted
- Balance levels once there is more than one audio track

## Due Friday

- Your edit, exported at 1080p
- A log of your split edits: timecode, J or L, and how much offset
- Your storyboard, photographed

**If you get there:** a second version of the same footage with different decisions, and three sentences
on which is better and why.
