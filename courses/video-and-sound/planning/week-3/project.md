# Week 3 Project — RAW Capture & First Edit

**Course:** Video and Sound | **Assigned:** Mon Aug 31 | **Due:** Fri Sep 4
**Category:** Labs/Projects | **Tools:** DSLR (RAW) + Adobe Camera Raw (through Photoshop) | **Late:** accepted, ask to reopen

Shoot in **RAW**, then make a controlled **first edit** in Adobe Camera Raw. The goal is control, not heavy
effects: get the white balance right, the exposure right, and a clean crop.

---

## Part A — Shoot RAW (Tue)

Set the camera to **RAW** (or RAW+JPEG). Shoot a small set of the same subject:

| # | Shot |
|---|------|
| 1 | The subject in **window/daylight** |
| 2 | The subject in **indoor/artificial light** (warmer or greener) |
| 3 | One frame with the **wrong white balance on purpose** (e.g., "tungsten" setting outdoors) to fix later |
| 4 | Your best composed frame of the subject |

Offload all RAW files to Drive, named `lastname-raw-01` … `-04`.

## Part B — Edit one photo in Adobe Camera Raw (Wed–Thu)

Open your best RAW frame in **Adobe Camera Raw** (it opens through Photoshop when you open a RAW file) and edit
it **non-destructively**:

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | **White balance corrected** so whites/skin look natural (use the eyedropper or the temp/tint sliders) |
| 2 | **Exposure** set so it isn't too dark or blown out |
| 3 | A deliberate **crop** that improves the composition |
| 4 | A small **contrast/tone** adjustment (highlights/shadows) — subtle, not extreme |
| 5 | **Export** the edited photo as a JPG, and export the untouched original as a JPG for comparison |

---

## Turn in

- [ ] Four RAW captures offloaded and named
- [ ] **Before** (original JPG) and **after** (edited JPG), side by side or as two files
- [ ] One or two sentences: what did the white balance and crop fix?
