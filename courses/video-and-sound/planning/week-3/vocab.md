## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Exposure** | How bright the image is. Controlled by aperture, shutter speed, and ISO. |
| **Aperture (f-number)** | How wide the lens opens. Low f-number = brighter image and a blurrier background. |
| **Shutter speed** | How long the sensor is exposed. Fast freezes motion; slow blurs it. |
| **ISO** | The sensor's sensitivity. Higher brightens a dark shot but adds grain (noise). |
| **Depth of field** | How much of the shot is in focus front to back. Shallow = soft background. |
| **Aperture priority (Av)** | You set the aperture; the camera sets the shutter speed. |
| **Shutter priority (Tv)** | You set the shutter speed; the camera sets the aperture. |
| **Manual (M)** | You set all three settings yourself. |
| **White balance** | Telling the camera the color of the light so white looks white. |
| **Shot size** | How much of the subject is in frame: wide, medium, close-up. |
| **Over-the-shoulder** | A shot from behind one person looking toward another. |
| **Composition** | How elements are arranged in the frame. |
| **Rule of thirds** | Placing the subject off-center on a 3×3 grid for a stronger frame. |
| **Headroom** | The space between the top of a subject's head and the top of the frame. |
| **Pre-production / production / post** | Planning / shooting / editing — the three phases of a project. |
| **Offload** | Copying files off the memory card to Drive. The card is not storage. |

## Key Distinctions

| Term 1 | Term 2 | Difference |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Av | Tv | Av = you pick aperture; Tv = you pick shutter speed |
| Fast shutter | Slow shutter | Fast freezes motion (darker); slow blurs motion (brighter) |
| Wide shot | Close-up | Wide shows the whole scene; close-up isolates one detail |
| Shallow DoF | Deep DoF | Shallow = soft background (low f-number); deep = mostly sharp (high f-number) |
