# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Video & Sound · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = finish the edit, export and submit a before/after, then a
**Gimkit review game** before Friday's quiz. Each block is one prompt to paste into Google Slides AI. Deck
order: As You Come In, Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now, Gimkit, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school photography class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Open
> your photo in Adobe Camera Raw (through Photoshop), or open your edited JPG from yesterday. Get your edit back
> on screen." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

> Create one standards and agenda slide titled "Today: Finish and Submit Your Edit." List these Ohio Interactive
> Media standards with full text: 7.3.5 Prepare and integrate photographic images and special effects with
> graphic images; 7.3.7 Edit video footage (applied to editing a photo); 7.7.4 Select a video recording format
> and shoot the video (we shot RAW); 2.10.7 Backup, restore, test, archive, and manage data (naming and
> submitting files). Then a short agenda: 1) Finish your edit, 2) Export before and after as JPGs, 3) Submit
> both to Classroom, 4) Gimkit review for tomorrow's quiz. Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Finish the edit

> Create one slide titled "Finish Your Edit." Checklist in Adobe Camera Raw: white balance corrected; exposure
> right; a sensible crop; a small contrast or tone adjustment (subtle, not extreme). Remind: editing is
> non-destructive, your original is safe. One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Export and name

> Create one slide titled "Export and Name Your Files." Steps: 1) Export your edited photo as a JPG. 2) Export
> the untouched original as a JPG. 3) Rename both: lastname-what-it-is.jpg, for example smith-daylight-edited.jpg
> and smith-daylight-original.jpg. One slide.

### Slide 5: Do Now (breakout to work)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school photography class. Big title: "Do Now: Finish, Export, Submit."
> Body: "Work on this now: finish your edit (white balance, exposure, crop), export your before and after as
> renamed JPGs, and submit both directly to the Google Classroom assignment. Add one sentence on what you fixed.
> Goal for today: a clean before/after turned in." Large, clean. One slide.

### Slide 6: Gimkit review

> Create one slide titled "Gimkit Review." Body: "Join the Gimkit review game: [paste your Gimkit join link or
> code here]. We are reviewing RAW, white balance, and editing terms for tomorrow's quiz." Big join-code space in
> the middle. One slide.

### Slide 7: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your before/after is submitted in Classroom.
> Tomorrow: the cumulative quiz." One slide, large text.
