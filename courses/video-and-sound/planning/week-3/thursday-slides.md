# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Video & Sound · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = review how to submit (from yesterday), then **Gimkit** where
students write their own review questions before playing. One prompt per slide for Google Slides AI. Order: As
You Come In, Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now (Gimkit questions), Play, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school photography class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Open
> Google Classroom and check your photo submission from yesterday. Is your before AND after there, both renamed?
> If something did not upload, get it ready to fix." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

> Create one standards and agenda slide titled "Today: Submissions and Gimkit." List these Ohio Interactive Media
> standards with full text: 7.3.5 Prepare and integrate photographic images and special effects with graphic
> images; 7.3.7 Edit video footage (applied to editing a photo); 2.10.7 Backup, restore, test, archive, and
> manage data (naming and submitting files). Then a short agenda: 1) Review how to submit correctly, 2) Fix any
> missing submissions, 3) Gimkit review (you write the questions). Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Submission check

> Create one slide titled "Submission Check." Checklist: both files submitted (original JPG and edited JPG); both
> renamed lastname-what-it-is.jpg; files attached directly to the Classroom assignment (not a link, not in a
> folder); one sentence on what you fixed; assignment turned in. Note: submitting correctly is part of the grade.
> One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Do Now (Gimkit, write your questions)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school photography class titled "Do Now: Add Gimkit Questions." Left side,
> the task: "Go to the Gimkit link on Google Classroom and use the tool to ADD YOUR OWN review questions. I will
> review them and add the good ones. You may also add a few school-appropriate pop culture questions if you
> want." Right side, a boxed "What to write questions about" with this week's terms as examples: RAW, JPEG, white
> balance, color temperature, Adobe Camera Raw, non-destructive editing, histogram, exposure, highlights and
> shadows, crop. Add: "Not sure? Check this week's vocabulary posted in Google Classroom." One slide, clear
> two-column layout.

### Slide 5: Play the game

> Create one slide titled "Now Play." Body: "Questions in? Now go to THIS link to join and play the Gimkit game:
> [paste your Gimkit play/join link or code here]." Big join-code space in the middle. One slide.

### Slide 6: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your before/after is submitted correctly. Tomorrow:
> the cumulative quiz." One slide, large text.
