# Yearbook

## Overview

The Yearbook course is a non-CTE elective course where students plan, design, produce, and distribute the school yearbook. This course integrates many of the same skills taught in CTE digital media courses (photography, graphic design, layout, writing) in an applied, deadline-driven context.

## Course Planning Areas

- Yearbook theme development
- Photography (events, portraits, candids)
- Page layout and design
- Copywriting and captions
- Advertising sales
- Distribution and marketing
- Deadline management
- Collaboration with school administration
