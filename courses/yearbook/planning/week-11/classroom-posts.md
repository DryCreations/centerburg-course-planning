# Yearbook Q2: Classroom Turn-In Posts

**Teacher-only.** One block per graded assignment. The first line is the title, and the paragraph is
the description. Written broadly so it works for anyone, whatever their specific assignment. Set points,
categories, and due dates in Classroom.

---

```
Fall Spread

Turn in your finished fall spread here. Check your assignment list for which one is yours. Attach a PDF or screenshot from eDesign showing every photo placed, the headline and captions done, and our theme applied, plus one or two sentences on what you changed after your reviews.
```

```
Winter Spread

Turn in your winter spread layout here. Check your assignment list for which one is yours. Attach a PDF or screenshot from eDesign showing the full layout, the photos you have placed so far, and a short list of any photos still missing and who is shooting them.
```

```
Assigned Shoot

Turn in the photos from your assigned shoot here, within 3 school days of the event. Attach your 15 best usable photos, named LastName_Event_01 and so on, and make sure they are also uploaded to eDesign. Add the names of at least three people in your photos so we can write captions.
```

```
Extra Credit Shoot

Shot something that was not your assignment? Turn it in here for extra credit. Attach 15 usable photos, named LastName_Event_01 and so on, uploaded to eDesign too, and tell me which part of the ladder they are for.
```

```
Photo Quality: Best 10

Turn in your 10 best photos from this quarter here. For each one, write a sentence on why it works: the focus, the light, the timing, the framing, or the moment you caught.
```

```
Theme on Your Fall Spread

Show me how your fall spread fits our theme. Attach a screenshot of your spread, and tell me which theme fonts, colors, and repeating elements you used and where.
```

```
Spread Review

Turn in your review here. Check your assignment list for which spread is yours to review. Leave comments on the spread in eDesign, then write here what you checked: empty photo boxes, names spelled right, the theme, anything touching the edge or the fold, and the one change that would help it most.
```

```
Final Edit

Turn in your final edit here. Check your assignment list for which spread is yours to edit. List every change you made or asked the designer to make, including the names you checked, the captions and headlines you proofread, and any theme or layout fixes.
```

```
Check-In

Every other Friday, tell me what you got done in the last two weeks. List the assignments you finished, how many photos you uploaded and for what, attach a screenshot of your spread, and tell me what is in your way and what you will finish in the next two weeks.
```

```
Photo Batch

If you were assigned photo tasks in place of a shoot, turn them in here. Tell me which 30 photos you worked on and what you did to them: edited, tagged and named in eDesign, or collected from a coach, advisor, or parent.
```
