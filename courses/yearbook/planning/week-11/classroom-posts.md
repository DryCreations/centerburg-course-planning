# Yearbook Q2: Classroom Assignment Posts

**Teacher-only.** One block per graded assignment. **Copy everything inside a block** into the Google
Classroom assignment: the first line is the title, the rest is the description. Plain text, so it pastes
cleanly.

**Set points and category in Classroom to match.** Points and categories come from
`program/q2-gradebook.md`. Replace every `[DATE]`.

## Production and Deadlines (40%)

```
Fall Spread: Finished in the Theme

50 points | Production and Deadlines | Due [DATE]

Your fall spread is listed next to your name on the Sections sheet.

WHAT TO DO
1. Place every photo the spread needs. Missing a photo? Ask the coach, advisor, or student who has it.
2. Apply the theme, Seasons of Our Lives: use the theme fonts, colors, and repeating element.
3. Add a headline, captions with names spelled right, and any quotes.
4. Fix everything your reviewers pointed out.

TURN IN
- A PDF or screenshot of the finished spread from eDesign, attached here.
- A short note: what you changed after your reviews.

GRADED ON (50)
- Every photo placed, nothing empty: 15
- Headline and captions, names checked: 15
- Fixes from your reviews made: 10
- Turned in by the due date: 10
```

```
Winter Spread: Laid Out

50 points | Production and Deadlines | Due [DATE]

Your winter spread is listed next to your name on the Sections sheet.

WHAT TO DO
1. Build the layout in eDesign: photo boxes, headline, caption spaces.
2. Place the winter photos that are in. Mark any box still waiting on a photo.
3. Use the theme fonts, colors, and repeating element from the start.

TURN IN

GRADED ON (50)
- Full layout built, nothing left blank without a note: 20

- Theme used: 5
- Turned in by the due date: 10

## Coverage (25%)

```
Shoot [NUMBER]: [SECTION NAME]

25 points | Coverage | Due within 3 school days of the event, by [DATE]

You are the [MAIN PHOTOGRAPHER / BACKUP] for this section. Check the Sections sheet.

WHAT TO DO
1. Sign out a camera. Go to the event or place.
2. Shoot a mix: wide shots, close shots, action, and faces.
3. Pick your best 15. Delete the blurry and the repeats.
4. Name each photo LastName_Event_01, LastName_Event_02, and so on.
5. Upload to eDesign AND attach here.

TURN IN
- 15 usable photos, named, in eDesign and here.
- The names of at least 3 people in your photos, for captions.

GRADED ON (25)
- 15 usable photos: in focus, well exposed, not repeats: 15
- Named and uploaded to both places: 5
- Names for captions: 5

If the main photographer has not uploaded within 3 school days, the backup's photos are used and the
backup gets the credit.
```

```

TURN IN

This does not replace your assigned shoots.

## Craft (20%)

```
Photo Quality: Your Best 10

30 points | Craft | Due [DATE]

Choose your 10 best photos from this quarter.

TURN IN
- The 10 photos, attached here.
- One sentence for each: why it works. Focus, light, timing, framing, or a real moment.

GRADED ON (30)
- In focus and well exposed: 10
- Strong framing and composition: 10
- Your sentences explain real choices: 10
```

```
Theme on Your Fall Spread

30 points | Craft | Due [DATE]

Your fall spread should look like it belongs in the same book as every other page.

TURN IN
- A screenshot of your spread next to one other fall spread.
- Answer: which theme fonts, colors, and repeating element did you use, and where?

GRADED ON (30)
- Theme fonts used: 10
- Theme colors used: 10
- The repeating element used: 10
```

```
Spread Review [1 or 2]

10 points | Craft | Due [DATE]

Your review assignments are on the Sections sheet.

Open the spread in eDesign. Leave your feedback as comments, then answer here.

1. Does any photo box sit empty?
2. Is every name in the captions spelled right? Check at least three.
3. Does the spread use the theme fonts, colors, and repeating element?
4. Does anything touch the edge or fall in the gutter (the fold)?
5. What is the one change that would help this spread most?

GRADED ON (10)
- All five answered with specifics, not "looks good": 6
- Comments left on the spread in eDesign: 4
```

```
Final Edit

20 points | Craft | Due [DATE]

Your editing assignment is on the Sections sheet. This is the last check before the spread is locked.

WHAT TO DO
1. Check every name against the roster or the team list.
2. Read every caption and headline out loud. Fix spelling and grammar.
3. Check the theme: fonts, colors, repeating element.
4. Check nothing touches the edge or falls in the gutter.
5. Fix what you can. Comment on what the designer must fix.

TURN IN
- A list of every change you made or asked for.

GRADED ON (20)
- Every name checked: 8
- Captions and headlines proofread: 6
- Theme and layout checked: 6
```
