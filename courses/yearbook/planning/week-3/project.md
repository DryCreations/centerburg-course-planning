# Week 3 Assignment — Frames-First Spread Layout (Friday PDF Check-In)

**Course:** Yearbook | **Assigned:** Mon Aug 31 | **Graded check-in due:** Fri Sep 4 (submit a PDF)
**Tool:** eDesign | **Category:** Production & Deadlines / Craft

Build the **layout** of a real spread using **placeholder frames** — the photo boxes, headline, and captions —
so the page is designed and ready before your photos come in. Some of you have a few photos to place now; most
will design with empty frames. **By Friday you turn in a PDF** of what you built (see "What you submit" below).
The layout keeps going after this week; Friday is a graded snapshot of where it is.

---

## Requirements (all required)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | A spread **claimed on the seasonal ladder** (which pages, which subject) |
| 2 | **One dominant photo frame** — clearly the biggest, the eye lands here first |
| 3 | **2–4 supporting photo frames**, varied in size (not all the same box) |
| 4 | A **headline** area |
| 5 | **Caption space** for each photo frame (room to name people and say what's happening) |
| 6 | Frames **aligned to columns**; consistent gutters/margins |
| 7 | **Nothing important crosses the gutter** (no faces in the center seam) |
| 8 | Any photos you already have, **dropped into frames** (bonus, not required) |
| 9 | The page **checked in** in eDesign so others can work |

---

## What you submit (choose one)

By Friday, export a **PDF** and turn it in on Classroom. Your PDF can be **a full two-page spread OR two
different pages.** Pick whichever fits where you are:

1. **A finished / edited spread** — a real spread with photos placed, captions written, headline set. Could be
   for the new book or a redone page from an old book.
2. **A from-scratch layout** — a spread you designed with **frames/templates** for the new *or* old yearbook,
   even if the photo boxes are still empty.
3. **A recreated finished page from an old yearbook** — rebuild an existing page as practice, fully laid out.
4. **A template layout + a plan** — a page you laid out with templates **plus a short written description**:
   what the page/spread will be, and **what photos you're looking for** (your photo wishlist).

> **If your photo boxes are still empty, option 4 is the move:** the layout plus the description and photo
> wishlist is a complete, gradable submission. You do **not** need finished photos to get full credit.

---

## Include with your PDF

- **A one-line label:** what spread/page this is (subject + which pages on the ladder).
- **The plan + photo wishlist** (for options 2 and 4): 2–4 sentences on what the page will be and a short list
  of the photos you still need. For a finished spread (options 1 and 3), this is your **captions** instead.
- **Reviewer name:** who checked your page.

---

## Grading (100 pts — weekly check-in)

| Criterion | Full credit means | Pts |
|-----------|-------------------|----:|
| **Submitted a full spread / two pages as a PDF** | A PDF is turned in on time, exported cleanly, one spread or two pages | 40 |
| **The layout is real** | An actual designed layout — dominant frame, supporting frames, headline, caption space — not a blank page or a single placeholder box | 25 |
| **Plan + photo wishlist** (or complete captions on a finished spread) | Clear one-line label plus the description + photo list, or written captions if photos are placed | 20 |
| **Reviewed by a partner** | A classmate reviewed the page and is named | 15 |

**Grading style:** this is a **weekly production check-in** — lighter and completion-leaning. The point is a real
spread turned in every week, not a polished final. Craft still counts (the "layout is real" band), but a solid
frames-and-plan submission earns full marks.

---

## What "good" looks like

- A stranger can tell **which photo matters most** before any photo is even placed.
- The frames feel organized (aligned, varied sizes), not a grid of identical boxes.
- There is room for captions; the gutter is respected.
- If photos aren't in yet, the **plan + wishlist** makes it obvious what the finished page becomes.

## Extension (ahead of the pack)

- Build a **second layout option** for the same spread and compare which is stronger.
- Start a **caption draft** for any placed photos (names correct, what's happening, one detail).
- Help a beat-mate frame their spread, or pull placeholder frames onto the next spread in your section.
