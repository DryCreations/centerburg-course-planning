**Course:** Yearbook

## What this week covers (present this today)

**Spread layout with frames.** How a two-page spread is built: a **dominant photo**, a few supporting photos,
a headline, captions, and clean columns across the gutter — all placed as **frames (placeholders)** first, so
the page has a strong structure before a single photo is dropped in.

## Weeks 1–2 recap

eDesign basics + old-book review, the 96-page ladder, beats/roles, the theme (**Seasons of Our Lives**, organized
by season), camera modes, and weekend camera sign-outs.

## Teacher Reference

### Pacing Guide

| Day | Activity | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Mon | Show 2–3 strong spreads from an old book and name the parts: **dominant photo, supporting photos, headline, captions, gutter, columns.** Assign the frames-first spread (`project.md`); students claim a spread from the seasonal ladder and start placing **empty frames**. | The point: design the structure first, photos later. |
| Tue | Build the frame layout. Push a **clear dominant photo** (one box noticeably bigger) and a sensible number of supporting boxes. Add the headline box and caption boxes. | Reuse the ladder assignments; this is real page work. |
| Wed | Columns and alignment: frames snap to a column grid; nothing crosses the gutter carelessly; leave caption room. Students with photos on hand drop them into frames. | Placeholder frames = the majority; photos are a bonus this week. |
| Thu | **Reviewer on every page** (the standing habit): a partner checks the layout reads clearly, captions have room, and no face sits in the gutter. Refine. | Being reviewed is normal, not a judgment. |
| Fri | **Weekly status** (finished / stuck / due next) + deadline check. | Yearbook's Friday routine, in place of a quiz. |

### Assessment Notes

- **No quiz.** Graded rhythm is production/deadlines, coverage, craft, and staff contribution (the weekly
  status counts).
- Spread evidence: a spread claimed on the ladder with a **frame layout** — one dominant photo box, supporting
  boxes, headline, captions, aligned to columns, gutter respected — ready for photos.
