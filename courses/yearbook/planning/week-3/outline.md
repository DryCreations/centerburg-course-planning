## Weeks 1–2 recap (what actually ran)

## Student Overview

### What Is This Week About?

You have a plan; now you make pages. Each of you takes real spreads from the ladder, covers events happening
this week, and starts the Friday habit of reporting what you finished, what is stuck, and what is due next.

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Own and build a real spread in eDesign, start to finish
- Cover an actual event and get usable, named photos in on time
- Give and take page reviews (names, captions, no faces in the gutter)
- Report your status every Friday

## Teacher Reference

### Pacing Guide

| Day | Activity | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Mon | Assign real spreads from the ladder by beat/role. Post the **first internal deadlines** and this week's coverage board. Confirm beat/role assignments are final and posted. | If assignments aren't final yet, close them this morning. |
| Tue | eDesign build time. Beat owners schedule coverage for events this week; camera sign-outs confirmed. | Editors/Design Lead circulate on consistency. |
| Wed | Coverage day: shoot or collect; offload, name, file; draft captions with correct names. | Names right = the standing rule. |
| Thu | **Reviewer on every page**: no spread is "done" until someone else checks names, captions, and the gutter. Legacy Lead keeps closing last year's pages. | Make being reviewed normal. |
| Fri | **Weekly status** (what you finished / what's stuck / what's due next) + deadline check. First real coverage of the year should be logged. | This is the recurring Friday routine — Yearbook's Friday, in place of a quiz. |

### Assessment Notes

- Yearbook has **no WebXam and no Friday quiz.** The graded rhythm is production and deadlines, coverage,
  craft, and staff contribution (the weekly status counts here).
- Spread evidence: a real spread in progress on the ladder with placed photos, drafted captions with correct
  names, and a named reviewer.

### Open items feeding this week

- **First internal deadline date** depends on the Varsity publisher calendar (question logged in
  `program/open-questions.md`).
- Confirm every student has a beat, a role, at least one event to cover, and their first deadline in hand.
