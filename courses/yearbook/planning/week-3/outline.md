# Week 3 — Laying Out a Spread (Frames First): Outline

**Course:** Yearbook
**Week:** 3 | **Dates:** Mon Aug 31 – Fri Sep 4 | **Duration:** 5 days

> **Design the page before the photos arrive.** Most of you don't have your photos yet, so this week you build
> a real spread **layout using placeholder frames** — the photo boxes, headline, and caption boxes — so the
> design is ready and you just drop photos in as they come. Some of you have a few photos to place now.
> **No quiz in Yearbook.**

---

## What This Week Covers

**Spread layout with frames.** How a two-page spread is built: a **dominant photo**, a few supporting photos,
a headline, captions, and clean columns across the gutter — all placed as **frames (placeholders)** first, so
the page has a strong structure before a single photo is dropped in.

## Weeks 1–2 recap

eDesign basics + old-book review, the 96-page ladder, beats/roles, the theme (**Seasons of Our Lives**, organized
by season), camera modes, and weekend camera sign-outs.

## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Claim a real spread from the seasonal ladder
- Build a spread **layout with frames** — dominant photo, supporting photos, headline, captions, columns
- Keep faces out of the gutter and align to a column grid
- Drop photos into the frames as they arrive, without redesigning
