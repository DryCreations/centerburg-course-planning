# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Yearbook · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = reviewer on every page: a partner checks each layout, then refine and
get ready for tomorrow's PDF check-in. (No quiz. Optional Gimkit is Friday.) Each block is one prompt to paste
into Google Slides AI. Deck order: As You Come In, Focus/Summary, Content, Do Now, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school yearbook class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Open
> your spread or page in eDesign and pull up your Page Checklist. Have it on screen and ready to improve." Large,
> clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Focus + Today

> Create one focus and agenda slide for a yearbook class titled "Today: Reviewer on Every Page." Text: Today's
> focus is CRAFT and PRODUCTION: every page gets a partner review, then you refine. There is no state exam in
> this class; your work is graded on production, coverage, craft, and staff contribution. Agenda: 1) Partner
> reviews your page, 2) You refine what they flag, 3) Get ready for tomorrow's PDF check-in. Large readable text,
> one slide.

### Slide 3: What a reviewer checks

> Create one slide titled "What a Reviewer Checks." Checklist a partner uses on your page: is there a clear
> dominant photo (focal point)? does every photo have caption room? is anything important crossing the gutter
> (faces in the seam)? is everything aligned to the columns? does each text box use the right preset (caption,
> byline)? One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Do Now (breakout to work)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school yearbook class. Big title: "Do Now: Get Reviewed and Refine."
> Body: "Work on this now: get a partner to review your page with the checklist, then fix what they flag. Make
> sure every photo has a caption box, your presets are set, and nothing important crosses the gutter. Goal: your
> page is ready for tomorrow's PDF check-in." Large, clean. One slide.

### Slide 5: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your page is reviewed and refined. Tomorrow: submit
> your spread as a PDF (old or new book), and an optional Gimkit review for fun. Sign your page with a 'design
> by' credit." One slide, large text.
