# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Yearbook · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = build against the posted assignment's criteria, use the preset styles
for consistency, leave room for photo credits and captions, and explore design elements beyond photo frames.
(No quiz. Optional Gimkit is Friday, PDF check-in is Friday.) One prompt per slide for Google Slides AI. Order:
As You Come In, Focus/Summary, Content, Do Now, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school yearbook class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Open
> your spread or page in eDesign AND open the assignment posted in Google Classroom. Read the criteria so what
> you build today actually meets it and you can submit it." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Focus + Today

> Create one focus and agenda slide for a yearbook class titled "Today: Consistency and Craft." Text: Today we
> make the book CONSISTENT and leave room for everything a page needs. Focus: use the preset text styles (not
> just random fonts), leave room for photo credits and captions, and explore design elements beyond plain photo
> frames. There is no state exam here; your work is graded on production, coverage, craft, and staff
> contribution. Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Use the preset styles

> Create one slide titled "Use the Preset Styles (Top-Left Dropdown)." Body: "Do not just change the font type.
> Open the preset dropdown in the TOP-LEFT and START with a preset (caption, byline, headline). The presets keep
> the whole book consistent and carry the right fonts. Leave ROOM for a photo credit ('Photo by Name') and a
> caption near every photo, a lot of pages are missing these." Add: "Want a font that is not in the presets?
> Tell me and I can add it to the yearbook." One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Design beyond photo frames

> Create one slide titled "You Can Do More Than Photo Frames." Body: "Shapes are not just for photos. Try: a
> banner running across the top of the page or spread; small colored boxes to frame or back an image; a shape
> with its color changed as an accent. Add color accents to make the page pop." Note: "Stick to the colors
> already in the book for now. Have a color idea? Bring it to me and we can talk about adding it." One slide,
> large text.

### Slide 5: Do Now (breakout to work)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school yearbook class. Big title: "Do Now: Build to the Criteria." Body:
> "Work on this now: check your page against the posted assignment criteria. Set your text with the PRESETS from
> the top-left dropdown. Leave room for photo credits and captions on every photo. Try one design element beyond
> a photo frame (a banner, a colored box, or a color accent) using the colors already in the book. Goal: a
> consistent page ready for tomorrow's PDF check-in." Large, clean. One slide.

### Slide 6: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Your page uses the presets, leaves room for credits
> and captions, and meets the assignment criteria. Tomorrow: submit your spread as a PDF, plus an optional Gimkit
> for fun. Sign your page with a 'design by' credit." One slide, large text.
