# Week 7 Teacher Notes: Yearbook

**Spirit week. The best coverage week of the quarter, and the photo assignment is due Friday.**

## The week

| Day | Push |
|-----|------|
| Mon | Hallways between classes. Get cameras out the door |
| Tue | **Students not already in the book.** Bring the list |
| Wed | Something being done, not posed |
| Thu | Setup and practice for homecoming. The before |
| Fri | **Due: photos and check-in.** Events |

## The one instruction that fixes most of it

**Wide, medium, close on every subject.** A page needs all three and a staff that has not been told this
shoots twelve mediums.

## Check the sign-out sheet Monday, not Thursday

Cameras are limited and this is the busiest week of the quarter. **Name who is not on the sheet, out
loud, on Monday.** Friday will be full and a student who waits will have a genuine excuse, which is worse
than no excuse.

Checkout is a **requirement** now, not an option: through the school day or after school, at least once.

## Both uploads

**eDesign and Classroom.** eDesign is where the book gets built, Classroom is how it gets graded. Students
will do one and consider it done. Say it more than once, and check eDesign before you grade Classroom.

## What to say about culling

They will turn in twenty frames because twenty is the number. **The selection is part of the job**, and
the line worth saying is: twenty mediocre frames is worse than twenty good ones out of two hundred taken.

## After Friday

Week 7 closes the two week assignment. The spirit week and homecoming material is also the strongest
content available for actual spreads, so the next check-in should push toward **building a spread from
photos they took themselves** rather than from the archive.
