# Making Yearbook Assignments: The Sheet

**Teacher-only.** Two CSV files become one Google Sheet that counts everyone's load for you.

## Set it up (5 minutes)

1. New Google Sheet. **File, Import, Upload** `assignments-sections.csv`, choose **Replace current
   sheet.** Rename the tab **Sections**, exactly
2. **File, Import, Upload** `assignments-students.csv`, choose **Insert new sheet.** Rename the tab
   **Students**, exactly. The count columns fill in on their own once names match
3. On **Students**, replace `Student 1` to `Student 12` with real names. **Type names the same way
   everywhere**, or the counts miss them

## Fill it in

**Sections:** one row per spread on the ladder. Fall rows first, winter after. Put **Shoots needed** on
winter rows: how many times someone has to go shoot it (a season of games might be 2, one concert 1).

Then fill in order:

1. **Q1 spreads kept:** real progress and wants to keep it (survey questions 3 and 4)
2. **Fall designers** from question 6
3. **Main and backup photographers** from questions 9 and 10. A reliable backup behind every less
   reliable main
4. **Winter designers** from question 11
5. **Reviewers and editors** from question 7. Nobody reviews or edits their own spread
6. **Other tasks** for anyone with a real answer to question 13. Type the tasks in the Students tab
7. **Blanks and missing surveys last**, at random from what is left

**Overlaps:** real Q1 progress first, then finished Q1 work.

## Read the Check column

- **OK:** 2 designs, 2 reviews, 1 edit, 4 main shoots
- **Short:** something is under. The count columns show what
- **Other path:** they have small tasks instead. Check the points match by hand: a batch of 30 photos
  edited or tagged equals one shoot

**Done when every row says OK or Other path, and every Sections row has a designer, a main, and a
backup.**

## Students who did not finish the survey

Have them finish it **first thing Thursday**. Hold their rows open until then, and fill whatever is
left. Post everyone else's assignments now.

## Posting

Share the **Sections** tab with students as **view only**. That is the assignment list. Each student
finds their name. Post one Classroom assignment per job type (spread design, reviews, edit, shoots),
each pointing to the sheet.
