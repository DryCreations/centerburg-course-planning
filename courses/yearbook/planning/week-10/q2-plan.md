# Yearbook: Q2 Plan

**Q2 | Tue Oct 20 to mid-January**

This quarter we finish fall, cover winter, and make the book look like one book. Our theme is
**Seasons of Our Lives.**

---

## The goals for Q2

1. **Fall pages finished:** every photo placed, missing photos found, the theme applied, and the pages
   edited
2. **Winter covered:** every winter area photographed by its main photographer, or its backup
3. **Winter spreads laid out:** photos placed by the end of the quarter
4. **The theme is consistent:** the same fonts, colors, and repeating elements on every page

## Your work in nine weeks

- **Shoot at least 4 times.** Be the main photographer for at least one winter area
- **Submit at least 15 usable photos per shoot**, named `LastName_Event_01`, to eDesign and Classroom
- **Design 2 spreads:** finish one fall spread and lay out one winter spread
- **Review 2 spreads** with written feedback
- **Edit 1 spread:** names, captions, spelling, and theme

**Cannot shoot or design?** There is another way to earn the same credit: editing photos, tagging in
eDesign, checking names, writing captions, or collecting photos from coaches. Tell me in the survey.

## How coverage works

Every winter section gets a **main photographer** and a **backup**. If the main photographer does not
cover it within three school days, the backup's photos are used. **Credit goes to whoever did the
work.**

keep it.

## How Q2 is graded

Your grade is out of **100 counted points.** Each category multiplies the points in it, so "counts as"
is what each assignment is worth in your grade.

| Assignment | Points | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| Fall spread, finished in the theme by its deadline | 50 | 20 |
| Winter spread, laid out with photos placed | 50 | 20 |
| 4 assigned shoots: 15 usable photos each, uploaded within 3 school days | 25 each | 6.25 each |
| Photo quality: your best 10 photos | 30 | 6 |
| Theme applied on your fall spread | 30 | 6 |
| 2 spread reviews with written feedback | 10 each | 2 each |
| 1 final edit of a spread | 20 | 4 |
| The Q2 survey | 10 | 1.5 |
| 5 check-ins, every other week | 18 each | 2.7 each |

**Extra credit:** shoot anything that is not your assignment and submit 15 usable photos. Each is 4
points and counts as 1, up to 5 shoots. **It does not replace your own assignments:** a missed assigned
shoot costs 6.25, more than all your extra credit can earn back.

**Look at the ladder for what needs covering.** If a section looks like it will be missed, shoot it.

**No work, no credit.** If you miss your assignment and your backup covers it, the backup gets the
credit.
