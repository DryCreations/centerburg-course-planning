# Week 10 Teacher Notes: Yearbook

**Teacher-only.** Student docs: `q2-plan.md`. Form build sheet: `handouts/q2-survey-FORM.md`.

**Tuesday:** walk through `q2-plan.md` (five minutes), then the survey. **Have the ladder open on the
projector or shared**, because the survey asks for spreads by page number.

---

## The workload, and why these numbers

| Per student, Q2 | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shoots | 4 | About one every two weeks. Enough to cover winter with a main and backup per area |
| Photos per shoot | 15 usable | Enough to choose from, small enough that nobody can claim it is too much |
| Spreads designed | 2 | One fall finish, one winter layout |
| Spreads reviewed | 2 | Every spread gets two sets of eyes |
| Spreads edited | 1 | A final pass by someone who did not design it |

**With 12 students** that is 24 spreads designed, 24 reviews, and 12 edits. **Adjust the design count to
the ladder.** If there are fewer than 24 fall and winter spreads, drop strong students to one designed
spread and give them more review and edit work. If there are more, the six strong students take a third.

## Turning the survey into assignments

1. **Q1 spreads first.** Anyone who answered "most photos placed" or further and wants to keep their
   spread keeps it
2. **Fall spreads next.** Fill from question 11. Give each fall spread one designer
3. **Winter coverage.** For each area, pick a main photographer from question 15 who can attend
   (questions 16 and 17). **Pair every less reliable main photographer with a reliable backup**
4. **Insiders.** Question 3 tells you who is on which team or club. They make the best photographers and
   caption sources for their own groups
5. **Winter spreads.** Fill from question 18
6. **Reviews and edits.** Assign from question 12. Nobody reviews or edits their own spread
7. **Alternative path.** Anyone with a real answer to question 21 gets small-task roles from question 22.
   Match the amount: for example, 60 photos edited or tagged equals 4 shoots

## The coverage sheet

One shared Google Sheet, one row per winter area:

| Area | Dates | Main | Backup | Photos in? | Count | Spread | Designer | Reviewers | Editor | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**The three-day rule:** if the main photographer has not uploaded within three school days of the
event, the backup's photos are used and the main gets no credit for it. **Say this rule once, out loud,
on the day assignments go up.**

## The theme

Question 19 gives you ideas, and question 20 gives you volunteers. **A two or three person theme group**
could write a one-page style sheet (fonts, colors, one repeating element, headline style) in the first
two weeks, so every fall page can be brought in line.
