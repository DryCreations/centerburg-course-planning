## The workload, and why these numbers

| Per student, Q2 | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shoots | 4 | About one every two weeks. Enough to cover winter with a main and backup per area |
| Photos per shoot | 15 usable | Enough to choose from, small enough that nobody can claim it is too much |
| Spreads designed | 2 | One fall finish, one winter layout |
| Spreads reviewed | 2 | Every spread gets two sets of eyes |
| Spreads edited | 1 | A final pass by someone who did not design it |

**With 12 students** that is 24 spreads designed, 24 reviews, and 12 edits. **Adjust the design count to
the ladder.** If there are fewer than 24 fall and winter spreads, drop strong students to one designed
spread and give them more review and edit work. If there are more, the six strong students take a third.

## Turning the survey into assignments

**The rules students were told:** blank choices are assigned at random from what is left. **When two
students want the same thing, priority goes to real Q1 progress on it, then to finished Q1 work.**

1. **Q1 spreads first.** Anyone with real progress (question 3) who wants to keep it (question 4) keeps it
2. **Fall spreads next.** Fill from question 6. Give each fall spread one designer
3. **Winter coverage.** For each section, pick a main photographer from question 9 who can attend
   (question 10). **Pair every less reliable main photographer with a reliable backup**
4. **Insiders.** Question 2 tells you who is on which team or club. They make the best photographers and
   caption sources for their own groups
5. **Winter spreads.** Fill from question 11
6. **Reviews and edits.** Assign from question 7. Nobody reviews or edits their own spread
7. **Alternative path.** Anyone with a real answer to question 13 gets small-task roles from question 14.
   Match the points: for example, a batch of 30 photos edited or tagged equals one shoot
8. **Blanks last.** Fill whatever is left at random

## Grading, as stated to students

**Points per assignment, by PowerSchool category, are in `program/q2-gradebook.md`.** Each category
totals 100 raw points, so the multipliers give exactly 40, 25, 20, and 15 counted points.

**Change the points there first** if you want something different, then update the Form description
and `q2-plan.md`.

**Why extra credit is capped at 5 counted points:** one missed assigned shoot counts 6.25, so extra
photos can never fully make up for skipping an assignment. It rewards students who shoot
everything, and it puts more cameras at events nobody was assigned.

**The survey does not list events.** Students find the sections that need photos on the ladder
(questions 6, 9, and 11), so the coverage list comes from them.

## Check-ins

**Every other week**, five in Q2, the first on **Fri Oct 30.** Each one is what the student finished
in those two weeks, checked against their assignments on the coverage sheet.

## The coverage sheet

One shared Google Sheet, one row per winter section:

| Area | Dates | Main | Backup | Photos in? | Count | Spread | Designer | Reviewers | Editor | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**The three-day rule:** if the main photographer has not uploaded within three school days of the
event, the backup's photos are used and the main gets no credit for it. **Say this rule once, out loud,
on the day assignments go up.**

## The theme

Question 12 gives you ideas. **A two or three person theme group**
could write a one-page style sheet (fonts, colors, one repeating element, headline style) in the first
two weeks, so every fall page can be brought in line.
