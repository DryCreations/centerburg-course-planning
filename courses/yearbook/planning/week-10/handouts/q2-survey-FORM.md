# Q2 Yearbook Survey: Google Form Build Sheet

**Teacher-only.** Copy each question into a Google Form. Question text is written to paste as is.
**Type** tells you which Form question type to pick. Turn on **Collect email addresses** so you know who
answered.

**16 questions, about 20 to 25 minutes.** Students take it **Tuesday**, after you walk through the Q2
plan (`q2-plan.md`). **Anyone who finishes keeps working on their spread.**

---

## Form description (paste at the top)

> This survey decides who does what in Q2. Have the ladder open in another tab.
>
> **If you leave a choice blank, you will be assigned whatever is left, at random.**
> **If two people want the same thing,** priority goes to whoever made real progress on it in Q1, then
> to whoever finished their Q1 work.
>
> **Your Q2 work:** 4 shoots of 15 usable photos, 2 spreads designed (finish one fall, lay out one
> winter), 2 spreads reviewed, 1 spread edited, and a check-in every other week.
>
> **How it is graded.** Your grade is out of 100 counted points:
> - Fall spread, finished in the theme by its deadline: counts 20
> - Winter spread, laid out with photos placed: counts 20
> - 4 assigned shoots, 15 usable photos each, uploaded within 3 school days: counts 6.25 each
> - Photo quality, your best 10 photos: counts 6
> - Theme applied on your fall spread: counts 6
> - 2 spread reviews: counts 2 each
> - 1 final edit: counts 4
> - This survey: counts 1.5
> - 5 check-ins, every other week: counts 2.7 each
>
> **Extra credit:** shoot anything that is not your assignment and submit 15 usable photos. Each counts
> 1, up to 5. **It does not replace your own shoots:** a missed one costs 6.25.
>
> **If you miss a shoot and your backup covers it, the backup gets the credit.**

## Section 6: Another way to earn credit

Small tasks earn the same points as the work they replace. Editing or tagging a batch of 30 incoming
photos earns the same as one shoot.

**13. Is there anything you cannot do this quarter, like stay after school, get a ride, or use a
camera? Explain.** | Paragraph | Required

**14. If you need another way to earn credit, which would you do?** | Checkboxes | Optional
- Editing incoming photos
- Tagging and naming photos in eDesign
- Checking names and spelling on spreads
- Collecting photos from coaches and advisors
