# Q2 Yearbook Survey: Google Form Build Sheet

**Teacher-only.** Copy each question into a Google Form. Question text is written to paste as is.
**Type** tells you which Form question type to pick. Turn on **Collect email addresses** so you know who
answered.

Students take it **Tuesday**, after you walk through the Q2 plan (`q2-plan.md`). It should take most of
the period. **Anyone who finishes keeps working on their spread.**

---

## Form description (paste at the top)

> This survey decides who does what in Q2. I will use your answers to make official assignments and
> spread the work evenly. Answer honestly and in full sentences where it asks for them. Have the ladder
> open in another tab while you fill this out.
>
> **Everyone's Q2 work, in nine weeks:**
> - **Shoot at least 4 times**, and be the main photographer for at least one winter area
> - **Submit at least 15 usable photos per shoot**, about 60 total, named and uploaded
> - **Design 2 spreads:** finish one fall spread in our theme, and lay out one winter spread
> - **Review 2 spreads** for a classmate, with written feedback
> - **Edit 1 spread:** a final check of names, captions, and theme
>
> If you cannot take photos or design pages, there is another way to earn the same credit. The last
> section asks about it.

## Section 3: Roles

**7. How interested are you in each role?** | Multiple choice grid | Required, one per row

Rows:
- Photographer: taking photos at games, events, and around school
- Designer: laying out spreads in eDesign
- Reviewer: giving written feedback on a classmate's spread
- Editor: final check of names, captions, spelling, and theme
- Photo editor: sorting, cropping, and fixing photos that come in
- Tagger and organizer: tagging and naming photos in eDesign
- Caption and quote writer: getting names, quotes, and facts for captions
- Photo collector: getting missing photos from coaches, advisors, and parents

Columns: Not interested, A little, Interested, Very interested

**8. Which ONE role do you most want, and why?** | Paragraph | Required

**9. How comfortable are you with a camera?** | Linear scale 1 to 5 | Required

**10. How comfortable are you designing in eDesign?** | Linear scale 1 to 5 | Required
