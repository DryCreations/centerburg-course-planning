# Weeks 6 and 7: Coverage: Outline

**Course:** Yearbook | **Weeks:** 6 to 7 | **Dates:** Mon Sep 21 to Fri Oct 2

> **Two weeks to go get real coverage.** Cameras can go out all day and after school, so you can be where
> something is actually happening.

---

## What This Covers

Shooting real photos for the book, on your own schedule, plus continuing spread work. The check-in and the
photos are both due **Fri Oct 2**.

## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Plan when and where to shoot around your own schedule
- Check out, carry, and return equipment responsibly for a full day
- Shoot enough coverage of an event that you have real choices afterward
- Judge which of your own frames are usable and which are not
- Keep a spread moving toward deadline

### The Two Weeks

| | |
|---|---|
| **Week 6** | Shoot. Keep building your spread. Photos accumulating |
| **Week 7** | Keep shooting. Finish your spread work. **Both due Friday Oct 2** |

### What You'll Get

- **The assignment:** how many frames, what counts, and the three ways to get a camera
- **Checkout rules** for all-day and after-school use

### Focus Areas

- **Coverage:** getting real photos of real things
- **Production and deadlines:** the spread keeps moving
- **Craft:** using the shutter speed and aperture work from the last two weeks

> **The target this quarter is something on every page.** An empty page cannot be improved.
