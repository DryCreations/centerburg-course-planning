# Weeks 6 and 7 Teacher Notes: Yearbook

**A two week photo assignment with all-day camera checkout, plus the check-in. Both due Fri Oct 2.**

## Why two weeks

**Real coverage cannot be scheduled into one class period.** The things worth photographing happen after
school, during other periods, and on days nobody planned for.

Two weeks plus an all-day checkout option means a student can actually be where something is happening.

## The three checkout options

| Option | How |
|--------|-----|
| During class | Sign out, shoot, return before the bell |
| **All day** | Sign out in the morning or during class, carry it, **return after 9th period** |
| After school | Sign up for an event, take it, **return the same day** |

**The all-day option is what makes the assignment work.** Say so explicitly, or students will default to the
class-period option and shoot the same hallway everyone else did.

## The rules that keep this available

On the assignment, and worth saying out loud on day one:

- Signed out and signed back in, every time
- **Back the same day.** After 9th period is fine, the next morning is not
- It stays with the student, not in a locker or a car
- Lens cap on when not shooting, strap on when out of the bag
- Offload before returning
- **Damage reported immediately.** Reported damage is a problem, discovered damage is a much bigger one

**Breaking one ends the privilege for that student.** Individually, not for the class, or the responsible
students pay for the others.

## What counts as usable

**20 usable frames from at least 2 different events or subjects.** Usable means in focus, correctly exposed,
and something is happening.

**Say the line on the assignment out loud: twenty frames is not twenty attempts.** Students who shoot exactly
twenty will turn in twenty mediocre frames. Students who shoot two hundred will turn in twenty good ones.

## Watch for

- **Everyone shooting the same event.** Spread the sign-ups, or the book gets three pages about one game.
- **Students who never sign out.** Check the log midway through week 6, not at the end of week 7.
- **The spread stalling** while attention goes to shooting. The check-in is due the same day.

## The quarter target

**Something on every page by the end of the quarter.** Keep saying it. An empty page cannot be improved; a
page with a rough layout and placeholder frames can be critiqued and finished.

This assignment feeds that directly: pages need photos, and photos have to exist first.
