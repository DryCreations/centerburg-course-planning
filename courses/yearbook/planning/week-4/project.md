# Week 4 Assignment: Aperture Practice Set

**Course:** Yearbook | **Assigned:** Wed Sep 9 | **Due:** Fri Sep 11
**Tool:** Camera (Av mode) | **Category:** Craft

A quick, in-class assignment: shoot **two portraits or candids of the same subject**, one with a low f number
and one with a high f number, so you can see and explain what aperture does to a shot.

---

## Requirements (all required)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Camera set to **Av mode** |
| 2 | **Shot A:** a low f number (blurry background, subject stands out) |
| 3 | **Shot B:** the same subject, a high f number (more of the background in focus) |
| 4 | Both shots in focus on the subject's eyes or face |
| 5 | Both shots offloaded and named |

## Turn in

- [ ] Shot A and Shot B, offloaded and named
- [ ] One sentence: which shot works better for a yearbook portrait, and why?

## What "good" looks like

A stranger can tell which shot has the shallower depth of field just by looking, and the subject is sharp in
both.

## Extension (ahead of the pack)

Shoot the same pair on a different subject in different light, and compare which lighting made the aperture
effect more obvious.
