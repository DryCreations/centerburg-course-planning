## What This Week Covers

**Av mode and depth of field.** You pick the aperture (the f number), the camera picks the shutter speed. A low
f number blurs the background so a face or subject stands out; a high f number keeps more in focus. This is the
mode you will use most for portraits, headshots, and candids.
