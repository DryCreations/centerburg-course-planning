# Week 4 Vocabulary: Av Mode & Depth of Field (cumulative)

Posted Wednesday.

## This week's terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Av mode** | You pick the aperture, the camera picks the shutter speed. |
| **Aperture (f number)** | How wide the lens opens. A low f number lets in more light and blurs the background. |
| **Depth of field** | How much of the shot is in focus, front to back. |
| **Shallow depth of field** | A low f number; the background is soft, the subject stands out. |
| **Deep depth of field** | A high f number; more of the scene is in focus. |

## Carried from Weeks 1 to 2 (still relevant)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Tv mode** | You pick the shutter speed, the camera picks the aperture. |
| **ISO** | How sensitive the sensor is; raise it in dim light. |
| **Gutter** | The fold in the middle of a spread; keep faces out of it. |
