# Week 5: Shutter Speed and Spread Production: Outline

**Course:** Yearbook | **Week:** 5 | **Dates:** Mon Sep 14 to Fri Sep 18

> **One new camera skill, then production.** Aperture controlled what was in focus. Shutter speed controls
> how motion looks, and it is the setting that makes or breaks every sports photo in the book.

---

## What This Week Covers

A short lesson Monday on shutter speed, a camera rotation through the week for the Shutter Speed Pair, and
spread work every day. **The biweekly PDF check-in is due Friday.**

## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Explain what shutter speed controls and how it is written
- Pick a shutter speed for a given situation, indoors or out
- Explain the trade: a faster shutter freezes motion but darkens the photo
- Shoot the same subject frozen and blurred, and defend which belongs in the book
- Keep a spread moving toward deadline
