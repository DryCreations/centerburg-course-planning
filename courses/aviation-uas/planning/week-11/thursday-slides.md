# Thursday Oct 29: Fly Check Slides

**Teacher-only.** Two slides. Paste each block into Google Slides' "Help me create a presentation," or
type them in. Slide 1 stays up all period.

**How to run it:** Slide 2 goes up at the bell and the 15 minutes start. Walk the room during that time
and check each student against Slide 1. Anyone with nothing to show: tell them what they need, and note
it. At the 15 minute mark, decide in front of the class: everyone ready, we go out; anyone not, we keep
working.

---

## Slide 1: Before we fly (standards and agenda)

```
Create ONE slide for a high school drone class. Dark navy background, bold pink and light blue headings, large readable text. Title: "Before We Fly."

Top section, headed "What we are covering," in smaller text: "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." "7.11.14 Perform and analyze a preflight assessment."

Middle section, headed "We go out ONLY if EVERY student shows me:" two numbered lines in large text: "1. Your chosen BPA competition. Promotional Photography is an optional SECOND event. It does not count on its own." "2. Real progress on your first draft or your assignment, on screen."

Then a bold box: "If anyone has nothing to show, nobody goes out."

Bottom section, headed "Agenda": "At least 15 minutes of silent work (1.2.5)." "I check every screen (1.2.5)." "Everyone ready: preflight and fly (7.11.14)." "Not ready: keep working on BPA or ODOT."

One slide.
```

## Slide 2: Do now

```
Create ONE slide in the same style. Title: "Do Now." Very large text: "At least 15 minutes of silent work." Then three short lines: "Work on your BPA project, or continue your ODOT course. ODOT is back up." "No competition chosen yet? Choose one first." "I will come around to see your progress." One slide.
```
