# The No-Go Hunt

**Aviation UAS | Monday to Thursday**

This week you search real weather reports for reasons not to fly.

## Standards

**7.11.4** Describe weather products and resources required for preflight planning, current and forecast
weather for departure, enroute, and arrival phases of flight.
**7.9.4** Describe operating limitations for small unmanned aircraft systems.
**7.11.8** Define and describe piloting requirements for special use airspace (SUA), special flight rules

## The tools (7.11.4)

- **aviationweather.gov, GFA map:** click a dot to read that station's METAR
- **aviationweather.gov, data page:** type a station ID to get its METAR and TAF
- **tfr.faa.gov:** every flight restriction in the country right now

**Dot colors:** green is good, blue is marginal, red is low cloud or poor visibility, purple is very
low. Search the red and purple dots first.

## Reasons to cancel (7.9.4)

1. **Wind or gusts over your limit:** `24018G29KT`
2. **Visibility under 3 miles:** `2SM`. Part 107 needs 3 miles
3. **Cloud too low:** `BKN007`. You must stay 500 ft below clouds. A layer at 500 ft or lower means no
   flight
4. **Thunderstorm:** `TS` or `VCTS`. Stay 20 miles away
5. **Rain, snow, or drizzle:** `RA`, `SN`, `DZ`. Your drone is not waterproof
6. **Fog coming:** `FG`, `BR`, or temperature and dew point within 2 degrees
7. **Freezing:** `FZRA`, `FZDZ`. Ice on the props
8. **Gets worse later:** a TAF with `FM` or `TEMPO` during your flight time
9. **A TFR:** no flight inside it, whatever the weather

**For every find:** paste the whole report with the station and time. Name the part that cancels the
flight and the rule it breaks.
