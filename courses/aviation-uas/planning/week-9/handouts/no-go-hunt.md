# The No-Go Hunt

**Aviation UAS | Week 9, Monday to Thursday**

**The weather here has been good for weeks, and "it's fine, let's go" has become a habit.** This week you
go looking for reasons **not** to fly, in **real reports**, on the real tools. Somewhere in the country
right now, every one of these is happening.

## Standards

**7.11.4** Describe weather products and resources required for preflight planning, current and forecast
weather for departure, enroute, and arrival phases of flight.
**7.9.4** Describe operating limitations for small unmanned aircraft systems.
**7.11.8** Define and describe piloting requirements for special use airspace (SUA), special flight rules
areas (SFRA), temporary flight restrictions (TFR), and other airspace areas.

## The tools

| Tool | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **The map** | aviationweather.gov, the **GFA** observation map | Colored dots for every station. **Click a dot** to read its METAR |
| **The text** | aviationweather.gov, the **data** page | Type a station ID, get its **METAR and TAF** |
| **TFRs** | **tfr.faa.gov** | Every temporary flight restriction in the country right now |

**The dot colors are your search tool:**

| Dot | Means |
|---|---|
| **Green** | VFR. Good |
| **Blue** | MVFR. Lower cloud or reduced visibility |
| **Red** | IFR. Low cloud or poor visibility |
| **Purple** | LIFR. Very low cloud or very poor visibility |

**Hunt in the red and purple.** If the lower 48 are all green, try **Alaska**, the **Pacific Northwest
coast**, or the **mountains**.

## The reasons to cancel

| Reason | What it looks like | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Wind or gust over your limit** | `24018G29KT` | **Your own limit**, from your brief |
| **Visibility under 3 miles** | `2SM`, `1/2SM` | **Part 107: 3 statute miles minimum** |
| **Cloud too low** | `BKN007`, `OVC004` | **Part 107: stay 500 ft below clouds.** Lowest layer at 800 ft means a 300 ft maximum. **500 ft or lower, you cannot fly** |
| **Thunderstorm** | `TS`, `VCTS` | **Twenty miles** |
| **Rain, snow, drizzle** | `RA`, `SN`, `DZ`, `-SHRA` | **Your aircraft is not waterproof** |
| **Fog, or about to be** | `FG`, `BR`, temperature and dew point within 2 | Visibility can collapse in minutes |
| **Freezing** | `FZRA`, `FZDZ`, moisture at 0 or below | **Ice on the props** |
| **It gets worse later** | A TAF with `FM` or `TEMPO` inside your flight window | **It has to hold for the whole flight** |
| **Not weather: a TFR** | tfr.faa.gov | **No flight inside it, whatever the weather** |

**Every find:** paste the **whole real report**, with the station and time. Then name the **one group that
cancels the flight** and **the rule it breaks.**

---

## Monday: learn the map, catch the obvious ones

**1.** Find a station with a **gust over your personal limit.** Paste the METAR. Which group decides it?

**2.** Find a **red or purple dot.** Paste its METAR. **Low cloud, low visibility, or both?** Name the
deciding number.

**3.** Same report: using the **500 foot rule**, what is the highest you could legally fly, or can you
fly at all?

## Tuesday: precipitation, storms, fog

**4.** Find a report with **precipitation**: `RA`, `SN`, or `DZ`. Paste it. **Why does this cancel the
flight even if visibility is fine?**

**5.** Find a **thunderstorm**: `TS` or `VCTS`. Paste it. How far away do you need to be?

**6.** Find a report where **temperature and dew point are within 2 degrees.** Paste it. **What is likely
to happen next**, and why does it matter if the sky is clear right now?

## Wednesday: the sneaky ones

**7.** Find a **TAF that looks fine now and goes bad later.** Paste it. **What time would you have to be
on the ground?**

**8.** Find a **current TFR** on tfr.faa.gov. Where is it, why does it exist, and could you fly inside it?

**9. The hardest one.** Find a report that **looks like a nice day** at a glance but has **exactly one**
reason to cancel. Paste it and name the reason.

## Thursday: our own call

**10.** Pull the **real KCMH METAR and TAF.** Go down **every row** of the reasons table, one line each:
**pass or fail.**

**11.** Check **tfr.faa.gov** for anything near us.

**12. Go or no-go**, and the deciding number. **If it is a go, it is because every row passed, not because
it looks nice.**

---

## Graded on

**Every find is a real report**, pasted whole, with the **deciding group** and **the rule** named. A
made-up report, or "it looks bad," earns nothing. **Question 10 is worth the most:** checking every row
on a day that looks fine is the whole skill.
