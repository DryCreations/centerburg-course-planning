# Watch a Front Come Through

**Aviation UAS | Week 9, Monday to Thursday**

**Four weather reports from the same airport, one a day.** Read them in order and you watch a weather
system arrive, pass over, and leave. **Every day ends in a go or no-go call.**

These four are **practice reports**, written to tell one story. **Every day, also pull today's real
METAR for KCMH** at aviationweather.gov and make the same call on it.

## Standards

**7.11.4** Describe weather products and resources required for preflight planning, current and forecast
weather for departure, enroute, and arrival phases of flight.
**7.6.9** Describe weather system formation, including air masses and fronts.
**7.6.6** Identify wind patterns based on weather systems.

**Use your own go or no-go limits from last week's brief.** Section 3, question 30.

     FM130000 21016G26KT 5SM -SHRA BKN030

**1.** Decode the METAR: wind, visibility, ceiling, and the temperature and dew point spread.

**2.** Warm, humid, and the wind is from the south. **Which air mass from last week is this?**

**3.** Read the TAF. **What changes, and when?** (00Z is 8 p.m. here. 18Z is 2 p.m.)

**4.** The wind swings from southwest to northwest, with gusts and showers. **What kind of front does that
describe, and what are you expecting tomorrow?**

**5.** **Go or no-go right now?** Name the number that decided it.
