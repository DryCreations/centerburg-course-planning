# Week 9: Watch a Front Come Through: Outline

**Course:** Aviation UAS (177024)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Week:** 9 | **Unit:** 2.1 Meteorology | **Dates:** Mon Oct 12 to Fri Oct 16

> **One weather report a day, from the same airport.** Read them in order and you watch a cold front
> arrive, pass over, and leave. Every day ends in a go or no-go call.
> **Last week of the quarter.**

## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Decode a METAR and a TAF without the decoder sheet
- Spot a front in the reports: wind shift, temperature and dew point, pressure
- Name the air mass from the temperature, dew point and wind
- Make a go or no-go call against your own limits, and name the deciding number
