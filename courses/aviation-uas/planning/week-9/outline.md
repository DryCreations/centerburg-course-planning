## For each mission, check

- **Airspace:** what class, and do you need authorization?
- **TFRs:** is there one at that place and time?
- **Weather:** the METAR and TAF from the nearest airport, for that time
- **The Part 107 rules:** 400 ft, 3 miles visibility, 500 ft below clouds

## Due

- All four missions, each with your decision and a client email
