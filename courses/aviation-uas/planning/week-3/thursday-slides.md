# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Aviation UAS · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = the **last briefing** before we fly. Go over everything they need,
summarize the drone settings to change, and make sure a **battery goes on the charger overnight** so tomorrow we
grab it, load the case, and walk out. One prompt per slide for Google Slides AI. Order: As You Come In,
Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now, Gimkit, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school drone class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Sit with
> your flight group and open your Mission Plan Worksheet and the Week 3 outline. Today is our LAST briefing
> before we fly tomorrow, so we lock in everything." Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

> Create one standards and agenda slide titled "Today: Final Briefing." List these review focus areas with short
> text: 7.11 Preflight (inspection and go/no-go), 7.13 Flight Environment (airspace and site), 7.6 Weather
> (reading tomorrow's forecast), plus safety, crew roles, and drone setup. Then a short agenda: 1) Final briefing
> and drone settings, 2) What to charge and pack tonight, 3) Gimkit ground review. Note: no quiz, we FLY
> tomorrow. Large readable text, one slide.

### Slide 3: Drone settings to change (summary)

> Create one slide titled "Drone Settings to Set Before We Fly." Checklist: switch the camera to VIDEO mode and
> pick a resolution and frame rate (for example 1080p or 4K at 30 fps); confirm the storage card is in and
> cleared; set Return to Home (RTH) altitude ABOVE the buildings and trees; set the max altitude and distance
> limits for our site; put the gimbal in a smooth or cinematic mode and know your tilt; update the home point at
> launch. Note: many of us have not flown yet, so we set these together. One slide, large text.

### Slide 4: Charge and pack tonight

> Create one slide titled "Charge and Pack Tonight." Body: "Put a drone battery ON THE CHARGER overnight so it is
> full for tomorrow. Tomorrow morning the plan is simple: grab the charged battery, put it and the drone in the
> case, and we walk out to fly. Also check: props on and clean, controller charged." Large, clear. One slide.

### Slide 5: Do Now (breakout)

> Create one "Do Now" slide for a high school drone class. Big title: "Do Now: Lock It In." Body: "With your
> group, finish your briefing card: the one shot, flight area and boundaries, go/no-go numbers, and who has each
> role (pilot, visual observer, logger). Confirm your drone settings from the checklist. Make sure a battery is
> charging tonight. Be ready to grab the case and fly tomorrow." Large, clean. One slide.

### Slide 6: Gimkit ground review

> Create one slide titled "Gimkit Ground Review." Body: "Join the Gimkit review game: [paste your Gimkit join
> link or code here]. We are keeping airspace, weather, preflight, and controls terms sharp before we fly." Big
> join-code space in the middle. One slide.

### Slide 7: Before You Leave

> Create one "Exit" slide titled "Before You Leave." Body: "Battery on the charger tonight. We fly tomorrow,
> weather permitting. Keep working ODOT Part 107 Module 1, due next week." One slide, large text.
