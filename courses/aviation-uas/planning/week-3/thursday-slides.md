# Thursday Sep 3: Slides (one prompt per slide)

**Aviation UAS · Week 3 · Day 4.** Thursday = final flight briefing, weather look-ahead, and a **Gimkit ground
review** (no quiz this week). We fly Friday. Each block is one prompt to paste into Google Slides AI. Deck
order: As You Come In, Standard/Summary, Content, Do Now, Gimkit, Exit.

### Slide 1: As You Come In

> Create one "As You Come In" slide for a high school drone class. Big title: "As You Come In." Body: "Sit with
> your flight group and open your Mission Plan Worksheet. We finalize the briefing for tomorrow's flight today."
> Large, clean, minimal. One slide.

### Slide 2: Standard + Today

### Slide 3: Final briefing checklist

> Create one slide titled "Final Flight Briefing." Checklist for each group: the one primary shot (subject +
> move); the flight area (launch/land, boundary, spectator line); go/no-go numbers (wind, visibility, precip);
> crew roles and rotation (pilot, visual observer, logger); preflight items ready (batteries, props, card
> cleared, home point). One slide, large text.

### Slide 7: Before You Leave
