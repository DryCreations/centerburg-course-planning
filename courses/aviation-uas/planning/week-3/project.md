# Week 3 Project — Video Mission (+ ODOT Module 1)

**Course:** Aviation UAS | **Assigned:** Mon Aug 31 | **Flight:** Fri Sep 4
**Category:** Labs/Projects | **Late:** flight reschedules on weather, no penalty

Two pieces this week: a **planned flight to capture a short video**, and the start of the **ODOT Part 107
course** (homework).

> **Your group picks the shot.** The class is split into **two groups**, and each group decides what it wants to
> film on Friday — the **front of the school**, a **walk/flight over to the track**, the **fields**, or another
> approved subject on campus. The rule is the same for everyone: **one clear primary shot your group can
> actually get safely** in the time and airspace we have.

---

## Part A — Plan it (Tue–Wed)

In your **group**, build the mission for Friday (use the `handouts/mission-plan-worksheet.md`):

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | **The primary shot:** one clean clip, ~20–40 sec, of your group's chosen subject (front of school, track, fields, or approved idea). Describe the move (approach, reveal, orbit, follow). |
| 2 | **Airspace check:** confirm the site's airspace class and whether authorization (LAANC) is needed |
| 3 | **Flight area:** boundary line, spectator line, launch/land spot marked |
| 4 | **Go/no-go criteria:** the wind, visibility, and precipitation limits that would scrub the flight |
| 5 | **Roles:** pilot, visual observer, logger — and the rotation order |
| 6 | **Scope check:** the primary shot is realistic for one flight; any extra "stretch" shots are attempted **only after** the primary shot is captured |

## Part B — Fly it (Fri)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Real-weather **go/no-go** call before anyone launches |
| 2 | Preflight the aircraft (no preflight, no flight) |
| 3 | Capture your group's primary clip; keep it smooth and in visual line of sight |
| 4 | **Return to Home / land**, power down, batteries to safe charge |
| 5 | **Offload the footage** to a computer and log the flight |

## Part C — ODOT Part 107, Module 1 (homework)

- Log into the **Ohio DOT Part 107 online course** and complete **Module 1**.
- **Due next week** (not this Friday). Work it in spare class time or at home.
- Bring one question or one thing you learned to share.
