# Controller Practice: Read the Path, Name the Sticks

**Aviation UAS · Week 3 · Wednesday Sep 2**

Two different questions for every move:
- **Heading** = which way the drone is **traveling** through the air.
- **Pointing** = which way the **nose / camera** is facing.

They are often **not the same** (you can fly sideways while the camera keeps pointing forward). For each path
below: name the **stick inputs**, say where the drone is **heading**, where it's **pointing**, and how you'd
**recreate the shot** smoothly.

> Sticks (Mode 2): **LEFT stick** = throttle (up/down) + yaw (rotate nose left/right). **RIGHT stick** = pitch
> (forward/back) + roll (slide left/right). Camera points where the **nose** points, plus **gimbal tilt** up/down.

---

## Worked examples (project the path, then reveal)

**1. Push in toward the building (straight approach).**
- Sticks: **right stick forward**, steady.
- Heading: forward, toward the building. Pointing: at the building (nose forward).
- Recreate: slow, even right-stick-forward; don't accelerate. Stop before you get too close.

**2. Rise and reveal the entrance.**
- Sticks: **left stick up** (throttle), while **tilting the gimbal down** to keep the entrance in frame.
- Heading: straight up. Pointing: starts low, camera tilts down to hold the doors.
- Recreate: gentle throttle up + slow gimbal tilt at the same time.

**3. Truck (strafe) left across the field.**
- Sticks: **right stick left** (roll) only. **No yaw.**
- Heading: sideways to the left. Pointing: still forward (camera looks across the field as you slide).
- Recreate: pure sideways roll, nose locked forward. This is the one people confuse: heading ≠ pointing.

**4. Orbit the flagpole (circle around it).**
- Sticks: **right stick sideways** (roll) to travel around it **plus left stick yaw** to keep the nose turned
  toward the flagpole the whole time. Both at once, balanced.
- Heading: in a circle around the flagpole. Pointing: always inward, at the flagpole.
- Recreate: the hardest one. Practice slow; match the yaw to the roll so the pole stays centered.

**5. Pedestal down and back (drop and retreat).**
- Sticks: **left stick down** (throttle) + **right stick back** (pitch).
- Heading: down and backward. Pointing: forward at the subject as you pull away.
- Recreate: small inputs; keep the subject centered as it shrinks in frame.

---

## Now you try (describe each one)

For each: **sticks? heading? pointing? how to recreate?**

- **A.** The drone stays in one spot but slowly spins to show everything around it.
  *(Answer: left stick left or right = yaw only. Heading: staying put. Pointing: sweeping around. Recreate:
  tiny, steady yaw, no other stick.)*
- **B.** The drone follows a student walking across the parking lot, staying behind them.
  *(Answer: right stick forward to match their speed + small yaw to keep them centered. Heading: forward with
  them. Pointing: at their back. Recreate: match walking speed, gentle yaw corrections.)*
- **C.** The drone flies forward down the track **but the camera stays pointed at the bleachers** on the side.
  *(Answer: right stick forward (travel) + a yaw offset so the nose faces the bleachers. Heading: down the
  track. Pointing: at the bleachers. This is heading ≠ pointing again.)*

---

## The takeaways

- Say both out loud before every shot: **"heading ___, pointing ___."**
- Small, smooth inputs beat big ones. The camera shows every twitch.
- If a move needs **two sticks at once** (orbit, reveal), practice it slow before Friday.
