## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Identify airspace, airports, and obstructions on a sectional chart and say how you knew
- Read floors and ceilings for a point with overlapping airspace
- Plan a flight at a location using the chart plus current weather
- Fly a reproducible pattern and log it
- Explain the Part 107 rules and limits that govern everything above
