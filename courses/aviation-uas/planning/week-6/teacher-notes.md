## Why sectionals get three more days

The exit tickets say it has not landed, and it is the hardest reading skill in the course. **It also feeds
the quiz and the WebXam directly**, which is the argument already made to them on Friday.

**Monday's move is from symbols to an area.** Last week was "find one of each." This week is "here is a
region, tell me everything about it, then tell me whether you could fly at this spot and what you would need
first." That last question is the whole point; everything before it is how you get there.

## Thursday's flight

**One pattern, every pilot, same altitude, same order.** `handouts/box-pattern-flight.md` has the diagram,
the holds, and the log.

**The three things being judged** are on the handout and worth saying out loud before the first flight:
does the nose stay put (right stick only, this is roll and pitch, not yaw), do they actually **stop** at each
corner, and does altitude hold.

**Stopping is the skill.** Moving is easy. Holding still at a corner for five seconds is what separates a
pilot who is in control from one who is reacting.

**The preflight gate is unchanged and not optional:** METAR, personal minimums card, go or no-go with the
deciding number, preflight walked out loud, roles assigned. A no-go is a real outcome and gets logged.

**Everyone waiting has a job**, listed on the handout. Watching somebody else make a mistake is the cheapest
way to learn it.

## ODOT Module 1

**Due Thursday night. Its material is on Friday's quiz.** Say it every single day.

A student who opens it Thursday evening will not finish, so Wednesday's work time exists specifically for
this. Let them choose between that and sectionals, but make sure they know the deadline is real.

## Friday's quiz

62 questions, cut to 20. The suggested cut in `quiz.md` weights it 6 sectional, 5 ODOT rules, 4 airspace and
LAANC, 5 weather.

**What it is really checking:** whether the rules and regulations landed. That was the argument for pausing
flying. **If they underperform specifically on the sectional questions** while doing fine elsewhere, that
tells you charts need more time and the rest does not.

## Looking ahead

If the box pattern goes cleanly, the reverse pattern and the yaw-at-each-corner variant are the next
progressions, both on the handout. Unit 2.2 preflight is the next content unit.
