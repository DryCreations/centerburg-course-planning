## What this quiz is actually checking

**Whether the rules and regulations landed.** That is the argument made to them on Friday: the Part 107 exam
does not test flying, it tests this. A weak result here is the evidence that class time needs to keep going
to it.

**The sectional questions are the hardest and most recent.** If they underperform there specifically and do
well elsewhere, the chart work needs more time and the rest does not.

## Timing

week. A student who leaves it to Thursday evening will not finish it.
