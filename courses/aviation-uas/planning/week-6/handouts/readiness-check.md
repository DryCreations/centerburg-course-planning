# Readiness Check

**Aviation UAS | Week 6, Wednesday Sep 23**

**This is not graded.** It is twelve questions in the same format as Friday's quiz, so that both of us
find out what is not solid while there are still two days to fix it.

Answer every one. **Guessing and finding out you were wrong is the entire point.** A blank tells you
nothing on Friday.

Chart out, notes closed.

---

## Airspace

**1.** You are standing at a spot with **no airspace ring around it at all** on the sectional. What class
of airspace are you in at ground level?

**2.** A Class E surface area is shown with which chart symbol?

**3.** At a spot under a Class C shelf, the stacked numbers read `70` over `40`. What is the floor and
what is the ceiling of that Class C shelf, in feet MSL?

**4.** Outside of surface areas, Class G generally goes from the surface up to what altitude, where Class
E begins?

**5.** You are looking at a **solid magenta** ring on the chart. What class of airspace is that?

---

## Sectional symbols and obstructions

**6.** An obstruction on the chart is labeled `1549 (285)`. What does each number mean?

**7.** What does the symbol with small dots or lines radiating from the base of a tower tell you, and why
does it matter to you specifically?

**8.** You find a magenta airport symbol on the chart. What does the magenta color tell you about that
airport?

---

## Rules and limits

**9.** What is the maximum altitude you may fly a small UAS under Part 107, and what is the exception
that lets you go higher?

**10.** What is the minimum flight visibility required under Part 107, and how far must you stay from
clouds?

**11.** At what weight does a UAS have to be registered?

**12.** You want to fly inside Class D airspace. What do you need before you can legally do it, and what
is the tool that gets it to you quickly?

---

## When you are done

Do not leave it blank and do not look anything up yet. We go over every one out loud as soon as everybody
has finished, and **the ones the class gets wrong are exactly what Thursday's review is.**

Then, honestly, mark each one:

- **Knew it**
- **Guessed it and got it**
- **Did not know it**

**The middle category is the one to study.** Getting it right by luck on Wednesday is getting it wrong on
Friday.
