**Aviation UAS | Week 6, Wednesday Sep 23** | **Teacher only**

Go over it out loud the same period. **Do not collect it for a grade.** The value is the conversation

## Answers

| # | Answer | If they miss it |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Class G.** No ring means uncontrolled airspace at the surface | The most common miss all week: they name the class *above* them, usually E. Standing on the ground with no ring over you is Class G, and G is a real answer |
| 2 | **Dashed magenta** | Pair it out loud with the other three every time: solid blue B, solid magenta C, dashed blue D, dashed magenta E at the surface |
| 3 | **Floor 4,000 MSL, ceiling 7,000 MSL** | They read 70 and 40 as feet. The numbers are hundreds of feet MSL. Top number is the ceiling, bottom is the floor |
| 4 | **700 or 1,200 feet AGL**, depending on the area. Accept either with reasoning | The shaded magenta band on the chart marks where E starts at 700 |
| 5 | **Class C** | Solid = the airspace touches the ground there |
| 6 | **1549 is MSL, the height above sea level. (285) in parentheses is AGL, the height above the ground** | Parentheses always mean AGL. Say it as a rule, not a fact to memorize |
| 7 | **Guy-wires.** They anchor the tower and extend well out from its base, they are nearly invisible from the air and from a distance on the ground, and a drone flying past the tower at a safe-looking distance can still hit one | This is the one that gets real drones. Worth the extra thirty seconds |
| 8 | **The airport does not have an operating control tower.** Blue means it does | |
| 9 | **400 feet AGL.** Exception: you may go higher than 400 AGL if you stay **within 400 feet of a structure**, measured from its uppermost limit | Both halves. They usually get the 400 and miss the structure exception |
| 10 | **3 statute miles** flight visibility. **500 feet below and 2,000 feet horizontally** from clouds | The 500/2000 pair gets reversed. Below is the smaller number |
| 11 | **0.55 pounds (250 grams)** and above | |

## Reading the room

Tally wrong answers by question number as you go over it. That tally **is** Thursday's review plan.

| If they miss | Thursday spends time on |
|---|---|
| 1, 2, 5 | The four ring symbols, drilled cold. This is pure recognition and it is cheap to fix |
| 3, 4, 6 | Altitude numbers: hundreds of feet, top/bottom, MSL versus AGL. The most fixable cluster |
| 7, 8 | Chart symbols beyond airspace. Lower stakes, cover quickly |
| 9, 10, 11, 12 | ODOT Module 1 material. If this cluster is weak, they did not do the module, and the fix is the practice set, not more instruction |

**If more than half the class misses the same question, it was taught badly, not learned badly.** Reteach
it rather than assigning more practice.

## What to say about scores

Nobody's number goes in the gradebook. The line worth saying out loud:

> **The middle category is the one that matters.** Right on Wednesday by guessing is wrong on Friday.
