# Aviation Unmanned Aircraft Systems — Course Planning

**Subject Code:** 177024
**Career Field:** Transportation Technologies (also listed under IT Interactive Media N1 and Information Support & Services N0)
**CTAG Eligible:** No

## Course Overview

**School Course Name:** Unmanned Aircraft

Students will learn the essentials of operating an unmanned aircraft in a variety of environments. Students will learn principles of regulations, operations, air space, and navigation. Additionally, students will acquire and use geospatial information for various applications.

This is a **one-year course** enrolling **juniors and seniors only**.

**Semester 1 (Weeks 1–18):** Aviation foundations, FAA regulations, aerodynamics, weather, preflight planning, and GIS concepts. Culminates with a complete mission planning package and BPA presentation project draft.

**Semester 2 (Weeks 19–36):** FAA Part 107 exam preparation, hands-on flight operations using the school’s DJI Mavic 3 fleet (6 drones), advanced GIS and aerial photography/video production, and the course capstone.

## BPA Competition Integration

See the [BPA competition planning document](../../../program/bpa/competition-planning.md) for the full season calendar, national deadlines, and how to run a local competition.

### Course Alignment

Aviation UAS is a Transportation Technologies course — there are no BPA events that assess aviation-specific technical content. Instead, BPA participation draws on **Strand 1** (Business Operations, Leadership, Entrepreneurship) and **Strand 2** (Regulations, Safety, Project Documentation). Two events are co-equal primary paths: **#555 Presentation Individual** (research and present on a UAS topic — most flexible fit for aviation content) and **#505 Entrepreneurship** (drone services business plan — aligns directly to the Unit 4.3 capstone; no separate project required). Students select one of these two events by the end of Week 9.

### Recommended Events

Students select **one primary event** (#555 Presentation Individual or #505 Entrepreneurship) and one backup. With 9 students, a natural split is ~6 taking #555 and ~3 taking #505 via the capstone business plan track.

## Prerequisites

Juniors and seniors only. No prior flying experience required. Students with prior recreational UAS or RC aircraft experience will have an advantage in S2 flight units.

## Safety Considerations

- **Site approval required:** All outdoor flight operations must be pre-approved by administration; teacher submits LAANC authorization (or verifies airspace clear) before any flight day
- **6-drone rotation:** Students fly in supervised groups; maximum 2 students per drone during skill-building phases; all non-flying students act as visual observers
- **Pre-flight checklist:** Every flight day begins with a physical pre-flight inspection of all drones (motors, props, battery, RC signal) before any aircraft leaves the ground
- **Incident protocol:** Any crash, fly-away, or incident is documented on a flight incident log and reported to teacher; aircraft is grounded until inspected
- **Restricted areas:** Flight only in approved school property zones; no flight over people, roads, or neighboring property; teacher maintains current site map and TFR awareness
- **Battery safety:** LiPo batteries stored in fireproof bags when not in use; charging only under direct supervision; never leave charging batteries unattended
- **Part 107 operating rules are the floor:** Even for school use, all Part 107 operating limitations apply (400 ft AGL max, 100 mph max, daylight only, visual line-of-sight)

## Certifications

**FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate** — Students who demonstrate readiness after Unit 4.2 exam prep are strongly encouraged to register for the official FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Knowledge Test at a PSI testing center (exam fee: $175). The school does not administer or sponsor the exam, but passing Part 107 is a genuine commercial-use credential. Any student scoring 70%+ on the Unit 4.2 practice exam is considered exam-ready. Teacher can provide a letter of recommendation.
