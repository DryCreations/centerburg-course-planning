# Week 4 Quiz: Weather, Regs & Flight Ops (Friday Sep 11)

**Teacher-only.** Aviation's **first quiz of the year**. Question bank: `quiz-bank.csv`, **40 questions**, cut
to your best 20. Same schema as the pretests, so the existing Apps Script imports it directly (`option_a` is
always correct, `answer` is always A). Turn on shuffle in the Form after import.

**Covers Weeks 1 to 4:** reading a METAR (wind, gusts, visibility, cloud layers, ceiling), TAF vs METAR vs
NOTAM, Part 107 limits (3 SM visibility, cloud clearance, 400 ft, registration), TRUST vs Part 107, RPIC and
visual observer, VLOS, airspace classes and LAANC, the four forces and center of gravity, Mode 2 controls,
heading versus pointing, preflight and airworthiness, lost link, and flight logging.

**One scenario set** (`AV-S1`): a real-format METAR with a gust, 2 SM visibility, and an OVC007 ceiling. Four
questions hang off it, ending in a go/no-go call. That set alone is worth keeping when you trim.

DOK split as written: 8 low, 32 high. Trim toward whatever balance you want; the recall items are the four
forces, RPIC, Class G, METAR/NOTAM definitions, and preflight timing.
