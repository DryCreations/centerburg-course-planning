# Week 4 Teacher Notes: Aviation UAS

Teacher-facing pacing for Week 4. Student doc is `outline.md`; assignment is `project.md`; vocab is `vocab.md`.
**First quiz of the year is Friday.** **Three day week** (Labor Day Monday, PD Tuesday). The flight stays on **Thursday**, which
makes Wednesday a single full prep day.

## Pacing Guide

| Day | Activity | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Wed | **METAR scavenger hunt** on aviationweather.gov (`handouts/metar-scavenger-hunt.md`): groups pull a real METAR for a home field, decode every group, then hunt for stations with gusts, low visibility, overcast, and precipitation, and make go/no-go calls on each. Then: quick footage critique, groups pick new roles, finalize the plan, and walk the preflight checklist. | The scavenger hunt IS the weather instruction, so it doubles as Friday quiz prep. Budget roughly 25 minutes for the hunt and 20 for the replan and preflight. |
| Thu | **Fly.** Real weather go/no-go first. Fly the adjusted shot with the new roles. Offload and log. | Same gates as always: no preflight, no flight. If it is a no-go, run the Week 3 rain-out Gimkit bank as quiz review and reschedule. |
| Fri | **Quiz** (Weeks 1 to 4, `quiz.md` and `quiz-bank.csv`, 40 questions cut to 20). Then review Thursday's footage, debrief, and close out flight logs. | First quiz of the year for this class. Same silent, quiz-tab-only rules as the other courses. |

## Assessment Notes

- Graded per the rubric in `project.md`.
- **Quiz is summative** under Assessments (30% per `grading-and-categories.md`). Cut `quiz-bank.csv` from 40
  questions to your best 20; keep the `AV-S1` METAR scenario set when you trim.
- Wednesday's scavenger hunt doubles as quiz prep, since the quiz leans on decoding real METAR groups.
- Use `handouts/metar-resources.md` for real weather sources; aviationweather.gov is the standard reference
  pilots actually use.

## Open items

  (`courses/aviation-uas/planning/week-3/gimkit-rainout-backup.csv`) and push the flight to the following week.
