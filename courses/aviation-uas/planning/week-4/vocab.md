# Week 4 Vocabulary: Weather Reading (cumulative)

Posted Tuesday. See `handouts/metar-resources.md` for real references to practice with.

## This week's terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **METAR** | A coded weather observation from an airport, updated hourly. |
| **Wind direction and speed** | In a METAR, given as a three digit direction and speed, such as 27008KT. |
| **Gust** | A short, sudden increase in wind speed above the sustained speed, shown as G in a METAR. |
| **Visibility** | How far you can see, given in statute miles; Part 107 requires at least 3. |
| **Cloud layer** | A reported layer of cloud with a height, such as FEW020 or BKN008. |
| **Ceiling** | The height of the lowest broken or overcast cloud layer. |
| **TAF** | A forecast for an airport, similar format to a METAR but looking ahead instead of reporting now. |
