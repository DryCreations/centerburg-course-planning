## What This Week Covers

Reviewing last week's flight footage as a class, reading real weather (a real **METAR**, real go/no-go limits),
and replanning your group's mission with **new roles** before flying again Thursday.

## Weeks 1 to 3 recap

TRUST, the four forces, airspace basics, controls, and last week's mission: a group-chosen video shot, briefed,
preflighted, and flown.

## Student Overview

- Watch and critique real flight footage against the original plan
- Read a real METAR and apply Part 107 go/no-go limits to it
- Revise a mission plan based on what didn't work the first time
- Fly the same or an adjusted shot with a different crew role
