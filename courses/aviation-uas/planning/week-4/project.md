# Week 4 Project: Mission 2 (Weather Call + New Maneuver)

**Course:** Aviation UAS | **Assigned:** Tue Sep 8 | **Flight:** Fri Sep 11
**Category:** Labs/Projects | **Late:** flight reschedules on weather, no penalty

Two pieces this week: reading real weather to make the go/no-go call, and flying a **second mission** using an
**orbit or survey pass**, a harder maneuver than last week's straight approach.

---

## Part A: Weather (Tue)

As a class, pull a real current METAR for the local area and answer:

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| 1 | What is the wind direction and speed? |
| 2 | What is the visibility? |
| 3 | What is the ceiling (if any layer is broken or overcast)? |
| 4 | Based on Part 107 limits, is this a go or a no-go, and why? |

## Part B: Plan Mission 2 (Wed to Thu)

With your group, plan a mission using an **orbit** (circle a subject, keep it centered) or a **survey pass**
(a level pass or repeated grid over an area):

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | The maneuver and the subject or area |
| 2 | Airspace and flight area, same as Mission 1 |
| 3 | Go/no-go numbers, using real limits |
| 4 | Roles and rotation |

## Part C: Fly it (Fri)

Real weather go/no-go first. Preflight. Fly the maneuver. Offload the footage and log the flight.

---

## Turn in
