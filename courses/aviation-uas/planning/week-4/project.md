# Week 4 Project: Footage Review & Mission Retry

**Course:** Aviation UAS | **Assigned:** Wed Sep 9 | **Flight:** Thu Sep 10
**Category:** Labs/Projects | **Late:** flight reschedules on weather, no penalty

Review last week's flight as a group, then your group revises its plan and flies again Thursday with **new
roles**.

---

## Part A: METAR Scavenger Hunt (Wed)

Complete `handouts/metar-scavenger-hunt.md` using real, live data on **aviationweather.gov**. You will:

| # | Task |
|---|------|
| 1 | Decode a full METAR for your home field, group by group |
| 2 | Hunt down stations reporting gusts, low visibility, overcast, and precipitation |
| 3 | Make a Part 107 go/no-go call for each one, citing the number that decided it |
| 4 | Pull a TAF and say whether the forecast is improving or worsening |

## Part B: Footage review & replan (Wed)

As a class, watch last week's footage. As a group, discuss and write down:

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | What worked in the shot |
| 2 | What you would change (the move, the framing, the timing) |
| 3 | **New roles** for Thursday: who is pilot, visual observer, and logger this time |
| 4 | Any adjustments to the flight area, boundaries, or go/no-go numbers |

## Part C: Fly it again (Thu)

Real weather go/no-go first. Preflight. Fly the adjusted shot with the new roles. Offload the footage and log
the flight.

---

## Turn in

- [ ] Completed METAR scavenger hunt
- [ ] Footage review notes and the revised plan with new roles
- [ ] Footage from Thursday's flight, offloaded and named
- [ ] Flight log entry

Pull a second real METAR from a different time and make a second go/no-go call, or note one thing to try on a
