## How it was built

- **Every question is a situation**, not a definition, except the three decoding items (10, 11, 13)
- **Options are written in parallel:** all four alike, or two matching pairs, so the odd one out is never
  the answer
- **Answer lengths:** the correct answer is the single longest option in 5 of 24 (21%)
- **Every answer matches the Week 9 teacher notes:** 3 miles, 500 below and 2,000 sideways from clouds,
  20 miles from storms, no Part 107 wind limit, UTC minus 4, the TAF window
