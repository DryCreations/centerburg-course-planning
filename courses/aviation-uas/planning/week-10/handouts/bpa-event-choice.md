# Choosing Your BPA Event

**Aviation UAS | Due Wednesday**

There is no BPA event for flying drones. The first two let you use the most of what this class covers.

## Standards

**1.2.5** Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an
intended audience and purpose.
**7.9.4** Describe operating limitations for small unmanned aircraft systems.

---

## The options (1.2.5)

- **Presentation, alone or as a team of 2 or 3:** research a drone topic and present it live. **The
  best fit**, because your topic can be anything this course covers
- **Entrepreneurship, alone:** plan a drone services business and pitch it. This becomes your capstone
  later in the year

**Virtual, submitted online:**

- **Promotional Photography:** photos shot and edited to this year's prompt. Aerial photos from our
  flights could fit
- **Multimedia and Promotion:** a short video, alone or a team. Flight footage could fit

**Virtual events are fine too.** You submit online and skip the in-person rounds. They usually need
registration in early December and a submission in January, so check the dates in the WSAP.
Read each event's guidelines in the WSAP before you choose. **The WSAP is what counts.**

## Topic ideas (7.9.4)

Pick one that uses what we study:

- Flying safely in bad weather: what pilots check and why
- Drones near airports: airspace and authorization
- Emergencies: lost link, flyaways, and battery failures
- Drones in search and rescue, farming, or inspections
- Why drone delivery is so hard
- Night flying: what changes

---

**1.** Which event, and alone or on a team? If a team, who?

**2.** Your topic, or your business idea, in one or two sentences. It can change later.

**3.** Which parts of this course will your topic use?

**4.** Why this one, in one sentence?

**Your first draft is due Fri Nov 13,** and you present it to the class Nov 16 to 18 and answer
questions.

## Graded on

All four answered. Question 3 counts the most.
