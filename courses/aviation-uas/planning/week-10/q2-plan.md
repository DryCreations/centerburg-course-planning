# Aviation UAS: Q2 Plan

**Course:** Aviation UAS (177024) | **Q2 | Tue Oct 20 to mid-January**

Part of each week is your BPA project. The rest is flying, review, Preflight, and ODOT modules. Plans
can shift with the weather and as we go.

---

## The quarter, roughly

| Weeks | BPA | The rest of the week |
|-------|-----|----------------------|
| **1** | Choose your event and topic | ODOT, review |
| **2 to 4** | Research | Preflight: charts, NOTAMs and TFRs, inspection |
| **5 to 7** | Build your presentation or plan | Flying when the weather allows |
| **8 to 9** | Practice and polish | Review |

## Quizzes

Every other week. **This Friday:** METARs, TAFs, Zulu time, the Part 107 weather limits, airspace,
TFRs, and writing to a client.

## Standards

  intended audience and purpose
- **7.9.4** Describe operating limitations for small unmanned aircraft systems
- **7.11.4** Describe weather products and resources required for preflight planning, current and
