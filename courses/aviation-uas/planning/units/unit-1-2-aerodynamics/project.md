# Project: Forces of Flight Analysis

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.2 — Aerodynamics & Physics of Flight
**Duration:** 2 weeks | **Weeks:** Weeks 3–4
**Tools:** Google Slides or Google Docs, calculator

---

## Overview

Before a drone ever leaves the ground, the pilot must understand *why* it flies and what affects its performance. This project asks you to apply the physics of flight to the specific platform you will fly — the DJI Mavic 3 — and communicate your findings clearly using diagrams and written analysis.

---

## Class Prompt

You will create a **Forces of Flight Analysis** for the DJI Mavic 3 using the specific platform's published specifications. Your final product is a technical slide deck or document that communicates the aerodynamics of this specific aircraft.

**You will create:**

- A labeled **four-forces diagram** for each of three flight states (hover, climb, forward flight)
- A **three-axis diagram** identifying pitch, roll, and yaw on the Mavic 3
- A **rotor diagram** showing CW/CCW rotation pattern and a written explanation of torque cancellation
- A **weight and balance scenario** worked to completion with numerical evidence and a conclusion
- A **density altitude analysis** for a provided weather scenario — go/no-go recommendation with justification

**Format:** Google Slides (8–12 slides) or a well-organized Google Doc with clearly labeled sections.

**Milestones:**

| Milestone | Description | Due |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| Part 1–2 complete | Four-forces + axis diagrams drafted | End of Week 3 |
| Parts 3–5 complete | Torque, weight/balance, density altitude | End of Week 4, Day 4 |
| Final submission | All parts polished; submitted to Google Classroom | End of Week 4, Day 5 |
