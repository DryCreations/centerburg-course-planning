### 1.
Which of the four forces of flight acts vertically downward on an aircraft?

A. Lift  
B. Drag  
C. Thrust  
D. Weight  

**Answer: D** | DOK 1 | 7.4.2

---

### 2.
What causes lift on an airfoil according to Bernoulli's Principle?

A. The downward pressure of the rotor blades compresses air below the aircraft  
B. Faster airflow over the curved upper surface creates lower pressure, producing an upward lift force  
C. The angle of attack pushes air forward, which generates thrust  
D. Increased air density above the wing pushes the aircraft upward  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.4.3

---

### 3.
On a multirotor drone, Newton's Third Law explains lift because:

A. Curved rotor blades create pressure differences between upper and lower surfaces  
B. Rotor blades push air downward, and the equal and opposite reaction pushes the aircraft upward  
C. Faster rotor speed reduces drag, increasing altitude  
D. The spinning rotors create centrifugal force that counteracts gravity  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.4.3

---

### 4.
Rotation of an aircraft around its lateral axis (wingtip to wingtip) is called:

A. Roll  
B. Yaw  
C. Pitch  
D. Torque  

**Answer: C** | DOK 1 | 7.4.2

---

### 5.
Adjacent rotors on a quadcopter spin in opposite directions primarily to:

A. Generate more lift at higher altitudes  
B. Reduce the battery drain from motor friction  
C. Cancel out torque reactions and prevent uncontrolled yaw  
D. Increase thrust without adding additional motors  

**Answer: C** | DOK 2 | 7.4.10

---

### 6.
The angle of attack is defined as the angle between:

A. The horizon and the aircraft's nose  
B. The chord line of the airfoil and the relative wind  
C. The rotor plane and the direction of gravity  
D. The aircraft's heading and the wind direction  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.4.4

---

### 7.
Density altitude affects drone performance because:

A. Higher density altitude means the air is denser, providing more lift  
B. Higher density altitude means thinner air, reducing rotor efficiency and lift  
C. Density altitude only affects fixed-wing aircraft, not multirotors  
D. Higher density altitude increases drag but has no effect on lift  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.4.5

---

### 8.
An aircraft is in steady, level flight at constant speed. Which statement about the four forces is correct?

A. Lift is greater than weight; thrust equals drag  
B. All four forces are in equilibrium (balanced)  
C. Thrust is greater than drag to maintain speed  
D. Weight is greater than lift to maintain altitude  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.4.2

### 10.
**[HOT]** It is a hot August afternoon, the flight site is at 2,100 ft elevation, and humidity is high. Compared to a cool, dry day at sea level, the drone's performance will:
