# Vocabulary: Preflight Preparation & Flight Planning

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 2.2 — Preflight Preparation & Flight Planning
**Weeks:** 13–15

> These terms cover FAA Part 107 preflight requirements, airport operations, and performance planning. They are among the most heavily tested topics on both the WebXam (7.11 = 14.58%) and the FAA Part 107 knowledge test. Many airspace/chart terms from Unit 1.3 will reappear here in operational context.

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK | FAA Guide Ch. |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|---------------|
| **Preflight Assessment** | A thorough inspection of the sUAS before flight, verifying airworthiness; required by Part 107 before every operation | 7.11.14 | 1 | Ch. 12 |
| **Airworthiness** | The condition of an aircraft being safe to fly; the remote PIC must verify airworthiness before every flight; any doubt = do not fly | 7.11.2 | 2 | Ch. 12 |
| **LAANC (Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability)** | FAA system providing near-real-time sUAS authorization in controlled airspace below approved altitude grids; accessed via B4UFLY, DroneZone, or approved apps | 7.9.4 | 2 | Ch. 2 |
| **B4UFLY** | FAA mobile and web application for checking airspace authorization status before a flight; uses current location to show restricted areas, TFRs, and LAANC availability | 7.11.8 | 1 | Ch. 2 |
| **FAA DroneZone** | FAA online portal (faadronezone.faa.gov) for UAS registration, LAANC authorization in NSAs, and voluntary authorization requests | 7.9.1 | 1 | Ch. 2 |
| **NOTAM (Notice to Air Missions)** | Time-critical aeronautical notices not published in regular charts; issued for hazards, TFRs, inoperable lights on obstacles, parachute operations, and other temporary conditions | 7.11.6 | 2 | Ch. 2 |
| **TFR (Temporary Flight Restriction)** | Regulatory action restricting aircraft in a defined area; common triggers: VIP movement, disasters, airshows, wildfires, sporting events; check tfr.faa.gov | 7.11.8 | 2 | Ch. 2 |
| **Cross-Country Flight Plan** | A flight plan documenting route, waypoints, altitude, estimated flight times, and emergency landing areas for a flight that covers significant distance | 7.11.7 | 3 | Ch. 2 |
| **Performance Limitations** | Manufacturer-specified limits on a UAS's speed, altitude, payload, wind tolerance, and temperature range; remote PIC must stay within these limits at all times | 7.11.9 | 2 | Ch. 8 |
| **Density Altitude** | Pressure altitude corrected for non-standard temperature; at high density altitude (hot, humid, high elevation), aircraft performance decreases — motors work harder for same thrust | 7.11.11 | 2 | Ch. 3b, 8 |
| **Weight and Balance (W&B)** | Calculation verifying total aircraft weight is within manufacturer limits and that payload weight does not shift center of gravity outside allowable range | 7.4.9 | 2 | Ch. 4 |
| **Center of Gravity (CG)** | The point at which all aircraft weight is concentrated; must remain within manufacturer limits; shifting CG (e.g., front-heavy payload) affects stability and control | 7.4.9 | 2 | Ch. 4 |
| **Load Factor** | The ratio of lift to the aircraft's weight; increases in banked turns; high load factors can exceed structural limits | 7.4.16 | 2 | Ch. 4 |
| **Non-Towered Airport** | An airport without an active control tower; traffic is self-managed; pilots use CTAF for position broadcasts; remote pilots must exercise extra vigilance near these airports | 7.5.1 | 1 | Ch. 11 |
| **CTAF (Common Traffic Advisory Frequency)** | A radio frequency at non-towered airports used by all aircraft to broadcast position and intentions; often UNICOM (122.8 MHz) or MULTICOM (122.9 MHz) | 7.7.7 | 2 | Ch. 7, 11 |
| **Chart Supplement U.S.** | FAA publication (formerly Airport/Facility Directory) listing airport information: runway data, frequencies, services, lighting; used with sectional charts for preflight planning | 7.5.3 | 1 | Ch. 11 |
| **Sectional Chart** | FAA aeronautical chart showing airspace classes, airports, terrain, obstacles, and navigation aids; scale 1:500,000; essential for flight planning and Part 107 exam | 7.11.7 | 2 | Ch. 2 |
| **Latitude and Longitude** | Geographic coordinates: latitude (parallels, N/S), longitude (meridians, E/W); used to precisely specify flight location for flight plans and airspace authorization | 7.10.10 | 1 | Ch. 11 |
| **Antenna Tower (Obstacle)** | A man-made structure depicted on sectional charts; may not appear on maps; must be checked during preflight site survey for low-altitude flights near structures | 7.11.5 | 2 | Ch. 11 |
| **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)** | Part 107 requirement that the remote PIC maintain unaided visual contact with the aircraft at all times; binoculars do not satisfy VLOS requirements | 7.9.3 | 1 | Ch. 1 |
| **Visual Observer (VO)** | A crew member who assists the remote PIC by maintaining VLOS with the aircraft while the PIC monitors the control station; VO must be briefed on emergency procedures | 7.9.7 | 2 | Ch. 5 |
| **Preflight Checklist** | A systematic step-by-step inspection sequence performed before each flight; covers aircraft physical condition, battery, controller link, GPS status, and airspace | 7.11.14 | 1 | Ch. 12 |
| **Before Takeoff Check** | Final check conducted immediately before lifting off; confirms GPS lock, compass calibration status, battery level, controller connection, and return-to-home setting | 7.11.18 | 1 | Ch. 12 |
| **Return to Home (RTH)** | Automated drone function that returns the aircraft to its takeoff point when triggered manually, on control link loss, or on low battery; RTH altitude must be set above obstacles before every flight | 7.11.13 | 2 | Ch. 12 |
| **Special Use Airspace (SUA)** | Airspace where operations are restricted (Prohibited, Restricted, Warning areas, MOAs, Alert areas, CFAs); requires coordination or authorization to enter | 7.11.8 | 2 | Ch. 2 |

## Key Distinctions

| Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| LAANC | FAA Waiver | LAANC provides near-real-time authorization for standard Part 107 flights in controlled airspace below published altitude grids; waivers are manual FAA approvals for flights that deviate from standard Part 107 rules |
| Towered Airport | Non-Towered Airport | Towered = ATC controls traffic; requires 2-way radio communication; Non-towered = pilots self-manage using CTAF broadcasts |
| NOTAM | TFR | NOTAMs cover a wide range of temporary aeronautical information; TFRs are a specific type of NOTAM that restricts all aircraft from a defined airspace area |
| VLOS | BVLOS | VLOS = required by standard Part 107; BVLOS = requires special FAA waiver |
| Preflight Assessment | Before Takeoff Check | Preflight = comprehensive inspection before you go to the field; Before Takeoff = final checks when aircraft is powered and ready to fly |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. You want to fly a commercial shoot 0.4 miles from a Class D airport at 150 ft AGL. The LAANC grid shows 0 ft authorized at that location. What are your options?
2. Explain the difference between a NOTAM and a TFR. Give an example of when each would be issued.
3. Your DJI Mavic 3 has a maximum certified payload of 0.79 lbs. You want to attach a thermal camera that weighs 1.1 lbs. What do you tell your client?
4. Before every flight you set the Return-to-Home altitude. What factors determine what altitude you should set? What happens if you set it too low?
5. You are flying near Knox County Airport (non-towered). Should you monitor the CTAF frequency? Why or why not? Should you transmit?

## Study Tips

- Memorize the Part 107 preflight legal minimum conditions: visibility ≥ 3 SM; 500 ft below clouds, 2,000 ft horizontal; daylight or civil twilight with anti-collision lighting
- LAANC vs. waiver: if the LAANC grid says 0 ft and you need to fly at 200 ft, your only option is a manual FAA waiver (can take weeks)
- Density altitude is tested heavily — remember: high temp + high humidity + high elevation = reduced battery life and reduced thrust efficiency
- Sectional chart reading: know Class B (solid blue), Class C (solid magenta), Class D (dashed blue), Class E (dashed magenta), Class G (no depiction) from memory
