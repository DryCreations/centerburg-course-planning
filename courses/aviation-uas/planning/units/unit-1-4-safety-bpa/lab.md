# Lab: DJI Hardware Orientation & Safety Inspection

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.4 — Safety, DJI Hardware Orientation & BPA Selection
**Duration:** 2 days | **Week:** Week 8
**Tools:** DJI Mavic 3 aircraft (classroom set), RC 2 controllers, DJI Assistant 2 (teacher device), LiPo storage bags, preflight checklist handout, Google Docs

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will:
- Handle the DJI Mavic 3 aircraft and RC 2 controller safely, following proper power-on/power-off procedures
- Complete a physical preflight safety inspection using the class preflight checklist
- Identify LiPo battery warning signs and demonstrate proper storage procedures
- Observe a firmware check demonstration using DJI Assistant 2 (teacher-led)

---

## Safety & Handling Rules

> These rules apply any time a drone is out of the case. No exceptions.

1. Aircraft stays on the table — no one holds it overhead or carries it while powered
2. Never spin motors by hand — the aircraft is only energized on teacher command
3. If you detect a swollen, hot, or damaged battery, set it down and call the teacher — do not attempt to fly or charge it
4. Props are NEVER installed during this lab — no flight will occur
5. No student activates the RC 2 or attempts to power on the aircraft without explicit instruction

## Day 1 — Battery Safety & Aircraft Inspection

### Station A — LiPo Battery Inspection (15 min)

Working in groups of 2–3, examine the drone batteries available at your station.

1. Inspect each battery for physical signs of damage: swelling, cracks, dents, discoloration, or unusual heat
2. Check the battery contact pins — are they clean and undamaged?
3. For each battery, fill out the **Battery Condition Log** (Google Doc):
   - Battery ID (marked on each battery)
   - Physical appearance (Normal / Concern — describe)
   - Contact pin condition (OK / Damaged)
   - Your recommendation: Ready to fly / Remove from service

4. Once complete, place each battery in its designated storage bag (do not leave batteries unbagged on the table)

### Station B — Aircraft Visual Inspection (20 min)

Using your **Aircraft Preflight Inspection Sheet** (provided), complete a visual inspection of one DJI Mavic 3:

| Component | What to Check | Status |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Airframe | No visible cracks, dents, or damage | ☐ |
| Arms | All 4 arms unfold fully; no damage at hinge points | ☐ |
| Motor mount | No cracking around each motor housing | ☐ |
| Propellers | *(Not installed during this lab — confirm props are stored separately)* | ☐ |
| Sensors (bottom) | Downward sensors clean and unobstructed | ☐ |
| Camera gimbal | Gimbal guard removed; gimbal moves freely | ☐ |
| SD card slot | Card properly seated or slot empty | ☐ |
| Battery compartment | Battery latch closes fully and locks | ☐ |
| RC 2 controller | Screen powers on (when teacher authorizes); sticks centered | ☐ |

5. Record any concerns in your Google Doc. Note that every aircraft discrepancy must be reported to the teacher before a real flight would occur.

### Station C — Fireproof Bag Demonstration (5 min)

Teacher demonstration: proper procedure for storing a LiPo battery in a fireproof bag and what to do if a battery becomes hot or begins to swell during a flight mission.

**Discussion:** Why can't a class C fire extinguisher be used on a LiPo fire? What IS the correct procedure?

## Day 2 — RC 2 Orientation & DJI Assistant 2 Demo

### Part 1 — RC 2 Controller Walkthrough (20 min)

Teacher demonstrates each component of the DJI RC 2 controller. Students annotate a printed or digital diagram identifying:

1. Power button (press-and-hold sequence)
2. Left stick (throttle / yaw) and right stick (pitch / roll)
3. Return-to-Home button
4. Take-off / land button
5. Video record button
6. Photo capture button
7. Antenna position (fold up for flight)
8. Built-in display screen — status indicators, battery, GPS, signal strength
9. Emergency Stop / kill switch combination (teacher explains position without demonstrating)

**Discussion:** What does the RC 2's built-in screen show you that eliminates the need for a phone? Why does this matter for outdoor flights?

### Part 2 — Firmware Check Demo (15 min)

Teacher connects the DJI Mavic 3 to the classroom iMac via USB-C and opens DJI Assistant 2.

Students observe and record in their Google Doc:
1. What version of firmware is currently installed on the aircraft?
2. Is there an available update?
3. What does the DJI Assistant 2 interface show about flight logs?
4. Why would a pilot always check firmware before a new flight season?

### Part 3 — Preflight Checklist Drill (10 min)

Using the annotated preflight checklist from the aircraft inspection, work through the entire checklist verbally as a class:
- Student A reads each item
- Student B physically checks (aircraft on table, no props, not powered)
- Class confirms or questions each step

**Exit Ticket (5 min):** In 3–4 sentences, describe what makes a LiPo battery dangerous and what two procedures this course uses to manage that risk.
