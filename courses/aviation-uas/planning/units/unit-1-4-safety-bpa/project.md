## Overview

This project has two parts delivered together at the end of Week 9:
1. A **UAS Safety Reference Portfolio** — a practical document you will use all year
2. A **BPA Event Selection Commitment** — your documented choice of primary competition event and a plan showing how this course leads to a competition-ready entry

---

## Class Prompt

### Part 1 — UAS Safety Reference Portfolio

Create a Google Doc titled: `[Your Name] — UAS Safety Reference Portfolio`

Your portfolio must include all of the following sections:

**Section 1: Hazard Identification & OSHA Overview**
- Define *hazard* in your own words
- Identify 3 specific hazards in the aviation UAS lab (battery, prop, hardware)
- For each hazard: what is the risk, and what is the mitigation procedure?
- Where can an SDS be found for the LiPo batteries used in this course? What information does it contain that a pilot should know?

**Section 2: LiPo Battery Safety Protocols**
- List the 4 warning signs of a compromised LiPo battery
- Describe the correct procedure if you discover a swollen battery at the start of class
- Explain what thermal runaway is and why a fireproof storage bag is used
- Include your completed Battery Condition Log from the Lab (paste or link)

**Section 3: DJI Hardware Checklist**
- Your completed and annotated preflight checklist from the lab
- A brief written explanation of why each section of the checklist matters (airframe, battery, RC, sensors)
- A note on firmware: what it controls and why it must be kept current

**Section 4: Emergency Procedures Quick Reference**
- Kill switch: when and how to use it; what NOT to do before using it
- Thermal runaway during flight: immediate response steps
- Loss of signal / flyaway: what the Return-to-Home system does; how you respond if RTH fails

**Section 5: Personal Reflection**
- In 1–2 paragraphs: What is the most important thing you learned from the hardware orientation that you will use when we begin actual flight operations in Semester 2?
