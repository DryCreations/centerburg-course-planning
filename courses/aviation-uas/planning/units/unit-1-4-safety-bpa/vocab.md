## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration)** | U.S. government agency responsible for creating and enforcing workplace safety and health standards | 2.1.1 | 1 |
| **Hazard** | Any source of potential damage, harm, or adverse health effects on persons or property | 2.1.2 | 1 |
| **Right-to-Know** | OSHA provision requiring employers to inform employees of hazardous substances in the workplace; employees have the legal right to know what they are working with | 2.2.1 | 1 |
| **Safety Data Sheet (SDS)** | A document that provides information on the properties of a hazardous chemical, including its health effects, first aid measures, and safe handling procedures; replaces the older MSDS format | 2.1.9 | 1 |
| **Lithium Polymer (LiPo) Battery** | Rechargeable battery technology used in most sUAS; provides high energy density; requires careful handling — prone to thermal runaway if punctured, overcharged, or stored improperly | 2.2.1 | 2 |
| **Thermal Runaway** | A condition in a LiPo battery where increasing temperature causes a self-reinforcing reaction that can result in fire or explosion; caused by overcharging, damage, or manufacturing defect | 7.14.14 | 2 |
| **Fireproof Storage Bag** | A fire-resistant container for storing LiPo batteries; used to contain a battery fire if thermal runaway occurs during storage | 2.2.1 | 1 |
| **Airworthiness** | The condition of an aircraft that makes it fit for safe flight; a UAS is airworthy when all systems function as designed and no damage or hazard exists | 7.11.2 | 1 |
| **Preflight Checklist** | A standardized sequence of checks performed before every flight to verify the aircraft and all systems are in an airworthy condition | 7.11.14 | 1 |
| **Firmware** | Permanent software programmed into a hardware device (such as the DJI Mavic 3 flight controller) that controls low-level operations; must be kept up to date for safety and performance | 1.4.4 | 1 |
| **DJI Assistant 2** | Desktop software application for DJI aircraft that enables firmware updates, flight parameter configuration, and diagnostic checks; installed on teacher computer | 1.4.4 | 1 |
| **DJI RC 2 Controller** | The DJI Mavic 3 remote controller used in this course; features a built-in high-brightness touchscreen display — no phone or additional app required for flight operations | 1.4.4 | 1 |
| **DJI Fly** | DJI's mobile app (iOS/Android) for controlling DJI drones via the RC-N1 controller; *not required in this course* (the RC 2 has a built-in screen), but widely used in the consumer drone industry and relevant for personal or freelance operations | 1.4.2 | 1 |
| **Propeller Guard** | Physical protection rings around drone propellers that reduce injury risk and prop damage; used during indoor/proximity flight | 2.2.12 | 1 |
| **Emergency Stop (Kill Switch)** | A control input that immediately stops all motor rotation; used as a last resort when the aircraft poses an imminent danger and cannot be controlled by normal means | 7.14.15 | 2 |
| **WSAP (Workplace Skills Assessment Program)** | BPA's scoring system for competition events; defines exactly what judges evaluate and how points are awarded | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **BPA Event Selection** | The process by which students choose the BPA competition event(s) they will prepare for; selection should align with student interests and the course projects already planned | 1.1.4 | 2 |
| **Portfolio** | A curated collection of student work that demonstrates skills, growth, and career readiness; used in BPA events and for post-secondary applications | 1.1.5 | 2 |
| **ANSI Safety Symbol** | Standardized symbols used to communicate hazards without relying on language; commonly used on industrial equipment and in workplaces (e.g., caution triangles, prohibition symbols) | 2.1.4 | 1 |
| **Confined Space** | Any space large enough for a person to enter and work, but not designed for continuous occupancy; associated with special OSHA entry procedures; not typical for UAS lab but required OSHA knowledge | 2.1.1 | 1 |

## Key Distinctions

| Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Thermal Runaway | Normal battery discharge | Normal discharge is controlled energy release during operation; thermal runaway is a dangerous, self-reinforcing chemical reaction that can cause fire |
| DJI RC 2 Controller | DJI Assistant 2 | RC 2 is the flight controller (used in class, built-in screen); DJI Assistant 2 is the desktop app for firmware updates and configuration — both are tools in this course but used at different times |
| Emergency Stop (Kill Switch) | Return to Home (RTH) | RTH commands the drone to navigate safely back and land; kill switch immediately cuts motors — it is a last resort used only when RTH is not possible |

## Study Tips

- LiPo battery safety (thermal runaway, storage, charging) appears in both the safety standards (Strand 2) and emergency operations standards (7.14.14) — learn it twice, from both angles
- Know the four signs of a damaged LiPo: swelling/puffing, heat, smell of chemicals, puncture or dent — any of these = do not use
- Connecting BPA event selection to career goals (DOK 2) is the kind of thinking tested in Strand 1 questions
