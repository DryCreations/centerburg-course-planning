## Questions

### 1.
Under Part 107, Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) means the remote pilot must be able to see the aircraft:

A. Using binoculars at any range necessary
B. With unaided vision (glasses or contacts are acceptable) at all times, without the aid of binoculars or telescopes
C. Via a first-person view (FPV) camera on the aircraft
D. Within 0.5 nautical miles regardless of visual conditions

> **Answer: B** — VLOS requires unaided natural eyesight; FPV does not substitute for VLOS (7.13.1)

### 2.
The phonetic word for the letter "G" is:

A. George
B. Golf
C. Good
D. Gamma

> **Answer: B** — NATO phonetic alphabet: G = Golf (7.7.1)

### 3.
"Cessna Eight-Seven-Niner-Kilo, downwind runway two-seven, Knox County." In this radio transmission, what is the aircraft's call sign?

A. Knox County Airport
B. Runway two-seven
C. Cessna 879K
D. Downwind

> **Answer: C** — The call sign is Cessna 879K (phonetically: Eight-Seven-Niner-Kilo) (7.7.1)

### 4.
UNICOM frequency 122.8 is used primarily for:

A. Filing IFR flight plans
B. Traffic advisories and airport information at non-towered airports
C. Emergency communications on any frequency
D. ATC clearances at Class B airports

> **Answer: B** — UNICOM 122.8 provides traffic advisories at non-towered airports (7.7.7)

### 5. [HOT]
A remote pilot is flying at a rural site 0.6 miles from a non-towered airport. A Cessna announces on CTAF that it is on a 2-mile final to land. What should the remote pilot do?

A. Land immediately — all UAS flights must stop whenever manned aircraft are operating
B. Continue the mission — the Cessna is still 2 miles out, which is far enough away
C. Monitor the situation; confirm the UAS altitude and position are not in the Cessna's flight path; be prepared to land if the aircraft approaches
D. Transmit on 122.8 to inform the Cessna pilot of the drone's position

> **Answer: C** — Monitor and be prepared to yield; remote pilots have right-of-way responsibility to avoid manned aircraft; transmitting on CTAF is only appropriate in emergency (7.13.1, 7.7.7)

---

### 6.
Crew Resource Management (CRM) in a single-pilot UAS operation means:

A. The pilot must always have a ground crew of at least two people
B. Effective use of all available resources (checklist, visual observer, automation, weather data) to safely complete the flight
C. Radio communication with ATC prior to every flight
D. Maintaining a crew roster for every commercial flight

> **Answer: B** — CRM/SRM is about using all resources effectively for safe operations (7.8.9)

---

### 7. [HOT]
During a flight mission, the DJI Mavic 3's GPS signal drops and the aircraft transitions to ATTI mode without warning. The pilot should:

A. Activate the FPV camera to regain visual awareness and continue the mission
B. Immediately use autonomous RTH
C. Manually fly the aircraft using visual reference, correcting for wind drift; land as soon as possible
D. Wait for GPS to re-acquire — ATTI mode is safe for extended flight

> **Answer: C** — In ATTI mode, the pilot must manually compensate for drift and land promptly (7.13.3)

---

### 8.
The primary difference between GPS mode and ATTI mode on a DJI Mavic 3 is:

A. GPS mode limits altitude; ATTI mode allows full range
B. GPS mode uses satellite position to hold place; ATTI mode uses only barometric and gyroscopic sensors — the aircraft will drift with wind
C. GPS mode is only available in Class G airspace
D. ATTI mode provides more precise positioning than GPS mode

> **Answer: B** — GPS uses satellite position hold; ATTI uses internal sensors only, causing wind drift (7.13.3)

---

### 9.
A ground reference maneuver is used to:

A. Practice emergency engine-out procedures
B. Test autonomous waypoint navigation
C. Practice maintaining a consistent ground track while compensating for wind drift
D. Calibrate the compass before flight

> **Answer: C** — Ground reference maneuvers develop pilot wind correction skills (7.12.7)

---

### 10.
The flight log is important for commercial UAS operators because it:

A. Is required by FAA Part 107 for every flight
B. Documents aircraft performance, maintenance needs, and operational history — essential for identifying patterns and demonstrating due diligence
C. Substitutes for the preflight checklist when time is limited
D. Only matters for flights conducted in controlled airspace

> **Answer: B** — Flight logs document performance history and maintenance needs; Part 107 does not mandate logs but professional practice requires them (7.13.6)

### 11.
Automation bias is best described as:

A. Preference for manual flight over automated systems
B. Over-reliance on automated systems leading to reduced active monitoring and missed warning signs
C. The drone's autopilot making biased decisions during waypoint missions
D. Bias in AI object-detection algorithms used in intelligent flight modes

> **Answer: B** — Automation bias is a human factors hazard; automation does not replace active monitoring (7.8.2)

### 12. [HOT]
A pilot sets up an automated waypoint mission over a construction site. Halfway through the mission, an excavator moves into the planned flight path. The pilot should:

A. Allow the autonomous mission to continue — obstacle avoidance sensors will detect the excavator
B. Override the autonomous mission immediately and manually fly the aircraft clear of the new obstacle
C. Reduce altitude below the waypoint plan and continue the mission
D. Notify the excavator operator via cell phone and continue the mission

> **Answer: B** — The remote PIC must be ready to override autonomous modes at any time; obstacle avoidance is not infallible (7.13.11)

---

### 13.
Which flight maneuver best tests a pilot's ability to manage wind drift during a real-world mapping mission?

A. Hover in place for 60 seconds
B. Yaw 360° while climbing to altitude
C. Fly a rectangular course while maintaining straight parallel legs
D. Activate RTH and observe the return path

> **Answer: C** — Rectangular course requires constant wind correction to maintain straight legs and 90° turns — mirrors actual survey grid flying (7.12.7)

---

### 14.
Before using a DJI intelligent flight mode (such as ActiveTrack), the remote pilot should:

A. Read and understand the mode's behavior and how to immediately override it
B. Disable VLOS requirements since the aircraft will navigate autonomously
C. Obtain a Part 107 waiver for automated flight
D. Switch to ATTI mode to enable intelligent flight

> **Answer: A** — Understanding the behavior and override process is required before engaging any autonomous mode (7.13.11)

### 15.
Situational awareness (SA) in UAS operations refers to:

A. Knowledge of the aircraft's battery percentage only
B. Continuous perception of aircraft status, airspace, weather, surrounding environment, and people — and the ability to project how those factors will change
C. The pilot's ability to maintain VLOS at distances greater than 1 mile
D. The visual observer's role in watching the aircraft while the pilot monitors the screen

> **Answer: B** — SA is a comprehensive, continuous awareness of all relevant environmental factors (7.8.1)

### 16.
The post-flight inspection should be completed:

A. Only after incidents or hard landings
B. Annually by a certified UAV technician
C. After every flight; before the next flight begins
D. At the end of each week regardless of how many flights occurred

> **Answer: C** — Post-flight inspection after every flight catches problems before the next flight (7.12.2)

### 17.
MULTICOM frequency 122.9 is used:

A. At Class B airports only
B. Exclusively for emergency communications
C. For pilot-to-pilot communications at areas without a CTAF or UNICOM designation
D. As an alternative to the CTAF at towered airports

> **Answer: C** — MULTICOM 122.9 is used where no UNICOM or CTAF is established (7.7.7)

### 18. [HOT]
A student pilot completes their preflight check and notes: wind from 240° at 12 knots, gusting to 20 knots. The DJI Mavic 3's maximum wind resistance is 26.8 mph. Can the pilot safely fly?

A. No — any wind over 10 knots makes UAS flight unsafe
B. Yes — 20 knots (23 mph) is within the Mavic 3's wind resistance rating, but conditions should be re-evaluated frequently given the gusts
C. Yes — gusts only last a few seconds so they are irrelevant to mission planning
D. No — the maximum wind resistance of 26.8 mph must be a sustained value, and gusts are unpredictable

> **Answer: B** — 20 knots ≈ 23 mph, within the Mavic 3's rated limit; however, gusty conditions warrant caution and continuous monitoring (7.13.5, Ch. 8)

### 19.
When completing a flight log, which field is most critical for aircraft maintenance tracking?

A. Pilot name
B. Flight purpose
C. Aircraft serial number / ID and total flight time
D. Wind direction

> **Answer: C** — Aircraft ID and accumulated flight time track when service intervals are due (7.12.2)

### 20.
The remote pilot in command (PIC) can share duties with a visual observer (VO) because:

A. Part 107 requires a VO for all commercial flights
B. A VO can maintain VLOS while the PIC monitors the flight controller screen, improving overall situational awareness and division of workload
C. The VO is licensed to fly the aircraft if the remote PIC becomes fatigued
D. VO duties are defined by ATC, not the remote PIC

> **Answer: B** — VO enables workload division and enhanced VLOS coverage (7.9.7, Ch. 6)
