# Vocabulary: Flight Operations & Communication

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.2 — Flight Operations & Communication
**Weeks:** 22–25
**FAA Study Guide Reference:** Chapters 6, 7

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | FAA Ch. | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|---------|-----|
| **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)** | The remote pilot's unaided ability to see the aircraft at all times during flight; required under Part 107 except by waiver | 7.13.1 | 1 | 1 |
| **Crew Resource Management (CRM)** | The effective use of all available resources — personnel, equipment, information — to achieve safe and efficient flight operations; also called SRM (Single-pilot Resource Management) when applied to solo operations | 7.8.9 | 6 | 2 |
| **Single-Pilot Resource Management (SRM)** | The application of CRM principles to situations where one pilot manages all tasks; requires task prioritization and automation management | 7.8.9 | 6, 10 | 3 |
| **Phonetic Alphabet** | NATO standardized letter pronunciations (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie…) used in aviation radio communication to eliminate letter confusion; remote pilots monitoring aviation frequencies must understand these | 7.7.1 | 7 | 1 |
| **Common Traffic Advisory Frequency (CTAF)** | A radio frequency (designated per airport) used for pilots to self-announce their position and intentions at non-towered airports; remote pilots should monitor CTAF when operating near non-towered airports | 7.7.7 | 7 | 2 |
| **UNICOM** | A private aeronautical advisory station, typically at non-towered airports, operating on 122.8 MHz; provides traffic advisories and airport information | 7.7.7 | 7 | 1 |
| **MULTICOM** | Frequency 122.9 MHz used for pilot-to-pilot communications at areas without an established CTAF or UNICOM | 7.7.7 | 7 | 1 |
| **Ground Reference Maneuver** | A flight exercise in which the pilot maintains a consistent ground track relative to a fixed point or shape (rectangle, circle, figure-8) while compensating for wind drift | 7.12.7 | — | 2 |
| **Attitude Mode (ATTI Mode)** | DJI flight mode that disables GPS positioning; aircraft uses only barometric altitude and internal sensors; pilot must manually compensate for wind drift; requires more skill | 7.13.3 | 8 | 2 |
| **Intelligent Flight Mode** | A DJI automated flight feature (ActiveTrack, Point of Interest, Waypoints, etc.) that automates specific flight behaviors; the remote PIC remains responsible for safety and must be ready to override | 7.13.11 | 12 | 2 |
| **Post-Flight Inspection** | A systematic check of the aircraft and all components after each flight to identify damage, battery wear, or mechanical issues before the next flight | 7.12.2 | 12 | 1 |
| **Flight Log** | A written or digital record of each flight including date, location, pilot, aircraft ID, flight duration, notes on performance or issues; required practice for commercial operators | 7.13.6 | 12 | 1 |
| **Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability (LAANC)** | FAA's automated airspace authorization system for sUAS; provides near-real-time authorization in designated airspace grids up to the LAANC altitude ceiling | 7.11.8 | 2 | 2 |
| **Automation Bias** | The tendency to over-trust automated systems (GPS, autopilot) and under-monitor aircraft performance; leads to missed warning signs; countered by active scan habits | 7.8.2 | 10 | 3 |
| **Situational Awareness (SA)** | The continuous perception and comprehension of all relevant factors in the environment — aircraft status, airspace, weather, people, obstacles — and projection of their future states | 7.8.1 | 10 | 3 |
