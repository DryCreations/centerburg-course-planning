# Lab: Hands-On Flight Operations + Radio & Communication Drills

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.2 — Flight Operations & Communication
**Duration:** 4 days | **Weeks:** 22–23
**Equipment:** DJI Mavic 3 fleet (6 aircraft), DJI RC 2 controllers, FAA sectional chart
**FAA Study Guide Reference:** Chapters 6, 7, 12

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will:
- Complete the basic flight sequence and at least two ground reference maneuvers
- Demonstrate correct preflight and post-flight procedures
- Recite the phonetic alphabet and correctly decode a call sign
- Fill out a flight log for each lab flight
- Describe the differences between GPS, ATTI, and intelligent flight modes

---

## Safety Briefing (Day 22-1 Opening — Before Any Aircraft Powers Up)

> This is required before every flight session. Complete as a class. No exceptions.

**Pre-flight Safety Review (5 minutes):**
Review the 4 flyaway scenarios:
1. **GPS Signal Loss** — aircraft enters ATTI mode; pilot must manually arrest drift
2. **Control Link Failure** — aircraft will execute failsafe (RTH or hover-then-land depending on settings)
3. **Battery Critical Warning** — aircraft will descend; pilot must land immediately; do not attempt to fly to a preferred landing zone if battery is critical
4. **Flyaway** — fly-away is rare with DJI but possible if compass interference occurs; cancel RTH and manually land if RTH heading is wrong

> All flights today operate within VLOS at all times. One person flies. Others observe. No one approaches the aircraft while rotors are spinning.

### Day 22-1 — Basic Flight Sequence (Ground Curriculum + Demo Flight)

**Classroom Portion (30 minutes):**

1. Walk through the DJI Mavic 3 preflight checklist from memory (reference lab from Unit 2.2):
   - Aircraft inspection
   - Battery level ≥ 75% for a 10-minute mission
   - GPS: 8+ satellites before takeoff
   - RTH altitude: set to clear tallest obstacle in return path
   - Controller firmware and app current

2. Identify the four basic flight maneuvers you will demonstrate today:
   - Hover in place (GPS mode) for 30 seconds — maintain within 2 ft of starting position

### Day 23-2 — Ground Reference Maneuvers

**Setup:** Mark a ground reference point (cone, flag, or natural feature). Wind direction must be estimated before flying.

**Maneuver 1 — Rectangular Course:**
- Fly a rectangle with sides approximately 100 ft long
- Goal: each leg is parallel or perpendicular to the ground reference
- Wind correction: increase bank/speed on downwind legs, decrease on upwind legs
- Complete 2 laps; switch pilots

**Maneuver 2 — Orbit (Point of Interest):**
- Manually orbit a fixed point at 50 ft AGL and 100 ft radius
- Maintain constant altitude, constant radius, and keep camera pointed at reference point
- Complete 1 clockwise orbit, 1 counterclockwise orbit

**Maneuver 3 — Figure-8 (Advanced / Optional):**
- Two overlapping orbits using a figure-8 pattern
- Tests pilot ability to transition between left and right turns smoothly

After maneuvers, transition to **Intelligent Modes Demo:**
- Teacher demonstrates DJI ActiveTrack on one aircraft (class observes — no student operation of autonomous modes until briefed on override procedures)
- Teacher demonstrates Waypoint Mission: pre-planned 3-point mission at 100 ft AGL over the flight area

### Day 24-3 — Communication Drills

**Part A: Phonetic Alphabet Drill (10 minutes)**
- Say each letter of the alphabet using the NATO phonetic word
- Teacher calls a random letter; students must respond with the correct phonetic word within 2 seconds
- Partner drill: one partner says a 3-letter combination (e.g., "GKP"), the other phonetically spells it back

**Part B: Radio Listen + Decode Exercise**
Using aviationweather.gov or LiveATC.net (class demo only — not student accounts):
- Listen to 3 sample pilot transmissions from a non-towered airport
- For each, decode and write:
  - Aircraft type and call sign (using phonetic alphabet)
  - Which airport are they announcing at?
  - What is the pilot's intended action?
  - Are they arriving or departing?

**Sample transmissions to decode (use these if live audio is not available):**
1. "Cirrus Seven-Seven-Four-Alpha-Bravo, downwind runway two-seven, Knox County"
2. "Cessna Five-Three-Two-Papa-Quebec, 10 miles south, inbound full stop, Knox County"
3. "Piper Eight-Eight-Lima, departing runway niner, left turn on course, Knox County"

**Part C: Airspace Question Drill**
Using the sectional chart (Knox County area), answer these questions:
1. What is the CTAF for Knox County Airport (KOXD)?
2. What class of airspace is Knox County Airport in?
3. Identify one airport within 20 NM that has a control tower. What is its ATIS frequency?
4. What frequency would you use to self-announce at an airport with no UNICOM published?

### Day 25-4 — Aerial Photography Mission

**Objective:** Complete a structured aerial photo/video mission using the DJI Mavic 3 as a real commercial operator would.

**Mission Brief (to be completed before flight):**
- Location: designated school-approved flight area
- Mission: photograph the school building from 4 cardinal directions at 100 ft AGL
- Authorization: confirm B4UFLY and airspace (Class G, no authorization required)
- Weather brief: check aviationweather.gov; record conditions on flight log
- RTH altitude: set appropriately for trees and flagpole in area

**Post-Flight Analysis:**
- Transfer photos to computer
- Review for: consistent framing, horizon level, exposure, sharpness
- Select your 2 best images from each of the 4 cardinal directions
- Write one sentence per photo: what was the composition goal and did you achieve it?
