# Vocabulary: FAA Part 107 Exam Preparation

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.2 — FAA Part 107 Exam Preparation & WebXam Review
**Weeks:** 31–32
**FAA Study Guide Reference:** All Chapters (1–12, Appendix 2)

> This unit is a focused review of the highest-weighted FAA Part 107 knowledge areas. All terms below appear frequently on the Part 107 Aeronautical Knowledge Test.

| Term | Definition | FAA Ch. | DOK |
|------|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Part 107 Knowledge Test** | The FAA written exam required to obtain a Remote Pilot Certificate for small UAS; 60 questions, 2-hour time limit, 70% passing score; administered at PSI testing centers | 1 | 1 |
| **Remote Pilot Certificate** | The FAA certificate required to operate a UAS commercially (or for compensation); renewed every 24 months via online recurrent training (ALC-677) | 1 | 1 |
| **Recurrent Training (ALC-677)** | Free FAA online course at FAASafety.gov; required every 24 months to maintain Remote Pilot Certificate currency | 1 | 1 |
| **Class G Airspace** | Uncontrolled airspace from the surface (or 700/1200 ft AGL where Class E begins) up to the base of Class E; sUAS may operate in Class G without ATC authorization | 2 | 1 |
| **Class E Airspace** | Controlled airspace that begins at 700 ft or 1,200 ft AGL in most areas, or at the surface near some airports; sUAS require authorization to operate in Class E at the surface | 2 | 2 |
| **METAR** | Routine aviation weather observation; key decode fields: ICAO ID, date/time (Z), wind (KT), visibility (SM), sky condition (BKN/OVC + altitude ×100 ft), temperature/dew point, altimeter | 3a | 2 |
| **TAF** | Terminal Aerodrome Forecast; covers a specific airport; valid 24 or 30 hours; uses same codes as METAR; includes TEMPO and BECMG change groups | 3a | 2 |
| **SIGMET** | Significant Meteorological Information — severe weather advisory affecting all aircraft: thunderstorms, extreme turbulence, icing, volcanic ash; must be avoided | 3a | 2 |
| **AIRMET Tango** | AIRMET for moderate turbulence, strong surface winds ≥30 kts, or low-level wind shear | 3a | 2 |
| **AIRMET Sierra** | AIRMET for IFR conditions (ceiling <1,000 ft or visibility <3 SM) and/or mountain obscuration | 3a | 2 |
| **AIRMET Zulu** | AIRMET for moderate icing | 3a | 2 |
| **Density Altitude** | Pressure altitude corrected for non-standard temperature and humidity; high density altitude (hot, humid, high elevation) reduces lift and motor efficiency | 3b, 8 | 3 |
| **Airspace Classes A–G** | A = 18,000+ ft MSL (FL180+); B = busiest airports, 0–10,000 MSL; C = moderate-traffic airports, 0–4,000 AGL; D = towers, surface to ~2,500 AGL; E = controlled 700+ AGL; G = uncontrolled | 2 | 2 |
| **Sectional Chart** | 1:500,000 scale aeronautical chart showing airspace, airports, obstacles, terrain, and navigation aids; primary tool for airspace interpretation | 2, 11 | 2 |
| **Chart Supplement U.S.** | Published database of airport operational information: runways, frequencies, services, hours; formerly called Airport Facility Directory (AFD) | 11 | 1 |
| **5 Hazardous Attitudes** | Anti-Authority, Impulsivity, Invulnerability, Macho, Resignation — with corresponding FAA-prescribed antidotes | 10 | 2 |
| **DECIDE Model** | 6-step ADM framework: Detect → Estimate → Choose → Identify → Do → Evaluate | 10 | 3 |
| **Load Factor** | The ratio of lift to the aircraft's weight; increases in banked turns; increased load factor increases effective weight and stall speed | 4 | 2 |
| **§107.51 — Operating Limitations** | No higher than 400 ft AGL (or within 400 ft of a structure); 3 SM visibility; 500 ft below clouds, 2,000 ft horizontal from clouds; daylight/civil twilight only without waiver | 1 | 1 |
| **Remote ID** | Broadcasting system broadcasting UAS identification, location, and operator location in real time; required for all registered UAS as of September 2023 | 1 | 1 |
