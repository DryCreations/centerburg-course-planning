## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will:
- Complete a 60-question timed practice exam simulating the Part 107 knowledge test
- Score and analyze results by FAA knowledge area
- Complete 3 targeted drill sessions on the lowest-scoring areas
- Decode a METAR and TAF from aviationweather.gov
- Demonstrate sectional chart reading at a timed drill pace

---

## Background

> The Part 107 Aeronautical Knowledge Test is 60 questions, 120 minutes, 70% passing (42/60 correct). The questions test practical application of the FAA study guide — not recall of definitions. Scenario-based questions with charts, METARs, and illustrations account for a large portion of the test. This lab mirrors actual test conditions.

### Day 32-2 — Targeted Drill Sessions + Live Weather Decode

**Drill Set A: Airspace (Ch. 2) — 15 minutes**

Using the Knox County, Ohio sectional chart excerpt (teacher provides), answer:

1. Identify the airspace class at KOXD (Knox County Airport) at the surface.
2. Identify the airspace class at 1,500 ft AGL over Knox County Airport.
3. A Restricted area is depicted at R-4401. What must a pilot do before entering an active Restricted area?
4. What is the floor of Class E airspace shown by the magenta vignette around a small airport?
5. A blue airport symbol with a tower icon = Class ___ airspace at the surface.
6. Identify one Prohibited area on the chart. Why are Prohibited areas established?
7. What symbol indicates a non-towered airport with no instrument approaches?
8. If you plan to fly within Class C airspace, you must first: ___

**Drill Set B: METAR/TAF Decode (Ch. 3a) — 15 minutes**

Access aviationweather.gov → Products → METARs. Pull the current METAR for:
- KOXD (Knox County Airport, OH)
- KLCK (Rickenbacker Airport, OH)
- KCMH (Columbus International, OH)

For each METAR, decode and record:
- Wind: direction ___ °, speed ___ kts, gusts ___
- Visibility: ___ SM
- Sky condition: ___ (___ ft AGL)
- Temperature / Dew point: ___°C / ___°C
- Go / No-Go for a Part 107 flight today?

Then access a TAF for KCMH. Find the period with the lowest forecasted visibility. What weather code causes it?

**Drill Set C: ADM / Human Factors (Ch. 9, 10) — 15 minutes**

For each scenario, identify the hazardous attitude and write the antidote:

1. A client insists you fly despite 25-knot winds. You take off anyway because "you've flown in worse."
2. You haven't flown in 3 months and feel nervous, but tell yourself "I'll figure it out in the air."
3. A new TFR was published near your site. You think "regulations are always overly cautious — I'll fly anyway."
4. Your IMSAFE check reveals you slept only 4 hours. You fly because you feel fine.
5. ATC issues a warning and you ignore it because "ATC doesn't control drones."
