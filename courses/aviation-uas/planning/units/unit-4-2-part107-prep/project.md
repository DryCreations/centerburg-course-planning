# Project: Part 107 Study Accountability & WebXam Preparation

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.2 — FAA Part 107 Exam Preparation & WebXam Review
**Duration:** Weeks 31–32
**WebXam Initial Test Target:** Week 32 (April 23)

---

## Overview

This is a study accountability and demonstration project — not a traditional deliverable. Students document their preparation process and demonstrate readiness for both the WebXam and the FAA Part 107 Knowledge Test. The project verifies that every student has done structured, evidence-based preparation rather than passive review.

---

## Deliverables

### Deliverable 1: Practice Test Score Analysis (from lab)
Submit your completed practice exam score sheet with:
- Total score: ___ / 60 (___ %)
- Chapter-by-chapter miss analysis table (completed)
- Your 3 highest-miss chapters identified
- Written explanation (3–5 sentences): What do you think caused your misses in each high-miss chapter?

### Deliverable 2: Personalized Study Plan
Based on your practice exam miss analysis, create a 5-day study plan covering the time between lab Day 31-1 and the WebXam. For each of the 5 days:
- Date
- Topic (FAA chapter + specific subtopics)
- Study resource (FAA study guide pages, Sporty's course, YouTube drill, flash cards)
- Goal: what can you correctly answer after this session?
- Evidence: attach a selfie/screenshot or brief written note confirming you completed it

### Deliverable 3: Flash Card Set
Create a minimum 30-card digital or physical flash card set covering the most heavily tested topics:
- Airspace class definitions (at least 6 cards)
- METAR decode fields (at least 6 cards)
- 5 hazardous attitudes + antidotes (5 cards)
- Part 107 operating limitations §107.51 (at least 4 cards)
- Density altitude factors and effects (at least 3 cards)
- Airport operations — Chart Supplement fields (at least 3 cards)
- Emergency procedures — §107.9 and §107.21 (2 cards)
- Your personal weak-area cards (remaining minimum)

Flash cards may be created in Quizlet, Anki, Gimkit, paper index cards (photographed), or equivalent.

### Deliverable 5: WebXam Reflection (submitted after the test)
Write a half-page reflection answering:
1. What score did you receive on the WebXam Initial Test?
2. Which question types were easiest? Which were hardest?
3. If you need to retake (before May 23), what is your specific 2-week plan?
4. How does the WebXam compare to the FAA Part 107 practice exam in difficulty and question style?

---

## Grading

This project is graded for completion and evidence of genuine effort. No student is graded on their WebXam score — the score is reported separately. The project earns full credit if:
- All 5 deliverables submitted on time
- Each deliverable demonstrates real preparation work (not minimal effort)
- Reflection is honest and specific

**Point Value:** 20 points (4 per deliverable)

---

## Note on FAA Part 107 Exam

> The Part 107 knowledge test is not required for this class. Students who wish to obtain their Remote Pilot Certificate may take the exam at a PSI testing center ($175 fee, paid by student or family). The Part 107 exam is available throughout the year — students who want to take it during their junior year should register before the end of Q4. The skills developed in this class directly map to the exam's knowledge areas.
