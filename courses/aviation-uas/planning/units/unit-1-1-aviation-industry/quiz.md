## Questions

### 1.
Which airspace class surrounds the nation's busiest airports and requires ATC clearance for all operations?

A. Class C  
B. Class D  
C. Class B  
D. Class E  

**Answer: C** | DOK 1 | 7.1.9

---

### 2.
Class G airspace is best described as:

A. Controlled airspace surrounding towered airports  
B. Uncontrolled airspace where ATC services are not provided  
C. Airspace reserved exclusively for military operations  
D. Airspace above 18,000 ft MSL  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.1.9

---

### 3.
LAANC provides sUAS operators with:

A. A temporary pilot certificate for recreational flying  
B. Near real-time airspace authorization to fly in controlled airspace below approved altitude ceilings  
C. Insurance coverage for commercial drone operations  
D. Automatic approval to fly anywhere in the National Airspace System  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.4

---

### 4.
Which of the following is an example of a commercial UAS operation requiring a Remote Pilot Certificate?

A. A hobbyist flying his personal drone in his backyard for fun  
B. A student flying a school-owned drone during a class exercise  
C. A real estate photographer charging a client for aerial photos of a home listing  
D. A child flying a toy drone under 0.55 lbs at a park  

**Answer: C** | DOK 2 | 7.9.1

---

### 5.
A Temporary Flight Restriction (TFR) is issued to:

A. Permanently close airspace around major airports  
B. Temporarily restrict certain aircraft from operating in a defined area  
C. Authorize UAS operations in Class B airspace  
D. Notify pilots of permanent changes to navigation procedures  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.11.8

### 6.
Which professional organization is most directly focused on the UAS industry?

### 7.
Remote ID requires a UAS to broadcast:

A. Its maintenance history and owner's insurance information

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.3

### 8.
A Notice to Air Missions (NOTAM) is best described as:

A. A permanent change to the airspace structure published in the Federal Register  
B. An official notice containing information about temporary hazards or changes in aeronautical information

### 9.
**[HOT]** A student researching UAS careers discovers that precision agriculture is one of the fastest-growing application areas. Which best explains why agricultural operations drive so much UAS demand?

A. Farmers are required by law to use drones to monitor crops  
B. Drones can survey large areas quickly and collect data (NDVI, soil moisture, irrigation mapping) that improves yield while reducing labor and chemical costs

### 10.
**[HOT]** Class D airspace requires two-way radio communication with the control tower before entering. A student asks: "Why would a drone operator need to know this if drones don't have radios?" Which is the best answer?

A. Part 107 exempts sUAS operators from all radio communication requirements
