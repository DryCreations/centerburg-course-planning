# Project: Career Landscape Presentation

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.1 — The Aviation Industry & Career Landscape
**Duration:** 1 week | **Weeks:** Week 2
**Tools:** Google Slides, aviationweather.gov (reference), Google Docs

---

## Overview

The UAS industry is one of the fastest-growing sectors in aviation. Before building technical skills, pilots and operators need to understand where they fit in the industry. This project builds career awareness and introduces BPA presentation skills that will develop throughout the year.

---

## Class Prompt

You will research one specific UAS career pathway and create a short presentation that maps the industry, the role, and the path to get there.

**You will create:**

- A **Google Slides presentation** (6–10 slides) covering the items below
- An **annotated sectional chart image** on one slide showing airspace relevant to your chosen career (e.g., a construction site inspection near a Class D airport)

**Required slides:**

1. **Title slide** — Your name, career title, date
2. **Industry overview** — What sector does this career belong to? (Agriculture, real estate, inspection, media, public safety, surveying, etc.) Give 2–3 facts about the size or growth of this sector.
3. **The role** — What does a person in this role actually do day-to-day? What kind of missions do they fly?
4. **Credentials and training** — What certifications are required? (Part 107? Other?) What education or training pathway leads here?
5. **Airspace and regulations** — What airspace do professionals in this role commonly operate in? What regulations apply? Include your labeled sectional chart excerpt.
6. **Professional network** — What organizations, publications, or communities exist for this career? (e.g., AUVSI, state drone associations, industry conferences)
7. **Your take** — Is this a career you could see yourself pursuing? What would you need to do to get there?

**Milestones:**

| Milestone | Description | Due |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| Career choice confirmed | Tell teacher which career you are researching | Week 2, Day 1 |
| Research notes | Google Doc with sources for slides 2–6 | Week 2, Day 3 |
| Draft presentation | All 7 slides drafted | Week 2, Day 4 |
