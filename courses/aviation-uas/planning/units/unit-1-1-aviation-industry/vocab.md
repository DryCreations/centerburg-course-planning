## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **National Airspace System (NAS)** | The network of U.S. airspace, air traffic control infrastructure, navigation aids, airports, and procedures that together enable safe air travel | 7.1.5 | 1 |
| **Class A Airspace** | Controlled airspace from 18,000 ft MSL to FL600; IFR operations only; not relevant for most sUAS ops | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **Class B Airspace** | Controlled airspace surrounding the nation's busiest airports; resembles an upside-down wedding cake; requires ATC clearance for all operations | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **Class C Airspace** | Controlled airspace around airports with radar approach control; requires two-way radio communication with ATC | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **Class D Airspace** | Controlled airspace around airports with a control tower; requires two-way radio communication with ATC; typically extends from surface to ~2,500 ft AGL | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **Class E Airspace** | Controlled airspace that is not Class A, B, C, or D; includes controlled airspace for en-route operations; extends from 1,200 ft AGL in most areas (or surface in some) | 7.1.9 | 2 |
| **Class G Airspace** | Uncontrolled airspace; no ATC service; typically the lowest few hundred to 1,200 ft AGL in rural areas | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **LAANC (Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability)** | FAA system that provides near real-time airspace authorization to sUAS operators; allows operators to fly in controlled airspace below approved altitude grids without waiting for manual authorization | 7.9.4 | 2 |
| **Temporary Flight Restriction (TFR)** | A regulatory action that temporarily restricts certain aircraft from operating in a defined area; can be issued for VIP movement, disasters, airshows, wildfires | 7.11.8 | 1 |
| **Notice to Air Missions (NOTAM)** | Official notices to pilots and operators about changes in aeronautical information, hazards, or temporary flight restrictions not yet published in official sources | 7.11.6 | 1 |
| **Remote ID** | FAA requirement for UAS to broadcast identification and location information during flight; like a digital license plate for drones | 7.9.3 | 1 |
| **Commercial UAS Operations** | Drone operations conducted for compensation or hire; requires a Remote Pilot Certificate under Part 107 | 7.9.1 | 1 |
| **Precision Agriculture** | The use of aerial and ground-based sensors (including UAS) to monitor crop health, soil conditions, and irrigation needs at a field-by-field or zone-by-zone level | 7.1.9 | 2 |
| **Infrastructure Inspection** | Using a UAS to visually or thermally inspect structures (bridges, power lines, cell towers, pipelines) without requiring workers to physically access dangerous heights | 7.9.3 | 2 |
| **Photogrammetry** | The science of obtaining reliable measurements and 3D models from photographs; used with drone imagery to create orthomosaics and point clouds | 7.10.2 | 1 |
| **Sector (Aviation Industry)** | A distinct segment of the aviation industry — commercial, military, recreational, public safety, agriculture, film/media, inspection, surveying | 7.1.5 | 1 |
| **Career Pathway** | A sequence of education, training, and credential steps leading to a specific occupation; for UAS, typically includes Part 107, college aviation programs, or military service | 1.1.2 | 2 |
| **Résumé** | A formal document summarizing a person's work experience, education, skills, and credentials for employment purposes | 1.1.5 | 1 |
| **Professional Organization** | An association that supports people working in a specific field through networking, publications, and advocacy (e.g., AUVSI — Association for Uncrewed Vehicle Systems International) | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **AUVSI** | Association for Uncrewed Vehicle Systems International; the leading professional organization for the UAS industry | 1.1.4 | 1 |

## Key Distinctions

| Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Controlled Airspace (B/C/D/E) | Uncontrolled Airspace (Class G) | Controlled = ATC is active and services are available; uncontrolled = no ATC service provided |
| LAANC Authorization | FAA Waiver | LAANC provides near-real-time authorization for standard flights in controlled airspace below defined altitudes; waivers are manual FAA approvals for operations that deviate from standard Part 107 rules |
| TFR | NOTAM | TFRs restrict flight in a specific area for safety/security; NOTAMs are informational notices about changes that pilots/operators need to know |

## Study Tips

- Airspace classes A–G: remember that **B, C, D** are around airports (busiest → least busy); **E** fills in the rest of controlled airspace; **G** is uncontrolled
- Memorize that LAANC = near-real-time authorization — it is tested on both the WebXam and Part 107 exam
- DOK 2 terms (LAANC, Class E, precision agriculture) require you to apply them in scenarios — practice by describing a real flight situation that involves each
