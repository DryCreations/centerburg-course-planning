# Project: UAS Drone Services Business Plan & Mission Portfolio

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.3 — Capstone: UAS Drone Services Business Plan & Mission Portfolio
**Duration:** Weeks 33–37
**BPA Connection:** This project IS the BPA #505 Entrepreneurship entry (for Track B students) and the final summative for all students.

---

## Overview

You have spent this year learning to fly, earning knowledge of FAA regulations, analyzing weather, planning missions, processing GIS data, and developing professional communication skills. The capstone project synthesizes all of it into one professional product: a complete drone services business plan with supporting mission portfolio.

This is not a school project about a fake business. This is a real business plan that you could use to start a drone services company in Knox County.

---

## Deliverables

### Deliverable 1: Business Plan (primary document)

A complete, professionally formatted business plan. Required sections in this order:

**1. Cover Page**
- Business name + logo (your design)
- Your name, date, course

**2. Executive Summary (1 page)**
- What your business does
- Who your target customers are
- Why your business will succeed
- One-paragraph financial summary (startup cost, projected monthly revenue at break-even)

**3. Company Description**
- Mission statement (1–2 sentences)
- Business structure (sole proprietorship, LLC)
- Location and service area
- Why this community needs drone services (specific to Knox County, OH)

**4. Market Analysis**
- Target market: who are your primary customers? (3–5 segments with specifics)
- Market size: how many potential clients exist in Knox County? (estimate with source)
- Competitive analysis: what other drone operators serve this area? What is your competitive advantage?
- SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)

**5. Services Menu**
- List all services offered with descriptions and pricing
- At least 3 distinct service types
- Include both flight services and deliverables (edited photos, orthomosaics, reports)

**6. Equipment & Technology**
- Aircraft fleet: DJI Mavic 3 (specs, cost, capabilities)
- Software: WebODM, QGIS, editing tools
- Total equipment investment

**7. Part 107 Compliance Strategy**
- How you maintain Part 107 currency (recurrent training schedule)
- Airspace authorization workflow
- Weather standards (your go/no-go criteria)
- Insurance coverage

**8. Marketing Plan**
- How you will reach customers (website, social media, direct outreach, referrals)
- Sample marketing piece (one social media post or one postcard design)

**9. Financial Projections**
- Startup costs (full table from lab)
- Monthly income statement (projected): revenue − expenses = net profit/loss
- Break-even analysis: ___ jobs/month to cover costs
- Year 1 financial goal

**10. Safety & Compliance Manual** (may be a standalone appendix)
- From the compliance manual draft in lab

### Deliverable 2: Mission Portfolio

A professional PDF document containing 3 complete mission packages (from lab):
- Mission 1: Agricultural, real estate, or construction scenario
- Mission 2: GIS/photogrammetry mission
- Mission 3: Free choice or new hypothetical with full planning package

Each mission includes: overview, authorization, weather brief, preflight checklist, imagery, and commercial value statement.

### Deliverable 3: Pitch Presentation (5–7 minutes + Q&A)

Deliver a business pitch to the class in Week 36. The pitch must include:
- Hook: why drone services in Knox County?
- The problem you solve for clients
- Your services and pricing (1 slide)
- Key financials: startup cost, break-even, year 1 goal
- Your competitive advantage
- Call to action: what you want your investor/client to do

Q&A: Teacher and optionally guest evaluators (BAC members if available) ask 3 minutes of questions.

*Note: Track B BPA students — this pitch IS your BPA #505 competition entry. Prepare as if judges are scoring you.*

## Rubric

| Category | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) |
|----------|--------------|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Executive Summary** | Concise, complete, compelling; financial summary accurate | All elements present; financial summary present | Missing 1–2 elements | Absent or vague |
| **Market Analysis** | Specific to Knox County; real data; SWOT complete | Mostly specific; 1 section generic | Generic; no local specifics | Not completed |
| **Financial Projections** | All 3 sections (startup, monthly, break-even); calculations correct | 2 of 3 sections; 1 calculation error | 1 section present | Not completed |
| **Services & Pricing** | 3+ services; specific pricing with rationale; competitive | 2–3 services; pricing present | 1–2 services; no pricing rationale | 1 service listed |
| **Compliance & Safety** | Covers Part 107, authorization workflow, weather minimums, incident reporting | Mostly complete; 1 area vague | Mentions compliance but no detail | Not addressed |
| **Mission Portfolio** | 3 complete packages with all elements; professional format | 2 complete packages; 1 partial | 1 complete package | No portfolio |
| **Pitch Delivery** | Confident, specific, compelling; handles Q&A well | Clear delivery; 1–2 weak sections; handles Q&A | Reads from notes; limited Q&A | Not delivered |
| **Professional Format** | Polished document; no major errors; branding consistent | Minor errors; consistent format | Inconsistent; several errors | Unprofessional |
