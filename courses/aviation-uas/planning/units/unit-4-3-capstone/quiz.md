# Quiz: Business Planning & Career Readiness

**Standards:** 1.4, 1.5, 1.6

## Questions

### 1.
The executive summary of a business plan should be:

A. The longest section — include everything a reader would need to understand the business
B. A brief, compelling overview that can stand alone; written after all other sections are complete
C. A list of the founder's personal qualifications and experience
D. A financial statement showing the first year's projected income

> **Answer: B** — Executive summary is brief and written last; it synthesizes the full plan (1.4.1)

---

### 2.
A "value proposition" in a business context means:

A. The total dollar value of the business's physical assets
B. A clear statement of how the business solves a customer's problem better than competitors
C. The price list for all services offered
D. The business owner's personal value system and ethics statement

> **Answer: B** — Value proposition = why customers choose you over competitors (1.6.1)

---

### 3.
Fixed costs are best defined as:

A. Costs that vary with the number of jobs or units produced
B. Expenses that remain constant regardless of activity level, such as insurance and software subscriptions
C. The initial one-time investment to start the business
D. Costs that are negotiated and fixed by contract with suppliers

> **Answer: B** — Fixed costs are constant regardless of production volume (1.5.2)

---

### 4.
A UAS business earns $350 per real estate photo job. Variable costs are $50 per job. Fixed monthly costs are $600. How many jobs per month are needed to break even?

A. 2 jobs
B. 3 jobs
C. 5 jobs
D. 6 jobs

> **Answer: B** — Break-even = $600 ÷ ($350 − $50) = $600 ÷ $300 = **2 jobs**... wait — let's recalculate: 600/300 = 2. Actually: **Answer: A — 2 jobs**.

> *Correction: 600 ÷ 300 = 2. Answer A (2 jobs) is correct.*

> **Corrected Answer: A** — Break-even = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price − Variable Cost) = 600 ÷ 300 = 2 jobs/month (1.5.3)

---

### 5.
Liability insurance is important for a commercial UAS business because:

A. Part 107 legally requires all commercial operators to carry liability insurance
B. Most commercial clients and venue operators require proof of insurance before permitting drone flights; it also protects the operator from financial loss if property is damaged during operations
C. Insurance reduces the cost of aircraft replacement parts
D. Insurance is required to qualify for LAANC authorization

> **Answer: B** — Liability insurance is industry-required (though not legally mandated by FAA) and protects against client claims (1.5.7)

---

### 6.
A SWOT analysis identifies:

A. Safety, Weather, Operations, and Terrain — four pre-flight risk factors
B. Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats — an internal and external business assessment
C. Survey, Waypoints, Overlap, and Tracking — photogrammetry mission parameters
D. Schedule, Workflow, Outcomes, and Timeline — project management milestones

> **Answer: B** — SWOT = Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats (1.6.1)

---

### 7.
Return on Investment (ROI) measures:

A. The total revenue generated in the first year of operations
B. The percentage return earned relative to the amount invested: (Net Profit ÷ Investment) × 100%
C. The ratio of fixed costs to variable costs in a business model
D. The number of clients retained over a 12-month period

> **Answer: B** — ROI = (Net Profit ÷ Total Investment) × 100% (1.5.4)

---

### 8.
A UAS operations compliance manual should include which of the following?

A. A list of the owner's personal certifications and education history
B. Standard operating procedures for airspace authorization, weather go/no-go criteria, emergency protocols, and incident reporting procedures
C. The business's year 1 financial projections and break-even analysis
D. Marketing materials and client testimonials

> **Answer: B** — Operations manual covers procedures, protocols, and compliance workflows (7.9.6)

---

### 9.
When pitching to an investor, the most important financial figure to communicate is:

A. Your equipment's current depreciated market value
B. The number of social media followers your business currently has
C. How much investment is needed, what it will be used for, and when/how the investor will see a return
D. The total cost of all equipment including accessories

> **Answer: C** — Investors want to know: ask, use of funds, and return timeline (1.4.1, 1.5.4)

---

### 10.
A market segment is:

A. A geographic area covered by a sectional chart
B. A specific portion of the total potential market with shared characteristics and needs that your business will target
C. The percentage of total market share your business currently holds
D. A 3-mile radius around your primary operating site

> **Answer: B** — Market segmentation identifies specific customer groups to target with tailored services (1.6.1)
