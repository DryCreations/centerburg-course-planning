# Vocabulary: UAS Business Development & Capstone

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.3 — Capstone: UAS Drone Services Business Plan & Mission Portfolio
**Weeks:** 33–37

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Business Plan** | A formal written document describing a business concept, market analysis, operational structure, financial projections, and management approach; required for BPA #505 Entrepreneurship entry | 1.4.1 | 2 |
| **Executive Summary** | A 1-page overview of the entire business plan; the first section readers see; must stand alone as a compelling summary of the venture | 1.4.1 | 3 |
| **Value Proposition** | A clear statement of how a business solves a customer problem or improves a situation in a way competitors do not; defines why a customer would choose your service | 1.6.1 | 3 |
| **Market Segment** | A distinct subgroup of potential customers with shared characteristics and needs; a UAS business might segment by agriculture, real estate, construction, or public safety | 1.6.1 | 3 |
| **Break-Even Analysis** | The calculation of how much revenue is needed to cover all costs; break-even point = total fixed costs ÷ (price per unit − variable cost per unit) | 1.5.3 | 3 |
| **Startup Costs** | All one-time expenses to launch a business before the first dollar of revenue; for a UAS business: aircraft, controller, batteries, Part 107 exam fee, insurance, software subscriptions, LLC formation | 1.5.1 | 2 |
| **Fixed Costs** | Expenses that stay constant regardless of number of jobs completed; examples: insurance, software subscriptions, equipment depreciation | 1.5.2 | 2 |
| **Variable Costs** | Expenses that change with activity level; examples: fuel/battery wear per flight, travel expenses, per-job equipment rental | 1.5.2 | 2 |
| **Liability Insurance** | Protection against financial loss from property damage or injury caused by UAS operations; most commercial clients and venues require a certificate of insurance before allowing a drone flight | 1.5.7 | 2 |
| **Operations Manual** | A written document defining a UAS business's standard operating procedures, safety checklists, emergency protocols, and compliance requirements; professional standard for commercial operators | 7.9.6 | 2 |
| **Mission Portfolio** | A curated collection of 3 or more complete mission packages demonstrating different UAS service types; each package includes planning, imagery, and analysis | 7.11.7 | 3 |
| **Pitch Presentation** | A concise, persuasive presentation designed to convince an investor or client to take action; BPA #505 requires a pitch as part of the Entrepreneurship entry | 1.2.2 | 3 |
| **Return on Investment (ROI)** | A measure of financial return relative to investment: ROI = (Net Profit ÷ Total Investment) × 100%; shows whether a business decision generates profit | 1.5.4 | 3 |
| **NLC (National Leadership Conference)** | BPA's national competition; Nashville, TN, May 6–10; students advancing from state competition attend NLC | 1.1.3 | 1 |
| **Compliance Manual** | A document outlining how a business adheres to all relevant regulations; for a UAS business: Part 107 rules, airspace authorization procedures, Remote ID, accident reporting protocols | 7.9.6 | 3 |
