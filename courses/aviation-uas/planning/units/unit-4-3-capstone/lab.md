# Lab: Business Plan Workshop — Financial Modeling + Mission Documentation

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.3 — Capstone: UAS Drone Services Business Plan & Mission Portfolio
**Duration:** 3 days | **Weeks:** 33–34
**Tools:** Google Docs/Sheets, flight logs from Unit 3.2, mission packages from earlier units

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will:
- Build a realistic financial model for a UAS drone services business (startup costs, pricing, break-even)
- Compile a 3-mission portfolio package from earlier unit work
- Draft the safety/compliance manual section of their business plan
- Conduct a peer review of one financial model using a structured critique format

### Day 35-3 — Compliance Manual Draft + Peer Review

**Part A: Draft Your Compliance Manual (20 minutes)**

Write the compliance manual section of your business plan. Required content:
1. Our Part 107 Compliance Procedures
   - Airspace check process (B4UFLY → DroneZone → TFR → NOTAMs)
   - Weather minimum standards (our go/no-go criteria)
   - LAANC authorization workflow
2. Safety Protocols
   - Pre-flight checklist: what we check before every flight
   - Emergency procedures: GPS loss, link failure, battery critical
   - Bystander safety: no flight over people; minimum 25 ft clearance from uninvolved persons
3. Incident Reporting
   - Internal: any flight anomaly is logged
   - FAA: §107.9 reporting threshold and 10-day window
   - Insurance: any potential liability claim reported to insurer same day

**Part B: Financial Model Peer Review (20 minutes)**

Swap your financial model (Google Sheet) with a partner. Review their model using this checklist:
- [ ] Are all major startup cost categories included? (List any you think they missed)
- [ ] Is the pricing realistic for the Knox County / Ohio market?
- [ ] Is the break-even calculation formula correct?
- [ ] What is one assumption in their model that seems too optimistic or too conservative?
- [ ] Write one specific recommendation for improving their financial model

Return the model with your comments added in a "Peer Review" tab in the Google Sheet.
