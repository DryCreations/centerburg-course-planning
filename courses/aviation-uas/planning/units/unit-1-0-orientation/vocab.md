## Terms

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS)** | An aircraft and its associated elements — including the communication links and components that control it — that are operated without a flight crew member on board | 7.9.1 | 1 |
| **Small UAS (sUAS)** | An unmanned aircraft system with a total weight of 55 pounds or less at takeoff, including payload and equipment | 7.9.1 | 1 |
| **Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC)** | The individual who is designated as the final authority for the operation of an sUAS and is responsible for its safe operation | 7.9.6 | 1 |
| **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)** | Unaided (except for corrective lenses) visual contact with the UAS sufficient to maintain situational awareness of its location, attitude, altitude, and direction | 7.9.3 | 1 |
| **Part 107** | The FAA regulation (14 CFR Part 107) that governs small UAS operations for non-recreational purposes in the United States | 7.9.1 | 1 |
| **Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)** | The U.S. government agency responsible for regulating all civil aviation, including UAS operations | 7.1.5 | 1 |
| **Airspace** | The air above a country and the associated legal and operational framework governing how aircraft may use it; divided into classes with varying rules | 7.1.9 | 1 |
| **Preflight Inspection** | A systematic check of an aircraft and all its systems before each flight to confirm airworthiness and readiness | 7.11.14 | 1 |
| **Payload** | Equipment or cargo carried by a UAS (e.g., camera, sensor) that is separate from the aircraft's normal flight systems | 7.4.9 | 1 |
| **Remote Pilot Certificate** | The FAA-issued credential (obtained by passing the Part 107 knowledge test) that authorizes a person to operate a small UAS commercially | 7.9.1 | 2 |
| **End-of-Course (EOC) Assessment** | The state-administered WebXam test measuring a student's mastery of the CTE standards for this course | — | 1 |
| **WebXam** | Ohio's web-based end-of-course assessment for CTE students; results are used for course credit and credential documentation | — | 1 |
| **Strand** | A grouping of related competencies in the Ohio CTE standards (e.g., Strand 7 = Aviation and Aeronautics) | — | 1 |
| **Competency** | A specific, measurable skill or knowledge statement in the CTE standards that students are expected to demonstrate | — | 1 |
| **BPA (Business Professionals of America)** | A career and technical student organization (CTSO) that provides competitive events and leadership development for students in business and technology programs | 1.1.4 | 1 |
| **WSAP (Workplace Skills Assessment Program)** | BPA's rubric-based scoring system used to evaluate competition entries across all BPA events | 1.1.4 | 2 |
| **Career Plan** | A documented set of short- and long-term goals related to education, skills development, and employment in a chosen field | 1.1.3 | 2 |
| **Flight Log** | A record documenting each flight including date, location, duration, purpose, conditions, and any incidents; required for professional UAS operations | 7.11.14 | 1 |
| **Baseline Score** | The initial assessment result used as a reference point to measure growth over time; in this course, the Week 1 WebXam pretest | — | 1 |

## Key Distinctions

| Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| UAS | sUAS | UAS is any unmanned aircraft system regardless of size; sUAS is specifically ≤55 lbs at takeoff |
| Remote Pilot in Command | Visual Observer | RPIC is the licensed, responsible operator; visual observer assists by maintaining visual contact — no certificate required |
| Part 107 | Recreational Rules | Part 107 governs commercial/non-recreational UAS operations; recreational operations follow a separate set of FAA rules (the Exception for Recreational Flyers) |
| WebXam | FAA Part 107 Test | WebXam is Ohio's CTE assessment of course standards; Part 107 is the FAA's federal knowledge test required to fly commercially |

## Bell Ringer Prompts

1. Define *small UAS* in your own words. What distinguishes it from a full-size aircraft?
2. What is the role of the FAA? Give one example of a rule it enforces for UAS operators.
3. What does VLOS mean, and why is it a critical safety requirement?
4. A student wants to become a commercial drone pilot after high school. What credential do they need, and how do they earn it?
5. What is the difference between a UAS and a sUAS?

## Study Tips

- For each term, create a flashcard: **Front** = term | **Back** = definition + one real-world aviation example
- FAA-specific terms (Part 107, RPIC, VLOS) are heavily tested on both the WebXam and the FAA Part 107 knowledge test — know these cold
- DOK 2 terms require you to *apply* the concept, not just recall it — practice by using the term in a sentence about a real flight scenario
