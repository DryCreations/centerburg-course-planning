# Project: Course Toolchain & Orientation Setup

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.0 — WebXam Pretest & Course Orientation
**Duration:** Week 1 (Days 1–2 and 5)
**Tools:** Google Drive, Google Docs, Felt (classroom account)

---

## Overview

Before any student touches an aircraft, this course requires a functional toolchain. In the first week you will set up every account and organizational structure you will rely on for the rest of the year. Starting with a clean, organized workspace makes every future unit easier.

---

## Class Prompt

You will set up the full course toolchain and create a mission-ready organizational structure in Google Drive. This is the foundation every future project builds on.

**You will create:**

- Google Drive folder structure for the course (see below)
- Blank flight log template (Google Sheets or Docs)
- Felt classroom map access confirmed (teacher sends invite)
- A short "Course Introduction" document introducing yourself and your experience with aviation/technology

**Folder Structure to Create in Google Drive:**

```
Unmanned Aircraft — [Your Name]/
├── Unit 1 — Foundations/
├── Unit 2 — Weather & Preflight/
├── Unit 3 — Flight Operations/
├── Unit 4 — GIS & Capstone/
├── BPA/
│   ├── Research/
│   ├── Drafts/
│   └── Final/

```

## Course Introduction Document

In 1–2 paragraphs, answer:

1. **Background:** Have you flown a drone before (recreational or otherwise)? Do you have experience with maps, GIS, or aerial photography? Any aviation interest (manned aircraft, military, space)?
2. **Goals:** What do you hope to learn or be able to do by the end of this course? What BPA event sounds most interesting to you right now?

This document is not graded on writing quality — it's a starting point for teacher-student conversation about your path through the course.
