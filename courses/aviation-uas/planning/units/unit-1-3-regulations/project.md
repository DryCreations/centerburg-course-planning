# Project: Part 107 Regulatory Compliance Analysis

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.3 — Small UAS Regulations & Operations
**Duration:** 3 weeks | **Weeks:** Weeks 5–7
**Tools:** Google Docs, B4UFLY, FAA DroneZone (no account required for lookup)

---

## Overview

Commercial drone operators must understand regulations deeply enough to apply them in real situations — not just recite them. This project asks you to analyze a realistic commercial UAS operation for full Part 107 compliance before the first flight ever happens.

---

## Class Prompt

You are a newly certified Part 107 remote pilot. A local business has hired you for a commercial drone job. You must review the job requirements and produce a **Regulatory Compliance Report** that confirms whether the job can be done legally under standard Part 107, what authorizations are needed, and what your operating plan will be.

**Choose one scenario:**

- **A:** A local real estate agency wants aerial photos of a home listing near a regional airport (Class D airspace). Flights at 200 ft AGL, daylight only.
- **B:** A construction company wants weekly progress photos of a downtown job site. The site is under a Class B airport's outer ring. Flights at 100 ft AGL.
- **C:** A film production company wants dramatic sunset footage with the aircraft flying near the Golden Hour (civil twilight). Class G airspace, rural location.

**You will create a Regulatory Compliance Report (Google Doc) containing:**

1. **Operation Summary** — Who is the client? What is the purpose of the flight? Where and when?
2. **Airspace Analysis** — What class is the airspace? Is LAANC authorization required? What altitude grid applies? Include a screenshot or sketch of the airspace.
3. **Part 107 Compliance Check** — For each relevant rule (altitude, speed, daylight, line of sight, people, registration), state whether the planned operation complies. Use a table.
4. **Authorization Plan** — If authorization is required, what is the process? How long does it take? What information do you need to submit?
5. **Waiver Analysis** — Is any waiver needed for this job? If yes, which rule requires the waiver and how would you apply?
6. **Risk Assessment** — What are the top 3 risks for this operation? For each, what mitigation steps would you take?
7. **Go / No-Go Decision** — Based on your analysis, is this job legal to execute as described? If not, what would need to change?

**Milestones:**

| Milestone | Description | Due |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| Scenario selection | Tell teacher your chosen scenario | Week 5, Day 1 |
| Airspace + compliance sections | Parts 1–3 complete | Week 6, Day 2 |
| Full draft | All 7 sections drafted | Week 7, Day 3 |
| Final report | Polished, submitted to Google Classroom | Week 7, Day 5 |
