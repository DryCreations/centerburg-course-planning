# Quiz: Small UAS Regulations & Operations

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 1.3 — Small UAS Regulations & Operations
**Week:** Weeks 5–7 — End-of-Week Quizzes
**Format:** 15 MCQ | ~15 min

> Questions modeled after FAA Part 107 Aeronautical Knowledge Test format in addition to WebXam style. Questions marked **[HOT]** are scenario-based.

---

## Questions

### 1.
Under Part 107, a small UAS is required to be registered with the FAA if it weighs:

A. Less than 0.55 lbs  
B. Between 0.55 lbs and 55 lbs  
C. More than 25 lbs  
D. More than 10 lbs  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.2

---

### 2.
The maximum altitude allowed for sUAS operations under Part 107 (without a waiver) is:

A. 400 ft MSL  
B. 400 ft AGL  
C. 500 ft AGL  
D. 1,200 ft AGL  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.4

---

### 3.
Under standard Part 107 rules (without a waiver), sUAS operations are permitted:

A. Any time of day or night, as long as anti-collision lights are used  
B. Only during official sunrise to official sunset, or during civil twilight with anti-collision lights  
C. Only between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM local time  
D. At any time with prior ATC approval  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.4

---

### 4.
A remote pilot must yield right-of-way to:

A. Other sUAS in the same airspace  
B. All crewed aircraft at all times  
C. Aircraft flying at a lower altitude  
D. Helicopters, but not fixed-wing aircraft  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.3

---

### 5.
Which of the following operations is NOT allowed under standard Part 107 without a waiver?

A. Flying at 350 ft AGL over an open farm field  
B. Flying during civil twilight with an anti-collision light  
C. Flying over moving vehicles and bystanders not involved in the operation  
D. Flying in Class G airspace without ATC authorization  

**Answer: C** | DOK 2 | 7.9.4

---

### 6.
A LAANC authorization allows a remote pilot to:

A. Fly above 400 ft AGL in any airspace class  
B. Operate in controlled airspace at or below an FAA-defined altitude ceiling in near-real time  
C. Waive the visual line-of-sight requirement  
D. Fly at night without anti-collision lights  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.9.3

---

### 7.
Under Part 107, the remote pilot in command is responsible for:

A. Only the safe operation of their specific aircraft  
B. The safe and legal operation of the entire UAS operation, including any crew members  
C. Filing flight plans with ATC before every operation  
D. Obtaining insurance for the aircraft before flying  

**Answer: B** | DOK 1 | 7.9.6

---

### 8.
A UAS accident must be reported to the FAA if it results in:

A. Any crash landing, regardless of injury or damage  
B. Serious injury to any person OR property damage exceeding $500  
C. Damage to the UAS itself exceeding $500  
D. Any operation outside of approved airspace  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.9.1

---

### 9.
**[HOT]** A commercial pilot asks a new remote pilot: "You're flying in Class D airspace. What did you have to do before takeoff?" Which is the correct answer?

A. Nothing — Class D is uncontrolled airspace  
B. Filed a flight plan with the FAA 24 hours in advance  
C. Obtained LAANC authorization or contacted the control tower directly  
D. Notified the NTSB of the planned flight  

**Answer: C** | DOK 3 | 7.9.3

### 10.
**[HOT]** A remote pilot wants to fly at night to capture time-lapse footage of a city skyline for a commercial client. Under Part 107, she must:

A. Obtain a waiver from the FAA before the operation  
B. Fly only during civil twilight with anti-collision lights visible for 3 statute miles, OR operate under a night waiver for deeper nighttime ops  
C. Notify the nearest ATC facility via phone 1 hour before the flight  
D. Night operations are completely prohibited for commercial UAS operators  

**Answer: B** | DOK 3 | 7.9.4

### 11.
Crew Resource Management (CRM) applied to a two-person sUAS operation (RPIC + Visual Observer) means:

A. The visual observer must also hold a Remote Pilot Certificate  
B. The RPIC and VO communicate actively to share situational awareness and catch errors before they become incidents  
C. The VO is responsible for all regulatory compliance  
D. CRM only applies to airline crews, not sUAS operators  

**Answer: B** | DOK 2 | 7.9.6

### 12.
**[HOT]** A remote pilot receives a B4UFLY notification indicating a TFR is active over a nearby stadium. The pilot's planned flight path is 0.5 miles from the TFR boundary. What should the pilot do?

A. Proceed with the flight — the TFR only applies inside the boundary  
B. Verify the exact TFR boundaries using FAA DroneZone or a NOTAM service, confirm the flight path is entirely outside the TFR, and document this pre-flight check  
C. Contact the stadium to request permission to fly nearby  
D. Cancel the operation automatically — any flight within 1 mile of a TFR is prohibited  

**Answer: B** | DOK 3 | 7.11.8

### 13.
A sUAS remote pilot certificate must be renewed every:

A. 12 calendar months  
B. 18 calendar months  
C. 24 calendar months  
D. 36 calendar months  

**Answer: C** | DOK 1 | 7.9.1

### 14.
**[HOT]** A student says: "I don't need a Part 107 certificate — I'll just use my dad's certificate number on my registration." What is wrong with this plan?

A. Nothing — family members can share a Remote Pilot Certificate for non-commercial operations  
B. The Remote Pilot Certificate is personal; the certificated pilot must personally act as RPIC during any commercial operation, and falsifying registration information is a federal violation  
C. Only the aircraft needs to be registered, not the pilot  
D. Part 107 certificates are only required for aircraft over 10 lbs  

**Answer: B** | DOK 4 | 7.9.1

### 15.
**[HOT]** You are flying a commercial mission when you notice a full-size helicopter approaching at your altitude from behind. You are currently at 380 ft AGL. What is the correct action?

A. Maintain position — the helicopter should yield to you since you were there first  
B. Immediately descend and land or move out of the flight path; crewed aircraft always have right-of-way over sUAS  
C. Activate Return-to-Home so the drone automatically avoids the helicopter  
D. Hover in place and alert the helicopter pilot via radio on 122.8 MHz  

**Answer: B** | DOK 4 | 7.9.3
