# Vocabulary: BPA Refinement & Local Competition

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.1 — BPA Refinement & Local Competition
**Weeks:** 19–21

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| **WSAP Rubric** | BPA's scoring rubric used by judges at all competition levels; defines point values for each section of a presentation, business plan, or other entry type | 1.1.9 | 1 |
| **Peer Critique** | Structured feedback from a classmate using a scoring framework; focuses on specific, actionable improvement suggestions rather than general praise or criticism | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Revision** | A purposeful change to content, structure, or delivery based on feedback; not the same as proofreading — revision may involve cutting, adding, or reorganizing material | 1.1.7 | 2 |
| **Consensus Building** | A decision-making process where a group works toward agreement that all members can support; used in team BPA events to resolve creative differences | 1.2.7 | 3 |
| **Conflict Resolution** | Techniques for addressing disagreements constructively; in team presentations, disagreements about content or approach are resolved by returning to the rubric as a shared standard | 1.2.4 | 3 |
| **Panel of Judges** | A group of evaluators (typically 3) who independently score BPA entries using the WSAP rubric; scores are averaged; judges may ask questions during or after the presentation | 1.2.2 | 1 |
| **Q&A (Questions and Answers)** | The session after a BPA presentation where judges ask follow-up questions; demonstrates depth of knowledge; inability to answer signals rehearsed but not mastered content | 1.2.3 | 3 |
| **Constructive Feedback** | Feedback that identifies a specific weakness and suggests a specific action to improve it; example: "Your third slide lacks evidence — add a statistic from an FAA source" | 1.1.9 | 2 |
| **Professional Appearance** | The standard of dress and grooming expected in a business or competition setting; BPA presentations require business professional attire | 1.1.8 | 1 |
| **Interpersonal Skills** | The ability to communicate, collaborate, and build positive working relationships; evaluated in team events and in the professionalism scores of individual events | 1.2.10 | 2 |
