# Project: Local BPA Competition Delivery

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.1 — BPA Refinement & Local Competition
**Event:** January — Centerburg Local Competition (exact date TBD by BPA chapter)

---

## Description

Students deliver their finalized BPA entries at the Centerburg local competition. This is a real competition scored by judges (ideally community members, business professionals, or faculty not previously involved with the entry). The competition functions as a summative grade for Unit 3.1 — scores are the rubric.

---

## Before the Competition (Week 20)

Each student must complete and submit:
- [ ] Final BPA entry document (Track A: slide deck; Track B: business plan)
- [ ] Annotated bibliography / sources page
- [ ] Completed self-assessment WSAP score sheet (from lab)
- [ ] Pre-competition checklist:
  - [ ] Entry meets all BPA format requirements (word count, slide count, font size, etc.)
  - [ ] All images are properly sourced (no copyright violations)
  - [ ] Business professional attire confirmed
  - [ ] Arrival time confirmed (30 min before competition)
  - [ ] Equipment tested (if using personal laptop/clicker)

---

## Day of Competition (Week 21)

- Set up in the competition room 30 minutes before your time slot
- Enter professionally — treat the entire school day as professional dress day
- After your entry: remain available for Q&A; thank the judges
- After all entries are complete: participate in debrief

---

## Scoring

Local competition entries are scored by judges using official WSAP rubrics. The competition score constitutes the Unit 3.1 project grade. Students who advance to state competition receive a brief additional coaching session before the state event.

**Advancing to State:** Top 1–2 entries in each event category will be submitted for state-level competition consideration (January deadline for state registration).

---

## Post-Competition Reflection (Due the following class period)

Write a half-page reflection answering:
1. What score did you receive? What did judges say about your strongest section?
2. What did judges identify as your biggest area for improvement?
3. If you competed again tomorrow with one more week to prepare, what would you change?
4. Rate your preparation: 1–10. What would have made it a 10?
