# Lab: WSAP Self-Assessment + Targeted Revision Sprint

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.1 — BPA Refinement & Local Competition
**Duration:** 2 days | **Weeks:** 19–20 (Days 1–2)
**Tools Needed:** Google Docs/Slides, WSAP rubric for selected event

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will have:
- Scored their own BPA entry against the WSAP rubric and identified gaps
- Completed at least 3 targeted revisions
- Practiced delivery twice with self-timed runs

---

## Instructions

### Day 19-1 — WSAP Self-Assessment

Print or open the WSAP rubric for your event (#555 Presentation Individual or #505 Entrepreneurship).

Score your current BPA entry against every rubric category as a judge would. For each category:
- Circle the score level that honestly reflects your current work (not the ideal)
- In your Google Doc, list every category where you scored below the top level
- For each gap, write one specific action: *"To improve [category], I will [specific action]."*

Create a priority list: which 3 improvements would raise your score the most? Start with those.

### Day 20-2 — Revision Sprint

Work time only. Make your 3 highest-priority revisions. After each revision, note in your doc what you changed and re-score that rubric category.

At the end of the period, run a timed practice delivery (present to yourself or a partner, timer running). Record the time. If you are outside your event's time limit, mark where you need to cut or expand.
