# Quiz: Communication, Leadership & Professional Standards

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.1 — BPA Refinement & Local Competition
**Format:** 10 MCQ | End of Week 21 | ~10 min
**Standards:** 1.1.x, 1.2.x

---

## Questions

### 1.
A judge scores your presentation lower than expected in the "Evidence and Research" category. The most constructive response is to:

A. Ask the teacher to challenge the judge's score
B. Review the specific rubric criteria for that category and identify what evidence was missing or uncited
C. Ignore the feedback — one judge's opinion doesn't matter
D. Drop the weaker sections from your next presentation and focus only on your strengths

> **Answer: B** — Review the rubric criteria; specific feedback drives targeted improvement (1.1.9)

---

### 2.
BPA competition attire is best described as:

A. School uniform or athletic wear
B. Business casual — nice jeans and a button-down shirt are sufficient
C. Business professional — formal attire appropriate for a job interview or corporate meeting
D. Personal expression — any attire that makes you feel confident

> **Answer: C** — BPA requires business professional attire (1.1.8)

---

### 3.
During a team BPA presentation (#560), two students disagree about whether to include a competitor analysis slide. The best approach is to:

A. Have the stronger student make the decision unilaterally
B. Return to the WSAP rubric — if competitor analysis is a scored category, include it; if not, make a group decision based on time
C. Flip a coin to resolve the disagreement quickly
D. Ask the teacher to decide for the team

> **Answer: B** — The rubric is the shared standard; use it to guide creative disagreements (1.2.7)

---

### 4.
A BPA judge asks a Q&A question and the student doesn't know the answer. The best professional response is to:

A. Make up an answer — judges can't verify everything in the moment
B. Say "I don't know" and immediately end the presentation
C. Acknowledge the question, explain the boundary of your current knowledge, and offer a relevant response based on what you do know
D. Ask the judge to skip the question and move on

> **Answer: C** — Honest acknowledgment plus a relevant response is more professional than silence or fabrication (1.2.3)

---

### 5.
Interpersonal skills in a professional context includes all of the following EXCEPT:

A. Active listening during a conversation
B. Resolving workplace disagreements constructively
C. Writing Java code efficiently
D. Giving constructive feedback to a colleague

> **Answer: C** — Technical coding skill is not an interpersonal skill; the others all describe interpersonal communication (1.2.10)

---

### 6.
Before submitting a BPA entry, a student should verify that all images in the slide deck are:

A. Designed by the student or licensed for use; no copyrighted images without permission or proper attribution
B. Downloaded from Google Images since those are in the public domain
C. Photographs taken on a smartphone — original photography is always acceptable
D. Limited to 3 per slide for visual clarity

> **Answer: A** — Copyright compliance is required; Google Images are not automatically in the public domain (1.2.11)

---

### 7.
A presentation runs 12 minutes when the maximum time allowed is 10 minutes. The most effective fix is to:

A. Talk faster to deliver the same content in less time
B. Cut one of the three main body sections entirely since the content must be wrong
C. Identify the lowest-scoring rubric category content and reduce or eliminate it while preserving the highest-scoring sections
D. Ask the judges for a time extension on competition day

> **Answer: C** — Strategic cutting preserves your highest rubric score potential (1.1.7)

---

### 8.
Constructive feedback is best characterized by:

A. Telling someone everything they did wrong in order of importance
B. Only positive comments — criticism should be avoided to maintain morale
C. Specific identification of what can be improved, paired with a concrete suggestion for how to improve it
D. A score or grade without written explanation

> **Answer: C** — Constructive feedback is specific and actionable (1.1.9)

---

### 9.
Professional correspondence (emails to judges, registration forms, sponsor requests) should:

A. Use informal language to sound approachable and friendly
B. Avoid specific details — general language is more professional
C. Use complete sentences, proper grammar, and an appropriate tone for the relationship
D. Include emojis to demonstrate personality

> **Answer: C** — Professional correspondence requires appropriate tone and grammar (1.2.11)

---

### 10.
The purpose of a post-competition reflection is to:

A. Provide documentation for the teacher's grade book
B. Force students to acknowledge their lowest scores
C. Develop metacognitive awareness — identifying what worked, what didn't, and what specific actions would improve future performance
D. Allow students to formally challenge judge decisions

> **Answer: C** — Reflection develops self-awareness and drives future improvement (1.1.7)
