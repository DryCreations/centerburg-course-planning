# Quiz: BPA Presentation & Research Skills

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 2.3 — BPA Project: Research & Presentation Development
**Format:** 10 MCQ | End of Week 18 | ~10 min
**Standards:** 1.2.1–1.2.14

---

## Questions

### 1.
A BPA judge is reviewing a #555 Presentation entry and notes "the student stated claims but provided no data to support them." This feedback most directly relates to which communication skill?

A. Nonverbal communication and posture
B. Claim-evidence-reasoning — claims must be supported by credible evidence
C. Proper grammar and sentence structure
D. The use of a clear call to action

> **Answer: B** — Claims without evidence fail CER framework; evidence is scored in WSAP (1.2.1)

---

### 2.
Which of the following would be the MOST credible primary source for a BPA presentation on UAS commercial industry growth?

A. A Wikipedia article on unmanned aerial vehicles
B. FAA UAS registration statistics published at faa.gov
C. A YouTube video by a popular drone hobbyist channel
D. A three-year-old industry blog post without author attribution

> **Answer: B** — FAA government data is a primary source with highest credibility (1.2.1)

---

### 3.
Before building your first slide, the recommended planning step is to:

A. Begin typing content immediately to maximize productivity
B. Create a storyboard that maps each slide's purpose, claim, and evidence
C. Select an attractive slide template and color scheme
D. Count the number of slides your competitors used in previous years

> **Answer: B** — Storyboard planning prevents disorganized content; structure before production (1.2.12)

---

### 4.
A student rehearsing their BPA presentation frequently says "um" and "like" between sentences. This is best described as:

A. A sign of authentic, conversational delivery that judges appreciate
B. Verbal filler — reduces perceived professionalism and is scored negatively in WSAP
C. Acceptable as long as content is accurate
D. A natural speech pattern that no judge would notice

> **Answer: B** — Verbal fillers reduce credibility; WSAP judges score verbal delivery quality (1.2.3)

---

### 5.
The final statement of a presentation that asks the audience to take a specific action is called:

A. A thesis restatement
B. A transition statement
C. A call to action
D. A logical argument

> **Answer: C** — Call to action closes the loop and tells the audience what to do next (1.2.5)

---

### 6.
A student is pitching a drone services business plan (#505 Entrepreneurship). The judges ask: "Who are your target customers?" This question is testing:

A. The student's ability to describe their aircraft fleet
B. Whether the student has conducted a market analysis — identifying target customers is a core component
C. The student's knowledge of Part 107 regulations
D. Whether the student has registered their business with the state

> **Answer: B** — Market analysis, including target customer identification, is a required BPA Entrepreneurship component (1.6.1)

---

### 7.
You are building a slide that argues drone delivery will reduce transportation emissions by 52% by 2035. To make this claim credible, you should:

A. Simply state the claim confidently — the judge will take your word for it
B. Cite the source of the 52% figure and show a data visualization (chart or graph) supporting the trend
C. Rephrase the claim to avoid stating a specific percentage
D. Use animation effects on the slide to draw attention to the number

> **Answer: B** — Specific claims require cited evidence and visualization for maximum WSAP score (1.2.1, 1.2.12)

---

### 8.
Identifying stakeholders in a presentation means:

A. Listing your personal qualifications and relevant experience
B. Naming the people or groups who have an interest in or would be affected by your topic
C. Crediting the sources you used during research
D. Describing the technical specifications of your presentation software

> **Answer: B** — Stakeholder identification shows awareness of who the topic matters to (1.2.13)

---

### 9.
A student receives peer feedback saying: "Your conclusion doesn't summarize your main points." The BEST response is to:

A. Disagree — the student knows their topic better than the reviewer
B. Accept the feedback and revise the conclusion to recap the three main claims and add a clear call to action
C. Add more slides to the body so the conclusion is no longer needed
D. Ask the teacher to tell the peer reviewer they were wrong

> **Answer: B** — Accepting constructive feedback and making specific improvements is the professional response (1.1.9)

---

### 10.
A BPA Entrepreneurship entry (#505) must include financial projections. This is important because:

A. BPA requires at least three charts in every entry regardless of type
B. Judges evaluate whether the business is financially viable — startup costs, pricing, and break-even analysis demonstrate realistic planning
C. Financial projections are only required for entries exceeding 10 pages
D. The financial section is the only part of the business plan that receives a WSAP score

> **Answer: B** — Financial viability is a core WSAP Entrepreneurship scoring category (1.5.1–1.5.8)
