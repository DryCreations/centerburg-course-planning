# Lab: BPA Entry Construction — Storyboard, Research, and First Draft

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 2.3 — BPA Project: Research & Presentation Development
**Duration:** 3 days | **Weeks:** 16–17
**Tools Needed:** Google Docs/Slides, BPA WSAP rubric for selected event (#555 or #505)

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, students will have:
- Completed a content storyboard or business plan outline for their BPA entry
- Sourced at least 5 credible references with working citations
- Drafted at least 60% of their presentation slides or business plan sections
- Received peer feedback using the WSAP rubric

---

## Background / Setup

> By Week 16, you have selected your BPA event. You know the rules. You know what judges score. Now you build. This is a structured work sprint — three days, clear daily goals, and a peer review at the end. The goal is to have a complete first draft by the end of Week 17.

---

## Instructions

### Day 16-1 — Storyboard or Outline

**Track A: #555 Presentation Individual**
1. Open the WSAP Presentation Individual rubric. Highlight every element judges score.
2. Build a storyboard (sketch or digital) for your 7–10 minute presentation. Required slides:
   - Slide 1: Title + speaker name
   - Slide 2: Agenda / roadmap (tell them what you'll tell them)
   - Slides 3–7: Body content (3 main points, each with claim, evidence, visualization)
   - Slide 8: Conclusion + call to action
   - Slide 9: Sources
3. For each body slide, note: what claim does this slide make? What evidence will you show?
4. Identify 3 data visualizations you will create (chart, table, map, infographic).

**Track B: #505 Entrepreneurship**
1. Open the WSAP Entrepreneurship rubric. Highlight every element judges score.
2. Build an outline for your business plan. Required sections:
   - Executive Summary
   - Company Description and Mission
   - Market Analysis (target customers, competitors, size of market)
   - Services Menu + Pricing
   - Equipment Inventory (DJI Mavic 3 specs + cost, accessories)
   - Part 107 Compliance Strategy (how you stay legal)
   - Marketing Plan
   - Financial Projections (startup costs, monthly revenue estimate, break-even)
3. For each section, note: what 1–2 key data points will you include?

---

### Day 16-2 — Research Sprint

Locate at least 5 credible sources for your topic. For each source:
- Full citation (APA or MLA — be consistent)
- One sentence: what does this source say?
- One sentence: why is this source credible? (government? peer-reviewed? industry expert?)
- One sentence: where in your presentation/plan will you use this?

**Acceptable sources for aviation research:**
- FAA publications (faa.gov) — primary source; highest credibility
- BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook — government statistics
- AUVSI.org — industry association
- FAA DroneZone data / UAS registration statistics
- University aviation research programs
- Major news organizations with aviation coverage (NYT, Reuters, AP)

**Not acceptable as primary sources:**
- Wikipedia (may use as a starting point to find cited sources, not as a citation)
- Anonymous blog posts
- Sources older than 5 years unless historical context is needed

---

### Day 17-3 — First Draft + Peer Review

**Part A: First Draft (first 20 minutes)**
Build your presentation slides or business plan document to at least 60% completion. Prioritize the sections that carry the most WSAP scoring weight.

**Part B: Peer Review (last 20 minutes)**
Swap drafts with a partner. Using the WSAP rubric for your partner's event, rate each scored category and write one specific comment per category:
- What is done well in this section?
- What is missing or unclear?
- What one change would most improve this section?

After peer review, each student adds a note to their Google Doc: the 3 biggest revisions to make before Week 18.
