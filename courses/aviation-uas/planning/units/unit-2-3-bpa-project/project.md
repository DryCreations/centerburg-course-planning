# Project: BPA First Run-Through — Presentation or Pitch Draft

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 2.3 — BPA Project: Research & Presentation Development
**Duration:** Week 18 deliverable (built Weeks 16–18)

---

## Class Prompt

By end of Week 18, each student delivers their first complete BPA entry run-through to the class. This is a practice delivery, not the final competition product — but it is scored using the same rubric judges will use. Feedback received here drives the Q3 refinement work.

---

## Track A: #555 Presentation Individual

**Deliverable:** 7–10 minute multimedia presentation delivered live to the class.

**Requirements:**
- Complete slide deck (all sections from storyboard)
- Minimum 3 data visualizations (chart, graph, or map)
- Minimum 5 cited sources
- Verbal delivery (not read verbatim from notes)
- Q&A: 2 minutes of teacher questions after the presentation

**Scored by:** WSAP Presentation Individual rubric (obtain from BPA national website)

---

## Track B: #505 Entrepreneurship

**Deliverable:** 3–5 minute business pitch + written business plan draft.

**Requirements:**
- Complete business plan document (all sections outlined in lab)
- 3–5 minute live pitch covering: company concept, market opportunity, services, pricing, and compliance strategy
- Financial summary: startup cost estimate and projected first-year revenue
- Q&A: 2 minutes of teacher questions after the pitch

**Scored by:** WSAP Entrepreneurship rubric (obtain from BPA national website)

---

## Rubric (both tracks)

Scored directly from the WSAP rubric for the student's selected event. The teacher applies the same rubric judges will use. Students receive their score sheet by end of Week 18 with written feedback for each category.
