# Vocabulary: Emergency Procedures, Human Factors & ADM

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 3.3 — Emergency Operations, Human Factors & Decision-Making
**Weeks:** 26–27
**FAA Study Guide Reference:** Chapters 5, 9, 10

| Term | Definition | Related Standard | FAA Ch. | DOK |
|------|-----------|-----------------|---------|-----|
| **Aeronautical Decision Making (ADM)** | A systematic, risk-based approach to making the best possible decisions under uncertain or time-pressured conditions; incorporates risk assessment, hazard identification, and evaluation of alternatives | 7.8.3 | 10 | 3 |
| **DECIDE Model** | A 6-step decision-making framework: **D**etect a change needing action → **E**stimate the significance of the change → **C**hoose a desired outcome → **I**dentify actions → **D**o the best alternative → **E**valuate the effects | 7.8.5 | 10 | 3 |
| **PAVE Checklist** | A pre-mission risk assessment tool: **P**ilot-in-command, **A**ircraft, en**V**ironment, **E**xternal pressures — evaluating each helps identify hidden hazards before flight | 7.8.4 | 10 | 3 |
| **5P Model** | Five risk areas continuously evaluated throughout flight: **P**ilot, **P**lane/UAS, **P**assengers/stakeholders, **P**rogram/route, **P**rogrammed destination | 7.8.5 | 10 | 3 |
| **Hazardous Attitude** | A mental predisposition that interferes with safe decision making; FAA identifies 5: Anti-Authority, Impulsivity, Invulnerability, Macho, and Resignation | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **Anti-Authority** | Hazardous attitude: "Don't tell me." Resentment of rules and regulations; antidote: "Follow the rules — they are usually right." | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **Impulsivity** | Hazardous attitude: "Do something quickly." Acting before thinking; antidote: "Not so fast — think first." | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **Invulnerability** | Hazardous attitude: "It won't happen to me." Underestimating personal risk; antidote: "It could happen to me." | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **Macho** | Hazardous attitude: "I can do it." Overconfidence that leads to unnecessary risk; antidote: "Taking chances is foolish." | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **Resignation** | Hazardous attitude: "What's the use." Fatalism; giving up rather than taking corrective action; antidote: "I'm not helpless — I can make a difference." | 7.8.2 | 10 | 2 |
| **IMSAFE Checklist** | Personal physiological self-assessment before flight: **I**llness, **M**edication, **S**tress, **A**lcohol, **F**atigue, **E**motion — any "yes" answer means do not fly | 7.8.7 | 9 | 2 |
| **Hyperventilation** | Rapid breathing that reduces carbon dioxide in the blood; symptoms include dizziness, tingling, and impaired thinking; can be triggered by stress; correct by deliberately slowing breathing | 7.8.6 | 9 | 2 |
| **Density Altitude Effect on Performance** | High temperature, high humidity, and high elevation reduce air density; motors must work harder to generate lift; batteries drain faster; performance degrades | 7.4.16 | 3b, 8 | 3 |
| **§107.27 — Alcohol and Drugs** | A remote pilot may not operate while impaired; no alcohol within 8 hours of flight; BAC must be below 0.04%; no drug use that impairs judgment | 7.8.8 | 9 | 1 |
| **Inflight Emergency** | Any unanticipated event requiring immediate deviation from normal procedures; under Part 107 the remote PIC may deviate from any rule to the extent necessary to respond to the emergency (§107.9) | 7.14.1 | 5 | 2 |
| **§107.9 — Accident Reporting** | A remote pilot must report any accident resulting in: serious injury, loss of consciousness, or property damage exceeding $500 (not counting the aircraft) to the FAA within 10 days | 7.14.16 | 5 | 1 |
| **Stress (Physiological)** | Physical or psychological tension that reduces cognitive performance; recognized stressors include time pressure, distractions, unfamiliar environments, and high stakes outcomes | 7.8.3 | 9 | 2 |
| **Fatigue** | A state of mental and physical exhaustion that impairs judgment and reaction time; cumulative fatigue from sleep deprivation is particularly dangerous; antidote: adequate rest, recognize limits | 7.8.7 | 9 | 2 |
| **Situational Awareness Loss** | The gradual degradation of accurate perception of the environment; often begins with fixation on one element while neglecting others; leads to accidents | 7.8.1 | 10 | 3 |
| **Lost Link Procedure** | A pre-planned response to control signal interruption; DJI aircraft default to RTH; the remote PIC should set RTH altitude and return path before flight, and notify anyone on the ground of the contingency | 7.14.5 | 5 | 2 |
