# Project: UAS GIS Mission — Survey to Deliverable

**Course:** Unmanned Aircraft (177024)
**Unit:** 4.1 — GIS, Remote Sensing & Photogrammetry
**Duration:** Weeks 29–30 deliverable
**Standards Focus:** 7.10.1–7.10.11

---

## Overview

Students complete a full professional UAS GIS workflow for a client scenario: survey planning → flight → photogrammetry processing → GIS analysis → final deliverable. The project demonstrates competency in the complete UAS data pipeline, from preflight to map product.

---

## Client Scenario (Assign one per student or let students choose)

**Scenario A — Agricultural Field Assessment**
A Knox County farmer wants to assess crop health patterns across a 10-acre field. Produce an annotated orthomosaic showing identified growth variation zones and a brief report on what the imagery suggests.

**Scenario B — Construction Site Documentation**
A local contractor needs a progress photo mosaic of a new building foundation pour. Produce a georegistered orthomosaic with perimeter measurements and a DEM showing earthwork elevation.

**Scenario C — Community Asset Inventory**
A township trustee wants to map all parking areas, structures, and green spaces on school property. Produce a QGIS map with labeled, digitized features and an area calculation for each category.

## Deliverables

### Deliverable 1: Pre-Mission Package (from Unit 2.2 format)
- Mission overview
- Airspace/authorization confirmation
- Weather brief with go/no-go decision
- Aircraft preflight checklist
- Survey mission parameters (altitude, overlap, estimated GSD, flight time)

### Deliverable 2: WebODM Processing Report
- Screenshot of WebODM project with image count and processing settings
- Screenshot of completed orthomosaic in WebODM viewer
- Screenshot of 3D point cloud (oblique angle)
- One paragraph: what did the processing results show? Any gaps or artifacts?

### Deliverable 3: QGIS Map Product
An exported map layout (PDF or PNG) containing:
- [ ] Orthomosaic as primary raster layer
- [ ] DEM as secondary raster layer (inset or transparency overlay)
- [ ] Minimum 5 digitized vector features with labeled "Feature_Type" attribute
- [ ] Area calculation for at least 3 of the 5 features (QGIS field calculator)
- [ ] Map elements: title, north arrow, scale bar, legend, CRS notation, your name, date

### Deliverable 4: Felt Shared Map
- Class Felt map contribution with your orthomosaic or vector layer uploaded
- At least 3 annotations/notes explaining features
- Evidence of engagement with 2 classmates' maps (screenshots of comments)

### Deliverable 5: Client Memo (1 page)
Write a brief professional memo to your scenario client explaining:
- What was collected and when
- Key findings visible in the map
- Accuracy limitations (GSD, weather on collection day, etc.)
- What they can use this data for
- One recommended next step (e.g., return flight to collect RGB+NDVI comparison)

## Rubric

| Category | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) |
|----------|--------------|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Pre-Mission Package** | Complete with accurate parameters; GSD calculated | Mostly complete; GSD missing or approximate | Major sections missing | Not submitted |
| **Orthomosaic Quality** | Clear, complete coverage; no significant gaps | Minor gaps; mostly complete | Notable gaps or distortion | Not processed |
| **QGIS Map Product** | All 5+ features; accurate areas; all map elements present | 4–5 features; 1–2 map elements missing | 2–3 features; several elements missing | Not completed |
| **Felt Collaboration** | Ortho/vector uploaded; 3+ annotations; 2 classmate comments | Contribution present; 1–2 annotations; 1 comment | Layer uploaded only; no annotation | Not contributed |
| **Client Memo** | Professional format; specific findings; accuracy limitations addressed; actionable recommendation | Mostly complete; 1 area vague or missing | Present but generic; no specific data referenced | Not submitted |
| **Lab Questions** | All 5 answered with specific, accurate responses | 4–5 answered; 1 vague | 2–3 answered | Fewer than 2 answered |

**Total: 24 points**
