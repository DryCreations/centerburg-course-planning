# Week 5: Meteorology and the Weather Decision: Outline

**Course:** Aviation UAS (177024)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Week:** 5 | **Unit:** 2.1 Meteorology | **Dates:** Mon Sep 14 to Fri Sep 18

> **Weather is the thing that actually scrubs your flights.** You have been reading it off a graphical
> interface all year. This week you learn to read the raw code, and you decide your own limits before you
> are standing in a field with everyone waiting on you.

---

## What This Week Covers

**Monday:** the anatomy of a METAR, field by field. **Tuesday:** decoding drills and sky condition.
**Wednesday:** the weather that actually threatens a small drone. **Thursday:** TAFs, and writing your own
personal minimums. **Friday:** quiz, then a live weather brief.

## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Decode a full METAR field by field without a reference
- Convert Zulu time to local time and say why a METAR's age matters
- Name the weather conditions that are dangerous to a small drone and explain why
- Read a TAF and say what is expected to change
- Write and defend your own personal minimums
