# Week 5: Weather, Airspace, and the Flight Decision: Outline

**Course:** Aviation UAS (177024)
**Quarter:** Q1 | **Week:** 5 | **Units:** 2.1 Meteorology, 1.3 Regulations | **Dates:** Mon Sep 14 to Fri Sep 18

> **Weather decides whether you can fly. Airspace decides whether you may.** This week covers both, and ends
> with the limits you set for yourself before you are standing in a field with everyone waiting on you.

---

## What This Week Covers

**Monday:** the anatomy of a METAR, field by field, and group decoding. **Tuesday:** airspace classes, LAANC,
and reading a sectional chart. **Wednesday:** the weather that actually threatens a small drone.
**Thursday:** TAFs, and writing your own personal minimums. **Friday:** quiz, then a live weather brief.

## Student Overview

### What You'll Be Able to Do

- Decode a full METAR field by field without a reference
- Convert Zulu time to local time and say why a METAR's age matters
- Name the airspace classes and say which require authorization before you fly
- Explain what LAANC does and what a grid ceiling means
- Identify airspace classes from sectional chart symbols and colors
- Name the weather conditions that are dangerous to a small drone and explain why
- Read a TAF and say what is expected to change
- Write and defend your own personal minimums
