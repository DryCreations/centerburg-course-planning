# Your Personal Minimums Card

**Aviation UAS, Week 5, Thursday.** Part 107 gives you the legal floor. Your personal minimums are the line
**you** draw, above that floor, before you are standing in a field with a drone in your hands and everyone
waiting on you.

**The whole point is that you decide now, calm, instead of then, under pressure.**

---

## The legal floor (you may never go below these)

| Item | Part 107 limit |
|------|----------------|
| Visibility | **3 statute miles** minimum |
| Distance from clouds | **500 ft below, 2,000 ft horizontally** |
| Altitude | **400 ft AGL** maximum |
| Groundspeed | **100 mph** maximum |
| Line of sight | Aircraft **visible to your own eyes** at all times |

---

## The three questions before every flight

Write your answers on the flight log, every time.

1. **What does the METAR say right now?** Read it out loud, field by field.
2. **Which number is closest to one of my limits?** Name it and say the value.
3. **What is the TAF saying will change while we are out there?**

---

## The rule that makes this worth doing

> **If any single number is outside your limits, it is a no-go. Not a discussion, not a vote, not "but we
> only need five minutes."**

You wrote these limits down when nothing was on the line. The version of you standing in a field with a
class watching is not the one who should be renegotiating them.

**Scrubbing a flight when the numbers say scrub is the job. It is not a failure and it is not a wasted day.**
Log the reason and go again another day.
