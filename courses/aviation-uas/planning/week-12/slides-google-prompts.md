# Aviation UAS Week 12: Daily Slides

**Teacher-only.** One Google Slides prompt per day. Paste each into "Help me create a presentation."
Slide 1 stays up, or post it on the board. Same three parts every day: start, a short lesson with one
practice, then BPA or ODOT with a target and an exit ticket. Post each exit ticket as a Classroom
question.

**To prepare:** a drone and printed preflight checklists for Monday and Thursday. Wednesday needs
`tfr.faa.gov` and the B4UFLY app or site.

---

## Monday

**Practice:** one drone on the front table. Students take turns reading a checklist line aloud while
another student checks it on the drone. Everyone has a printed checklist and marks each line.

```
Create a presentation for a high school drone class called "Monday: Preflight." Dark navy background, bold pink and light blue headings, large readable text, simple flat icons. Keep text short. 6 slides:

Slide 1, "Today": top section headed "What we are covering" lists in full, in smaller text: "7.11.2 Describe airworthiness requirements." "7.11.14 Perform and analyze a preflight assessment." "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." Bottom section headed "Agenda": "Do now (7.11.2)." "Who decides it is safe to fly (7.11.2)." "Practice: walk the preflight checklist on a real drone (7.11.14)." "BPA or ODOT (1.2.5)." "Exit ticket."

Slide 2, "Do Now": "A propeller has a tiny crack. The flight is in five minutes. Do you fly? Who decides?"

Slide 3, "Airworthiness": "Before every flight, the remote pilot in command must check that the drone is in safe condition to fly." "The RPIC makes the final go/no-go call. Not the client. Not the boss." "Not sure? It is a no-go."

Slide 4, "The Preflight Checklist": two columns. Left, headed "The drone": "Propellers: no cracks, tight." "Arms locked, body undamaged." "Battery charged and clicked in." "Gimbal cover off, lens clean." Right, headed "Before takeoff": "Controller charged." "GPS locked, home point recorded." "RTH altitude above the tallest obstacle." "Airspace and weather checked. Area clear of people." Include a simple top-down drone icon.

Slide 5, "Work Time (1.2.5)": "Target: one visible step on your BPA competition. Or continue ODOT." "Choose a competition first if you have not."

Slide 6, "Exit Ticket": "Name two things on the preflight checklist that would make you say no-go."
```

**Do-now answer:** no-go. The RPIC decides, and a cracked prop fails.

## Tuesday

**Practice:** scenario slides. Pairs decide what to do, then share out.

```
Create a presentation for a high school drone class called "Tuesday: Performance and Emergencies." Dark navy background, bold pink and light blue headings, large readable text, simple flat icons. Keep text short. 9 slides:

Slide 1, "Today": top section headed "What we are covering" lists in full, in smaller text: "7.11.11 Describe atmospheric conditions affecting performance." "7.14.15 Describe loss of aircraft control link and fly-aways." "7.14.16 Describe loss of Global Positioning System (GPS) signal during flight and potential consequences." "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." Bottom section headed "Agenda": "Do now (7.11.11)." "Performance: thin air and cold batteries (7.11.11)." "Emergencies: lost link, flyaways, lost GPS (7.14.15, 7.14.16)." "Practice: what do you do? (7.14.15, 7.14.16)." "BPA or ODOT (1.2.5)." "Exit ticket."

Slide 2, "Do Now": "Hot, humid afternoon. Will your drone's battery last longer or shorter than usual? Why?"

Slide 3, "Performance (7.11.11)": "Hot, high, humid: thin air. Less lift, motors work harder, shorter flights." "Cold: batteries lose power fast. Keep them warm until takeoff." "Wind and extra weight: more battery used."

Slide 4, "Lost Link (7.14.15)": "The drone loses its connection to the controller." "Most drones then Return to Home on their own." "That is why you set the home point and the RTH altitude BEFORE takeoff." Include a simple graphic of a drone flying back along a curved arrow to an H on the ground.

Slide 5, "Flyaway (7.14.15)": "The drone flies off and will not respond." "Keep trying to regain control. Note its last position and direction." "Tell anyone in its path." "If someone is seriously hurt, or there is over $500 of damage to other property, it must be reported to the FAA within 10 days."

Slide 6, "Lost GPS (7.14.16)": "Without GPS, the drone switches to ATTI mode." "It holds its height but DRIFTS with the wind." "Return to Home may not work. You fly it back by hand." "Fly near tall buildings or under trees? GPS can drop."

Slide 7, "What Do You Do?": three numbered situations: "1. Your screen says 'Signal lost.' The drone starts climbing." "2. Your drone starts drifting sideways on its own. The app says 'GPS weak.'" "3. It is 30 degrees out and your battery was in the car all morning." Bottom: "Talk with a partner. Be ready to explain."

Slide 8, "Work Time (1.2.5)": "Target: one visible step on your BPA competition. Or continue ODOT."

Slide 9, "Exit Ticket": "Your drone loses GPS. What happens, and what do you do?"
```

**What Do You Do answers:** 1. Lost link, RTH is climbing to its RTH altitude. Watch it, keep trying
to reconnect, and clear the landing spot. 2. Lost GPS, ATTI mode. Fly it back by hand, against the
drift. 3. Cold battery: warm it up before flight, and plan a shorter flight.

## Wednesday

**Practice:** each student opens `tfr.faa.gov` (or B4UFLY) and finds one active TFR or airspace
restriction, and what it is for.

```
Create a presentation for a high school drone class called "Wednesday: NOTAMs and TFRs." Dark navy background, bold pink and light blue headings, large readable text, simple flat icons. Keep text short. 6 slides:

Slide 1, "Today": top section headed "What we are covering" lists in full, in smaller text: "7.11.6 Describe the Notice to Air Missions (NOTAM) system and its use." "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." Bottom section headed "Agenda": "Do now (7.11.6)." "What NOTAMs and TFRs are (7.11.6)." "Practice: find a real TFR (7.11.6)." "BPA or ODOT (1.2.5)." "Exit ticket."

Slide 2, "Do Now": "The president is visiting Columbus tomorrow. Can you fly your drone near the event? How would you find out?"

Slide 3, "NOTAMs": "Notice to Air Missions." "An alert about something new or changed in the airspace: a closed area, a crane, an airshow." "Check them before every flight."

Slide 4, "TFRs": "Temporary Flight Restriction: airspace closed for a time." "Common causes: VIP visits, wildfires, big stadium events, disasters." "Flying in one can mean big fines." Include a simple map graphic with a red circle labeled "TFR."

Slide 5, "Practice": "Open tfr.faa.gov or B4UFLY." "Find one active TFR or restriction." "Write: where it is, why it is there, and when it ends."

Slide 6, "Work Time and Exit Ticket": "Target: one visible step on your BPA competition. Or continue ODOT." Bottom, headed "Exit ticket": "Paste the TFR you found: where, why, and when it ends."
```

## Thursday

Same rule as last Thursday: **everyone shows progress, or nobody goes out.** Check screens during the
first 15 minutes. **Rain:** stay in, run the preflight checklist on the drone again, then BPA.

```
Create a presentation for a high school drone class called "Thursday: Fly Day." Dark navy background, bold pink and light blue headings, large readable text, simple flat icons. Keep text short. 4 slides:

Slide 1, "Today": top section headed "What we are covering" lists in full, in smaller text: "7.11.14 Perform and analyze a preflight assessment." "1.2.5 Communicate information (e.g., directions, ideas, vision, workplace expectations) for an intended audience and purpose." Bottom section headed "Agenda": "15 minutes of silent work. I check every screen (1.2.5)." "Everyone ready: full preflight from the checklist (7.11.14)." "Fly, then a Return to Home test (7.11.14)." "Not ready: BPA or ODOT."

Slide 2, "Before We Fly": "We go out ONLY if EVERY student shows me: your chosen BPA competition, and real progress on it." "Promotional Photography does not count on its own." Bold box: "If anyone has nothing to show, nobody goes out."

Slide 3, "On the Field": "1. Preflight checklist, every line, out loud." "2. Set the RTH altitude above the tallest obstacle." "3. Fly." "4. Trigger Return to Home. Watch where it climbs and lands."

Slide 4, "Exit Ticket": "Did the drone come back where you expected? What would you change?"
```
