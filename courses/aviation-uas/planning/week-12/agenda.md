## Vocab

- **Airworthiness:** the drone is in safe condition to fly
- **Preflight inspection:** a check of the drone, controller, and batteries, from a checklist, before
  every flight
- **Remote pilot in command (RPIC):** the person responsible for the flight. Makes the final call on
  whether it is safe
- **Go/no-go:** the decision to fly or not
- **Density altitude:** how high the air "feels" to the aircraft. Hot, high, and humid air is thin,
  so there is less lift
- **Lost link:** the drone loses its connection to the controller
- **Flyaway:** the drone flies off on its own and will not respond
- **Return to Home (RTH):** the drone flies back to its home point on its own: on command, on lost
  link, or on low battery
- **Home point:** the spot the drone recorded at takeoff. RTH flies back here
- **RTH altitude:** the height the drone climbs to before returning. Set it above the tallest obstacle
- **GPS:** satellites that tell the drone where it is, so it can hold its position
- **ATTI mode:** what happens without GPS. The drone holds its height but drifts with the wind
- **NOTAM:** Notice to Air Missions. An alert about changes or hazards in the airspace
- **TFR:** Temporary Flight Restriction. A NOTAM that closes airspace for a time, like for a stadium
  event or a VIP visit
