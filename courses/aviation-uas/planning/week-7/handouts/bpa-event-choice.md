# Choosing Your BPA Event

**Aviation UAS | Week 7** | **Your choice is due by the end of Week 9**

Aviation is a Transportation Technologies course, so there is no BPA event that tests drone flying. What
BPA does have is events that test **the business and professional side**, which is most of what a drone
career actually is.

**Two paths. They are equally supported and equally respected.**

---

## The two

### #555 Presentation, Individual

**What it is:** research a UAS topic and present on it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You produce** | A presentation, delivered live |
| **Subject** | Your choice, within the UAS field |
| **Good if** | You have a topic you actually care about, you are comfortable presenting, or you want the freedom to change direction |
| **The work** | Research, a clear argument, slides that support rather than repeat you, and rehearsal |

**Topic ideas that are genuinely interesting:** drone delivery and why it keeps not happening; UAS in
agriculture; search and rescue; counter-UAS and airport shutdowns; BVLOS and what it would take; privacy
law; powerline and cell tower inspection; wildfire mapping.

### #505 Entrepreneurship

**What it is:** design a drone services business with an operating plan and an investor pitch.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You produce** | A business plan and a pitch |
| **Subject** | A drone services company you invent |
| **Good if** | You would rather build one thing all year and go deep on it |
| **The work** | Market research, services and pricing, equipment and cost, regulations you would operate under, and the pitch |

> **The Unit 4.3 capstone IS this event.** If you choose #505 and take the capstone seriously, your BPA
> entry is nearly finished as a side effect. That is not a trick. It is the most efficient path through
> this course, and it is worth saying out loud.

---

## Choosing honestly

| Ask yourself | Leans #555 | Leans #505 |
|---|---|---|
| Do I have one topic I am genuinely curious about? | Yes | Not really |
| Would I rather explore several things or build one? | Explore | Build |
| Do I like presenting, or does the thought of it help me prepare? | Yes | Either |
| Do I want my capstone and my BPA entry to be the same work? | No | **Yes** |
| Am I interested in how businesses actually run? | Maybe | Yes |

**Neither is easier.** #555 has less scaffolding and more freedom. #505 has more structure and more
total work, most of which you were doing anyway.
