# Middle School CS: Q2 Plan

**Q2 | Tue Oct 20 to mid-January**

New tool this quarter: **Tinkercad.** First you design 3D objects, then you build and program circuits.
Plans can shift as we go.

---

## The quarter, roughly

| Weeks | What |
|-------|------|
| **1 to 3** | **3D design.** Shapes, holes, grouping, measuring. A few designs get 3D printed |
| **4 to 7** | **Circuits.** Build circuits on screen and program them with code blocks: lights, buttons, sensors |
| **8 to 9** | **Final project:** a 3D design and a circuit that work together |

Nothing gets broken or burned out. The circuits run in a simulation.
