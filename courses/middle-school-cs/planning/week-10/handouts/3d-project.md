# 3D Design Project: Make Something That Matters

**Middle School CS | Plan this week. Build next week. Due Mon Nov 2**

Design something bigger than anything you have built so far, for a real reason. Pick one track.

---

## Pick a track

**A. Solve a problem for a real person.** Something they would actually use. Measure the real things it
has to fit.

- A phone or tablet stand for someone's desk
- A hook or holder for headphones, keys, or a controller
- A cable organizer, or a stand for chargers
- A desk organizer sized to your actual supplies
- A plant pot with a drainage hole and a saucer
- A tool that makes something easier: a jar opener, a pencil grip, a bag hook

**B. Build a scale model.** Something real, at a size you choose and write down.

- Your dream bedroom, with furniture
- A redesign of a space at school
- A house, a store, or a landmark
- A car, a rocket, or a drone frame

**C. Design a game.** Every piece of it.

- A board and its pieces
- A marble maze or a mini golf hole
- A dice tower
- A set of chess or checkers pieces

**Have a different idea?** Ask me. It has to be as big as these.

---

## Requirements

- **At least 15 shapes**, using **holes, groups, and align**
- **Exact sizes in millimeters.** Track A: at least one part fits a real object you measured. Track B:
  write your scale, like 1 mm = 10 cm
- **Text** somewhere: a name, a label, or a title
- **One part print ready:** grouped, flat, and no wider than 80 mm
- Named `LastName_Project`

---

## Plan it (due Friday)

**1.** Which track, and what are you making?

**2.** Who is it for, or what is it for? What problem does it solve, or why does it matter to you?

**3.** Sketch it on paper from **two sides**, with the **sizes in mm** written on it. Take a photo and
add it here.

**4.** List the parts you will build, and the shapes each one will need.

**5.** Track A: what did you measure, and what were the numbers? Track B: what is your scale? Track C:
how is the game played?

**Plan done?** Start building.

## Build it (next week)

**6.** What was the hardest part to build, and how did you solve it?

**7.** What would you change if you built it again?

**Turn in:** your Tinkercad link, and screenshots from the **front, side, and top.**

---

## Graded on

- **Plan:** questions 1 to 5, with a sketch that has sizes
- **The build:** every requirement met, and it looks like what you planned
- **Questions 6 and 7**

A few print-ready parts will be 3D printed.
