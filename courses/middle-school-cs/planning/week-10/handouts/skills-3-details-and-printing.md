# Tinkercad Skills 3: Details and Printing

**Middle School CS | Friday**

New design. Rename it `LastName_Skills3`.

**Words for today:** text shape, ruler, workplane tool, tolerance, export, print ready.

---

## Part 1: Text

1. Copy your pencil cup from yesterday into this design
2. Add your **initials** with the **Text** shape: about **15 mm tall, 2 mm thick**
3. Stand them up and push them **1 mm into** the cup wall. Group

**1.** What would happen when printing if the letters floated 1 mm away from the cup?

## Part 2: The ruler

1. Drag the **Ruler** onto the workplane
2. Click a shape. Read its distances from the ruler, then type new ones

**2.** How is the ruler different from the size numbers you used Wednesday?

## Part 3: The workplane tool

1. Make a **wedge** shape
2. Use the **Workplane tool** to put a new workplane on its slanted face
3. Drop a small cylinder onto that face. It sits at the same angle

## Part 4: Measure something real

Pick a small object: an eraser, a phone, a charging case. Measure it with a ruler in **millimeters**.

1. Model it as close as you can, **within 1 mm**
2. Build a holder or case for it. Leave **0.5 mm of tolerance** so it fits

**3.** What object, and what were its three measurements?

## Part 5: A keychain, print ready

- **40 by 20 mm, 3 mm thick**, with a **4 mm hole** for the ring and your name on it

**Print-ready check:**

- [ ] Grouped into one object
- [ ] Flat on the workplane
- [ ] Nothing wider than 80 mm

Export your keychain as an STL. Then open **3D Design Project** on Classroom and start your plan.
