# Tinkercad Skills 1: Getting Around

**Middle School CS | Wednesday**

New design. Rename it `LastName_Skills1`. Do everything in this one design.

**Words for today:** workplane, solid, handles, millimeter, view cube, orbit, pan, zoom, lift arrow,
nudge, snap grid.

---

## Part 1: Move your view

1. **Orbit** around your empty workplane. **Pan** left and right. **Zoom** in and out
2. Click **Top** on the view cube, then **Front**, then the **Home** button

**1.** How do you orbit, pan, and zoom? Write the mouse move for each.

## Part 2: Exact sizes

Click a shape, then click a size number to type an exact size.

1. A box: **20 by 30 mm, 10 mm tall**
2. A cylinder: **25 mm wide, 40 mm tall**
3. A sphere: **15 mm** wide

**2.** What happens to the shape when you drag a corner handle compared with typing a number?

## Part 3: Stack and move

1. Make a **10 mm cube**. Use the **lift arrow** to raise it exactly **10 mm**, then set it on top of
   your box
2. **Nudge** a shape 25 mm to the right with the arrow keys
3. Change the **snap grid** to 0.5 mm, then to 5 mm. Drag a shape each time

**3.** What does the snap grid change?

## Part 4: Build a table

Exact sizes. Check each one.

- **Top:** 60 by 40 mm, 4 mm thick, raised **30 mm** off the workplane
- **Four legs:** 4 by 4 mm, **30 mm** tall, one under each corner

## Part 5: Build a snowman

Three spheres, **30, 22, and 15 mm**, stacked so each one just touches the one below.

**4.** How high off the workplane did you raise the middle sphere? Show how you figured it out.
