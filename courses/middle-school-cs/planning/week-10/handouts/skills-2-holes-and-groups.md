# Tinkercad Skills 2: Holes and Groups

**Middle School CS | Thursday**

New design. Rename it `LastName_Skills2`.

**Words for today:** hole, group, ungroup, align, duplicate, mirror, rotate.

---

## Part 1: Cut a window

1. A box, **40 by 10 mm, 30 mm tall**
2. A smaller box, **15 by 20 mm, 15 mm tall**. Click **Hole**. Push it through the middle of the wall
3. Select both and **Group**

**1.** What happened to the hole when you grouped? What happens if you **Ungroup**?

## Part 2: A die

1. A **20 mm cube**
2. A **4 mm sphere**, set to **Hole.** Push it halfway into the top face
3. **Duplicate** it (Ctrl+D) and move the copies to make **five** dots on top
4. Use **Align** to line them up
5. Do two more faces: **two dots** and **three dots**. Group everything

**2.** How does Align help? What would be harder without it?

## Part 3: A pencil cup

1. A cylinder, **50 mm wide, 60 mm tall**
2. A cylinder, **44 mm wide, 60 mm tall**, set to **Hole.** Raise it **3 mm**
3. **Align** both, centered. **Group**

**3.** Why is the hole 6 mm narrower than the cup, and not 3 mm?

## Part 4: Mirror and rotate

1. Build **half** of a bridge or an arch
2. **Duplicate** it and **Mirror** it to make the other half. Line them up
3. Rotate one extra shape exactly **45 degrees** and add it somewhere on your design

---

## Done early?

A box with a lid that fits. Make the lid's inside **0.5 mm** bigger than the box so it slides on.

## Graded on

The window, the die, the cup, and the mirrored build, plus three answers.
