# Tinkercad: Getting Started

**Middle School CS | Tuesday**

## Step 1: Your account

1. Go to **tinkercad.com**
2. **Already have an account?** Sign in
3. **New?** Click **Join Class** and type the class code from the board. Use the nickname I give you
4. Click **Create**, then **3D Design**

## Step 2: Try these

1. Drag a **box** onto the workplane
2. Drag the white corner squares to resize it. Drag the black arrow on top to change its height
3. Drag the curved arrows to rotate it
4. Click the **Hole** button on the right. The box turns see-through
5. Drag a **cylinder** onto the box. Make it a hole too, or keep it solid
6. Select both, then click **Group**. What happened?

**1.** What does the black arrow on top of a shape do?

**2.** What happens when you group a hole with a solid shape?

## Step 3: Rename it

Click the random name at the top left. Rename your design `LastName_FirstShapes`.

## Done early?

Build anything from at least five shapes. Use at least one hole.

## Graded on

Your account works, your design is renamed, and both questions are answered.
