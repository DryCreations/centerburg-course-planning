# Build Along: Pencil Cup

**Middle School CS | Wednesday to Friday**

We build this together, step by step. You will use every tool that is on next week's quiz.

## The goals

- A cup that holds pencils: **50 mm wide, 60 mm tall**
- A **floor** and **walls 3 mm thick**
- **Your initials** on the side
- **Grouped**, flat on the workplane, ready to print

---

## Wednesday: the cup

1. New design. Rename it `LastName_PencilCup`
2. Drag a **cylinder** onto the workplane. Set it to **50 by 50 mm, 60 mm tall**
3. Drag a second cylinder. Set it to **44 by 44 mm, 60 mm tall**. Click **Hole**
4. Raise the hole **3 mm** off the workplane. That leaves a floor
5. Select both. **Align** them, centered both ways
6. **Group.** You have a cup

**1.** Why is the hole 6 mm smaller than the cup, and not 3 mm smaller?

## Thursday: your initials

1. Drag the **Text** shape onto the workplane. Type your initials
2. Make the letters about **15 mm tall** and **2 mm thick**
3. Turn them to stand up, and move them against the side of the cup
4. Push them **1 mm into the wall** so they are attached, not floating
5. Group the letters and the cup

**2.** What would happen when printing if the letters were floating 1 mm away from the cup?

## Friday: make it yours, and check it

Add one thing: a second compartment, a pen slot, a pattern of holes, a handle.

**Before you finish, check:**

- [ ] Everything is **grouped** into one object
- [ ] It sits **flat on the workplane**
- [ ] Nothing is wider than **80 mm**
- [ ] It is named `LastName_PencilCup`

**3.** What did you add, and which tools did you use to make it?

---

## Graded on

The cup meets all four goals and the checklist, plus three answers. A few will be 3D printed.
