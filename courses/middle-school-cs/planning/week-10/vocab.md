# Tinkercad Vocabulary

**Middle School CS | Q2** | Next Friday's quiz uses these words.

## Wednesday: getting around

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Workplane** | The blue grid you build on |
| **Shape** | A ready-made 3D object you drag onto the workplane |
| **Solid** | A shape that stays as material |
| **Handles** | The small squares on a selected shape. Drag them to resize |
| **Millimeter (mm)** | The unit Tinkercad measures in. 10 mm is 1 centimeter |
| **View cube** | The cube in the corner. Click a side to look from that side |
| **Orbit** | Turn your view around the design. Right-click and drag |
| **Pan** | Slide your view. Hold Shift and right-click and drag |
| **Zoom** | Move closer or farther. Scroll wheel |
| **Lift arrow** | The black cone on top of a shape. Drag it to raise the shape |
| **Nudge** | Move a shape with the arrow keys: 1 mm a press, 10 mm with Shift |
| **Snap grid** | How far a shape jumps when you drag it |

## Thursday: cutting and combining

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hole** | A shape that cuts away whatever it overlaps, once grouped |
| **Group** | Join shapes into one object. Holes cut when you group |
| **Ungroup** | Split a group back into its shapes |
| **Align** | Line shapes up by their edges or centers |
| **Duplicate** | Make an exact copy in the same spot (Ctrl+D) |
| **Mirror** | Flip a shape to its opposite side |
| **Rotate** | Turn a shape with the curved arrows |

## Friday: details and printing

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Text shape** | Letters you can type, size, and group like any shape |
| **Ruler** | A tool that shows exact distances from a point you choose |
| **Workplane tool** | Puts a new workplane on any face, even a slanted one |
| **Tolerance** | A small gap left so two parts fit together, like 0.5 mm |
| **Export** | Save the design as a file a 3D printer can use (STL) |
| **Print ready** | Grouped into one object, flat on the workplane, and the right size |
