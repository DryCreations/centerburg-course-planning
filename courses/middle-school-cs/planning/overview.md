# Middle School Computer Science — Overview

Grades **7 and 8 combined**, one section, year-long elective. Non-CTE, not part of the Interactive Media
pathway, no WebXam. Meets daily, full period, in Room 173 alongside the high school courses.

## Confirmed

- Aligned to the **Ohio K–12 Computer Science Standards, grades 6–8** — see [standards.md](../standards.md)
- Each quarter covers a breadth of topics rather than one long unit; at least three of the five strands per quarter
- **MakeCode Arcade** is the programming backbone (pulling activities from Code.org where useful)
- Robotics: **VEX IQ** (count unknown) and **two Spheros** — Spheros run as a station with a queue, never a
  whole-class activity
- 3D printers available; filament budget still to be discussed
- No purchases right now: physical computing uses VEX IQ sensors rather than micro:bits
- Drones are a topic only — no middle school flight
- No prior CS exposure assumed; first two weeks mix unplugged and tool work
- Grading close to the CTE model but flexible, with a redo policy; designed to feed the pathway courses
