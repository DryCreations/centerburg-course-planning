## The six requirements, from your plan

1. A **list** the game reads to make a decision
2. A **for or while loop** where the count matters. Not `forever`
3. A **conditional** that can go either way
4. A **variable** that changes during play
5. A way to **win**
6. A way to **lose**

> **Remove it. If the game still plays the same, it was not meaningful.**
