## Part 1 (slides 1 to 9)

```
Create a presentation for a middle school computer science class called "3D Design Vocab Review." Bright, friendly style: white background, bold orange and teal headings, large readable text, simple flat icons of 3D shapes. Keep text short. 9 slides:

Slide 1, "Today": headed "Focus: 3D design in Tinkercad." Then headed "Agenda": "Vocab review: answer out loud, explain why." "Work on your 3D Design Project, due Monday." "Optional: the Gimkit review on Classroom. Vocab quiz Friday, Nov 6."

Slide 2, question slide, title "Question 1": "You want to see the BACK of your design. What do you do?" Four lettered options: "A. Right-click and drag." "B. Rotate the shape with the curved arrows." "C. Hold Shift, then right-click and drag." "D. Scroll the wheel." Bottom: "Which is best? Why not B?"

Slide 3, explanation slide, title "Moving Your View": "Answer: A, Orbit. Moving your view never changes your design." Then four short lines: "Orbit: right-click and drag to turn around the design." "Pan: Shift and right-click and drag to slide." "Zoom: scroll wheel, closer or farther." "View cube: click a side to look from that side." Include a simple graphic of a computer mouse with the right button and scroll wheel labeled.

Slide 4, question slide, title "Question 2": "You need to move a shape exactly 10 mm to the right. What is fastest?" Four lettered options: "A. Drag it and hope." "B. Tap the right arrow once." "C. Hold Shift and tap the right arrow once." "D. Drag the black cone." Bottom: "Which is right? What does B do?"

Slide 5, explanation slide, title "Moving Shapes Exactly": "Answer: C. Nudge: arrow keys move 1 mm, with Shift 10 mm." Then short lines: "Millimeter (mm): Tinkercad's unit. 10 mm is 1 cm." "Handles: the white squares. Drag to resize." "Lift arrow: the black cone. Drag to raise a shape." "Snap grid: how far a shape jumps when you drag it." Include a simple graphic of a box with white squares on its corners and a black cone on top, each labeled.

Slide 6, question slide, title "Question 3": "You put a hole cylinder inside a box. Nothing got cut. Why?" Four lettered options: "A. The hole is the wrong color." "B. You have not grouped them yet." "C. The box needs to be a hole too." "D. You have not exported yet." Bottom: "Which is right? Why?"

Slide 7, explanation slide, title "Solids, Holes, and Groups": "Answer: B. A hole only cuts once you group it." Then short lines: "Workplane: the blue grid you build on." "Shape: a ready-made object you drag in." "Solid: stays as material." "Hole: cuts away what it overlaps." "Group: join shapes into one. Holes cut now." "Ungroup: split them back apart." Include a simple three-step graphic: a box and a striped see-through cylinder, a plus sign, then a box with a round hole through it.

Slide 8, question slide, title "Question 4": "You built a left wing. How do you make a matching right wing?" Four lettered options: "A. Build a new one from scratch." "B. Duplicate it, then rotate the copy." "C. Duplicate it, then mirror the copy." "D. Ungroup it, then align it." Bottom: "Which is right? What would B give you?"

Slide 9, explanation slide, title "Copy, Flip, Turn, Line Up": "Answer: C. Mirror flips it to the opposite side." Then short lines: "Duplicate: an exact copy in the same spot (Ctrl+D)." "Mirror: flip to the opposite side." "Rotate: turn with the curved arrows." "Align: line shapes up by edges or centers." Include a simple graphic of a left wing and its mirrored right wing on either side of a dashed line.
```

## Part 2 (slides 10 to 16)

```
Continue the same "3D Design Vocab Review" presentation for a middle school computer science class, same style: white background, bold orange and teal headings, large text, simple flat graphics. 7 slides:

Slide 1, question slide, title "Question 5": "You want your name on the slanted roof of your house. What do you use?" Four lettered options: "A. Text shape and the Workplane tool." "B. Text shape and the lift arrow." "C. The ruler and the snap grid." "D. Align and mirror." Bottom: "Which is right? Why?"

Slide 2, explanation slide, title "Building on Any Face": "Answer: A. The Workplane tool puts a new workplane on any face, even a slanted one." Then short lines: "Text shape: letters you type, size, and group like any shape." "Ruler: shows exact distances from a point you choose." Include a simple graphic of a small house with a new grid laid on the slanted roof and letters sitting on it.

Slide 3, question slide, title "Question 6": "You print a box and a lid the exact same size. The lid will not go on. What went wrong?" Four lettered options: "A. No tolerance was left." "B. The snap grid was too big." "C. The lid was not mirrored." "D. The box was not exported." Bottom: "Which is right? How would you fix it?"

Slide 4, explanation slide, title "Tolerance": "Answer: A. Tolerance is a small gap so parts fit together." "Make the lid about 0.5 mm bigger on each side than the box." "Printers are not perfect. Same size means too tight." Include a simple graphic of a lid over a box with a tiny gap between them, labeled "0.5 mm."

Slide 5, question slide, title "Question 7": "Which design is ready to print?" Three lettered options: "A. Three loose shapes, flat on the workplane." "B. One grouped object, floating 5 mm above the workplane." "C. One grouped object, flat on the workplane, the right size." Bottom: "Which is right? What is wrong with the other two?"

Slide 6, explanation slide, title "Print Ready, Then Export": "Answer: C." A three-item checklist with check boxes: "Grouped into one object." "Flat on the workplane." "The right size." Then: "Export: save it as an STL file, the kind a 3D printer uses." Include a simple graphic of a 3D printer.

Slide 7, title "How to Review": four short lines in large text: "Play the Gimkit review on Classroom. It is optional." "Try each word in Tinkercad. Doing it beats reading it." "Explain three words to the person next to you." "Vocab quiz: Friday, Nov 6. Now: work on your 3D Design Project."
```
