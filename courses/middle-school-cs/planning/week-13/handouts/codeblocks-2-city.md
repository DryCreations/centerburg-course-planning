# Codeblocks 2: Build a City

**Middle School CS | Tuesday to Friday | Due Friday**

**Goal for the week:** build a city block with **a loop inside a loop.** One small piece of code builds
a whole grid of buildings.

Name your design `LastName_City`. Click **Run** often.

---

## Tuesday: A row, then a grid

A **loop inside a loop** is called a **nested loop.** The inside loop runs all the way through every
time the outside loop runs once.

1. Make a **row of 5 boxes:** count with `i` from 1 to 5, add a box, move it `i × 25` on the x axis
2. Put that whole loop **inside** another loop: count with `j` from 1 to 4
3. Inside, also move each box `j × 25` on the **y** axis. Run it

**1.** How many boxes did your grid make? How do you know without counting them?

## Wednesday: Buildings

1. Make a variable called `height`. Use it for each box's height
2. Set `height` to a different number inside the outer loop, so each row has its own height. Or try a
   **random** number, so every building is different

**2.** Change your grid to 6 by 6. How many buildings now?

## Thursday: Make it a city

Add at least **two** of these, with code:

- Roofs: a cone or wedge on top of each building
- Streets: a flat box under the grid
- Windows: small holes in each building
- A park: leave one spot empty

**3.** Which part of your code would you change to make the streets wider?

## Friday: Turn it in

Screenshot and link, the same way as always.

## Graded on

A city built with a nested loop and a variable, at least two added details, answers to all three
questions, and a screenshot and working link turned in.
