# How to Submit Your Tinkercad Work

**Middle School CS**

Every Tinkercad assignment needs **two things** on Classroom: **a screenshot** and **the link.**

## 1. Take a screenshot

Make your design fill the screen first. Then:

- **Chromebook:** press **Ctrl + Shift + Show windows** (the key with boxes), then drag around your
  design
- **Windows:** press **Windows + Shift + S**, then drag around your design
- **Mac:** press **Command + Shift + 4**, then drag around your design

## 2. Copy the link

1. Open your design in Tinkercad
2. Click **Share** (or **Invite**) and **copy the link**

## 3. Turn it in

1. Open the assignment on Classroom
2. Click **Add or create.** Upload or paste your **screenshot**
3. Click **Add or create** again, choose **Link**, and paste your **Tinkercad link**
4. Click **Turn in**

## Check before you leave

**1.** Open your link in a new tab. Does it show your design?

**2.** Does your screenshot show the whole design, big enough to see?

## Graded on

Both the screenshot and a working link, turned in on time.
