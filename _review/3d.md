## Alignment Notes from DT Review (apply to 3D)

**Vocab (all units):** Strictly tie vocabulary to course content and standards. No bell ringer prompts that reference specific assignments, units, or "on Day X." Keep only terms students need to communicate in class and terms pulled from the standards.

**Build culture time into early units (1.0 and 1.1):** First two units should include low-stakes, get-to-know-you activities. Students should feel comfortable in the room before being asked to produce creative work. Lab 1 in unit 1.0 should be a social/orientation activity, not a production task.

**Give students topic choice wherever possible:** In labs and projects, provide 3–4 options for subject matter so students can work on something they care about. At minimum, allow students to direct work toward personal interest, a chosen theme, or a BPA prompt.

**No background sections in labs:** Remove any "Background" section that explains why the skill is tested or references the WebXam/AC PRO exam. Students don't need to know the test rationale in the assignment instructions.

**Tool accuracy:** 3D uses Blender (3D modeling, rendering, animation) and potentially OnShape or Tinkercad for CAD/engineering-style work. Make sure all tool references match what is actually accessible to students. Remove references to Maya, 3ds Max, or tools students don't have.

**BPA Connections:** Note BPA event options in unit outlines and project files, keep them opt-in. Relevant 3D BPA events include 3D Architectural Model, Digital Media Production (if applicable), etc. Flag events for students who want to compete.

**In-person grading for projects:** Projects should note "graded in person — student walks teacher through evidence" rather than implying rubrics are checked against a submitted artifact alone.

**Tool accuracy and free tools only:** 3D uses Blender and potentially OnShape/Tinkercad. Students get Adobe generative credits as part of the education plan if needed. No paid tools beyond what is already part of the school's software access.

**Review Q3 and Q4 units for engagement:** As Q3/Q4 units are planned or revised, apply the same principles: approachable language, student choice in topics, no superfluous "Background" blocks, in-person grading for projects, and accurate tool references.
