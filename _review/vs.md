## Alignment Notes from DT Review (apply to VS)

**Vocab (all units):** Strictly tie vocabulary to course content and standards. No bell ringer prompts that reference specific assignments, units, or "on Day X." Keep only terms students need to communicate in class and terms pulled from the standards.

**Build culture time into early units (1.0 and 1.1):** First two units should include low-stakes, get-to-know-you activities. Students should feel comfortable in the room before being asked to produce creative work. Lab 1 in unit 1.0 should be a social/orientation activity, not a production task.

**Give students topic choice wherever possible:** In labs and projects, provide 3–4 options for subject matter so students can work on something they care about. At minimum, allow students to direct their work toward personal interest or a BPA prompt.

**No background sections in labs:** Remove any "Background" section that explains why the skill is tested or references the WebXam/AC PRO exam directly. Students don't need to know the test rationale in the assignment instructions.

**Tool accuracy:** VS uses DaVinci Resolve (video editing) and GarageBand or similar (audio). Make sure all tool references in labs and projects match what is actually installed and accessible to students. Remove any references to Adobe Premiere, Final Cut, or tools students don't have.

**BPA Connections:** Note BPA event options in unit outlines and project files, but keep them opt-in. Don't assume all students are pursuing BPA. Flag relevant events for students who want to compete — VS BPA events include Video Production Team, Digital Short Film, etc.

**Adobe Express only, generative credits included:** The entire DT course was rebuilt around Adobe Express as the sole tool. VS should verify that all tool references match what students actually have access to (DaVinci Resolve, GarageBand or equivalent). Students do get generative credits via the education plan for Adobe. No other Adobe Suite tools assumed.

**Review Q3 and Q4 units for engagement:** As Q3/Q4 units are planned or revised, continue applying the same principles: approachable language, student choice in topics, no superfluous "Background" blocks, in-person grading for projects, and accurate tool references.
