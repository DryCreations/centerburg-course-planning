# Day 05 - Tue, Aug 25 (Week 2)

**Course:** Yearbook

## Objective

Students understand the coverage points system and the coverage board, and submit beat and role preferences.

## Materials & Setup

*Coverage & Points* handout, coverage board (Google Sheet) live with the next four weeks of known events,
beat/role preference form in Classroom, fall athletics and arts calendars.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*A yearbook has a full page for the football team and nothing for the fall play. How does that happen, and who
is responsible?*

## Direct Instruction (18 min)

1. **The coverage problem**: the bell ringer *is* the lesson. Nobody decided to skip the play; nobody was
   scheduled to be there.
2. **Points system**: walk the table: what earns points, the bonus for filling gaps and covering your own
   beat, the quarter target.
3. **What counts as covered**: 20 usable frames, names verified, submitted within 48 hours, named and filed
   correctly. Say plainly: showing up is not coverage; submitting is.
4. **The board**: demo signing up. Sign-ups close 48 hours before the event. A conflict told in advance costs
   nothing; a no-show costs points and costs the book.
5. **Transportation**: say out loud that in-school coverage counts and nobody is stuck because they cannot
   get to a Friday game. Tell me and we plan around it.

## Work Time (20 min)

1. **Beat & role preference form** (Week 2, Part A): rank top three beats from the list built yesterday, name
   any role of interest, and state honestly what coverage they can get to.
2. **Sign up for one event** in the next three weeks, live, on the board.
3. Photo Editor / Coverage Coordinator candidates: help load the remaining known events onto the board.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

**Exit ticket:** *The event you signed up for, and what you need to do before that day.*
