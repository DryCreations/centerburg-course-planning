# Day 08 - Fri, Aug 28 (Week 2)

**Course:** Yearbook

## Objective

The staff is fully operational: beats and roles assigned, coverage board populated and signed up, first weekly
status submitted, deadline calendar live.

## Materials & Setup

Beat/role assignments finalized and printed, deadline board ready, coverage board on the projector, weekly
status form in Classroom.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*What is one thing happening at school in the next two weeks that nobody has signed up to cover yet?*
(Check the board while you answer.)

## Direct Instruction (15 min)

1. **Announce beats and roles.** Read them out. Explain a couple of the harder calls honestly: the ones that
   did not get a first choice deserve a reason.
2. **The deadline board goes up.** Every publisher deadline we know, plus internal deadlines leading to each.
   Point out that the first internal deadline is closer than it looks.
3. **Weekly status**: introduce the Friday routine: what you finished, what is stuck, what is due next. Five
   minutes, every Friday, counts in Staff Contribution.

## Work Time (20 min)

- **Submit the first weekly status.**
- **Fill the coverage gaps** from the bell ringer: anything in the next two weeks with no name on it gets one
  today.
- Finish and submit the Week 2 Staff Setup Package.
- Beat owners: start a beat sheet, the schedule for your beat through October, and who to ask for information.

## Closure (10 min)

**Photo review.** Put a dozen Day 03 and Day 07 photos on the screen. The staff votes: which three would go in
the book? Say why. This is the standard we are holding ourselves to, and it is the first time they set it
themselves rather than hearing it from me.

**Exit ticket:** *What is your first deadline, and what is the first step toward it?*

## Handouts Used

- Deadline board, coverage board (Classroom)

## Assignment Given / Due

- **Due:** Week 2, Staff Setup Package
- **Due:** first weekly status
- Preview: production begins Monday, real pages, real deadlines

## Teacher Notes

- Every student should leave today knowing: their beat, their role, at least one event they are covering, and
  their first deadline. If anyone cannot state those four things, catch them before they walk out.
- Legacy Lead: confirm the legacy plan is scheduled, not just written.
- First real coverage event is likely next week: check that whoever signed up has camera access sorted.
