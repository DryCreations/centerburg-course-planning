# Day 03 - Fri, Aug 21 (Week 1)

**Course:** Yearbook

## Objective

Everyone has had a camera turn, gotten photos in focus, offloaded them, and submitted both week 1 assignments.

## Materials

All camera kits out and charged, checkout form live, Drive folders ready, projector for the focus review.

## Bell Ringer

Look at these two photos on the board. Both are of the same thing. Which one could we print, and what is wrong
with the other one?

## Direct Instruction

Short, then get out of the way:

1. Review focus. Half press, check the face, shoot. If it comes out soft, get closer and try again.
2. Offloading. Card to Drive folder, rename, then upload to Classroom. Photos left on a card are gone when the
   next class clears it.
3. Both assignments are due at the end of today.

## Work Time

Rotation. Cameras are shared, so run it in turns:

**Camera turn:** check out, shoot, offload, check in. Anyone who has not had a real turn yet goes first.

**Everyone else:** finish the About Me Square in Express, and cull your photos down to the 10 you are
submitting.

I circulate on focus. Look at a few photos on the back of the camera with each student while they still have
it in hand.

## Closure

Both assignments submitted before anyone packs up. Cameras checked in first.

Exit ticket: which of your 10 photos is your best, and what makes it better than the others?
