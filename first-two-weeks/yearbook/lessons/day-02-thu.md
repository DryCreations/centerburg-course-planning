# Day 02 - Thu, Aug 20 (Week 1)

**Course:** Yearbook

## Objective

Students know how the class is graded and how deadlines work, they can hold a camera and get a photo in focus,
and the About Me Square is started in Express.

## Materials

Printed syllabus and staff procedures, one camera case at the front, card reader, checkout form live, Express
open on the projector.

## Bell Ringer

Your name is spelled wrong in the yearbook. How long does that last, and who can fix it?

## Direct Instruction

**Grading and deadlines, short:**

1. The four categories and what is in each.
2. Late work is no penalty. Ask me to reopen it. Events and publisher deadlines are marked no late
   submissions, because they do not repeat.
3. Tell me before, not after. A conflict I hear about ahead of time gets reassigned and costs you nothing.
4. Names right, quotes real, no staged photo passed off as candid.

**Camera basics, with a camera in hand:**

1. Checkout form, inspection, strap around the neck.
2. Holding it: two hands, elbows in, feet set.
3. Focus. Half press, confirm it locked on the face, then shoot. Put a soft photo next to a sharp one on the
   projector.
4. Get closer. Say it twice.
5. Card out, into the reader, copy to Drive, rename. Check in: card cleared, battery charging, lens cap on.

**Adobe Express, five minutes.** Everyone signed in yesterday. Re-show the pieces the square needs: alignment
guides, spacing at the edges since these get printed, and export at 2560 x 2560.

## Work Time

- Camera turns start. Two or three students at a time.
- Everyone else starts the About Me Square. Sketch the layout on paper first.
- Procedures scavenger hunt in Classroom if there is time.

## Closure

Exit ticket: name two things that have to be true before a camera goes back in the cabinet.

- Do not soften the deadline policy on day two.
- Focus is the whole camera lesson this week. Skip aperture and ISO.
- Track who has had a camera turn so nobody misses one before Friday.
