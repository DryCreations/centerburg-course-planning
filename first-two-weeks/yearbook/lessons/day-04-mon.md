## Objective

Every student can log into eDesign, find the ladder, open a spread, place a photo and a caption, and check the
page back in. Everyone leaves with an example spread submitted and a question written down.

## Materials

before class, week 1 photos in a shared Drive folder so nobody is stuck without images, last year's book open
on a table, cameras out and charged, camera signup sheet ready to post.

## Vocabulary and Standards, About Ten Minutes

Put the week 2 sheet on the wall and walk it. Do not read definitions out loud. Open a real spread from last
year's book and point at each thing as you name it.

**Spread, ladder, dominant photo, gutter, eyeline, bleed, caption, copy, index, checked in and checked out.**

- Point at the dominant photo and ask what the page would feel like without it.
- Show a face crossing the gutter in an old book. Nobody forgets that after seeing it once.

Then say what the week is working toward.

- Own a beat, and know what owning it means.
- Get into eDesign and build a page without help.
- Write a caption that says something the photo cannot.
- Get off Auto on the camera. That is Wednesday.

## Explore Block: eDesign

The bulk of the period. Nothing here is graded on quality.

**Walk it once on the projector, about eight minutes.** Log in, the ladder, open a spread, the template
library, place a photo, place a text box, save, check the page in. Say plainly what happens if you do not check
a page in: that page is locked and nobody else can touch it.

**Then they explore.** Say out loud that you want them clicking things they do not understand.
