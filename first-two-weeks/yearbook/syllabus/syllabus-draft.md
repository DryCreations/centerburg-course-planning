# Yearbook Syllabus

**Course:** Yearbook, elective
**Teacher:** Mr. Mankin | **Room:** 173 | **Email:** hayden.mankin@centerburgschools.org
**Full year. Meets daily.**

## What This Class Is

This class makes the book. Every student in the building gets a copy and keeps it. The deadlines belong to the
publisher, not to me, and a page nobody worked on stays empty.

You will photograph events, interview people, write copy, design pages, and hit deadlines. We are also
finishing last year's book, which is still open.

Publisher: Varsity Yearbook. We design in eDesign. InDesign is on the lab machines for advanced work.

## What You Will Do

- Cover events with a camera: games, concerts, competitions, regular school days
- Own a beat, a section of school life you are responsible for all year
- Interview people and write captions and copy
- Design spreads in eDesign
- Hit deadlines on a schedule none of us control
- Help finish last year's pages

## What We Will Cover

Yearbook does not run in units the way an academic course does. It runs on deadlines. Roughly, the year looks
like this.

| Stretch | What we are doing |
|---------|-------------------|
| Start of the year | Staff setup, beats and roles assigned, camera and eDesign training, coverage board live |
| Fall | Fall sports and activities coverage, theme chosen, first publisher deadline, last year's book finished |
| Winter | Winter coverage, the bulk of the page production, portraits and index work |
| Spring | Spring coverage, final deadline, proofing, index verification |
| End of the year | Distribution, signing, and handing the process to next year's staff |

The publisher deadlines are the real calendar. Once Varsity confirms them they go on the board in this room,
and they do not move.

## Grading

There is no state exam in this class. What matters is that the work gets done, on time, at a quality we are
willing to print.

| Category | Weight | The grades in it |
|----------|-------:|------------------|
| Production and Deadlines | 40% | One grade per spread or page you are assigned, production tasks, weekly Friday status |
| Coverage | 25% | One coverage grade per quarter from your points, plus each event submission |
| Craft | 20% | A photo grade and a caption grade each quarter, plus design on each spread you build |
| Staff Contribution | 15% | One staff work score per week, plus your role duties if you hold a role |

Quarter grades are not recorded below 50%.

### Deadlines

I do not plan to take points off for being late.

If an assignment will not accept late work, the assignment says so. In this class that is more common than in
my other courses, because a lot of what we do only happens once.

I close submissions once I finish grading what came in. If you want to turn something in late, check that the
assignment does not say no late submissions, then come ask me to reopen it. I will.

| Situation | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| Regular work turned in late | Ask me to reopen it. No penalty. |
| Publisher deadline missed | Cannot be made up. The page ships as is or gets pulled. |
| Event you signed up for and missed | Cannot be made up. The event happened without you. |
| Conflict you told me about ahead of time | We reassign it, no penalty |

The bottom three are the ones marked on the assignment, and they are not me being strict. A game does not
happen twice and the publisher does not wait for us.

Tell me before, not after. Saying "I cannot cover Friday" on Tuesday is doing your job.

## Coverage

Covering events is part of this class, not an extra. Most events are outside the school day. Some coverage
happens during the day too: checking with teachers and coaches about what is coming up, and photographing
classes and events in the building.

If getting to events is a problem, tell me at the start of the quarter and we will work it out.

## Equipment

DSLR cameras are checked out through the Room 173 checkout system, the same one every class in here uses. You
can take a camera to events once you have shown you handle the checkout process correctly.

No phones during the school day. That is district policy. Phone photos from evening and weekend events are
accepted, but cameras are better and it shows in print.

## Expectations

1. This is a staff. Everyone has a job and class time is work time.
2. No phones during the school day. No headphones.
3. Deadlines are commitments to other people.
4. Names spelled right, facts right, every time. A misspelled name in a yearbook is permanent.
5. Cover the whole school, including people who are not your friends.

## Accuracy and Ethics

- Verify every name against a real source, not memory.
- Quotes are what the person actually said.
- Do not stage a photo and present it as candid, and do not edit a photo to change what happened.
- If someone asks not to appear, bring it to me.

## AI Use

Fine: brainstorming headline options, checking spelling and grammar, helping you organize an interview.

Not allowed: AI written copy or captions turned in as your reporting, and AI generated or altered images of
real people or events. This book is a record of what happened at this school.

If you are not sure, ask me.
