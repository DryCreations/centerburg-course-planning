## SLIDE 6: How the year works

- **Beats.** You own a part of school life all year and make sure it gets covered.
- **Coverage.** You sign up for events ahead of time and turn in photos.
- **Spreads.** You build pages in eDesign, our publisher's design platform.
- **Deadlines.** Weekly status every Friday, publisher deadlines all year.

Notes: One sentence each. Beats and coverage get built out next week, so do not go deep today.

## SLIDE 8: Look at the old books

- Get in pairs, grab a yearbook
- Find a page you like. Why does it work?
- Find a page that does not work. Why not?
- Find a group or a person you think got left out

Notes: Give them real time here, 10 to 15 minutes. Walk around and listen. Keep the "who got left out"
answers, they seed the beat list next week.
