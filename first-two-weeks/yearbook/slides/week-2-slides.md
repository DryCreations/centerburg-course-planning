# DAY 05 — The Ladder

---

## 96 pages = 48 spreads

- The ladder is the whole book on one sheet
- Nothing (beats, deadlines) works until we can see all of it
- Today we draft it together

---

## Build it in order

- Block the **fixed** spreads first: cover, opening, contents, dividers, index, closing
- Assign the rest to beats
- **Count as you go:** past 48, something merges; short of 48, student life fills it

---

## Two rules while we build

- A spread is a **subject**, not a leftover. "Volleyball senior night," not "sports, page 5."
- **Reference, don't invent.** For every new idea, find the closest thing in an old book and say what you would
  change.

---

## Your turn — the whole period

- Help build the ladder on the shared sheet
- Every spread gets a section; count against 48

# DAY 06 — Beats, Points, and Cameras

---

## Coverage is scheduled, not whoever feels like it

- A page fails one way: nobody was there with a camera
- Points reward covering your beat and filling gaps
- Covered = 20 usable frames, names verified, within 48 hours
- In-school coverage counts. Nobody is stuck.

---

## Your turn — pick where you fit

- Beat & role preference form: top three beats, any role, what coverage you can get to
- Rank from the ladder sections we just built
- This is what I assign from. Not a formality.

---

## Camera modes for the gym and hallway

- **Av:** portrait mode. Low f number, face separates from a busy background.
- **Tv:** sports mode. 1/500 indoors and up, or the spike is a smear.
- **ISO:** what saves you in a dark gym. Grain is the price.
- A grainy sharp photo goes in the book. A clean blurry one does not.

---

## Your turn — camera lab

- Nine frames: Av, Tv, and ISO, including a panning shot
- Rotation so everyone gets a camera; finish the preference form while you wait
- (theme brainstorming comes later, with Unit 2)

# DAY 07 — Captions and Spreads

---

## A caption adds what the photo cannot

- "Students in the hallway" says nothing
- Name the people, say what is happening, add one detail the photo does not show
- Names have to be right. Ask twice.
