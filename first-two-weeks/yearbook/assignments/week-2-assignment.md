# Week 2: Staff Setup Package

**Assigned:** Mon Aug 24 (Day 04) | **Due:** Fri Aug 28 (Day 08)
**Category:** Production and Deadlines, plus Craft | **Points:** 60
**Late:** accepted. Ask me to reopen it if submissions have closed. Tell me ahead of time if there is a problem.

By Friday you are a working staff member. You know your beat, you are signed up for coverage, you can build a
page in eDesign, and you have turned in your first status.

## Part A: Beat and Role Preferences (15 pts), Day 05

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Your top three beats, ranked, from the list we built |
| 2 | One sentence per beat on why you would be good at it |
| 3 | Any role you want to be considered for, and why |
| 4 | An honest answer about what coverage you can get to: weeknights, weekends, or school day only |

Be honest on number 4. A coverage plan built on a guess falls apart in October.

## Part B: First Coverage Signup (10 pts), Day 05

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Signed up for at least one event in the next three weeks on the coverage board |
| 2 | You know what you need to arrange before that day: camera, ride, list of names |
| 3 | If getting to that event is a problem, you have talked to me and we set you up with daytime coverage instead |

## Part C: Practice Spread in eDesign (25 pts), Day 07

Same idea as your About Me spread from week 1, but built in eDesign with your own photos.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Four photos placed from your week 1 shoot |
| 2 | A caption for each: names, what is happening, and one detail the photo does not show |
| 3 | A headline that says something, not just a label |
| 4 | Every name checked, not guessed |
| 5 | Page saved and checked in |

Weak caption: "Students walk in the hallway."

Better: "Between fourth and fifth period, Elena Ruiz and Marcus Vail argue about Friday's game, which they had
been going back and forth on since Tuesday."

## Part D: First Weekly Status (10 pts), Day 08

The Friday routine starts now.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | What you finished this week |
| 2 | What is stuck or unclear |
| 3 | What is due next week and what your first step is |

Five minutes. It is how we catch deadline problems in week one instead of week four.

## Checklist

- [ ] Beat and role preferences submitted
- [ ] Signed up on the coverage board, or daytime coverage arranged with me
- [ ] Practice spread: 4 photos, 4 captions, headline, names checked, page checked in
- [ ] Weekly status submitted

## Grading

Scored on the yearbook rubric, in Production and Deadlines and in Craft. A misspelled name in a caption costs
Craft points, the same way it would cost the book in print.
