## What This Is

Your first time with a staff camera. We are sharing cameras, so you take turns. What I am looking for is that
you can check out a camera, hold it right, get a photo in focus, get it off the card, and turn it in.

Nothing goes into eDesign yet. Photos go to Classroom.

## What You Turn In

**10 photos**, uploaded to the Classroom assignment, named:

```
practice-lastname-01.jpg  through  practice-lastname-10.jpg
```

## What Makes a Photo Usable

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| In focus, on the face | Blurry photos cannot be printed at any size |
| Bright enough to tell who it is | If we cannot identify a person we cannot caption them |
| The person is doing something | Posed waving shots fill space and say nothing |
| Close enough | Almost every first photo is taken from too far away |
| Different framings | Wide, medium, close. Not ten versions of one shot. |

## Steps

1. Check out a camera on the room checkout form. Inspect it with me.
2. Take your photos in the room.
3. Ask before you photograph someone. If they say no, that is the answer.
4. Get your photos off the card and into your Drive folder, then rename them.
5. Upload the 10 photos to Classroom.
6. Check the camera back in: card cleared, battery on the charger, lens cap on.

Cameras are shared, so plan your turn. If you are waiting, work on your About Me Square.

## Notes

Getting focus right is most of the battle in week one. If a photo is soft, take it again from a little closer
and try holding your elbows against your body.

We will look at these together next week.
