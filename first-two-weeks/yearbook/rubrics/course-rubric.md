# Yearbook Production Rubric

Yearbook does **not** use the shared CTE rubric. This class is graded on whether the book gets made.

Every category below is tied to **specific, recurring gradebook items**, so when a student asks "what is my
Craft grade made of?", there is an actual list.

---

## Grade Categories and What Goes In Them

| Category | Weight | The actual gradebook items |
|----------|-------:|----------------------------|
| **Production & Deadlines** | 40% | Every assigned **spread or page** (one grade each, at its deadline), assigned production tasks (portrait organization, index work, legacy pages), **weekly Friday status** |
| **Coverage** | 25% | **One coverage grade per quarter**, calculated from coverage points, plus each individual **event submission** graded pass/revise on the 48-hour rule |
| **Craft** | 20% | **Photo batch grade** (one per quarter, on your submitted photos), **caption and copy grade** (one per quarter), **design quality grade** on each spread you build |
| **Staff Contribution** | 15% | **Weekly staff-work score** (one per week), role responsibilities for students holding a staff role |

**Rule of thumb for where something lands:** did it have a deadline? *Production.* Was it about being at an
event? *Coverage.* Is it about how good the work is? *Craft.* Is it about being a functioning staff member
this week? *Staff Contribution.*

---

## Production & Deadlines (40%)

Scored per deliverable, per spread, per page, per assigned task.

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 100% | Delivered complete, on or before the deadline |
| 85% | Delivered on time but incomplete in a way that made someone else finish it |
| **50%** | **Two or more days late** |
| 0% | Never delivered, or missed a publisher deadline so the page shipped without it |

Late rule: no penalty. Submissions close when grading is done, and reopening on request is routine. Assignments that cannot repeat, meaning events and publisher deadlines, are marked no late submissions.

If you tell me ahead of time and we reassign it, there is no penalty. That is why you tell me.

**Publisher deadlines are different** only in that they cannot be resubmitted late: once the book ships, the
page is what it is.

## Coverage (25%)

**Direct percentage:** points earned  /  points required for the quarter.

```
Coverage grade = (points earned  /  quarter minimum)  x  100, capped at 100%
```

Going past the minimum does not inflate your grade above 100, but extra points **roll over** to the next
quarter, up to 4. Cover heavily in the fall, coast slightly in February.

Every student is expected to cover events. Advance notice of a conflict costs nothing, we reassign and you
pick up a different event. A no-show costs the points.

## Craft (20%)

Standard: **would we print this?**

| Criterion | Strong | Acceptable | Not yet |
|-----------|--------|------------|---------|
| **Photos** | In focus, well exposed, subjects identifiable and doing something; variety of framing | Usable, mostly technically sound | Blurry, dark, backs of heads, unusable |
| **Names & accuracy** | Every name verified and correct | Names present, one or two unverified | Missing or guessed names |
| **Writing** | Specific, in the subject's voice, tells a reader something new | Clear and correct, a bit generic | Vague, cliché, or padded |
| **Design** | Consistent with the book's style, clean spacing, readable hierarchy | Follows the template, minor issues | Inconsistent, cramped, hard to read |

> **A misspelled name is a craft failure, not a typo.** It is permanent, printed, and someone's name.

## Staff Contribution (15%)

One score per week. Not a behavior grade, a *did the staff work* grade.

| Score | Description |
|------:|-------------|
| Full | Worked the whole period, met commitments, told me early when something changed, helped others |
| Partial | Worked most of the period; commitments occasionally slipped without notice |
| Low | Regularly idle during work time, or commitments dropped without telling anyone |

Students holding a **staff role** (Photo Editor, Copy Editor, Coverage Coordinator, Legacy Lead, etc.) are also
scored here on whether the role's standing duties actually happened.

---

## Weekly Friday Status

Every staff member submits: what they finished, what is stuck, what is due next week. Graded in **Production &
Deadlines**. Five minutes, and it is how deadline problems get caught in week 1 instead of week 4.
