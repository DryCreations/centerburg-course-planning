# Yearbook: Staff Procedures

**Room 173, Mr. Mankin**
This is a staff room, not a study hall. These procedures exist so the book gets made.

---

## Coming In

1. Assigned seat, machine on, logged in.
2. **Bell ringer** on the board: 5 minutes.
3. Check the **deadline board** and the **coverage board** before you start working. Every day.

## Phones & Headphones

- **No phones during the school day**: district policy, and it applies here.
- **No headphones.**
- Phone photos from evening and weekend events are fine to submit; cameras are better and it shows in print.

## Food & Drink

Closed water bottle on the floor. Nothing else near the machines or the cameras.

## Class Time

Class time is production time. Every day you should be able to answer: *what am I moving forward today?*

- If you finish your task, check the coverage board or ask the Editor-in-Chief: there is always work.
- "I don't have anything to do" means you have not looked at the boards.

## Files and Naming

- **Photos:** `event-date-lastname-##.jpg`, e.g. `fbvsutica-2026-09-04-mankin-07.jpg`
- Upload to the correct event folder in Drive. Not your desktop. Not your phone.
- **48-hour rule:** photos are submitted within 48 hours of the event, while you still remember the names.
- Names go in a caption file alongside the photos, verified spelling.

## eDesign & InDesign

- eDesign is where the book is built. Sign in with the account I set up for you.
- **Never work on someone else's spread** without telling them: the platform will let you, and it will
  overwrite their work.
- Check pages back in when you are done for the day, or nobody else can touch them.
- InDesign is available for advanced work; ask before starting a spread there.

## Cameras

- Checked out and checked back in every time, on the **shared room checkout form**: the same one every class
  in this room uses. Pick "Yearbook" as the course.
- Strap around your neck whenever it is in your hands.
- Card offloaded before check-in, battery on the charger, lens cap on.
- Evening and weekend checkout: ask at least **two days ahead**.
- **Report damage immediately.** Reporting is not the part you get in trouble for.

## Names and Accuracy

- Ask twice. Write it down. Verify against a roster, program, or the person themselves.
- Never guess a spelling. Never use "we'll fix it later": later is print.

## Deadlines

- **Internal deadlines** are mine and carry a penalty if missed.
- **Publisher deadlines** are Varsity's and cannot be moved by anyone in this room.
- Conflict coming? **Tell me before.** Reassignment costs you nothing; a no-show costs the book a page.

## Turning Work In

Google Classroom for assignments and status checks; Drive for photo submissions; eDesign for spreads.

## Weekly Status (Fridays)

Five minutes, in Classroom: what you finished, what is stuck, what is due next.

## End of Period

1. Spreads checked in on eDesign.
2. Files saved to Drive, cameras returned and logged.
3. Exit ticket / status submitted.
4. Station reset, chairs in.
5. Log off.

## Emergencies

Routes posted by the door. Take nothing, stay with the class.
