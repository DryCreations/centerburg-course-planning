# Yearbook: Beats and Roles

**How this staff is organized.** Read this before we brainstorm beats on Day 04.

---

## What a Beat Is

A **beat** is a slice of school life that one or two people are responsible for **all year**. If it happens in
your beat, you know about it, you cover it, and the pages about it are yours.

Reporters have worked this way for a century because it fixes the thing that kills yearbooks: nobody thought
about the bowling team until March.

**Your beat means you:**
- Know what is happening in it: schedules, dates, who to ask
- Make sure it gets photographed
- Get names, spelled correctly
- Write or arrange the copy
- Flag it to me when a deadline for your beat is coming

**What is missing from this list?** That is the Day 04 conversation. Every school has things a generic list
does not know about.

## Staff Roles

Roles are on top of your beat, not instead of it. Most are open to anyone willing to do the work, this is a
staff of mostly new people, so nobody is disqualified for being new.

| Role | What it means | How many |
|------|---------------|---------:|
| **Editor-in-Chief** | Owns the book overall; runs deadline check-ins with me | 1-2 |
| **Photo Editor** | Tracks what has been shot and what has not; manages the photo library and naming | 1 |
| **Copy Editor** | Last set of eyes on every name, caption, and story before submission | 1-2 |
| **Design Lead** | Keeps spreads consistent, type, spacing, style across the book | 1 |
| **Coverage Coordinator** | Runs the event signup board; chases gaps before they become holes | 1 |
| **Index / Names Lead** | Makes sure every student in the building appears, and appears correctly | 1 |
| **Staff Photographer / Writer / Designer** | The core job. Most of the staff. | everyone |
| **Legacy Lead** | Owns finishing **last year's book** | 1-2 |

### About the Legacy Lead

Last year's book is not finished. Somebody has to own closing it out. It is unglamorous and it is the most
appreciated job on staff, because until it is done nobody's copy of last year exists.

## How Roles Get Assigned

1. **Day 04:** we build the beat list together.
2. **Day 05:** you submit a short interest form, your top two beats, any role you want, and why.
3. **Day 06-07:** I assign. I try to give people their first choice; some gaps have to be filled anyway.
4. **Quarter check:** roles can change at semester if something is not working. Beats stay all year.

## What Makes Someone Good at This

Not talent. Three habits:

1. **You show up** to the thing you said you would cover.
2. **You get names right**, which means asking twice and writing them down.
3. **You tell me early** when something is not going to happen.

Everything else, camera skill, design skill, writing, is teachable and we will teach it.
