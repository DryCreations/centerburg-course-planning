# Yearbook: Vocabulary and Learning Targets to Post

Print this and put it on the wall. Post the same text in Classroom on Monday. Yearbook has no state exam, so
these are our targets rather than someone else's. They are still what you are graded against.

---

## Week 1 (Aug 19 to 21)

### We Are Learning To

- Build a layout in Adobe Express and export it so it can actually be printed.
- Take a photo of a person that is sharp, lit, and framed on purpose.
- Get files off a card, name them, and put them where the staff can find them.
- Say what a yearbook staff does and what part of it I want to own.

### Words to Know

| Word | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Spread | Two facing pages designed as one thing. The book is built in spreads, not pages. |
| Ladder | The map of the whole book, page by page, showing what goes where. |
| Coverage | Getting a person or an event into the book. If nobody shot it, it does not exist. |
| Caption | The text under a photo. Names, what is happening, and something the photo cannot show. |
| Deadline | The date the publisher takes the pages. It does not move for us. |
| Beat | One slice of school life that one person owns all year. |

## Week 2 (Aug 24 to 28)

### We Are Learning To

- Own a beat: know its schedule, make sure it gets shot, and get the names right.
- Use Av, Tv, and ISO on the camera so a dim gym and a bright hallway both come out usable.
- Log into eDesign, open a spread, place a photo and a caption, and check the page back in.
- Write a caption that tells a reader something the photo does not.
- Read the coverage board and sign up for something before somebody has to ask me to.

### Words to Know

| Word | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Aperture, the f number | How wide the lens opens. Low number means a blurry background and more light. |
| Shutter speed | How long the sensor is exposed. Fast freezes a spike or a sprint. Slow blurs it. |
| ISO | How sensitive the sensor is. Raise it in a dim gym. Raising it too far adds grain. |
| Av mode | You pick the aperture, the camera picks the shutter. The portrait mode. |
| Tv mode | You pick the shutter speed, the camera picks the aperture. The sports mode. |
| Depth of field | How much is in focus front to back. Shallow separates a face from a busy background. |
| Panning | Turning with a moving subject so they stay sharp and the background streaks. |
| Gutter | The fold in the middle of a spread. Do not put a face there. |
| Dominant photo | The one big photo on a spread that everyone sees first. Every spread needs one. |
| Eyeline | Which way people in a photo are looking. Faces should look into the spread, not off the edge. |
| Bleed | Artwork that runs off the edge of the page so there is no white border. |
| Checked in and checked out | In eDesign, only one person can work on a page at a time. Check it back in or you lock everyone else out. |
| Index | The list at the back of every name in the book and what page they are on. Spelling matters here more than anywhere. |
| Copy | The written article on a spread, separate from the captions. |

### The Standard We Are Holding

A photo is usable if it is sharp, the face is lit, the name is confirmed, and it was submitted within 48 hours.
Three out of four is not usable. This is the rule the staff set on Day 04, not one I handed down.
