# Yearbook at a Glance

Mr. Mankin | Room 173 | Full year elective

## What This Class Is

You make the book. Photograph events, interview people, write copy, design spreads, hit publisher deadlines.
Everyone in the building keeps what you make.

We are also finishing last year's book, which is still open.

## The Setup

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Varsity Yearbook |
| Design platform | eDesign, with InDesign for advanced work |
| Cameras | DSLRs checked out from the room |
| Where work lives | eDesign, Google Drive, Google Classroom |

## How It Works

- You get a beat, a slice of school life you are responsible for all year.
- You sign up for coverage ahead of time on the coverage board.
- You earn coverage points each quarter by showing up and turning in usable material.
- You build spreads in eDesign on a deadline schedule.

## Grading

| Category | Weight | Made up of |
|----------|-------:|-----------|
| Production and Deadlines | 40% | Each spread or page you are assigned, plus weekly Friday status |
| Coverage | 25% | Your quarterly coverage points and each event submission |
| Craft | 20% | Quarterly photo grade, caption grade, design on your spreads |
| Staff Contribution | 15% | Weekly staff work score and your role duties |

Late work is accepted with no penalty. If submissions have closed, ask me to reopen them. Events and publisher deadlines are marked no late submissions, because they do not repeat.
If you have a conflict, tell me before. That costs you nothing.

## Coverage Happens Outside Class

Most events are evenings and weekends, and going to events is part of this class. Camera checkout is available
for them. Some coverage happens during the school day too: checking with teachers and coaches about what is
coming up, and shooting classes and events in the building.

If getting to events is a problem, tell me at the start, not in October.

## Three Habits That Make This Work

1. Show up to what you signed up for.
2. Get names right. Ask twice, write them down.
3. Tell me early when something is not going to happen.
