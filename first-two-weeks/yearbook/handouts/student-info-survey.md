# Yearbook: Staff Info Survey (Google Form, copy-paste ready)

**Build:** new Google Form -> paste each block. Question type in **bold**.
**Assign:** Day 01 via Classroom. Collect emails ON, one response per person.
This doubles as the **beat and role interest form**. Day 05 adds the beat ranking once the list exists.

---

**Form title:** Yearbook Staff, Getting to Know You

**Form description:**
> Welcome to the staff. About five minutes. This tells me who is in the room, what you are good at, and where
> you already have access, which is how beats get assigned well.

---

### Section 1: Basics

**1. What name should I call you?** *(Short answer, required)*

**2. How do you say it?** *(Short answer)*

**3. Grade level** *(Multiple choice, required)*: 9 / 10 / 11 / 12

**4. Any pronouns I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*

**5. Were you on yearbook staff before?** *(Multiple choice, required)*
- Yes, last year
- Yes, but more than a year ago
- No, this is my first year

---

### Section 2: Skills

**6. What are you already decent at?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Taking photos
- Editing photos
- Writing
- Interviewing / talking to people I don't know
- Graphic design or layout
- Organizing things and tracking details
- Being on top of what's happening at school
- Honestly none of these yet

**7. Have you used any of these?** *(Checkboxes)*
- A DSLR camera
- Photoshop, GIMP, or Lightroom
- InDesign
- eDesign or another yearbook platform
- Canva or Adobe Express
- Spreadsheets for tracking things
- None of these

**8. Comfort talking to people you don't know, to ask for a name or a quote** *(Linear scale 1-5)*
Description: 1 = I would rather not. 5 = no problem. Both answers are useful to me, there is real work either way.

---

### Section 3: Access and Coverage

**9. What are you involved in at school?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Sports, band, clubs, theater, work. This matters: you already have access to things nobody else does.

**10. What do you already know about that most people don't?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: A team, a club, a group of friends, a corner of the school. This is how beats get assigned well.

**11. Can you get to evening or weekend events?** *(Multiple choice, required)*
- Yes, most of them
- Sometimes, with notice
- Rarely: transportation is a problem
- No

Description: An honest answer here helps me build a coverage plan you can actually meet. Answering "rarely"
or "no" does not hurt your grade, there is plenty of in-school coverage.

**12. Which of these would you actually want to cover?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Fall sports
- Winter sports
- Spring sports
- Band / choir / drama
- Clubs and organizations
- Academics and classrooms
- Student life / everyday moments
- Dances and big events
- Seniors
- Faculty and staff

**13. Any role on staff that sounds interesting?** *(Checkboxes: see the Beats and Roles handout)*
- Editor-in-Chief
- Photo Editor
- Copy Editor
- Design Lead
- Coverage Coordinator
- Index / Names Lead
- Legacy Lead (finishing last year's book)
- Just staff for now, thanks

---

### Section 4: Logistics

**14. Seating: anything that helps you focus?** *(Paragraph)*

**15. Do you have a computer and internet at home?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, my own computer and reliable internet
- Yes, a shared computer
- Phone only
- Not really

**16. Do you have your own camera?** *(Short answer)*

**17. What do you want the book to be like this year?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: What was missing from past yearbooks? What would make people actually open it?

**18. Anything else you want me to know?** *(Paragraph)*
