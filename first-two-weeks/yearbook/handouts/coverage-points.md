# Part 1: Student Handout: How Coverage Works

## The Problem This Solves

A yearbook fails in one specific way: an event happens, nobody was there with a camera, and there is nothing to
put on the page. It cannot be fixed later. So coverage is not "whoever feels like it", it is scheduled, signed
up for, and tracked.

## Coverage Points

Every quarter you owe **coverage points**. You earn them by showing up to events and turning in usable
material.

| What you did | Points |
|--------------|-------:|
| Covered a **major event** (game, concert, production, dance), photos submitted | 3 |
| Covered a **minor event** (practice, club meeting, class activity, hallway/daily life) | 2 |
| **Interview** conducted and written up (3+ usable quotes with names verified) | 2 |
| Covered an event **nobody had signed up for** (filled a gap) | +1 bonus |
| Covered something in **your own beat** | +1 bonus |
| Submitted photos that end up **used in the book** | +1 each spread |

**Quarter minimum:** you must reach the quarter's point minimum. It will be set once the fall calendar is
locked and posted on the coverage board, plan on somewhere near 12. Some of you will go well past it, and
that is good: points past the minimum **roll over** to the next quarter, up to 4.

## What Counts as "Covered"

Showing up is not coverage. Coverage means you submitted, within **48 hours** of the event:

1. **At least 20 usable photos**: in focus, correctly exposed, showing identifiable people doing something
2. **Names** for the people in your best shots, spelled correctly and verified: not guessed
3. Files uploaded to the correct folder, named `event-date-lastname-##.jpg`
4. A one-line note: what happened, who was there, anything notable

Twenty blurry photos of the back of a crowd is not coverage. Five good frames with names beats fifty without.

## Signing Up

The **coverage board** (in Classroom, updated weekly) lists every known event. Sign up at least **48 hours
ahead** so we know a gap when we see one.

- Signing up is a commitment. Somebody else did not sign up because you did.
- **Cannot make it? Tell me before, and it costs you nothing.** We reassign it.
- No-show without notice: no points, and the coverage requirement does not shrink.

## Home Games, Away Games, Evenings, Weekends

Most coverage happens outside class, and **attending events is a required part of this class.** That is what
makes a yearbook possible.

- Camera checkout for evening and weekend events is available once you have shown you can handle the equipment
  process.
- If transportation is a problem, tell me at the **start** of the quarter, not in week eight. We can plan
  around a real constraint; we cannot fix a page that nobody shot.
- Nobody covers everything. You hit your minimum and you own your beat.

## During-the-School-Day Coverage

Not all coverage is evening events. Part of owning a beat is **finding out what is coming up**, checking in
with teachers, coaches, and advisors about projects, competitions, performances, and anything else worth
photographing.

That means you may be assigned to:

- Photograph what is happening in a class (arranged with that teacher in advance)
- Interview a teacher or advisor about something coming up in their area
- Cover an assembly, a competition, or a daytime event
- Photograph the ordinary school day: lunch, hallways, labs, practices

These earn points on the same scale. **Always check in with the teacher or advisor before showing up with a
camera.** You are representing this staff.

## Photos From Your Phone

Allowed for evening and weekend events, and better than nothing. But phone photos rarely survive being printed
large, so they usually end up small on the page. Use a camera when you can.

## Tracking

Your points are visible in the coverage tracker in Classroom, updated weekly. If you think it is wrong, tell me
,  it probably is, and I would rather fix it than argue about it in May.

# Part 2: Teacher Setup

## Decided

| Item | Decision |
|------|----------|
| Grade conversion | **Direct percentage**, points  /  quarter minimum  x  100, capped at 100 |
| Rollover | Yes, up to 4 points into the next quarter |
| Quarter minimum | Set once the fall calendar is locked; plan on ~12 |
| Event attendance | **Required**, not optional |
| In-school coverage | Real and points-earning, beats include checking with staff about upcoming things and shooting during the day |
| Backup photographer | None available. Coverage is you and the students, so gaps on the board are real gaps. |

## Still to set

1. **The actual quarter minimum**, once the fall athletics and arts calendars are in hand. Consider a lower
   number for Q3 if winter coverage is thinner.
2. Whether the minimum differs for students with a documented transportation constraint, or whether they meet
   it entirely through daytime coverage. Recommend the latter, same number, different mix.

## Build list

- [ ] Coverage board: Google Sheet, one tab per quarter: date, event, time, location, slots, who signed up, status
- [ ] Publish it in Classroom as view-only + a signup form, or as edit-access with a rule about not deleting rows
- [ ] Coverage tracker: Sheet with a row per student and running totals, updated weekly
- [ ] Photo submission folders in Drive, one per event, with the naming rule posted
- [ ] Pull the fall athletics and arts calendars into the board before Day 04
- [ ] Set and post the quarter minimum once that calendar is in
- [ ] Add daytime/in-class coverage opportunities to the board, not just evening events
- [ ] Enforce the 48-hour submission window from the very first event

## Open questions for the teacher

- Should the Coverage Coordinator role own the board day-to-day, with you approving?
- With no backup photographer available, do you want to shoot a few early events yourself so there is a
  baseline of usable material while the staff is still learning?
