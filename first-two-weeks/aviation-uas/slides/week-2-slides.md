# DAY 04 — Four Forces (taught Monday)

---

## What actually holds it up

- **Lift** up, **weight** down, **thrust** forward, **drag** back
- **hold it up:** point at where each one acts
- A bigger battery = more flight time AND more weight. Not free.

---

## Where you can fly

- Open the FAA UAS Facility Map on our school
- Find the ceiling
- The rule is not "you can't fly here." It is "you have to ask."

# DAY 05 — TRUST and Safety

---

## The week has a finish line

- Today: TRUST. Wednesday: preflight and controls. Thursday: pretest.
- **Friday: we fly.**
- Nobody flies Friday who skips Wednesday's preflight drill.

---

## What TRUST is

- The recreational UAS safety test
- It proves you know the safety rules
- It is **not** a Part 107 certificate, and it is our honest baseline for flying Friday

---

## The safety rules (hold the aircraft)

- Under 400 feet
- Keep it in sight
- Not over people
- Give way to crewed aircraft
- Know your airspace before you go

# DAY 06 — Preflight and Controls

---

## Why a checklist, every time

- Not a memory aid for beginners
- A defense against experienced people skipping steps
- **hold it up:** walk one full preflight: airframe, arms, motors, props, battery, gimbal, controller,
  firmware, environment

---

## Document it like a pro

- "Inspected, no defects" — a record
- "Left front prop, small nick, about 2 mm" — a better record
- Vague notes are a shrug, not a log

---

## The basic controls (controller in hand)

- **Throttle:** up and down (altitude)
- **Yaw:** rotate in place, nose left/right
- **Pitch and roll:** move forward/back, left/right
- Launch → low hover → hold → slow descent to the **same spot**

---

## Your turn

- Preflight drill in pairs, one reads aloud, one inspects, then swap
- Find the discrepancy I planted on some aircraft
- Then rehearse the launch/hover/land call-outs, dry, nothing powered

# DAY 07 — Pretest, Then the Plan

---

## The pretest

- Not graded. Most of it has not been taught. Low is the point.
- It sets your baseline. We attack your lowest strands all year.

---

## Friday flight briefing

- **Where:** the cleared area, the do-not-cross line, where spectators stand
- **Your turn:** preflight, power on, launch to a low hover, hold, one or two slow moves, land on the same spot
- **Weather:** we make the go/no-go call Friday morning, together
- **Roles:** pilot, visual observer, logger. Everyone rotates.

---

# DAY 08 — First Flight

---

## Go / No-Go first

- Real weather now: wind, gusts, visibility, ceiling
- We make the call as a group
- A pilot who scrubs a bad day made the right call

---

## The one rule above all

- **Anyone may call an all-stop, for any reason**
- The pilot lands immediately. No argument.

---

## How the flights run

- Preflight the aircraft. No preflight, no flight.
- Short flights, everyone gets a real turn
- Rotate: pilot, visual observer, logger
- A clean 30-second flight beats a long shaky one
- No TRUST proof = observer and logger today
