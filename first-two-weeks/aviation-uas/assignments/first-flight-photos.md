# First Flight: The Photos

**Course:** Aviation UAS, **Flown Thu Aug 27 (Day 07)**, **Category:** Labs, **Points:** 10
**Late:** accepted. Ask me to reopen it if submissions have closed.

Your first real flight has a job: come back with two usable photos. You take them from the aircraft, in the
air, during your turn on the sticks.

---

## Your Flight, Start to Finish

Everyone flies the same short sequence. It is short on purpose: get up, do a few clean things, get down safely.

1. **Preflight** the aircraft against the checklist. No preflight, no flight.
2. **Boot up:** controller on, then aircraft. Wait for GPS lock and the home point.
3. **Up:** ease the throttle up to a low, steady hover.
4. **Forward:** a little forward on the right stick, then stop and hover.
5. **Turn around:** yaw 180 so the camera faces back toward the group.
6. **Photo 1, the group selfie:** frame the class and take the shot.
7. **Zoom in:** use the zoom, reframe.
8. **Photo 2, zoomed:** take the second shot.
9. **Return to Home:** press and hold RTH and let it come back and land, or land it yourself on the spot.

Then it is the next pilot's turn. Nine of us, so everyone flies and everyone observes.

---

## After Everyone Flies

Back inside:

1. **Offload** the photos from the aircraft/card onto a computer.
2. **Upload** your two photos to Classroom.
3. Name them `lastname-flight-selfie.jpg` and `lastname-flight-zoom.jpg`.

---

scrubbed the flight, this rolls to the next clear day, no penalty.
