| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Every checklist item documented, no blanks |
| 2 | Findings written the way they would be reported for real |

## Part D: Mission Planning Worksheet (20 pts), all week

Plan a hypothetical flight over **school property**. You are not flying it, you are proving you could plan it.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | **Objective**, what the flight is for, in one sentence |
| 2 | **Location**, where, with a map or satellite image marked up |
| 3 | **Airspace**, class, ceiling, whether authorization would be needed, how you know |
| 4 | **Weather**, today's actual conditions from a real METAR, decoded, with a go/no-go call |
| 5 | **Crew**, who is Remote Pilot in Command, who is Visual Observer, what each does |
| 6 | **Hazards**, three specific to this site (people, buildings, trees, wires, roads) and your mitigation for each |
| 7 | **Emergency plan**, what you do on lost link, low battery, and a person entering the flight area |
