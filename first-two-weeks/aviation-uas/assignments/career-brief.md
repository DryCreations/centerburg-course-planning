## What This Is

Pick one field where unmanned aircraft do real work and find out what that job actually involves. Not "drone
pilot" in the abstract, a specific kind of work, with specific requirements and specific pay.

## Choose One

- Surveying & mapping
- Infrastructure inspection (power lines, cell towers, bridges, roofs)
- Agriculture (crop health, spraying, livestock)
- Public safety (fire, search and rescue, accident reconstruction)
- Cinematography & media
- Delivery & logistics
- Another one you find: clear it with me first

## What You Turn In

One page (or one slide-deck page), submitted in Classroom as `lastname-careerbrief.pdf`.

## Required Content

| # | Section | What it needs |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **The work** | What a person in this job does on an ordinary day. Not the highlight reel. |
| 2 | **Requirements** | Certifications, training, and experience. Part 107 is a given, what *else*? |
| 3 | **Pay** | A real range with a **cited source** (BLS, a job posting, an industry survey). Link it. |
| 4 | **Course connection** | Two specific things we cover this year that feed this job, and how |
| 5 | **Your question** | One thing you could not find out, or that surprised you |

## Sources

Use at least two, and name them. A job posting counts. A YouTube video does not, unless the person in it is
doing the job and you cite who they are.

## A Note

"Drone pilot" as a job title is rarer than people expect. The work is usually **someone in an industry who
also flies**, a surveyor, an inspector, an agronomist, a firefighter. That is worth noticing while you research,
and it is worth saying in your brief if you find it.
