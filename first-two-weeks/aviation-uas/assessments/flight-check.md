understanding, not a graded test)

## Questions

**1.** In Mode 2 (our setup), pushing the **left stick up** makes the aircraft:

A. Fly forward
B. Climb
C. Rotate left
D. Slide left

**2.** You take both thumbs off the sticks. The aircraft:

A. Falls straight down
B. Flies away in the last direction
C. Stops and hovers in place
D. Lands automatically

**3.** **Yaw** means the aircraft:

A. Climbs or descends
B. Rotates in place, nose swinging left or right
C. Flies forward or backward
D. Slides left or right

**4.** The **right stick** pushed up makes the aircraft:

A. Climb
B. Fly forward
C. Rotate right
D. Take a photo

**5.** Which flight mode do we use with the class?

A. Sport
B. Cine or Normal
C. Whatever is fastest
D. Manual

**6.** You need to bring the aircraft back right now and you are not sure where it is. You:

A. Fly Sport mode toward yourself
B. Press and hold **Return to Home (RTH)**
C. Turn off the controller
D. Wait for the battery to die

**7.** Before flying, we wait for:

A. The controller battery to reach 100%
B. A solid GPS lock and the home point to set
C. The camera to zoom in
D. The sun to go down

**8.** Which two numbers on the screen matter most while flying?

A. Time of day and temperature
B. Aircraft battery percent and height
C. Photo count and zoom level
D. Controller serial number and firmware

**9.** "Line of sight" means:

A. The camera can see the subject
B. You can see the actual aircraft with your own eyes
C. The controller has signal
D. The screen is bright enough

**10.** Anyone on the ground can call an all-stop. When they do, the pilot:

A. Finishes the current move first
B. Asks why
C. Lands or hovers immediately, no argument
D. Hands the controller to a teacher
