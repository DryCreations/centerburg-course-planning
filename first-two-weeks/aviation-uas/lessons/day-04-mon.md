## Direct Instruction: Four Forces

Twelve minutes, aircraft in hand and unpowered.

1. Lift, weight, thrust, drag. Where each one acts.
2. What changes between a hover and forward flight.
3. Connect it to something they already have a body memory of: a bike, a car, a hand out a car window at
   speed.
4. The line that lands: a bigger battery is more flight time and more weight at the same time. It is not free.
   Every payload decision this year is that trade.

## Explore Block: Where Can You Actually Fly

Open the FAA UAS Facility Map on the projector and look up the school together. Find the ceiling. Say out loud
what a pilot would have to do to fly here legally, and mention that LAANC authorization in most controlled
airspace is close to instant now. The rule is not that you cannot fly there. It is that you have to ask.

Then they go look things up themselves.
