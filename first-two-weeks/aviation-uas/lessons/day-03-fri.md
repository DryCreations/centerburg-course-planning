# Day 03 - Fri, Aug 21 (Week 1)

**Course:** Aviation UAS, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

## Objective

Students can explain what Part 107 is and what the test covers, know the room procedures, and both week 1
assignments are submitted.

## Materials

Part 107 Overview handout printed, procedures handout printed, scavenger hunt in Classroom, cameras out for
anyone who still needs a turn.

## Bell Ringer

True or false: if you own the drone and you are flying it over your own yard, no federal rules apply.

## Direct Instruction

**Part 107, about fifteen minutes.** Walk the handout: what the certificate is, what the test covers, the 70%
pass line, no cost to you, dates to be announced. Recreational versus Part 107 comes down to purpose, not
aircraft size. Point out that the WebXam and Part 107 overlap heavily, so the year serves both.

**Room and equipment procedures, about ten minutes.** File naming, Drive over desktop, how work is turned in,
camera checkout, aircraft ground rules, checklists read aloud, the end of period reset.

## Work Time

- Procedures and safety scavenger hunt, open notes, Readiness credit.
- Last camera turns for anyone who still needs one.
- Finish and export the square. Finish and upload the six documentation photos.

## Closure

Both assignments submitted before anyone packs up.

Exit ticket: one thing about Part 107 that surprised you.
