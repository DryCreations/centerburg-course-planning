# Aviation UAS: Student Info Survey (Google Form, copy-paste ready)

**Build:** new Google Form -> paste each block. Question type in **bold**.
**Assign:** Day 01 via Classroom. Collect emails ON, one response per person.

---

**Form title:** Aviation UAS, Getting to Know You

**Form description:**
> Welcome. About five minutes. This helps me set seating, aim examples at what you care about, and know what
> to call you.

---

### Section 1: Basics

**1. What name should I call you?** *(Short answer, required)*

**2. How do you say it?** *(Short answer)*

**3. Grade level** *(Multiple choice, required)*: 9 / 10 / 11 / 12

**4. Any pronouns I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*

---

### Section 2: Experience

**5. Have you done any of these?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Flown a drone (any size)
- Flown an RC plane or helicopter
- Flown in a small aircraft
- Played a flight simulator seriously
- Built or repaired something mechanical or electronic
- Used mapping software (Google Earth counts)
- None of these

**6. If you have flown a drone, tell me what and where.** *(Paragraph)*

**7. Comfort with learning new software** *(Linear scale 1-5)*: 1 = nervous, 5 = I figure it out myself

**8. Comfort with math and reading technical material** *(Linear scale 1-5)*
Description: Honest answers help me. Part of this course is chart reading and weather math, and I would rather
know now.

---

### Section 3: You

**9. Why did you sign up for this class?** *(Paragraph, required)*

**10. When you hear "drone," what job or use comes to mind first?** *(Paragraph)*

**11. Are you interested in getting the FAA Part 107 certificate this year?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, that's a big reason I'm here
- Interested, tell me more
- Not sure yet
- Not interested

**12. What are you into outside of school?** *(Paragraph)*

**13. Career or college plans right now?** *(Short answer)*: "no idea" is fine

---

### Section 4: Logistics

**14. Seating: anything that helps you focus?** *(Paragraph)*

**15. Do you have a computer and internet at home?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, my own computer and reliable internet
- Yes, a shared computer
- Phone only
- Not really

**16. Any activity that regularly pulls you out of class?** *(Short answer)*

**17. Anything about being outdoors I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*
Description: Flight operations happen outside, allergies, heat, mobility, anything at all.

**18. Interested in hearing about BPA competitions?** *(Multiple choice)*: Yes / Maybe later / No

**19. Anything else you want me to know?** *(Paragraph)*

---

## Teacher follow-up

- [ ] Seating chart from Q14
- [ ] Part 107 interest list from Q11: drives Q3 planning and any funding request
- [ ] Flag low confidence on Q8 for scaffolded chart and weather work
- [ ] Note outdoor considerations from Q17 before the first flight day
