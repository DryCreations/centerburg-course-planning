# Aviation UAS at a Glance

Mr. Mankin | Room 173 | Full year, 1 credit

## What This Class Is

An aviation course built around unmanned aircraft. Airspace, weather, regulations, aerodynamics, flight
planning, and the work drones actually do: mapping, inspection, agriculture, public safety, film.

## What You Will Do

| Quarter | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| Q1 | Aviation industry and careers, how flight works, regulations, safety |
| Q2 | Weather and what grounds a flight, preflight planning, checklists |
| Q3 | Mapping and GIS, mission planning, emergency operations |
| Q4 | WebXam review, Part 107 practice, capstone mission |

ask me to reopen them. Flight days are marked no late submissions, because they cannot be recreated.

## Two Exams

- Ohio WebXam, the state end of course exam, in the spring.
- FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot, a real federal certificate. No cost to you. See the Part 107 handout.

They overlap a lot. Studying for one studies for the other.

## Safety

Aviation runs on procedure. Checklists get read out loud. Nobody touches an aircraft until we have covered the
procedure for it. Anyone can call a stop to an operation for safety, and nobody gets questioned for it in the
moment.
