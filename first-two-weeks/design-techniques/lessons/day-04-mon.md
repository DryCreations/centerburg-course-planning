## Vocabulary and Standards, About Eight Minutes

Post the week 2 sheet. One example on screen per term rather than reading definitions.

**Brand kit, hex code, RGB, CMYK, raster, vector, typeface, serif and sans serif, hierarchy, visual weight,
transparent background, monogram.**

Two of these get the demo treatment, because they explain something students already noticed:

- **Raster and vector.** Blow one of each up to 800 percent on the projector. Do not describe it, show it.
- **RGB and CMYK.** This is why the printed squares will not match what they saw on their monitors.

## Explore Block: Express Past the Surface

Run each demo in about two minutes, then let them try it. Take the bell ringer questions first where they fit.

1. **Brands panel.** Where a saved set of colors and fonts lives, and how it appears inside a new document.
2. **Layers.** Order, lock, group, and why a grouped element stops moving by accident.
3. **Remove background.** On a real photo. Then show the edge where it gets it wrong, because it always does
   somewhere.
4. **Export options.** PNG, JPG, PDF, and the transparent background checkbox.
5. **Resize and the canvas.** Take a finished square and resize it to a different shape. Watch what breaks.

Then turn them loose. Tell them explicitly to click things they do not understand.

## What You Are Turning In Today

A **feature test.** One document, five things proven.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | A shape or text element with a background removed photo behind it |
| 2 | At least three layers, two of them grouped |
| 3 | One element exported as a **PNG with a transparent background**, submitted separately |
| 4 | The same document exported as a JPG, submitted separately |
| 5 | One sentence: which of those two files would you send to a printer, and why? |

Fifteen points, Labs. Not graded on how it looks. Graded on whether the five things are there.
