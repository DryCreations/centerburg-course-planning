# Day 03 - Fri, Aug 21 (Week 1)

**Course:** Design Techniques, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation and Portfolio Setup

## Objective

Students know how the room and the equipment work, and both week 1 assignments are submitted.

## Materials

Printed procedures handout, scavenger hunt in Classroom, cameras out for anyone who still needs a turn,
projector for the framing review.

## Bell Ringer

Put four student photos on the board with no names. Which one has the cleanest background, and what did that
person do to get it?

## Direct Instruction

**Room and equipment procedures, about fifteen minutes.** File naming, Drive over desktop, how work gets turned
in, camera checkout and return, what to do when something breaks. Demo the naming format on screen.

**Framing review, five minutes.** Use the bell ringer photos. Name the two fixes that solve most first week
problems: get closer, and move so the background is not busy.

## Work Time

- Procedures scavenger hunt, open notes, Readiness credit.
- Last camera turns for anyone who still needs one.
- Finish and export the square. Finish culling and uploading the 8 photos.

## Closure

Both assignments submitted before anyone packs up. Cameras checked in first.

Exit ticket: which of your photos would you actually put in a design, and why that one?
