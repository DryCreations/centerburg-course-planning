# Day 05 - Tue, Aug 25 (Week 2)

**Course:** Design Techniques, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation & Portfolio Setup

## Objective

Students can build a brand kit in Adobe Express, a color set, a font pairing, and a set of brand adjectives,
and explain why they chose them.

## Standards / Outcomes

- AC PRO 4.1.a: asset libraries / brand kit
- 7.4: color and graphics (introduced)

## Materials & Setup

Express brand kit panel on projector, teacher exemplar brand kit ready (Ridgeline Supply Co. from the existing
exemplar work), color-picking references.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*Name a brand you can identify by color alone. What colors?*

## Direct Instruction (20 min)

1. Take the bell ringer answers into **why brands lock their colors and fonts**: consistency is the point.
2. Show the teacher exemplar brand kit and one real brand's public style guide.
3. **Demo:** Express -> Brands -> create brand -> add colors (hex), add fonts, add logo slot. Show how it then
   appears inside a new document.
4. Three brand adjectives: model choosing them badly ("cool, nice, good") vs. usefully ("rugged, warm, plainspoken").

## Work Time (25 min)

**Week 2 Assignment, Part B: Brand Kit.** Students build their own kit in Express: 3-5 colors, 2 fonts
(heading + body), 3 adjectives. Then write two sentences in their portfolio About page explaining the choices.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

**Exit ticket:** *Your three brand adjectives, and one color you rejected and why.*
