## Bell Ringer

other one?

## Policies, Keep It Short

Ten minutes, no more. The full procedures handout comes tomorrow.

- Grading categories, and that the 50% floor exists
- Late work: no penalty. Submissions close after grading, ask me to reopen. Exceptions are marked on the assignment.
- No phones, no headphones
- AI: if it does the part of the assignment that was the point, it is out. Ask when you are unsure.

## Camera Intro

1. Handling: strap around the neck, two hands, elbows in.
2. Focus: half press, confirm it caught the subject, then shoot. Show a soft photo next to a sharp one.
3. Framing for design work: watch the background, leave room around the subject, put the subject off center.
4. Card out, into the reader, copy into your Drive folder, rename. Nothing lives on a card.
