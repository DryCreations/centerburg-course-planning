# Day 08 - Fri, Aug 28 (Week 2)

**Course:** Design Techniques, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation and Portfolio Setup

> **Friday is the WebXam pretest.** Proctor script, prep checklist, and the fallback if the login is not
> ready are in `../_shared/webxam-pretest.md`. Quiz 1 moved to Thursday so Friday can be the pretest plus the
> final submission window.

## Objective

Students complete the WebXam pretest under test conditions, record a strand by strand baseline, and submit a
complete portfolio shell, brand kit, and monogram.

## Materials

WebXam login confirmed and tested on a lab machine, strand recording sheet in Classroom, submission checklist
on the board, seating spread out.

## Bell Ringer

This test does not count for a grade. Why do you think we take it anyway?

## Instruction, Five Minutes

Read the proctor script from `../_shared/webxam-pretest.md`. Do not improvise this part. The framing is what
keeps a low score from reading as failure.

## Pretest

Rest of the period until students finish. As each one finishes, they record strand scores on the sheet and
move to the submission window.

## Submission Window

Self check before turning in.

- [ ] Site published, URL submitted, unit pages exist
- [ ] Brand kit saved in Express: colors, fonts, adjectives
- [ ] Monogram exported as a transparent PNG and placed on the site
- [ ] Files named `lastname-item-version.ext`

## Closure

Exit ticket: your two lowest strands, and one sentence on which one you want to attack first.

## Teacher Notes

- Do not start new content on a testing day. Finishers go back to week 2 work.
- Note anyone who finishes very fast with a low score. That usually means they gave up, and it is worth a
  quiet conversation rather than a grade.
- Grade check for the week: every student should have Readiness from 8 bell ringers and exit tickets, at least
  two Labs, two Projects, and one Assessment.
- If the login did not come through, run the fallback in `../_shared/webxam-pretest.md` and get the pretest on
  the calendar for Unit 1.1.
