# Design Techniques Syllabus

**Course:** Design Techniques (Engineering Design), Subject Code 145095
**Teacher:** Mr. Mankin | **Room:** 173 | **Email:** hayden.mankin@centerburgschools.org
**Full year, 1 credit. Meets daily.**

## What This Course Is

Design Techniques is the first course in the Interactive Media pathway. You will learn to create and edit
digital images, build graphics and layouts, work with type and color, shoot and edit photos, and plan the
marketing around a design. Adobe Express is our main tool. We also use GIMP, Figma, and DSLR cameras.

No experience needed. Everyone starts in the same place.

## What You Will Walk Out With

- A portfolio website in Google Sites showing your work from every quarter
- A personal brand kit (your colors, fonts, and mark) that you use all year
- A full brand identity project: logo, style guide, website, and campaign
- A shot at the Adobe Certified Professional credential
- A competition entry through BPA or our local competition

## What We Will Cover

Roughly in this order. Dates get added once the year settles, and the order can shift if a competition
deadline or an opportunity moves.

**Quarter 1**

| Unit | What it is |
|------|-----------|

**Quarter 2**

**Quarter 3**

| Unit | What it is |
|------|-----------|

**Quarter 4**

| Unit | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| Entrepreneurship | Pricing work, clients, freelancing |

### Late Work

I do not plan to take points off for being late.

If an assignment will not accept late work, the assignment itself says so. That is the only place that rule
lives, so read the assignment first.

Here is the part that matters. I close submissions on an assignment once I finish grading what came in. So if
you want to turn something in late, check that the assignment does not say no late submissions, then reach out
and ask me to reopen it. I will.

What I need from you is the asking. An assignment that sits closed and never gets mentioned stays a zero, and
that is the only way late work hurts you in this class.

## AI Use

Learning is about the process. AI can help you, or it can let you skip the part of the assignment that was the
point. Most assignments here ask you to plan, draft, revise, and explain your choices. If AI does that part for
you, it is not allowed.

Some units use AI on purpose. Unit 3.2 is about AI in design and the ethics around it. When AI is part of the
assignment, you say what you used and what you did yourself.

If you are not sure whether AI use is okay on something, ask me.
