# Design Techniques at a Glance

Mr. Mankin | Room 173 | Full year, 1 credit

## What This Class Is

You learn to make things people actually look at: logos, graphics, layouts, photos, and the campaigns around
them. Adobe Express is the main tool. You build a portfolio website that grows all year.

## What You Will Make

| Quarter | Work |
|---------|------|
| Q1 | Portfolio site, brand kit, infographic, type style guide, brand website |
| Q2 | Photo essay, project proposal and marketing plan, social media campaign |
| Q3 | Competition piece, AI design study, freelance business package |
| Q4 | Client delivery package, brand identity capstone, portfolio presentation |

## Grading

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Projects | 40% |
| Labs | 20% |
| Assessments | 30% |
| Readiness and Participation | 10% |

Quarter grades are not recorded below 50%. Late work is accepted with no penalty. If submissions have closed,
ask me to reopen them. A few assignments are marked no late submissions, and those say so on the assignment.

## What You Need Every Day

- Your school Google login
- Phone away, no headphones
- Something to write with for bell ringers and sketching

## Exam and Credential

- Ohio WebXam in the spring. We take an ungraded pretest early so we know where to aim.
- Adobe Certified Professional for Adobe Express, tested in the spring after the WebXam.

## Competition

We are starting a BPA chapter this year and every student competes in some form, either a BPA event or our
local competition in January. Your class projects are the entry, so it is not extra work on top of the course.
