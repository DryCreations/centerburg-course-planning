# Design Techniques: Classroom Procedures

**Room 173, Mr. Mankin**
Keep this in your Classroom folder. We will walk through it on Day 2 and I will hold you to it after that.

---

## Coming In

1. Come in, sit in your assigned seat, wake your machine and log in.
2. The **bell ringer** is on the board and in Google Classroom. Start it immediately: you have 5 minutes.
3. Bags on the floor or hooks, not on the desks. Nothing on top of the machines.

## Phones & Headphones

- Phones are away, per district policy. Not in your lap, not face-up on the desk.
- No headphones. If an assignment needs audio monitoring, I will say so and hand out what you need.

## Food & Drink

Water in a closed bottle, on the floor. Nothing else near the keyboards.

## Your Files

- Everything lives in your **school Google Drive**, not on the desktop of a lab machine. Desktops get wiped.
- Naming format: `lastname-project-version.ext`, for example `mankin-logo-v2.png`.
- Work turned in through **Google Classroom**. Share links must be set so I can open them.

## Adobe Express

- Sign in with your **school Google account** every time. A personal Adobe account will lose your work and
  your brand kit.
- Save happens automatically, but export finished pieces and store them in Drive too.

## Cameras & Equipment

The room uses **one checkout system for all classes**, the same form Video & Sound, Yearbook, and Aviation
UAS use. What you sign out is visible to every other class, so an unreturned camera is everyone's problem.

1. Ask before touching. Nothing leaves the cabinet without being signed out.
2. Complete the checkout form: your name, **this course**, item ID, where it is going.
3. Strap on your neck any time a camera is in your hands. Two hands, always.
4. Lens caps on before it goes back. Cards returned in the body unless I say otherwise.
5. Returned the same period unless we arrange something different.
6. **Report damage immediately.** Telling me is not the thing you get in trouble for.

## Getting Help

Work the ladder before you raise your hand:

1. Re-read the assignment or handout.
2. Try one thing.
3. Ask the person next to you.
4. Ask me.

## Critique Days

You will show unfinished work. Feedback is about the work, not the person. Say what is working, then what is
confusing, then one specific suggestion.

## Turning Work In

- Google Classroom, on the assignment post: not emailed, not shared as a stray link.
- Exported files use the naming format above.
- If it is a link (portfolio, site, Express doc), check the sharing settings before you submit.

## Leaving the Room

Sign out on the pass log. One person out at a time. Not during a demo.

## End of Period

1. Finish and submit the **exit ticket**.
2. Save and close your work.
3. Equipment returned and logged.
4. Chairs pushed in, keyboards and mice square, trash picked up.
5. Log off: do not shut down unless I ask.

## Emergencies

Fire, tornado, and lockdown routes are posted by the door. In a drill: leave machines running, take nothing,
follow me, stay with our class.
