# Design Techniques: Student Info Survey (Google Form, copy-paste ready)

**Build:** new Google Form -> paste each block below. Question type is noted in **bold**.
**Assign:** Day 01, Google Classroom. **Collect:** email addresses ON, one response per person.

---

**Form title:** Design Techniques, Getting to Know You

**Form description:**
> Welcome to Design Techniques! This takes about five minutes. Answers are just for me, they help me set up
> seating, plan projects around what you actually care about, and know what to call you.

---

### Section 1: Basics

**1. What name should I call you?** *(Short answer, required)*
Description: If it is different from what shows up on my roster, tell me here.

**2. How do you say it?** *(Short answer)*
Description: Only if I am likely to get it wrong.

**3. Grade level** *(Multiple choice, required)*
- 9
- 10
- 11
- 12

**4. Do you go by any pronouns I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*

---

### Section 2: Experience

**5. Have you used any of these before?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Adobe Express
- Photoshop or GIMP
- Canva
- Google Sites or another website builder
- A DSLR / mirrorless camera
- Video editing software
- None of these: this is all new

**6. How comfortable are you with computers in general?** *(Linear scale 1-5, required)*
- 1 = I get nervous clicking around
- 5 = I figure out new software on my own

**7. Have you made anything you are proud of: a drawing, edit, video, poster, meme, model, anything?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Describe it. You do not have to show me.

---

### Section 3: You

**8. Why did you sign up for this class?** *(Paragraph, required)*

**9. What do you want to be able to do by the end of the year?** *(Paragraph)*

**10. What are you into outside of school?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Sports, music, games, work, animals, cars, this is how I pick project examples that are not boring.

**11. Any career or college plans right now?** *(Short answer)*
Description: "No idea" is a completely fine answer.

---

### Section 4: Logistics

**12. Seating: anything that helps you focus?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Front of the room, away from a specific person, near the door, vision or hearing needs, tell me
and I will do what I can.

**13. Do you have a computer and internet at home?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, my own computer and reliable internet
- Yes, a shared computer
- Phone only
- Not really

**14. Are you in any activity that pulls you out of class regularly?** *(Short answer)*
Description: Sports, band, CCP, work-study, etc.

**15. Would you be interested in hearing about BPA (competitions in design, photography, and media)?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, tell me more
- Maybe later
- Not interested

**16. Anything else you want me to know?** *(Paragraph)*

---
