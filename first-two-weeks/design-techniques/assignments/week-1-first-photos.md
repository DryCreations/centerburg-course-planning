# Week 1 Assignment 2: First Photos

**Assigned:** Fri Aug 21 (Day 03) | **Due:** end of the day Fri Aug 21
**Turn in:** Google Classroom | **Category:** Labs | **Points:** 20

Companion to the About Me Square. That one is about building a page. This one is about getting images worth
putting on a page.

## What This Is

Your first time with a class camera. We share cameras, so you take turns. I am looking for whether you can get
a photo in focus, frame it on purpose, get it off the card, and turn it in.

## What You Turn In

**8 photos**, uploaded to the Classroom assignment, named:

```
lastname-photo-01.jpg  through  lastname-photo-08.jpg
```

## What I Am Looking For

This is a design course, so frame these like someone who is going to use them in a layout later.

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| In focus | A soft photo cannot be printed at any size |
| Bright enough to see the subject | Underexposed images fall apart when you edit them |
| A clean background | Clutter behind the subject is the most common problem in a first shoot |
| Something is off center | Practice putting the subject a third of the way in instead of dead middle |
| Two of the eight are close ups | Detail, texture, an object. Not a face across the room. |

## Steps

1. Check out a camera on the room checkout form.
2. Strap around your neck. It stays there.
3. Shoot around the room. Ask before you photograph a person. If they say no, that is the answer.
4. Card out, into the reader, copy into your Drive folder, rename.
5. Upload the 8 photos to Classroom.
6. Check the camera back in: card cleared, battery on the charger, lens cap on.

If you are waiting for a camera, work on your About Me Square.
