## What This Is

Three connected pieces of setup you will use for the entire year: the website that holds your work, the brand
kit that keeps your work consistent, and the personal mark that identifies it.

Do them in order. Each part builds on the one before.

---

## Part A: Portfolio Site (20 pts), started Day 04

Build and publish a Google Sites portfolio.

**Requirements**

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Site created in **Google Sites** with your school account |
| 2 | Home page with your name and a one-sentence introduction |
| 3 | An About page, with your printed About Me Square posted on it |
| 4 | Placeholder pages for units: *Unit 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4* |
| 5 | Site **published**, link shared so anyone at the school can view |
| 6 | Published URL submitted in Classroom |

> The most common failure is a site that exists but was never published, or is published but locked. Open the
> link in a private window to test it before you submit.

---

## Part B: Brand Kit (20 pts), started Day 05

Build a brand kit in Adobe Express that you will use in every project this year.

**Requirements**

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | **3-5 colors** saved in an Express brand, with hex codes |
| 2 | **2 fonts**, one for headings, one for body text |
| 3 | **3 adjectives** describing the feel you are going for |
| 4 | Two sentences on your About page explaining why you chose these colors and fonts |

**On the adjectives:** "cool," "nice," and "good" tell you nothing. "Rugged," "playful," "quiet," "loud,"
"expensive," "handmade" tell you what to do next. Pick words that would help someone else design for you.

This kit is not permanent. You will rebuild it in Unit 1.3 once you know more about type and color.

---

## Part C: Monogram (20 pts), started Day 07

Design a personal mark from your initials.

**Requirements**

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Three paper sketches first (photo them and submit) |
| 2 | Final mark built in Express using your brand kit colors and fonts |
| 3 | Exported as **PNG with a transparent background** |
| 4 | Still legible when shrunk to about the size of a thumbnail |
| 5 | Placed on your portfolio site header or About page |
| 6 | Named `lastname-monogram-v1.png` |

**Test before you submit:** shrink it to 32 px on screen. If it turns into a smudge, simplify it.
