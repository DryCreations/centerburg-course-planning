# Week 1: About Me Square

Used in Design Techniques, Video and Sound, Aviation UAS, and Yearbook. Same assignment in all four, posted
separately in each Classroom.

**Started:** Wed Aug 19 (Day 01) | **Due:** end of the day Fri Aug 21
**Built in:** Adobe Express | **Turn in:** Google Classroom
**Points:** 30

These get printed and put up in the room, so build it like something you are willing to have on the wall.

## What This Is

A square design about you, built in Adobe Express. Two things are happening. You get used to Express, which we
use in every course in this room, and I get to see how you think about a page.

## Setup

Adobe licenses are on your student account now. Sign in to Adobe Express with your **school Google account**,
not a personal one. If you use a personal account your work will not be there later.

## What You Turn In

One exported PNG or PDF, submitted in Classroom, named:

```
lastname-aboutme.png
```

## Requirements

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Square canvas, 2560 x 2560 pixels, which is the Express default square. It prints clean at 8 x 8. |
| 2 | Your name, readable from across the room |
| 3 | At least 4 images that say something about you |
| 4 | A short caption or label with each image |
| 5 | At least one block of text about you, three sentences or more |
| 6 | Two fonts at most, and colors you picked on purpose |
| 7 | Exported and submitted in Classroom |

## Steps

1. Adobe Express, sign in with your school Google account.
2. New project, square. The default 2560 x 2560 is what we want, so you do not have to change it.
3. Sketch where things go before you make anything look good. Name, images, text.
4. Place your images. Your own photos, screenshots, or images you have the rights to use.
5. Write your captions and your text block.
6. Set your fonts and colors. Two fonts is the limit.
7. Export and submit.

## Grading

| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|-------:|
| All seven requirements met | 12 |
| Captions are real sentences, not labels | 6 |
| Text and images line up and are readable | 6 |
| Font and color choices you can explain | 4 |
| Submitted correctly and on time | 2 |

## Notes

Square is harder than it looks. There is no long edge to hide behind, so weak spacing shows up fast.

Leave a little breathing room at the edges. Anything jammed against the border looks cut off once it is printed.

We will hang these up and look at them together next week.
