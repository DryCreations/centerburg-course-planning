# Weekly Structure

The same shape every week in all five courses. Students should be able to predict the week by Tuesday of the
first one. Middle School CS runs the same shape with checkpoints instead of quizzes and no WebXam language.

---

## The Week

| Day | What it is | What students do |
|-----|-----------|------------------|
| Monday | Set the week | New vocabulary and standards go up. Look at last week's work as a group. Start the new content. |
| Tuesday | First skill | Direct instruction, then Lab 1. |
| Wednesday | Second skill | Direct instruction, then Lab 2. |
| Thursday | Build day | Shortest lesson of the week, longest work block. The project moves. |
| Friday | Close the week | Quiz, final submission window, then a closing activity that reviews the quiz content without calling itself review. |

Every day opens with a bell ringer and ends with an exit ticket. Those two are the entire Readiness grade, so
they are short and they are never skipped.

---

## What Goes Up Monday and Stays Up

Three things, posted in the room and in Classroom:

1. **This week's vocabulary.** Six to ten terms, no more. Friday's quiz comes from this list and students are
   told that on Monday.
2. **This week's standards**, written the way a student would say them. The outcome number goes next to it so
   the connection to the exam is visible, but the sentence is the part they read.
3. **This week at a glance.** What is due, when, and what is graded.

The vocabulary and standards for each course are in `<course>/handouts/vocab-and-standards.md`.

## Standing Rules That Do Not Change Week to Week

- Bell ringer is on the board when the bell rings. Answer it before anything else.
- Work lives in Drive, not on the desktop. File names are `lastname-item-version.ext`.
- Gear is checked out and checked in the same period unless I say otherwise.
- Quizzes are Friday. Same format every time: mostly recall and application, a few scenario questions.
- Late work is accepted. Time sensitive work is marked as such and is the exception.

---

## Grading Rhythm

Over a normal week each student should generate:

| Category | Per week |
|----------|----------|
| Readiness | 5 bell ringers, 5 exit tickets |
| Labs | 2 |
| Projects | 1 running project, submitted at the end of the unit |
| Assessments | 1 quiz |

If a week does not produce those, the grade book gets thin and the quarter grade stops meaning anything. Check
it on Friday, not in October.
