## Proctor Script

Say this at the start of each period. It matters that the framing is honest, because the scores are going to
be low and students will read low scores as failure unless you get ahead of it.

> This is a pretest. It does not count for a grade and it never will.
>
> It covers the whole course, and we are two weeks in, so most of it has not been taught. Your score is going
> to be low. That is not a problem, it is the point.
>
> What I care about is which parts you are weakest in, because that is where we spend the year. If you guess
> randomly, I get bad information and I teach the wrong things. So answer what you know, and when you do not
> know, pick what seems most likely and move on.
>
> No notes, no phones, no talking. When you finish, record your scores by strand on the sheet in Classroom,
> then go back to your week 2 work.

---

## During

- Circulate. Do not help with content.
- Note who finishes fast. Fast plus low usually means they gave up, and that is worth a quiet conversation.
- As students finish, they record strand scores on the sheet and return to week 2 submissions.

## After

- Record every strand score in the tracker. This is the only day it is easy to do.
- Rank the strands lowest to highest per class. That ranking is the plan for Q1 and Q2 emphasis.
- Anyone absent takes it Monday. Do not let makeups drift, the data is only useful as a full class picture.

## Exit Ticket

Your two lowest strands, and one sentence on which one you want to attack first.
