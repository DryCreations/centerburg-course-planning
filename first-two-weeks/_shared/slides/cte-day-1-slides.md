## SLIDE 4: This is a CTE course

- CTE stands for Career Technical Education
- It is a real career field program, not an elective version of one
- You end the year with an exam score, work in a portfolio, and something to put on an application
- The state has a list of what you have to be able to do. We work off that list.

Notes: Say plainly that the standards are public and you teach to them, not around them.

### SLIDE 12-UAS: FAA Part 107

- A real FAA certificate, not a class badge
- Required to fly a drone for work rather than for fun
- 60 questions, 2 hours, 70% to pass, taken at an approved testing center
- **No cost to you** for the attempt we arrange
- Dates and location are still being worked out

Notes: Be honest that the logistics are not set. Do not name a date you are not sure of.

---

### SLIDE 13-UAS: Safety, up front

- Nobody touches an aircraft until we have covered the procedure for it
- Checklists get read out loud, every time
- Anyone can call a stop to an operation for safety, and nobody gets questioned for it in the moment
- Report damage and near misses right away

Notes: This is the tone setter for the course. Say it on day one and hold to it.
