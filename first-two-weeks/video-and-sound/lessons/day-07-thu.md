# Day 07 - Thu, Aug 27 (Week 2)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

## Objective

Students can import footage into Premiere, cut clips onto a timeline in order, add their audio, and export a
short piece.

> **Platform: Adobe Premiere** (or Premiere Rush for students who need the simpler interface). Confirmed
> installed. The four moves today are import, trim, order, export.

## Standards / Outcomes

- 2.9.1: production workflow
- 7.7: video production (post entry skills)

## Materials & Setup

Premiere open and tested on the teacher machine and two student machines before class. Day 03 shots, Day 05
audio, and (if it exists) Day 06 footage available in Drive. A new project template saved so students are not
building project settings from scratch on day one.

## Gallery Walk (first 10 minutes)

Moved here from Day 04, because the squares were not printed yet. Run it first, before anything else.

1. Everyone walks the room and looks at the printed squares. No talking for the first few minutes.
2. Then a fast round: each person names one thing they saw in someone else's square that they want to try.
3. I name three things I saw across the whole set: what worked, what repeated, and one habit to break.

Print reads differently than screen. Point out anything that looked fine on a monitor and does not hold up on
paper, especially thin type and low contrast color.

The squares stay on the wall. They are the first thing up in this room and they set the tone.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*You shot 40 minutes of footage for a 2-minute video. What is the first thing you do when you sit down to edit?*

## Quiz 1

Fifteen minutes, after the gallery walk and before the work block. Paste ready in `assessments/quiz-1.md`.

Moved here from Friday, because Friday is the WebXam pretest. Format repeats: mostly recall and application,

## Direct Instruction (18 min)

Live demo, students watching first, then repeating:

1. **Import**: new project, media browser, bring in the media; explain why the source files live in Drive and
   not on the desktop, and what that means for a project file that only *points* at media.
2. **Timeline**: drag in a clip; scrub; understand the playhead.
3. **Trim**: cut the head and tail off a clip. Do it three times.
4. **Order**: arrange into a sequence that makes sense.
5. **Audio**: drop the Day 05 recording underneath.
6. **Export**: settings, file name, where it goes.

## Work Time (22 min)

**Lab: First Cut.** Using their own Day 03 stills, Day 05 audio, and any Day 06 footage, build a 20-30 second
piece: at least four clips, one audio track, titles optional. Export and submit.

Also: finish outstanding Week 2 parts, everything is due tomorrow.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

**Exit ticket:** *What was the most annoying part of the edit? (This is real feedback, it tells me what to
teach next.)*
