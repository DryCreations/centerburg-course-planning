# Day 07 - Thu, Aug 27 (Week 2)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

> **Finish shutter, start Manual.** The class is moving slower than planned: most groups are not through the
> shutter-speed frames yet, and they are ready to start Manual today. So today is finishing shutter and getting
> into Manual, no rush; whatever is not done can be finished **tomorrow during the pretest window**. Early
> finishers work silently and independently. The class knows the vocabulary, so the review is quick. Audio and
> editing come in a later unit.

## Objective

Students finish the shutter-speed frames of the Camera Modes Shoot and start Manual (matching a known
exposure), and can explain what aperture, shutter, and ISO each trade against.

## Materials & Setup

Printed squares on the wall, Quiz 1 as a Google Form (answer key deleted), cameras charged for the challenge,
the Day 07 slides.

## Gallery Walk (first 10 minutes)

Moved here from Day 04, because the squares were not printed yet. Run it first.

1. Everyone walks the room and looks at the printed squares. No talking for the first few minutes.
2. A fast round: each person names one thing they saw in someone else's square they want to try.
3. I name three things across the whole set: what worked, what repeated, one habit to break.

The squares stay on the wall. They set the tone for the room.

## Bell Ringer / Review (5 min)

*You want a portrait where the face is sharp and the background is soft. Name the mode and the setting, and
which direction you move it.* Then a fast round-the-room review, one word each: aperture, shutter, ISO,
exposure, depth of field, Av, Tv, M. They know these; this is a warm confidence check, not a re-teach. If
anyone is shaky on ISO or Manual, that is the 60-second thing to demo before they shoot.

## Quiz 1 (10 min, optional today)

Short, from the vocabulary, paste ready in `assessments/quiz-1.md`. Run it if the class is ready; if groups are
still mid-shoot, it can wait for Friday. Do not let it eat camera time when the shoot is behind.

## Manual, quick demo (8 min)

Before work time, a fast demo of **Manual**: set aperture, shutter, and ISO yourself, and **match the Auto
baseline** from shot 1. That is the point of Manual, and it is where the groups are headed today.

## Work Time (25 min): Finish Shutter, Start Manual

1. **Finish the shutter/motion frames first** (the water shots, the frozen and panned walk-by). This is where
   most groups still are.
2. **Then Manual:** the matched-exposure frame and the low-light frame.
3. **Not done? That is fine.** Finish tomorrow during the pretest window. Early finishers keep working
   **silently and independently**, or start the optional five-frame photo challenge.
4. Portfolio and SMART goals are the other thing to close out before the week ends.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

Gear checked in, cards cleared, batteries charging first.
**Exit ticket:** *Which of your five frames are you proudest of, and which setting made it work?*
