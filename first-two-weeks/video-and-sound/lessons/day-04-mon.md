## Direct Instruction: The Three Settings

Live, with the camera on the document camera or tethered. Ten minutes, no diagram.

1. **Aperture.** Change the f number. The background goes soft and the image gets brighter.
2. **Shutter speed.** Have someone wave a hand. Shoot at 1/30, then at 1/500. Both frames on the screen.
3. **ISO.** Walk to the dim corner, raise ISO, zoom in on the grain.

Say the trade out loud each time. Gain light in one place and you give something back somewhere else.

**The mode dial, three minutes.** Auto, P, Av, Tv, M, one sentence each on who decides what. The rule for the
year: Auto is not banned, but you have to know what it chose.
