# Day 05 - Tue, Aug 25 (Week 2)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

> **This week is the camera, in a photo context.** Audio and editing come later. Right now the whole job is
> learning what the three settings do by shooting stills. Monday covered all three and most groups got through
> the aperture shots. Today is **shutter speed and motion.**

## Objective

Students can shoot in Tv (shutter priority) on purpose, predict whether a shutter speed will freeze or blur
motion, and get a usable panning shot.

## Materials & Setup

Kits staged and charged, cards cleared, **mode dials preset to Tv**, card readers out, the sink identified for
the water shots, a hallway or open space for the motion shots, the Day 05 slides, camera signup sheet posted.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*Two photos on the board: a water drop frozen in the air, and the same water as a smooth streak. What did the
camera do differently between them?*

## Direct Instruction (15 min)

Live, camera tethered or on the document camera. Slides Day 05.

1. **Recap the trade in one line:** change one setting, you give something back on another. They saw it with
   aperture Monday.
2. **Shutter speed.** Fast (1/500, 1/1000) freezes motion and darkens the shot. Slow (1/30, 1/15) blurs motion
   and brightens it. Demo with a waving hand at 1/30 and 1/500, both on screen.
3. **Tv mode.** You pick the shutter speed, the camera picks the aperture to keep it exposed.
4. **The trade to name:** a fast shutter lets in less light, so the camera opens the aperture or raises ISO to
   compensate. They will watch the numbers move.
5. **Panning**, the fun one: 1/30, turn with the subject, keep them in the same spot in the frame. Sharp
   subject, streaked background. Expect ten tries.

## Work Time (25 min): Camera Modes Shoot, Part B

Students shoot the Tv / motion frames of the Camera Modes Shoot (`assignments/week-2-camera-modes.md`, shots 6
to 10): the frozen and streaked water, the frozen and panned walk-by, and their own choice of motion.

Rotation if kits are short: half shoot while half work on the portfolio (Part A) or catch up on week 1, swap.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

Everything offloaded and checked in before the exit ticket.
**Exit ticket:** *Which shutter speed froze the motion, and which one blurred it? Give a number for each.*

## Teacher Notes

- Set every dial to Tv before class or ten minutes disappear into people hunting for the mode.
- The panning shot is the one they will keep trying. Let them, it is the shot that makes them feel like a
  photographer, and the throwaways do not count against them.
- Water shots need the lights on. A fast shutter means less light, so they will see ISO climb. That is the
  lesson, not a problem.
- No audio this week. If anyone asks, it is coming, after the camera is solid.
