# Day 05 - Tue, Aug 25 (Week 2)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

## Objective

Students can set levels on a microphone, explain why room tone matters, and write three SMART goals for the course.

## Standards / Outcomes

- 7.8: audio production (entry skills)
- 1.1.7: goal setting

## Materials & Setup

One mic + recorder per pair (or the built-in setup if recorders are short), headphones for monitoring,
**today is an audio-monitoring day, so headphones are permitted and required**. SMART goals scaffold in Classroom.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

*Play 15 seconds of clean audio, then the same line recorded badly (too quiet, clipped, echoey). What is wrong
with the second one? Use any words you have.*

## Direct Instruction (18 min)

1. **Levels**: demo the meter. Too quiet is unusable; clipped is unfixable. Aim around the middle, peaks
   below the top.
2. **Mic distance**: same line at 6 inches, 2 feet, and across the room. Let them hear it.
3. **Room tone**: 30 seconds of "nothing" at every location, and why the editor needs it.
4. **SMART goals** (5 min): one strong example, one weak one, side by side.

## Work Time (22 min)

**Lab: Sound Check (Week 2, Part B).** In pairs:

- Record the same sentence at three mic distances.
- Record 30 seconds of room tone.
- Deliberately record one clipped take so they hear what it sounds like.
- Offload, name files, submit.

Then, individually: **Part C, three SMART goals** for the course, submitted in Classroom and posted on the
portfolio About page.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

Headphones back in the bin, gear checked in, then:
**Exit ticket:** *Which mic distance sounded best, and why do you think so?*
