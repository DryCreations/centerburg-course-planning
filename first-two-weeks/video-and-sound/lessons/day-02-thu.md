# Day 02 - Thu, Aug 20 (Week 1)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

## Objective

Students can check a camera out correctly, hold it, focus it, and start the ten shot list.

## Materials

Every kit staged and tagged, checkout form live, kit cards in the cases, charged batteries, cleared cards, card
readers out, printed syllabus.

## Bell Ringer

You check out a camera Friday and bring it back Monday with a dead battery and a full memory card. Name three
problems that just created for someone else.

## Policies, Keep It Short

Ten minutes. The full procedures handout comes tomorrow.

- Grading categories and the 50% floor
- Late work, and that shoot days are the exception that cannot be made up
- No phones. No headphones, except when we monitor audio, and I will tell you when.
- AI: if it does the work the assignment asked you to do, it is out.

## Checkout Walkthrough

With a real case on the table.

1. Open the case, read the kit card, inventory out loud.
2. The form: name, course, item ID, where it is going.
3. Inspection: body, lens, battery, card, strap.
4. Return: offload, clear the card, battery on the charger, case complete, confirmed by me.
5. Report damage right away. That is not the part you get in trouble for.

## Camera Intro and First Turns

1. Strap first, always.
2. Power on, lens cap off, card confirmed.
3. Focus: half press, confirm, shoot.
4. Shot sizes: wide, medium, close up, over the shoulder. One example of each on the projector.

Then rotation. Camera turns start on the ten shot list. Everyone else works on the About Me Square, which is
the same tool you will use for titles and thumbnails later.

## Closure

Everything back in the cabinet, offloaded, before the exit ticket.

Exit ticket: list the four things that must be true before a camera case goes back in the cabinet.
