# Day 03 - Fri, Aug 21 (Week 1)

**Course:** Video and Sound, **Unit:** 1.0 Orientation

## Objective

Every student completes a full checkout cycle on their own and finishes the ten shot list. Both week 1
assignments are submitted, and the room procedures are covered.

## Materials

Kits staged, checkout forms live, card readers out, printed procedures handout, scavenger hunt in Classroom,
projector for the shot review.

## Bell Ringer

From memory: the first three steps of checking out a camera.

## Direct Instruction

**Room procedures, about twelve minutes.** File naming, Drive over desktop, how work gets turned in, shooting
around the building and asking permission, the studio and audio rules, and the end of period reset.

**Shot review, five minutes.** Put four student frames from yesterday on the board. Name the shot size on each
one. Anyone who cannot name it needs to reshoot that one today.

## Work Time

Rotation continues.

**Camera turn:** finish the ten shot list, and run the whole cycle yourself this time, from the form to the
teacher-confirmed check in.

**Everyone else:** procedures scavenger hunt, then finish and export the About Me Square.

## Closure

Everything back in the cabinet, offloaded and logged, before the exit ticket.

Exit ticket: what did you get wrong in the checkout process, and what will you do differently Monday?
