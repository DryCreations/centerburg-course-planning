# Week 1 Assignment 2: Camera Checkout and First Shots

**Assigned:** Fri Aug 21 (Day 03) | **Due:** end of the day Fri Aug 21
**Turn in:** Google Classroom | **Category:** Labs | **Points:** 25

## What This Is

Your license to touch the gear, plus your first framing practice. You run one full cycle on your own: check
out, handle, shoot, offload, check in. The photos matter less than the process, but the framing counts.

## What You Turn In

**10 photos**, uploaded to the Classroom assignment, named:

```
lastname-shots-01.jpg  through  lastname-shots-10.jpg
```

## The Shot List

Video is built out of shot sizes, so start there now.

| # | Shot |
|---|------|
| 1-2 | Wide. The whole space, so a viewer knows where they are. |
| 3-4 | Medium. A person from about the waist up, doing something. |
| 5-6 | Close up. Hands, an object, a detail. |
| 7-8 | Over the shoulder, looking at what someone is working on |
| 9 | One shot from an unusual height. Low or high, not eye level. |
| 10 | One deliberately bad frame, and be ready to say what makes it bad |

## Steps

1. Check out your kit on the form: name, course, item ID, where it is going.
2. Inspect it with me: body, lens, battery, card, strap, kit card complete.
3. Strap around your neck. It stays there.
4. Shoot the list. Ask before you photograph a person.
5. Card out, into the reader, copy to your Drive folder, rename.
6. Upload to Classroom.
7. Check in: card cleared, battery on the charger, lens cap on, case complete, confirmed by me.

## Grading

| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|-------:|
| Checkout form and inspection done correctly | 5 |
| Safe handling: strap, two hands, cap on | 5 |
| All ten shots on the list, in focus | 8 |
| Files offloaded, named right, submitted | 4 |
| Check in complete and correct | 3 |

Anything that comes back with a full card, a dead battery, or a missing piece gets handed back to you to fix
before you shoot again.
