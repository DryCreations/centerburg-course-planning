## Part C: Three SMART Goals (20 pts), Day 05

Write three goals for this course. SMART means **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**chievable, **R**elevant,
**T**ime-bound.

**Weak:** "Get better at video."
**Strong:** "By the end of Q1, I can shoot and cut a 60-second interview with clean audio and no shot held
longer than 8 seconds."

**Requirements**

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Three goals, each one meeting all five SMART criteria |
| 2 | At least one about a **technical skill** (camera, audio, or editing) |
| 3 | At least one about a **finished piece** you want to make |
| 4 | Posted on your portfolio About page and submitted in Classroom |

We revisit these at the end of each quarter, so write goals you would actually want to be measured against.
