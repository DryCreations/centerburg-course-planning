## Multiple Choice

**1.** In Av mode, what do you control?

A. The shutter speed
B. The aperture
C. The ISO
D. All three

**2.** In Tv mode, what do you control?

A. The shutter speed
B. The aperture
C. The white balance
D. Nothing, the camera decides

**3.** You are shooting a volleyball spike indoors and every frame is a blur. What do you change first?

A. Lower the ISO
B. Raise the shutter speed
C. Raise the f number
D. Switch to Auto

**4.** You want a portrait with a soft, out of focus background. You should use:

A. A high f number like f/16
B. A low f number like f/2.8
C. A fast shutter speed
D. A high ISO

**5.** Raising the ISO makes a dark photo brighter. What does it cost you?

A. Focus
B. Color accuracy only
C. Grain, also called noise
D. Nothing, ISO is free

**6.** Which mode lets you set the aperture, the shutter speed, and the ISO all yourself?

A. Auto
B. Av
C. Tv
D. Manual

**7.** You raise the shutter speed to freeze fast motion. All else equal, the image gets:

A. Brighter
B. Darker
C. More colorful
D. Sharper in the background

**8.** You want as much of the scene as possible in focus, front to back. You should use:

A. A low f number like f/2.8
B. A high f number like f/16
C. A slow shutter speed
D. A high ISO

**9.** Panning means:

A. Zooming in slowly while you shoot
B. Turning the camera with a moving subject at a slow shutter, so the subject is sharp and the background streaks
C. Holding the camera perfectly still for a long exposure
D. Switching to Auto for a moving subject

**10.** Before a camera case goes back in the cabinet, which of these must be true?

A. The card is offloaded and cleared, the battery is on the charger, the case is complete, and I confirmed it
B. The battery is charged
C. It is in the cabinet by the bell
D. The lens cap is on

## Short Answer

**11.** You are shooting a basketball game in a dim gym. Name the mode you would use, one setting you would
watch, and why.

**12.** Explain, in two sentences, what the three settings trade against each other. Use aperture, shutter
speed, and ISO in your answer.

---

## Answer Key

1: B, 2: A, 3: B, 4: B, 5: C, 6: D, 7: B, 8: B, 9: B, 10: A

**11.** Tv mode is the strongest answer, with shutter speed at 1/500 or faster and ISO watched because the gym
is dark. Av with a low f number is acceptable if the reasoning is sound. The reasoning is what is being
graded, not the mode.

**12.** Full credit needs the idea that gaining light in one place costs something somewhere else, plus at
least two specific trades named. Aperture costs depth of field. Shutter costs motion or light. ISO costs
grain.
