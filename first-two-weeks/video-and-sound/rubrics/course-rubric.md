## Project Rubric, 100 points

| Criterion | Exemplary (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|----------------|---------------|-------:|
| **Meets the Brief** | Every requirement met, plus deliberate choices that go beyond the ask | All requirements met | Most requirements met; one significant gap | Several requirements missing | 30% |
| **Technical Skill** | Precise, efficient tool use; clean, correctly-specified output | Competent tool use; minor technical issues | Basic tool use; issues affect the result | Technical problems dominate the work | 25% |
| **Craft & Design Quality** | Polished and intentional throughout | Solid, mostly consistent choices | Inconsistent or unresolved choices | Little evidence of craft | 20% |
| **Process & Deadlines** | Milestones met early; process clearly documented | Milestones met; process documented | Some milestones late or thinly documented | Milestones missed | 15% |
| **Reflection & Communication** | Specific, insightful explanation of choices | Clear explanation of choices | Surface-level or generic | Missing or off-topic | 10% |

**Score conversion:** criterion score (1-4)  /  4  x  weight. A "3" across the board is an 75%, Proficient means
*you did what was asked, and did it well*. A 4 is for work that is finished and thought through.

## Lab Rubric, 20 points

| Criterion | Points | Description |
|-----------|-------:|-------------|
| Steps completed | 8 | All lab steps attempted and finished |
| Accuracy / technique | 6 | The skill was performed correctly |
| Documentation | 4 | Worksheet, log, or screenshots filled in |
| Turned in correctly | 2 | Right place, right format, right file name |

## Readiness & Participation, bell ringers and exit tickets

| Points | Description |
|-------:|-------------|
| 2 | Complete and on topic |
| 1 | Attempted but incomplete |
| 0 | Not submitted |

Bell ringer each day + exit ticket each day. Absent days are excused, not zeroes.

## Assessments

Weekly quizzes (typically Friday), unit tests, and, in CTE courses. WebXam-style practice and credential
prep. Quizzes follow the 70% DOK 1-2 / 30% DOK 3-4 mix used in `courses/_templates/quiz-template.md`.
