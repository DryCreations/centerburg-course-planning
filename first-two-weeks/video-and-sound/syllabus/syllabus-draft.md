# Video and Sound Syllabus

**Course:** Video and Sound Production, Subject Code 145110
**Teacher:** Mr. Mankin | **Room:** 173 | **Email:** hayden.mankin@centerburgschools.org
**Full year, 1 credit. Meets daily.**

## What This Course Is

You plan, shoot, record, edit, and publish video and audio. Camera work, lighting, sound, interviewing,
editing, and getting a finished piece in front of people. You will use DSLR cameras, microphones, and Adobe
Premiere, and you will keep a portfolio of what you produce.

No experience needed.

## What You Will Walk Out With

- A portfolio site with your finished video and audio work
- Camera skills: exposure, framing, focus, movement
- Audio skills: mic placement, levels, cleanup, mixing
- A short piece edited start to finish
- A competition entry through BPA or our local competition

### Late Work

I do not plan to take points off for being late.

If an assignment will not accept late work, the assignment itself says so. That is the only place that rule
lives, so read the assignment first.

Here is the part that matters. I close submissions on an assignment once I finish grading what came in. So if
you want to turn something in late, check that the assignment does not say no late submissions, then reach out
and ask me to reopen it. I will.

What I need from you is the asking. An assignment that sits closed and never gets mentioned stays a zero, and
that is the only way late work hurts you in this class.

Shoot days, crew days, quizzes, and tests are the assignments most likely to be marked no late submissions,
because they only happen once. If a crew does not show up, that day is gone.

### Absences

Check Google Classroom. If you missed a shoot or a demo, come see me and we will find time.

## Equipment Rules

This class runs on shared gear.

1. Everything gets signed out and signed back in.
2. What is under your name is your responsibility until it is checked back in.
3. Strap around your neck any time a camera is in your hands.
4. Batteries charged and cards cleared before you return a kit.
5. Tell me right away if something breaks. Reporting it is not the part you get in trouble for.
6. If gear keeps coming back wrong, you shoot with a partner instead of on your own.

The full procedure is in the Equipment Checkout handout.

## Classroom Expectations

1. Bell ringer when you come in, exit ticket before you leave.
2. No phones. That is district policy. No headphones, except when an assignment has you monitoring audio. I
   will tell you when that is.
3. Take care of the room and the gear.
4. You will screen unfinished work and get feedback. That is part of the class.
5. What you shoot for this class is school appropriate, and everyone on camera has agreed to be on camera.

## AI Use

Learning is about the process. If AI does the planning, writing, shooting, or editing that the assignment asked
you to do, it is not allowed.

Fine: brainstorming interview questions with you, explaining a technique, helping with an error.

Not allowed: AI generated footage, voice, or music turned in as your work, and AI written scripts or
reflections.

If an assignment does allow AI, say what you used. If you are not sure, ask me.
