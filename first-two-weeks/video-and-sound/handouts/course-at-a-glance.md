# Video and Sound at a Glance

Mr. Mankin | Room 173 | Full year, 1 credit

## What This Class Is

You plan it, shoot it, record it, cut it, and publish it. Cameras, microphones, lighting, and editing in Adobe
Premiere, with real gear you check out and are responsible for.

## What You Will Make

| Quarter | Work |
|---------|------|
| Q1 | Portfolio site, camera and audio drills, a short interview piece |
| Q2 | Pre-production package, a lighting and camera project, a full edited short |
| Q3 | Competition piece, sound design work, emerging tech project |
| Q4 | Capstone production, portfolio presentation |

## Grading

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Projects | 40% |
| Labs | 20% |
| Assessments | 30% |
| Readiness and Participation | 10% |

Quarter grades are not recorded below 50%. Late work is accepted with no penalty. If submissions have closed,
ask me to reopen them. Shoot days and crew days are marked no late submissions, because they only happen once.

## Gear

DSLR bodies and lenses, microphones and recorders, tripods and lights, and Adobe Premiere on the lab machines.
All of it is checked out and checked back in. See the Equipment Checkout handout.

## What You Need Every Day

- Your school Google login
- Phone away, headphones off except when we are monitoring audio
- Something to write with, since shot lists get sketched on paper

## Exam

Ohio WebXam in the spring. We take an ungraded pretest early so we know where to aim.

## Competition

We are starting a BPA chapter this year and every student competes in some form, either a BPA event or our
local competition in January. Video production, broadcast news, and audio events all fit this class.
