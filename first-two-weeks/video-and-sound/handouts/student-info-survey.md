# Video and Sound: Student Info Survey (Google Form, copy-paste ready)

**Build:** new Google Form -> paste each block. Question type in **bold**.
**Assign:** Day 01 via Classroom. Collect emails ON, one response per person.

---

**Form title:** Video and Sound, Getting to Know You

**Form description:**
> Welcome! About five minutes. This helps me set seating, plan projects around what you actually watch and
> listen to, and know what to call you.

---

### Section 1: Basics

**1. What name should I call you?** *(Short answer, required)*

**2. How do you say it?** *(Short answer)*

**3. Grade level** *(Multiple choice, required)*: 9 / 10 / 11 / 12

**4. Any pronouns I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*

---

### Section 2: Experience

**5. Have you used any of these?** *(Checkboxes)*
- A DSLR or mirrorless camera
- A microphone or audio recorder
- Video editing software (any: including CapCut, iMovie, Premiere, Resolve)
- Audio editing software (Audacity, GarageBand, etc.)
- Streaming or recording software (OBS)
- Made and posted videos of my own
- None of these

**6. If you have edited video before, what did you use and what did you make?** *(Paragraph)*

**7. Comfort with learning new software** *(Linear scale 1-5)*: 1 = nervous, 5 = I figure it out myself

---

### Section 3: You

**8. Why did you sign up for this class?** *(Paragraph, required)*

**9. What kind of video or audio do you actually watch or listen to?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Creators, shows, films, podcasts, music. Be honest, this is how I pick examples that aren't boring.

**10. If you could make anything this year and it would definitely turn out well, what would it be?** *(Paragraph)*

**11. What are you into outside of school?** *(Paragraph)*

**12. Career or college plans right now?** *(Short answer)*: "no idea" is fine

---

### Section 4: Logistics

**13. Seating: anything that helps you focus?** *(Paragraph)*

**14. Are you comfortable being on camera?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes
- Only if I have to
- I would strongly prefer to work behind the camera

Description: There is real work on both sides of the camera. This does not limit your grade.

**15. Do you have a computer and internet at home?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, my own computer and reliable internet
- Yes, a shared computer
- Phone only
- Not really

**16. Do you have your own camera or recording gear?** *(Short answer)*

**17. Any activity that regularly pulls you out of class, or that you'd want to cover on camera?** *(Short answer)*
Description: Sports, band, theater, work, also tells me where the good footage is.

**18. Interested in hearing about BPA (video, broadcast, and audio competitions)?** *(Multiple choice)*: Yes / Maybe later / No

**19. Anything else you want me to know?** *(Paragraph)*
