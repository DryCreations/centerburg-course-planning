# Video and Sound: Classroom Procedures

**Room 173, Mr. Mankin**

---

## Coming In

1. Assigned seat, machine awake, logged in.
2. **Bell ringer** on the board and in Classroom: start immediately, 5 minutes.
3. Bags off the desks. Nothing on top of the machines or near the gear cabinet.

## Phones & Headphones

- Phones away, district policy.
- **No headphones**: with one exception: when an assignment requires you to monitor audio, I will say so and
  hand out what you need. That is the only time.

## Food & Drink

Closed water bottle on the floor. Nothing else, not in the lab, not near the cameras, never in the studio space.

## Your Files

- Footage and projects live in your **school Google Drive** and, for large files, the location I assign for
  that project. Never only on a lab machine desktop and never only on a memory card.
- Naming: `lastname-project-scene-take.ext`, e.g. `mankin-interview-jenkins-take3.mp4`.
- **Offload before you check gear back in.** A cleared card is not a place your project lives.

## Equipment

The full procedure is in the *Equipment Checkout* handout. The short version:

1. Nothing leaves the cabinet without being checked out on the form.
2. Inspect it with me before you walk away.
3. Strap around your neck any time a camera is in your hands.
4. Back with 10 minutes left in the period: footage offloaded, card cleared, battery charging, case complete.
5. **Report damage immediately**: that is the part you do not get in trouble for.

## Shooting Around the Building

- You are representing this class. Doors you were not invited into stay closed.
- Nobody appears on camera who did not agree to appear on camera. Ask first, every time.
- Do not disrupt another class. If a teacher says no, that is the answer.
- Stay in the areas we agreed on and be back on time.

## Audio Recording

- Levels checked before you roll, not after.
- Headphones on while recording: this is the exception to the headphone rule and it is required, not optional.
- Room tone: 30 seconds of silence at every location. You will thank yourself in the edit.

## Getting Help

1. Re-read the assignment. 2. Try one thing. 3. Ask a neighbor. 4. Ask me.

## Screening & Critique

We watch each other's cuts. Feedback is about the work: what is working, what confused you, one specific
suggestion. Nobody apologizes for their own work before it plays.

## Turning Work In

Google Classroom, on the assignment post. Video submitted as a link (Drive or the platform we agree on) with
sharing set so I can actually open it. Check the link in a private window first.

## Leaving the Room

Sign out on the pass log. One at a time. Not during a demo or a screening.

## End of Period

1. **Gear back and checked in first**: before anything else, with enough time left to do it properly.
2. Exit ticket submitted.
3. Project saved, files in Drive.
4. Chairs in, station reset, trash gone.
5. Log off.

## Emergencies

Routes posted by the door. Take nothing, leave everything running, stay with the class.
