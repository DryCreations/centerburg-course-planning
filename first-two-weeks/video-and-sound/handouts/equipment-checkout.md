# Equipment Checkout System: Room 173 (all courses)

**One system for the whole room.** Video & Sound, Yearbook, Design Techniques, and Aviation UAS all check gear
out through the same form and the same sheet, with a course column, so a camera signed out sixth period is
visible to the student asking for it seventh.

**Two audiences in one document.** Part 1 is the student-facing handout (print / post in Classroom for every
course that touches gear). Part 2 is the teacher-side setup and build list.

---

# Part 1: Student Handout: Checking Out Gear

## The Basic Rule

**Nothing leaves the cabinet without being checked out. Nothing goes back without being checked in.**
If it is under your name, it is your responsibility until I confirm it is back.

## How to Check Out

1. Ask. Gear does not come out during a demo or during the first five minutes of class.
2. Open the **Checkout Form** (link in Google Classroom) and record: your name, **which course you are in**,
   class period, the item's **ID tag number**, and where it is going (in the room / around the building /
   off campus).
3. Inspect the item with me or with the checkout partner before you walk away:
   - Body and lens for damage
   - Battery charged
   - Memory card in and empty
   - All pieces present against the kit card in the case
4. Take the whole case. Cases keep small parts from disappearing.

## While You Have It

- Camera strap around your neck any time it is in your hands. Not on your wrist. Not "just for a second."
- Lens cap on when you are not shooting.
- Never set a camera on the edge of anything.
- Do not swap lenses in a dusty or windy spot. Body facing down, quick and clean.
- Do not hand your gear to someone who did not check it out.

## How to Check In

1. Come back with **at least 10 minutes left** in the period. Returns are not a bell-beater.
2. Offload your footage first: anything left on the card may be gone.
3. Card cleared (after offload), battery on the charger, lens cap on, everything back in the case against the
   kit card.
4. Complete the **Check-In Form** and hand the case to me. I confirm.

## Off-Campus and After-Hours Use

Possible, not automatic. Ask at least **two days ahead**, tell me what you are shooting and when it comes back,
and get a yes from me before you make plans. Overnight checkout is a privilege that starts once you have shown
you return gear correctly.

## Damage, Loss, and Problems

**Report it immediately.** Broken gear is a problem I can solve. Hidden broken gear is a problem for the next
student, and a discipline issue for you.

If something stops working while you are shooting: stop, do not force anything, bring it back and tell me what
happened.

## What Happens If You Do Not Follow This

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Late return, first time | Reminder, noted |
| Repeated late returns | You shoot in class only, with a partner, for two weeks |
| Gear returned incomplete or uncharged | You reset the kit before you shoot again |
| Unreported damage | Loss of independent checkout for the quarter |
| Damage that you reported | We deal with it, no penalty |

# Part 2: Teacher Setup (not for students)

**Decided:** one **shared checkout system across all courses**: Video & Sound, Yearbook, Design Techniques
(photo unit), and Aviation UAS all use the same Google Form and the same response Sheet, with a **course
column**. That way a camera signed out sixth period is visible to the student asking for it seventh period.

## System design

| Piece | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Tracking | Google Form -> response Sheet (laminated paper backup by the cabinet for when a device is not handy) |
| Scope | **One system, all courses.** Course is a required dropdown on the form. |
| ID tagging | Numbered label on every item; QR sticker that opens the form pre-filled with the item ID |
| Kit unit | **Case kits**, body + lens + battery + card + strap, with a laminated kit card inside |
| Return confirmation | Teacher-confirmed for at least Q1 |
| Off-campus | Earned privilege, requested two days ahead (as written in Part 1) |

## Sheet structure

One response Sheet, one row per transaction:

| Column | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Timestamp | automatic |
| Student name | |
| **Course** | Video & Sound, Yearbook, Design Techniques, Aviation UAS |
| Period | |
| Item ID | dropdown, all tagged items |
| Out / In | which action this row is |
| Destination | in room, in building, off campus, event *(name it)* |
| Condition notes | |
| Card cleared / battery charging | check-in only |

Filter views to build once:
- **Currently out**: items with a checkout row and no matching check-in
- **By course**: what each class has out right now
- **Overdue**: out longer than one period without an approved off-campus request
