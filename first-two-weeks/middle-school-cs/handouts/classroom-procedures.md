# Middle School CS: Lab Procedures

**Room 173, Mr. Mankin**
This room is shared with four high school classes. The equipment in here is real and it is expensive, and you
get to use all of it as long as we take care of it.

---

## Coming In

1. Assigned seat. Machine on, logged in with your school account.
2. **Bell ringer** is on the board: 5 minutes, start right away.
3. Bags on the floor, not on the desks.

## Phones & Headphones

- Phones away. District policy.
- No headphones.

## Food & Drink

Closed water bottle on the floor. Nothing else, not near the computers, the robots, or the printers.

## Your Files

- Everything saves to your **school Google Drive**. Not the desktop: desktops get erased.
- Name files like this: `lastname-project.ext`
- Turn work in through **Google Classroom**.

## Robots (VEX IQ and Sphero)

1. Robots are handed out and collected: you do not go get one.
   **We have two Spheros**, so robot time runs as a station with a queue. Your written plan is what earns your
   run, and waiting your turn is part of how this class works.
2. **On the floor or on the table, never both at once.** A Sphero at speed on a table becomes a Sphero on the floor.
3. Drive at a speed where you can stop it. Not at people, not at walls, not at the printers.
4. Batteries go back on the charger, every time.
5. Parts stay with their kit. A VEX kit missing three pieces is a broken kit for the next class.

## 3D Printers

1. Nobody starts a print without me.
2. Do not open, touch, or reach into a running printer. The nozzle is around 200°C and it does not look hot.
3. Do not pull a print off the bed yourself: come get me.
4. Prints take hours. Plan for it; do not expect to design something at 9:00 and hold it at 10:00.

## Cameras and Other Gear

Checked out and checked back in, with the strap around your neck any time a camera is in your hands.
Same rules the high school classes follow.

## When Something Breaks

**Tell me.** Immediately. Broken equipment is a normal thing that happens in a lab. Hidden broken equipment is
the problem, the next person gets hurt or loses their work.

Nobody has ever gotten in trouble in this room for reporting a problem.

## Getting Help

1. Read the error or re-read the instructions.
2. Try one thing.
3. Ask the person next to you.
4. Ask me.

**Helping means explaining, not taking over.** Do not grab someone else's mouse or drive someone else's robot.

## When Things Do Not Work

Your code will break. Your robot will drive into a wall. Your print will fail. **That is the class.** The skill
being taught is what you do in the next five minutes, not whether it worked the first time.

## End of Period

1. Exit ticket submitted.
2. Work saved to Drive.
3. Robots, parts, and gear returned; batteries charging.
4. Chairs in, station clear, trash picked up.
5. Log off.

## Emergencies

Fire, tornado, and lockdown routes are posted by the door. Leave everything, follow me, stay with our class.
