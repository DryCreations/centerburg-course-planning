# Middle School CS: Student Info Survey (Google Form, copy-paste ready)

**Build:** new Google Form -> paste each block. Question type in **bold**.
**Assign:** Day 01 via Classroom. Collect emails ON, one response per person.
Kept shorter and simpler than the high school versions.

---

**Form title:** Computer Science, Getting to Know You

**Form description:**
> Welcome! This takes about five minutes. It just helps me learn who you are, what you already know, and what
> to call you.

---

### Section 1: Basics

**1. What name should I call you?** *(Short answer, required)*

**2. How do you say it?** *(Short answer)*

**3. Grade** *(Multiple choice, required)*: 7 / 8

**4. Any pronouns I should know?** *(Short answer, optional)*

---

### Section 2: What You've Done Before

**5. Have you tried any of these?** *(Checkboxes)*
- Scratch
- MakeCode (Arcade or micro:bit)
- Code.org
- Minecraft redstone or command blocks
- Roblox Studio
- Typed real code (Python, JavaScript, anything)
- Built with LEGO robotics, VEX, or similar
- Driven a Sphero or a robot
- Used a 3D printer
- None of these

**6. How do you feel about computers?** *(Multiple choice)*
- I love them and mess around with them a lot
- I use them fine, nothing special
- They frustrate me
- I mostly use a phone or tablet, not a computer

**7. When something doesn't work on a computer, what do you usually do?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Try to figure it out myself
- Look it up
- Ask someone
- Give up and do something else

Description: There is no wrong answer here. I just want to know.

---

### Section 3: You

**8. What are you into?** *(Paragraph)*
Description: Games, sports, animals, art, music, building things, cars, anything. This helps me pick project
examples that aren't boring.

**9. What is something you're good at?** *(Short answer)*
Description: It does not have to be school-related.

**10. Which of these sounds the most fun this year?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Programming and coding
- Building and driving robots
- 3D printing things I designed
- Working with data and charts
- Learning how the internet and hacking work
- Not sure yet

**11. Is there anything you're worried about in this class?** *(Paragraph)*

---

### Section 4: Logistics

**12. Where do you like to sit to focus best?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Front
- Middle
- Back
- Wherever, I don't care

**13. Is there anyone you should probably not sit next to?** *(Short answer)*
Description: Just between us.

**14. Do you have a computer and internet at home?** *(Multiple choice)*
- Yes, my own computer and good internet
- Yes, a computer I share
- Phone or tablet only
- Not really

**15. Anything else you want me to know?** *(Paragraph)*

---

## Teacher follow-up

- [ ] Seating chart from Q12 and Q13
- [ ] Note the "they frustrate me" and "give up" answers (Q6, Q7). Those students need an early win.
- [ ] Interest split from Q10: helps sequence Q1 and pick the Q4 choice-project options
- [ ] Home tech from Q14: never assign anything that requires a home computer
- [ ] Q11 worries: address the common ones out loud on Day 02 without naming anyone
