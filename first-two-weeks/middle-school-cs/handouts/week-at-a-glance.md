# Middle School CS: This Week

**Aug 24 to 28**

## Standards

Ohio Computer Science, grades 6 to 8.

- Programming, Variables: make and use a variable to keep score
- Programming, Control: use a conditional to win or end a game
- Algorithmic Thinking: use velocity and events to make sprites move and interact
- Program development: test, find a bug, change one thing, and log it
