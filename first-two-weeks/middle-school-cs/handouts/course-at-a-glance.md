# Middle School Computer Science at a Glance

Mr. Mankin | Room 173 | Grades 7 and 8 | Full year

## What This Class Is

Computer science is more than typing code. This year you will program, build and drive robots, work with data,
learn how the internet works, design and 3D print things, and talk about what technology should be allowed to
do.

Every quarter covers several topics, so you get a look at the whole field.

## What You Will Do

| Quarter | Work |
|---------|------|
| Q1 | Take apart how computers work, build your first games in MakeCode Arcade, drive Spheros |
| Q2 | Build and program VEX IQ robots, learn debugging, see how the internet moves data |
| Q3 | Work with data and charts, design and 3D print something you invented |
| Q4 | A build project of your choice, AI, cybersecurity, showcase |

## Grading

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Projects | 40% |
| Labs and Activities | 25% |
| Checkpoints | 25% |
| Readiness | 10% |

You can redo projects. If it did not go well, come talk to me, fix it, and turn it in again. Learning it on the
second try still counts.

Late work is accepted with no penalty. If submissions have closed, ask me to reopen them.

## The Rule That Matters Most

You have to be able to explain what you turned in. Not memorized, explained. If AI wrote it, or a friend wrote
it, and you cannot explain how it works, it does not count yet.

## What You Need Every Day

- Your school Google login
- Phone away, no headphones
- Something to write with. A lot of this starts on paper.

## Things Will Go Wrong

Your code will break. Your robot will drive into a wall. Your 3D print will fail. That is normal here, and what
you do next is the skill.

## Where This Leads

This class feeds the high school courses in this same room: Design Techniques, Video and Sound, and Aviation
Unmanned Aircraft.
