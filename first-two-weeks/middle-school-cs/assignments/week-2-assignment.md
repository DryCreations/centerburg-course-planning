## Part B: Add a Score and a Win Condition (20 pts), Day 05

Take the Arcade program you turned in last week and make it a game.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | A variable that keeps score, set to 0 at the start |
| 2 | The score changes when something happens in the game |
| 3 | A conditional that does something when the score reaches a number: game over, a message, a new level |
| 4 | Your loop and event from last week still work |
| 5 | An explanation, in your own words, of what your variable does and what your conditional does |
| 6 | Project link shared and submitted on Classroom |

If your week 1 program was thin, rebuild it here instead of patching it. That is not starting over, that is
what a second draft is.

Requirement 5 is the one that matters. If you cannot explain it, it does not count yet. Ask me.

## Part D: Debug Log (15 pts), Day 07

We have two Spheros, so you are logging **either** your Sphero run **or** your Arcade debugging. Both count
the same, the skill is identical.

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | The program you wrote **on paper** (Sphero) or your starting version (Arcade), before changes |
| 2 | Every run logged: what it did, what you thought was wrong, what you changed, what happened |
| 3 | Your final working program |
| 4 | One sentence: what is different between the version you started with and the one that worked? |
| 5 | Everyone on your team can explain the final program |

A log with no failures in it is not a good log. **Failures are the content.**
