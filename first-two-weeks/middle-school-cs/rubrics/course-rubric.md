# Middle School CS: Project & Activity Rubric

Close to the high school pathway rubric, since these students move into those courses, but with fewer
criteria and more room for a rough first attempt.

---

## Grade Categories

| Category | Weight | What lands here |
|----------|-------:|-----------------|
| Projects | 40% | Builds, programs, finished work |
| Labs & Activities | 25% | Guided practice, unplugged activities, drills |
| Checkpoints | 25% | Short quizzes and skill checks |
| Readiness | 10% | Bell ringers and exit tickets |

---

## Project Rubric, 40 points

| Criterion | Got it (4) | Almost (3) | Getting there (2) | Not yet (1) | Weight |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------|-------:|
| **It works** | Does everything the assignment asked | Does most of it | Partly works | Does not run or function | 35% |
| **You can explain it** | Explains how and why every part works | Explains most of it | Explains some of it | Cannot explain it | 30% |
| **You kept going** | Tried multiple approaches; debugged persistently | Worked through problems with some help | Needed a lot of prompting | Stopped at the first problem | 20% |
| **Finished and turned in right** | On time, complete, named correctly | Minor pieces missing | Late or incomplete | Not submitted | 15% |

**"You can explain it" is the AI check and the learning check at the same time.** If you cannot explain what
you turned in, it does not count yet, and that is a fixable situation, not a punishment.

## Lab & Activity Score, 10 points

| Points | Meaning |
|-------:|---------|
| 10 | Completed all steps, documented what happened |
| 8 | Completed the work, thin documentation |
| 6 | Partly done |
| 3 | Started, did not get far |
| 0 | Not attempted |

## Readiness, 2 points per day

| Points | Meaning |
|-------:|---------|
| 2 | Bell ringer and exit ticket done and on topic |
| 1 | One of the two |
| 0 | Neither |

Absent days are excused, not zeroes.

## Redo Policy

Any project can be redone for a better grade. Come talk to me, fix it, resubmit. Getting it on the second try
still means you got it.

## Group Work

When you build in a group, each person still submits their own explanation of how it works. The build is
shared; understanding it is not.
