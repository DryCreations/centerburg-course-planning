## What Happened

- Lateral thinking puzzles as the opener
- Intro to MakeCode Arcade, worked through the built in tutorial

## Why That Worked

The puzzles do the same job the sandwich activity was going to do: they force students to question the
assumption they walked in with and try a second approach. That is the debugging habit, and it is now the
reference point for the rest of the course. When a program does not work, the question is the same one from
the puzzles: what am I assuming that is not true?

## Carry Forward

- Keep a couple of puzzles in reserve as bell ringers. They are a good Monday opener all year.
