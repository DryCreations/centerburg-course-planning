# Day 02 - Thu, Aug 20 (Week 1)

**Course:** Middle School CS, **Unit:** Orientation and First Programs
**Strands:** Algorithmic Thinking and Programming, Computing Systems

## Objective

Students know the lab procedures, and they can build a small Arcade program of their own using an event, a
sequence, and a loop.

## Materials

Printed lab procedures, a VEX IQ kit and a Sphero out on the front table for the procedures walk, Arcade open
on the machines, scavenger hunt in Classroom.

## Bell Ringer

One more lateral thinking puzzle. Then: what assumption made it hard?

Tie that straight to code. When a program does not work, the bug is almost always an assumption you did not
know you were making.

## Direct Instruction

**Lab procedures, about twelve minutes, walking the room.**

1. Robots are handed out, not taken. We have two Spheros, so robot time is a station with a queue.
2. VEX IQ parts stay with their kit. A kit missing pieces is a broken kit for the next class.
3. 3D printers: nobody starts a print alone, nothing gets touched while it runs, the nozzle does not look hot.
4. Tell me right away when something breaks. Nobody gets in trouble here for reporting a problem.
5. Helping means explaining, not grabbing someone's mouse.

**Grading, short.** The four categories, the redo policy, and the rule that you have to be able to explain what
you turned in. Lead with the redo policy, that is the part they care about.

**Arcade, moving past the tutorial.** Show the three blocks the tutorial used and name them: event, sequence,
loop. Then build something small live, break it on purpose, and fix it out loud.

## Work Time

Start the Week 1 assignment: your own small Arcade program with an event, at least four actions, and a loop,
plus an explanation of what each part does.

## Closure

Exit ticket: what is your loop repeating, and what would happen if you deleted it?
