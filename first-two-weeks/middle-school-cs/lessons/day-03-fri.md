# Day 03 - Fri, Aug 21 (Week 1)

**Course:** Middle School CS, **Unit:** Algorithms
**Strands:** Algorithmic Thinking and Programming

## Objective

Students can write an algorithm precise enough for a machine to follow, and can explain the difference between
what they meant and what they wrote.

Two Spheros, so the robot half runs as a station with a queue. The wait is useful here: teams have to write and
check a plan before they get a run.

## Materials

Both Spheros charged, painter's tape course on the floor, planning sheets printed one per team, Arcade open at
the desks.

## Bell Ringer

Write instructions for tying a shoe. Five steps, no pictures.

## Whole Class: Exact Instructions

1. Students write instructions for making a peanut butter sandwich, six steps or fewer.
2. Follow one exactly as written, like a machine. Closed jar on the loaf. Play it straight.
3. Do two or three, then have them revise.
4. Name the vocabulary: algorithm, bug, debugging. Connect it back to Wednesday's puzzles, since the failure is
   the same one. You assumed something the machine did not know.

## Station Rotation

Teams of three, rotating every six to eight minutes.

**At the Sphero station, two teams at a time:**

1. Handling rules in thirty seconds: floor only, speed you can stop, back on the charger after.
2. Hand me the written command sequence before touching the robot.
3. One run. Watch what goes wrong, write the fix.
4. Second run if there is time, then rotate out.

**At the desks:**

- Write and refine the team command sequence: direction, speed, duration for each move.
- Predict in writing what will go wrong. This gets scored.
- Finish the Week 1 Arcade program, which is due today.

## Closure

Spheros back and charging.

Exit ticket: what was your first bug, and how did you fix it? If your team has not run yet, what did you learn
watching someone else?
