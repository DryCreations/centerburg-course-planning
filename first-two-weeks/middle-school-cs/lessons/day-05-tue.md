# Day 05 - Tue, Aug 25 (Week 2)

**Course:** Middle School CS, **Unit:** MakeCode Arcade, Build a Game
**Strands:** Algorithmic Thinking and Programming

> **The week is one Arcade game, built a piece a day.** Last week they made a card where things moved. This
> week they build a game you can play, win, and lose. Today is the moving pieces: a player they control and a
> sprite that moves on its own with velocity.

## Objective

Students can create a player sprite they control with the buttons and a second sprite that moves on its own
using velocity, and can explain the difference between position and velocity.

## Materials

Arcade open and tested, projector, the Day 05 slides (`slides/week-2-slides.md`), the game assignment posted in
Classroom. No printouts needed.

## Bell Ringer (5 min)

**Bell Ringer 1** from `handouts/bell-ringers-week-2.md`, on the board. A sprite with a velocity: which way does
it move and how far. Two minutes, then go over it out loud. This is the new daily routine.

## Direct Instruction (12 min)

Slides Day 05, but the teaching is the **live demo**, not the slides.

1. Frame the week: same tool, more depth. They already have sprites, events, position, velocity.
2. **Position vs. velocity.** Position is where it is; velocity is how fast and which way. Set a velocity once
   and it keeps moving with no loop.
3. **Which way is which:** vx right/left, vy down/up, x=0 left edge, y=0 top.
4. **Demo:** make a sprite at the top, set vy to 40, watch it fall with no loop. That is the bell ringer, alive.

## Work Time (25 min): Part A

Students start their game (Catch it or Dodge it, their pick):

- A player sprite they move with the buttons
- One other sprite that moves on its own with velocity
- No score yet. That is tomorrow.

## Closure / Exit Ticket (5 min)

**Exit ticket:** *In one sentence, the difference between position and velocity.*
